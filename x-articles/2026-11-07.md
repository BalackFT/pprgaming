# Alpha 5.0 Isn't a Number Change. It's the Planet Update We've Been Waiting For.

Look.....I've been logging into Star Citizen most days for a long time now. Lately it's grab a mission, fly it, log out, because the planet side still feels like a pretty rock with POIs taped on. So when Cloud Imperium renamed Alpha 4.11 to Alpha 5.0 this week, I didn't shrug it off as marketing. That rename is CIG saying Genesis is finally the real deal.

## What actually happened this week

On Wednesday, RSI put it on X and Nicou-CIG dropped the November 4 Roadmap Roundup on Spectrum: **Alpha 4.11 is now Alpha 5.0**. CIG's own wording is that the new number reflects "the significance of the new technologies and features coming together in this release," and that it's the first time Genesis technologies come together as "the foundation for how planets are built and explored going forward."

The Release View lists Alpha 5.0 for Q4 2026. No specific LIVE date. Here's what they marked Committed:

- **Starchitect:** procedural world-building that spreads locations across a planet from modular tiles, with planetside sandbox missions and faction-owned ground POIs.
- **Planet Tech V5:** a full rework of how planets are built, populated and rendered, with emergent biomes, smarter flora and rock placement, and GPU-driven generation.
- **Population Manager:** load-balances servers and fills areas with inhabitants that fit the surroundings.
- **Contract Boards:** brand new and straight in as Committed. Guild Halls, missions from local through system scale, faction filtering. Hub-and-spoke, on paper.
- **DGS Planet Subdivision:** splits planets and moons into smaller server territories so one box isn't running an entire world.

**Nyx I** is first in line. Nyx II and Nyx III slid tentatively to **Alpha 5.1** in Q1 2027.

Then they stopped arguing about a graphic and opened the **first true Alpha 5.0 PTU**. Wave 1 build 13119881. Wakapedia-CIG's notes open with "Welcome to the first 5.0 PTU!" Guild Halls on Nyx I with mission boards. A real environment and weather pass. Flora that stops spawning on ground it doesn't belong on. Lighting that reacts to actual cloud cover. More contracts on the boards. As always on PTU: Wave 1, features can move before LIVE. I'm not inventing a ship date out of that build.

And while that was landing, RSI locked **IAE 2956** for November 13 through 27 at Tobin Expo Center in New Babbage, Free Fly the whole window, 200+ rentals. Big ship week stacked on a big tech week. That's a hell of a Star Citizen week.

## Why this matters if you actually play

4.0 was server meshing and Pyro. That changed how the 'verse *connects*. 5.0 is trying to change what it feels like to be *on* a planet.

If Starchitect and Planet Tech V5 hold up, you stop treating a planet like wallpaper you land on for a bunker. You get POIs that belong to factions, biomes that make physical sense, and a planet split across servers so crowds don't melt the box. Contract Boards in Guild Halls are the part I care about most as a player who currently one-and-dones a mission. Give me a board on the ground, reputation with a guild, and a reason to stay planetside past the first turn-in, and my login habit changes overnight.

I already poked at the planet tech myself after the rename. Short exploration pass. Enough to convince me the 4.11 to 5.0 branding was the right call for what they're shipping. Fricken amazing from the cockpit. Wave 1 PTU is not LIVE polish. But Guild Halls and a real planet pass in the FIRST 5.0 PTU is CIG putting their money where the roadmap mouth is.

Fair part: Committed isn't LIVE. I'd rather get one planet done RIGHT than three half-baked ones, so Nyx II and III waiting for 5.1 is fine by me. Item Recovery / ship loadout improvements got pulled from the Release View for more work. And on the wipe chatter this week: Nicou-CIG made clear a wipe is **not confirmed**. That is NOT the same thing as "there will be no wipe." I'm not writing a no-wipe story. Treat wipe talk as unconfirmed until CIG says it plain.

## My take

Creators and commenters spent part of this week acting like a version bump is a crime. Look. If they want to brand it 5.0, so be it. Genesis is enough of a change that I really don't blame them. Let them cook how they want.

I've watched "Planet Tech" and "Genesis" sit on slides for years. Seeing CIG put a whole major version number on it, open PTU the next day, and park Guild Halls on Nyx I tells me they think this is the foundation, not a patch note. I respect the skepticism. But I'm not dunking on the most ambitious planet pass this project has lined up just because the number jumped.

Ambition isn't a bug. It's the point. Nyx I, I'm coming for you. Clear the calendar for IAE while you're at it. Full breakdown of the rename and the Genesis stack is here:
