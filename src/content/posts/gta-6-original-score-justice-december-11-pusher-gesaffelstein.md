**The GTA 6 score has a date.** Rockstar Games will release **Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score** by French electronic duo **Justice** on **December 11**, [Gematsu reports](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/10/grand-theft-auto-vi-original-score-launches-december-11), a little over three weeks after the game's November 19 launch.

**Formats:** The album is coming to all major music streaming services, plus **double CD** and **triple vinyl**. The [Justice site](https://justice.church/pages/gtavi) lists a Double CD, a Triple Black Vinyl, a Limited Triple Crystal Clear Vinyl and a Digital Edition, and says all physical formats include bonus content. Pre-saves and pre-orders are open now.

**What's on it:** 30 tracks from Justice's material for the game, including **"Flower in a Hurricane"** featuring Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard, the song from the GTA VI launch trailer. Rockstar's [press release](https://prtimes.jp/main/html/rd/p/000000024.000106039.html) adds that digital bonus tracks are included as well, without saying how many.

**Out now:** The new single **"Pusher,"** featuring French electronic artist **Gesaffelstein**, is streaming now. The Justice site calls it a preview of the instrumental score and their first-ever collaboration with Gesaffelstein.

This is separate from *Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album*, the collection of licensed singles Rockstar has been rolling out ahead of launch.

**PPR take:** A score album dropping after launch is a smart move. By December 11 people will have heard these tracks in the game, and that's when you go looking for them.
