**Valorant's next Duelist is one sleep away.** **Hush**, the game's 30th agent and its ninth Duelist, goes live on **Tuesday, October 27** with Patch 13.09. Riot revealed her on October 18 with the [RISE AND FALL trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AfcCyTsh4ag) before the Champions Shanghai grand final. She's a telekinetic operative from China, and her whole kit is built around moving things after she's already thrown them.

**The kit, per Riot's ability text** (as published by [Bleeding Cool](https://bleedingcool.com/games/valorant-shows-off-latest-agent-hush-arriving-october-27/)):

- **Telekinesis (passive):** pick up weapons and the Spike, and hit switches, from much farther away than other agents. It sounds minor, but grabbing a dropped rifle or the Spike without stepping into a sightline wins rounds.
- **Flow (C):** instantly drop an anchor, then launch toward it after a short delay. Use it in the air and Hush hovers in place for a moment first.
- **Flashbulb (Q):** throw it, or prepare it with alt-fire, then reactivate to send it toward your crosshair. It pops on any surface or after a delay and blinds anyone looking at it. Yes, you can curve it around corners.
- **Displacement (E, signature):** same throw-and-steer controls. On detonation it drags everyone toward the centre and **concusses anyone who hits a wall or another player**. It refreshes every two kills.
- **Suspension (X, ultimate):** fire a projectile you steer with your crosshair, then detonate it to **levitate every player** in the area. Floating targets can't dodge your team's follow-up.

**Things to know before you queue.** Riot hasn't posted the full 13.09 patch notes yet. The [game updates page](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/news/game-updates/) still tops out at 13.08, so exact costs and cooldowns aren't confirmed. Hush is also the first agent since Riot's new Agent Mastery system, with her own in-game reward track. Depending on time zone, some Asia-Pacific players will get her on October 28 local time, [AllThingsHow notes](https://allthings.how/valorant-hush-release-date/). Also note that Suspension lifts *every* player it catches, your own team included. Be careful where you aim it.

**PPR take:** A flash you can steer around a corner and a pull that slams people into walls will make for some brutal highlight reels. Duelist mains, start practising your crosshair placement.
