**Azeroth gets a fresh start tonight.** Blizzard says [World of Warcraft: Forever goes live globally today, November 4](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24315820/world-of-warcraft-forever-goes-live-globally-november-4), at **3 p.m. PST, which is 6 p.m. ET** (23:00 UTC). It's a permanent third version of WoW that sits alongside modern WoW and Classic, and it's set after Warcraft III Reforged: Forsaken Kingdom and before Molten Core, with an adventure across the original continents up to level 60.

You don't need to buy anything new to play. Blizzard's [pre-purchase page](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24301508/pre-purchase-world-of-warcraft-forever-upgrades-and-begin-your-next-journey-in-azeroth) says access is included with a World of Warcraft subscription or Game Time. The paid upgrade packs mostly matter for one thing: as [Wowhead's editions guide](https://www.wowhead.com/forever/guide/pre-order-packs-rewards) points out, you need at least the Skyborne Heroic Pack to play the new Skyborne race and its starting zone.

## What's actually new

**A bigger old world.** Forever adds new and reimagined zones and more than 1,000 new quests across the 1–60 journey. Mount Hyjal deals with the aftermath of Archimonde's defeat, Shen'dralas turns a hidden area between Mulgore and Desolace into a new adventure, and the Riverglades alone has more than 150 quests covering much of the mid-30s to mid-40s.

**New race and class combos.** The Skyborne start on Zephras Isle (levels 1–12) and can join either side: Horde Windshapers become Shaman, and Alliance High-Order Skyborne become Mages. Blizzard also lists new pairings like Orc Mages, Troll Warlocks, Undead Paladins, Human Hunters, Gnome Priests and Dwarf Shamans.

**Rulesets instead of realms.** You pick Normal, PvP, Roleplay or Roleplay PvP rather than a specific server, and characters get a two-part name that's unique across the region. Hardcore is coming "sometime after launch."

**Your choice of old or new feel.** On first login you choose a Classic or Enhanced preset, and each character can use HD or the original-style SD models. There's also an account-wide Legacy System for horizontal progression, a group Camping system and gamepad support.

If you pre-ordered a pack, your Invite-A-Friend codes let friends play without a subscription from November 4 through November 11. If you missed it, our earlier [stress test story](/news/world-of-warcraft-forever-stress-test-october-21/) covers how we got here.

**PPR take:** This is a smart move by Blizzard. Classic proved a lot of people want the old pace and the old world, but Classic is also stuck being a museum. Forever is the first time Blizzard is saying the original Azeroth gets to keep growing, with new zones and stories that never existed back in the day. The rulesets-over-realms change is the part I'm most curious about, since realm communities were a big reason the old game felt alive, and a realmless setup could either fix dead servers or make the world feel less personal. Having it included with the regular sub instead of a separate box is the right call too, since it means anyone already paying can try it tonight without a second thought. The big question is launch night itself. Queues and server strain are almost a WoW tradition, so if you're logging in at 6 p.m. ET, have some patience ready.
