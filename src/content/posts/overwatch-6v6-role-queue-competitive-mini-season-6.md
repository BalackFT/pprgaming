**Two tanks, two damage, two supports. The old Overwatch lineup is coming back to ranked, at least for a month.** In a new [Director's Take](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/24316733/directors-take-5v5-and-6v6-better-together/), game director Aaron Keller says Season 6 will launch with a four-week 6v6 Role Queue Competitive mini season, running right alongside the modes players already have.

"There will be three Competitive options of the game available on Day 1 of Season 6: our standard 5v5, Open Queue 6v6, and our Role Queue 6v6 test," Keller writes. The 2-2-2 mini season starts at season launch and runs for the first four weeks. [VGC reports](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/overwatch-brings-back-6v6-role-queue-following-player-feedback/) that Season 6 starts on December 8.

## Why now, and what's different

Keller admits Blizzard was "a little hesitant" to bring Role Queue back to 6v6 because queue times "could increase significantly." Needing two tanks per team, instead of one, is the obvious problem. He says the team ran two tests earlier this year with different queueing methods, and neither "really delivered on the promise" of what players wanted. He also says the audience turned out to be "very split."

This time, the mini season ships with what Keller calls "an impactful, specific balance patch for 6v6," which has been the other big request from 6v6 players. That patch also applies to 6v6 Open Queue, which stays in the game. Blizzard wants to see how many people play Open Queue 6v6 for the role flexibility and how many play it for the bigger teams, and running both at once should answer that. Keller says Season 6 also brings matchmaking changes that the team thinks will keep queues "more manageable," and that it will watch queue times closely.

What isn't changing: "Currently, we don't have any intention of moving the main mode of Overwatch to 6v6," Keller writes, though Blizzard now thinks of it as "a second among equals." The [PC Gamer write-up](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/fps/overwatch-is-adding-6v6-role-queue-to-its-competitive-play-weve-realised-that-there-doesnt-have-to-be-a-winner-between-5v5-and-6v6/) has more on how the community got here.

**PPR take:** This is the smart way to do it. Blizzard isn't ripping out 5v5 to make the 6v6 crowd happy, and it isn't pretending Open Queue 6v6 was good enough for ranked. It's adding a real test, giving it its own balance patch, and saying out loud that it's collecting data. The catch is the one Keller already named: tanks. Role Queue lives or dies on whether enough people want to tank, and doubling the tank slots is a big ask. If queues blow up in week one, a lot of folks will drift back to 5v5 no matter how much they love the old format. Four weeks isn't long, so if you've been asking for 2-2-2 for years, this is the time to actually show up and queue for it. Blizzard has been pretty clear that player time is what decides these things. And that "Just don't start asking for 7v7!" line in the post made me laugh.
