**The Division 2 finally has crossplay.** Ubisoft's [Spearfall season post](https://news.ubisoft.com/en-us/article/7A3TP0iVVCjafKPmuTTLoS/the-division-2-spearfall-white-tusk-invades-washington-dc-crossplay-available-now-and-more) went up Thursday, and the new season is out now with a long-requested feature riding along: you can party up with anyone, no matter what platform they're on.

## Crossplay, and how to turn it on

Crossplay is a toggle in the options menu from the character screen. Clans are crossplay-enabled by default, but clan leaders can still filter who joins by platform. If you leave crossplay off, leaderboards and clan spaces only show players on your own platform. Ubisoft is also pushing its New Friend Referral program alongside it, and anyone who already finished that program gets their progress refreshed on January 12.

## What else Spearfall changes

The story setup has Black Tusk leader Natalya Sokolova pulling back to New Mexico and sending her elite **White Tusk** units into D.C. For the first time, four main missions can be played on **Legendary** difficulty against them: the Air and Space Museum, Space Administration HQ, Grand Washington Hotel and Viewpoint Museum. White Tusk can also ambush you anywhere in the open world on Legendary.

The bigger build-crafting news is a permanent overhaul of **Prototype Gear**. Every Augment is now unlocked on every Prototype item so you pick what goes where, and a new Schematics system lets you tune those items further.

**Escalation** was rebuilt too. It goes from 10 tiers down to 4, there's no token cost to start a run anymore (tokens become Prototype Credits you earn for finishing), and Mutators are replaced by Directives meant to balance things across group sizes and builds.

On top of that there's a new season modifier built around capturing White Tusk tech, new gear including the Field General set and the August exotic AR, a new Classified Assignment called Black Tusk Razorback Salvage, and Legendary difficulty coming to the Echoes of Central Park DLC later in the season. The [full patch notes](https://thedivision2.com/spearfall) have the details on every modifier.

**PPR take:** Crossplay is the headline, and it's about time. The Division 2 has hung around a long time, and the biggest thing holding a lot of us back from jumping back in was that our friends were on a different box. That problem is gone now. The Escalation change is the other one I like. Making people pay tokens just to start a run never felt great, and cutting it to four tiers with rewards at the end is a lot friendlier for folks who only get a couple hours a night. Whether the White Tusk Legendary stuff holds up is something we'll find out once the hardcore crowd chews through it. But for a game this old, a season like this on Thanksgiving is a pretty good reason to dust it off and call up the squad....whatever they're playing on.
