**The toy factory is open for co-op.** Mob Entertainment's **Escape From Playtime** is out now in Steam Early Access, priced at $9.99 and discounted to $8.99 for launch ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2087540/Escape_from_Playtime/)). It's a co-op survival horror spin-off set in the Poppy Playtime universe, and it went live at noon ET today ([Tech Times](https://www.techtimes.com/articles/329345/20261021/poppy-playtime-goes-co-op-escape-playtime-hits-steam-early-access-today.htm)).

**How it plays.** You play as the Smiling Critters, the plushies from Chapter 3. A mysterious Voice sends your team into shifting sections of the factory to scavenge materials for "a promised home for toys," while rejected Playtime Co. experiments hunt you ([Games Press](https://www.gamespress.com/en-US/New-Trailer-Escape-From-Playtime-Beta-Announced-in-28-Games-Later-Show)). Runs mix scavenging with puzzles, stealth and light combat, and proximity voice chat is a double-edged sword, since talking too loud can get your whole crew caught.

**Bigger than the beta.** Mob's [launch post](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1721470/announcements/) says player feedback from the open beta pushed the game from four players to **1-8 player co-op**. All eight Smiling Critters are in. CatNap, Hoppy Hopscotch, Bobby Bearhug and DogDay are playable from the start, and CraftyCorn, KickinChicken, Bubba Bubbaphant and PickyPiggy unlock as you progress. A roadmap covers updates through the rest of 2026 and into 2027.

**And then there's Chapter 6.** Mob also put up a short teaser called ["What's the time?"](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3uURd82kFd4) on its YouTube channel today. Fan news accounts like [Mascot Horror News](https://x.com/mascotmassacre/status/2112974619129106827) say it's the first teaser for **Poppy Playtime Chapter 6** and that it points to a **2027** release. Mob hasn't put out a press release or a store page for Chapter 6 yet, so treat 2027 as a target year for now.

**PPR take:** Co-op horror on Steam has been huge since Lethal Company, and dropping a Chapter 6 tease on the same day is smart timing. Eight-player chaos for under ten bucks is an easy sell for a Halloween week game night.
