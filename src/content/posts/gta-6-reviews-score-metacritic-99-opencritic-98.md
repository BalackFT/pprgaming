**Grand Theft Auto VI has a 99 Metascore on Metacritic from 115 critic reviews, and every one of them is listed as positive.** OpenCritic has a 98 Top Critic Average with 100% of critics recommending it.

## The scores

- **Metacritic:** 99 from 115 critic reviews, "Universal Acclaim", 100% positive, 0% mixed, 0% negative. Metacritic's PS5 tab shows 99 from 10 reviews at the time we checked. The user score isn't up yet.
- **OpenCritic:** 98 Top Critic Average, 100% of critics recommend it.
- **Highest and lowest we've seen:** several outlets give it a full 100 on Metacritic, including [the Guardian](https://www.metacritic.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi/) and PlayStation Universe. The lowest listed score we saw was a 90 from Digitally Downloaded. [TheSixthAxis](https://opencritic.com/game/15398/grand-theft-auto-vi) gave it a 9/10.

These numbers move as more reviews land, so treat them as a snapshot from the morning of Wednesday, November 18.

## What reviewers keep saying

The praise lines up on the size and detail of Leonida. The Guardian's review points to the game's nightclubs as a showcase for how much is going on at once, and PlayStation Universe calls it "a truly generational video game." Giant Bomb says Rockstar "overdelivers" on the hype.

The few notes of caution are small. TheSixthAxis says the need to keep raising the stakes across a long story lessens its impact, and VGC mentions minor performance issues while still calling it a must-play. We're not going into the plot here.

## How it stacks up

On Metacritic's own pages, Grand Theft Auto V shows a 97 and Red Dead Redemption 2 shows a 97. A 99 puts GTA VI above both, at least for now.
