**ExileCon put a date on 1.0.** Grinding Gear Games [announced on the official Path of Exile forums](https://www.pathofexile.com/forum/view-thread/4012710) that **Path of Exile 2 goes free-to-play on December 11 (PST)**. The post from Natalia_GGG (November 6) wraps the live ExileCon 2026 keynote: complete campaign with **Acts 5 and 6**, two new classes (**Shadow** and **Duelist**), a new endgame storyline, the **Conquerors of Wraeclast** challenge league, and a big balance pass. [Steam's ExileCon news post](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2694490/view/698779962638862149) pointed players at the same reveal window.

## What's landing with PoE2 1.0

GGG's forum post keeps the headline list tight. [MMORPG.com's write-up of the keynote](https://www.mmorpg.com/news/exilecon-2026-announces-path-of-exile-2-full-release-and-new-path-of-exile-league-2000139270) fills in the scale GGG showed on stage: Acts 5 and 6 bring **24 new areas**, **18 new bosses**, and more than **70 rare encounters**. The Shadow leans stealth, daggers, traps, and a Darkness resource, with Assassin and Saboteur ascendancies in that coverage. The Duelist dual-wields into Knight and Weapon Master, including a squire companion and banner support for the Knight side. Expect that detail to get fuller patch notes closer to launch; the official forum confirmation is the date, F2P status, the two classes by name, Acts 5–6, and the new league.

**Conquerors of Wraeclast** is the launch league. GGG describes sieging enemies as the hook. MMORPG's report says it mixes base-building and real-time army play: recruit mercenaries, pick a faction (Ezomytes, Maraketh, or Karui), run campaign sieges, then fight over a shared endgame map where castles and hunting grounds matter. There are also broad system passes in that coverage — unique reworks, new skills and maps, passive-tree changes — but treat the forum post as the locked announcement and the keynote recap as the detail pass.

## PoE1 isn't sitting this one out

Same forum thread: **Path of Exile: Covens of Umbra** goes live **November 13 (PST)** for free across available platforms. It's a witch-coven challenge league plus fundamental endgame changes, including Atlas work and improvements to existing systems, new uniques, and more. So PoE1 gets its next league a month before PoE2's 1.0, which is classic GGG pacing — keep the original healthy while the sequel leaves early access.

**PPR take:** Almost two years of early access and GGG finally stamped a real 1.0 date. Free-to-play on day one is the right call for a game this deep, and finishing the campaign with Acts 5 and 6 plus two new classes is the actual "we're done with the foundation" signal. Conquerors of Wraeclast sounds like the riskiest swing — army sieges and shared castles can either be the most memorable league in years or a balance headache — but that's GGG being GGG. Pairing it with Covens of Umbra on November 13 means the original still gets a real patch week before the sequel's launch window. December 11 just became a big ARPG night on the calendar.
