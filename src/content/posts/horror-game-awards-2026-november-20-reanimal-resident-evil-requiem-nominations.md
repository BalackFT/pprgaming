**Before The Game Awards hogs the spotlight in December, horror gets its own night.** The Horror Game Awards 2026 stream live on **Friday, November 20 at 2 PM ET** (11 AM PT, 7 PM GMT), from a sold-out show in London, [according to the organizers' announcement](https://www.gamespress.com/One-Week-to-Go-The-Horror-Game-Awards-2026-Streams-Live-Worldwide-on-N). It's the show's fifth year, and you can catch it on its official YouTube, Twitch and TikTok channels, plus "hundreds of co-streamers."

## Who's leading

Tarsier Studios' **REANIMAL** tops the board with **10 nominations**. Capcom's **Resident Evil Requiem** is right behind with nine, and **Silent Hill: Townfall**, **Clive Barker's Hellraiser: Revival** and **Silver Pines** each picked up eight.

Sixteen games are up for Game of the Year, and it's a wild mix of big names and tiny indies. Requiem, Townfall, Hellraiser, Supermassive's *Directive 8020*, Rebel Wolves' *The Blood of Dawnwalker* and Team Ninja's *Fatal Frame 2: Crimson Butterfly Remake* share the list with smaller projects like *The Skin Stapler*, *Endacopia*, *ROUTINE*, *Project Songbird* and *Tenebris Somnia*.

## New categories and how winners get picked

There are 21 categories this year, five of them new: Best Cosy Horror Game, Scariest Horror Game of the Year, Best Ongoing Horror Game, The Critics' Choice Award and Best Adaptation. Public voting ran from October 31 to November 11, [per the official site](https://www.thehorrorgameawards.com/news/vote-2026). Players' Choice and Content Creator of the Year go straight to the fan vote. Most Accessible Horror Game is a 50/50 split between fans and a jury. For everything else, the top five from the public vote go to jury panels that pick the winner.

## Trailers, too

Between awards, the show promises **more than 20 trailers** for upcoming horror games. Most are being kept secret, but the organizers have confirmed **ILL**, **Sally Face 2** and **Sker: The Bleeding Veil** will be there. Co-creators Phil Dickens and Will Penkeyman are back to host with co-producer Ash Millman, and presenters include developers from Bloober Team, Supermassive, Creative Assembly and Romero Games.
