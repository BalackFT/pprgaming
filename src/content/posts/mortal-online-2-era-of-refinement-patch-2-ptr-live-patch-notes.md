**The second Era of Refinement patch for Mortal Online 2 is on the Public Test Realm today, right on the November 18 date Star Vault gave.** The studio posted [the full PTR patch notes](https://www.mortalonline2.com/era-of-refinement-patch-2-ptr-opens-today-patch-notes) this morning, and most of what it previewed in [Into the Vault #163](/news/mortal-online-2-era-of-refinement-patch-2-aggressive-flag-housing-ptr-november-18/) made the cut.

To get on, right-click Mortal Online 2 in Steam, open Properties, then Game Versions & Betas, and pick "Mortal Online Public Test Realm." Star Vault says there's limited to no GM support on the PTR, and bugs go through the in-game Report Bug button.

## The big ones

- **Aggressive flag:** it replaces the Criminal Actions toggle. If you're not flagged, you can't damage innocent targets or commit criminal actions at all. Swings and spells will play out but do nothing. While flagged, anyone can legally attack and loot you.
- **Anti-zerg damage:** the game now tracks how much health you lost in the last second and gives you a defensive bonus against all damage that scales with it. Get dogpiled and the follow-up hits won't land for full damage. It doesn't apply to normal AI, but it does apply to pets.
- **House skins:** houses, strongholds, keeps and keep walls now have parts you can upgrade one at a time, and there are over 100 skins to find in the world. They're on the broker filters too.
- **Concentration rework:** taking damage no longer interrupts a spell. Each hit over 10 damage cuts charged spell power by 30%, down to a 70% floor. Magic damage still interrupts.

## Also in the patch

- 10 new spells, including Mana Shield (20% of damage drained from mana instead), Repelling Ward, Levity, Beast of Burden and Breath of Efficiency for faster mining and woodcutting.
- You can now mount Snapping Turtles, Hunter Lizards, Lynx, Coralis Crabs, Belbus and Nightsnatchers, and there are 8 new rare mount variants with different stats.
- A new Hunting Prowess skill takes over the animal damage and defense bonus from the Zoology skills.
- Clade gift changes across the board, Town Criers in lawful towns that call out criminals within 500m, guild heraldry on controlled regions, the return of Tagmaton invasions, a Lasting Respite buff from campfires, hitching posts in every city and a new "Taunt" ability.
- The engine moves up to version 5.8.4.

Star Vault hasn't given a live date for the patch yet. Separately, its [livestream recap](https://www.mortalonline2.com/development-livestream-recap-table-games-and-ships-announcements) says Table Games arrive in December along with a new tavern building, and ships and sailing come with a new water continent in a later update.

**PPR take:** The anti-zerg mechanic is the one I'd be testing first. If you've ever had five guys land on you in the same second, you know exactly why it's there....and you also know the big guilds are going to poke at it hard. The Aggressive flag is the other big one, since it touches every single PvP fight in the game. Hop on the PTR and actually break it now, because this is the stuff that's a lot harder to fix once it's live.
