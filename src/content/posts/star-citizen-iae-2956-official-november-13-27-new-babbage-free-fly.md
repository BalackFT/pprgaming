**It's official.** [@RobertsSpaceInd](https://x.com/RobertsSpaceInd/status/2118736577425719759) posted Friday that **Roberts Space Industries leads Intergalactic Aerospace Expo 2956**, and the celebration **lands in New Babbage, microTech on November 13**. The same post says you can **play Star Citizen for free until November 27**, try **over 200+ ships for free via rental kiosks**, and expect **new ship releases**. The discover link points to [play.sc/iae2956](https://play.sc/iae2956), which opens RSI's [Countdown to IAE 2956](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/transmission/21389-Countdown-To-IAE-2956) Comm-Link.

That closes the gap left earlier this week, when CIG had [opened IAE 2956 for PTU testing at Tobin](/news/star-citizen-iae-2956-ptu-testing-tobin-expo-center-new-babbage/) without publishing live dates or Free Fly details. Those dates are no longer a data-mine rumor.

## Confirmed dates, location and Free Fly

The Comm-Link overline states the window plainly: **November 13 to 27**. The calendar block on the page titles the event **IAE 2956 Free Fly**, puts the venue at the **Tobin Expo Center, New Babbage, microTech**, and describes Free Fly as running **throughout the entire event**. Page timestamps on that calendar block resolve to a start around **11:00 a.m. ET on November 13** and an end around **3:00 p.m. ET on November 27**. RSI's X post matches the same bookends: lands November 13, free play until November 27.

RSI's copy says New Babbage is hosting "the biggest ship show in the 'verse," and that celebrating the launch means playing Star Citizen **for free** for the whole expo. Every attendee can:

- **Try out over 200 ships and ground vehicles for free** from show-floor kiosks
- **Discover the latest spacecraft** from the manufacturers on the floor
- **Pick up exclusive IAE armor, vehicle paints, and in-game items**

The page also promises **brand-new vehicle announcements**, **live releases**, exclusive gear and event-themed paints. The Comm-Link text itself still does **not** name those new ships. RSI's X post only says "New ship releases," so that is all we are treating as confirmed on paper.

## What the official promo actually shows

The 30-second video on [RSI's announcement](https://x.com/RobertsSpaceInd/status/2118736577425719759) is a New Babbage flyover packed with fleet shots. Over the icy planet, the dark multi-engine hull with orange thruster glow is the **RSI Perseus**, flying in a rear trio with the **RSI Polaris** and **RSI Galaxy**. Elsewhere in the same promo, the small Drake is the **Drake Marauder**, and the bulky industrial Drake silhouette is the **Drake Ironclad**. Those hulls are what the promo shows. They are still **not** named as the "new ship releases" in the X post or Comm-Link, so we are not inventing which ones get a fresh pledge drop, prices or a pledge page during the expo.

## Manufacturer schedule is up; full ship lineup still TBA

The Comm-Link includes an official **IAE 2956 pledge and free rental schedule** graphic and tells players to check back for the full list of ships and ground vehicles you can obtain and try. From that graphic, the manufacturer days run as follows:

- **Nov. 13–14:** RSI
- **Nov. 14–15:** Origin Jumpworks
- **Nov. 15–16:** Anvil Aerospace
- **Nov. 16–17:** Crusader Industries and Tumbril
- **Nov. 17–18:** Drake Interplanetary
- **Nov. 18–19:** Argo Astronautics, Consolidated Outland, Greycat Industrial and Kruger Intergalactic
- **Nov. 19–20:** Mirai and MISC
- **Nov. 20–21:** Aegis Dynamics
- **Nov. 21–22:** Aopoa, Banu, Esperia and Gatac Manufacture
- **Nov. 22:** Best in Show
- **Nov. 23–27:** Finale — last chance to try all IAE vehicles for free

That lines up with RSI leading the celebration on the opening days. Individual ship SKUs, warbond wording and any Best in Show winner are still not listed on the page.
