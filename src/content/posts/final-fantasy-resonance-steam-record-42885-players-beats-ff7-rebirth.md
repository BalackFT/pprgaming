**Pixel art, big numbers.** **Final Fantasy Resonance** peaked at **42,885 concurrent players on Steam** on Sunday, October 25, according to [SteamDB](https://steamdb.info/app/3259780/charts/). As [GamesRadar+ reports](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/final-fantasy/final-fantasy-resonance-dethrones-final-fantasy-7-rebirth-with-the-series-largest-steam-peak-for-a-single-player-jrpg/), that makes it the most-played single-player Final Fantasy ever on the platform. It just edges out **Final Fantasy VII Rebirth's 40,564**.

**It's still growing.** Most big launches peak on day one, but Resonance didn't. It opened just short of 28,000 players, which was already Square Enix's second-best Steam launch for a turn-based RPG after Dragon Quest III HD-2D Remake. Then it kept climbing through its first weekend. [Game*Spark](https://www.gamespark.jp/article/2026/10/26/174073.html) says its Steam user reviews are at **97% positive** ("Overwhelmingly Positive"), and producer Keisuke Nakashima and longtime Brave Exvius scenario writer Yukinori Kitajima both posted thanks to players.

**The asterisks.** Rebirth reached PC almost a year after its PS5 debut, so many of its biggest fans had already played it. Resonance launched on PC and consoles at the same time, which pushed more day-one players to Steam. Final Fantasy XIV is also still the series' overall Steam champ, with a 95,150 peak around Endwalker, but comparing an MMO with a single-player RPG isn't really fair. GamesRadar+ expects next April's simultaneous-launch **Final Fantasy VII Revelation** to take the crown back.

**Catch up:** critics liked it too. We covered its [85 Metacritic launch reviews](/news/final-fantasy-resonance-reviews-85-metacritic/) last week. Resonance is out now on PC (Steam and Microsoft Store), PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Switch 2 and Switch. Each store has a free demo that includes the whole first chapter.

**PPR take:** A pixel-art Final Fantasy beating a blockbuster remake on Steam, by word of mouth, in its first weekend. Square Enix should take the hint and keep making HD-2D games.
