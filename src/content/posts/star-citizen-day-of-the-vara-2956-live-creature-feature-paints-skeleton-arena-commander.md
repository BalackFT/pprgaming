**The Vara crowds are here.** As [promised in Monday's This Week in Star Citizen](/news/star-citizen-day-of-the-vara-2956-roadmap-update-week-ahead/), **Day of the Vara 2956** went live today. RSI's [Comm-Link post](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/transmission/21337-Day-Of-The-Vara-2956) is titled "Don't Wake the Dead" and promises to "honor the lost with reverence, revelry, and the comfort of company. There's safety in numbers… supposedly." The official [Star Citizen account on X](https://x.com/RobertsSpaceInd/status/2112939124369891549) boiled it down to three things: **monstrous ship paints**, a **skeletal invasion in Arena Commander** and a **pumpkin decorating contest**.

**Skeletons in Arena Commander.** The free part of the event is a skeleton-themed mode in Arena Commander, Star Citizen's built-in combat simulator. Players report that if you **survive two rounds against the skeletal horde**, you unlock the **Snarling Vanduul Mask**, a returning Halloween helmet ([CMDR Quattro on X](https://x.com/QuattroBaje3na/status/2112955000125317527)). Last year's version of this was the FPS Kill Collector mode, which handed out older Halloween masks to the top scorers ([Massively Overpowered](https://massivelyop.com/2025/10/02/star-citizen-celebrates-halloween-with-contests-returning-arena-commander-mode-and-ship-paint-sales/)), so this year's "just survive" requirement sounds friendlier.

**Creature Feature 3 paints.** The "monstrous" paints are a new set inspired by classic horror monsters. According to [CMDR Quattro's breakdown](https://x.com/QuattroBaje3na/status/2112948181583470735) of the store listings, the three-paint bundle is **$21.60 with a 20% discount**, or you can buy them one at a time:

- **Kruger L-21 Wolf, Lycan:** $7
- **Drake Vulture, Abomination:** $9
- **Aegis Eclipse, Desmodus:** $11

Paints are optional cosmetics, and nothing in the event requires buying them. Community posts also note that Halloween event ships are back in the pledge store for the season.

**Pumpkin contest.** The yearly pumpkin decorating contest is back too: carve, paint or decorate a real pumpkin with a Star Citizen theme and enter it on Spectrum. RSI hasn't changed the basic idea from past years, but check the [Comm-Link](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/transmission/21337-Day-Of-The-Vara-2956) for this year's deadline and prize list before you start carving.

**PPR take:** The Vanduul mask for surviving two rounds is the easy win here, and it costs nothing, so it's worth a quick Arena Commander session tonight. The Eclipse Desmodus is the paint people are talking about, but if you're on a budget, the Discovery Month missions and the free Phoenix Insignia Twitch Drop (ends October 27) are still running alongside the event.
