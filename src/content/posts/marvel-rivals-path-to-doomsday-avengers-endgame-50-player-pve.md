**Avengers, assemble. All fifty of you.** NetEase today revealed **Path to Doomsday - Avengers: Endgame**, a new PvE mode for Marvel Rivals that drops **50 players** into the ruins of the Avengers Compound to take on Thanos and his army at once. It arrives **October 24 UTC**, which is the evening of October 23 for players in the Americas, according to the [official trailer](https://in.ign.com/marvel-rivals/274964/marvel-rivals-official-path-to-doomsday-avengers-endgame-new-pve-mode-trailer). [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/marvel-rivals-50-player-pve-avengers-endgame/) says it's part of the game's Season 10.5 update.

The trailer loosely recreates the film's final battle. This version of Thanos is **Giant-Man-sized**, and he swings a huge dual-bladed sword that can instantly kill heroes. He can also call down an aerial strike from his ship that wipes out whole crowds of players at once. [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/marvel-rivals-avengers-endgame-mode-thanos-iron-man-2000748003) calls him essentially a raid boss, and says winning plays a scene of Tony Stark snapping him away with the Infinity Gauntlet.

The event adds a few extras:

- A **free Captain America costume** based on *Captain America: The Winter Soldier* (2014) for taking part
- Thanos-themed accessories, sprays and other rewards
- Interactive activities in the game's **Times Square** hub

Polygon points out that this builds on things Rivals already does: a four-player zombie PvE mode, a chaotic 18-versus-18 mode, and a Times Square hub that holds up to 100 players. Season 10 also brought an earlier Path to Doomsday mode based on *Infinity War*. It's all building up to *Avengers: Doomsday*, which hits theaters on **December 18**.

**PPR take:** Fifty heroes against one raid boss sounds more like Dynasty Warriors than a hero shooter, and that's the appeal. Marvel Rivals is free on PC, PlayStation and Xbox, so it's an easy one to jump into with friends this weekend.
