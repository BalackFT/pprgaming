The whip is back. **Castlevania: Belmont's Curse** releases **today, October 15**, on PS5, Xbox, PC and Switch.

[Nintendo](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/News/2026/October/Upcoming-Games-October-2026-3208377.html) describes it as a new tale "blending acrobatic action with in-depth exploration," where you play as **Rose Belmont, daughter of Trevor Belmont**, wielding the family's holy whip to uncover a dark conspiracy across Gothic landscapes.

Pixel Herald named it its pick of the week. Also landing this week: **Ratatan**, rhythm-action from the creators of Patapon (Oct 14–15 depending on platform), and Switch 2 versions of **Resident Evil 2, 3 and 4** on October 16.
