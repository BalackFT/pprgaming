The whip is back. **Castlevania: Belmont's Curse** [launches today, October 15](https://www.konami.com/games/us/en/topics/3538/), on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Xbox on PC, Steam and Nintendo Switch.

## What it is

Konami calls it "an exploration-based 2D action game," developed jointly by **Konami** and **Evil Empire**, the studio that handled updates and DLC for *Dead Cells*, with **Motion Twin** (the original *Dead Cells* team) in an advisory role, per [Konami's June announcement](https://www.konami.com/games/eu/en/topics/19188/). [Nintendo](https://www.nintendo.com/en-gb/News/2026/October/Upcoming-Games-October-2026-3208377.html) sums it up as a new tale "blending acrobatic action with in-depth exploration."

You play as **Rose Belmont, daughter of Trevor Belmont**. The story is set in **Paris in 1499**, 23 years after *Castlevania III: Dracula's Curse*, and has Rose exploring the city's streets and catacombs and Dracula's castle with the family's holy whip, the **Vampire Killer**, plus other weapons and skills.

## How we got here

Konami [revealed the game in February](https://www.konami.com/games/us/en/topics/3097/) as part of the series' **40th anniversary**, without naming the lead. The October 15 date and a Midnight Edition followed in June, when a new trailer ran during the Xbox Games Showcase with original artwork by manga artist **Katsuya Terada**. That's also when Konami confirmed Rose as the protagonist on the official site. As [Time Extension reported](https://www.timeextension.com/news/2026/06/konami-confirms-new-female-lead-for-castlevania-belmonts-curse-alongside-stunning-katsuya-terada-artwork), Sony had earlier identified the heroine as Sonia Belmont, and Konami's announcement settled it. At that point, the Switch date was still to be confirmed. It's now launching on Switch the same day as everything else.

## Also this week

Pixel Herald named it [its pick of the week](https://pixelherald.com/october-2026-game-releases/). Also landing: **Ratatan**, rhythm-action from the creators of Patapon (Oct 14–15 depending on platform), and Switch 2 versions of **Resident Evil 2, 3 and 4** on October 16.

**PPR take:** On paper, this is a great pairing. The people who kept *Dead Cells* going for years, with that game's original studio advising, on a 2D Castlevania is about as good a fit as you'll find. Setting it after *Castlevania III*, with a brand-new Belmont in 15th-century Paris, also gives Konami room to tell a fresh story without stepping on the classics. The big question is how it plays over a full run, and launch reviews and player impressions will tell us that over the next few days. For now, it's just nice to have a brand-new 2D Castlevania out in the world.
