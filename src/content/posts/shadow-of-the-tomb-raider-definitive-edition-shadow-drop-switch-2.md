**No warning, just Lara.** On Tomb Raider's 30th anniversary week, Aspyr quietly put **Shadow of the Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition** on the Nintendo Switch 2 eShop today, [Gematsu reports](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/10/shadow-of-the-tomb-raider-definitive-edition-now-available-for-switch-2). It's the last piece of the Survivor Trilogy, so all three reboot games are now playable on Switch 2.

**What you get**, per Aspyr and [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/10/surprise-shadow-of-the-tomb-raider-definitive-edition-has-shadow-dropped-on-switch-2):

- **The full game** plus all seven DLC Challenge Tombs, every downloadable weapon, outfit and skill, and two-player online co-op.
- **$39.99**, with 10% off on the eShop until **November 4**.
- **Tomb Raider: Definitive Survivor Trilogy**, a new bundle of Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition, Rise of the Tomb Raider: 20 Year Celebration and Shadow, for **$69.99** (also 10% off at launch).

Nintendo Life noted the eShop listing was still rolling out by region when the news broke, so give it a few hours if it isn't showing up for you yet.

**How we got here:** Aspyr has shadow-dropped every one of these. [Game Rant](https://gamerant.com/shadow-of-the-tomb-raider-switch-2/) points out that Tomb Raider: Definitive Edition landed on Switch and Switch 2 last November, and Rise of the Tomb Raider followed in June as a Switch 2 exclusive. Shadow originally launched on PS4, Xbox One and PC on September 14, 2018, and sits at an 80 Top Critic Average on OpenCritic.

**PPR take:** Forty bucks is steep for an eight-year-old game that goes for pocket change on other stores, but having the whole trilogy portable is a real draw. Nintendo Life gave the first two Switch 2 ports an 8/10, so the odds look good. And it's a nice warm-up before Legacy of Atlantis on February 12, 2027.
