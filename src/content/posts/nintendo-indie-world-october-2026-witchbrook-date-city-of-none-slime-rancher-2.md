**Indie World is back.** Nintendo ran its second Indie World of the year this morning, a roughly 15-minute show that aired at 10 a.m. ET, [Nintendo World Report noted](http://www.nintendoworldreport.com/news/77924/second-indie-world-of-year-to-air-october-28). It was a fast run of Switch and Switch 2 dates, with a couple of genuine surprises.

**Witchbrook finally has a date.** The long-awaited cozy witch life sim opened the show with a **January 28, 2027** release date, [GameSpot reports](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/nintendo-indie-world-october-2026-showcase-all-the-biggest-announcements/).

**City of None, from a Celeste co-creator.** Extremely OK Games revealed **City of None**, a platforming adventure through a city run by "ravenous Barons," with new abilities to find and five sectors you reach by train. It's due on Switch 2 and Switch in early 2027, [per Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/city-of-none-announced-for-nintendo-switch-2-and-switch-from-co-creator-of-celeste/).

**The other big dates:**
- **Sword of the Sea** comes to Switch 2 tomorrow, October 29, ending its PlayStation console exclusivity.
- **Slime Rancher 2** lands on Switch 2 in January 2027.
- **Spirit Crossing**, the life sim MMO from the makers of Cozy Grove, arrives November 16, and [TheGamer says](https://www.thegamer.com/everything-revealed-nintendo-indie-world-october-2026/) it will be console-exclusive to Nintendo.
- **Raccoin: Chaos Max Edition** hits Switch 2 on November 10, with a demo out now.
- **Super Battle Golf** comes to Switch 2 on March 25, 2027, and the six-player co-op horror hit **REPO** is coming to Switch in 2027.
- **Winds of Viridia** launches in early 2027 and has a demo available now.
- **Petal Runner** is out March 8, 2027, **My Arms Are Longer Now** on March 11, 2027, and **Dumpster Gang** in March 2027.
- **Haunted Paws** arrives in May 2027, **Kitsune: The Journey of Adashino** in spring 2027, **Threads of Time** in summer 2027, and **Tenjutsu** sometime in 2027.

**PPR take:** No shadow-drop bombshell this time, but a firm Witchbrook date and a new game from a Celeste co-creator make this one worth the 15 minutes. REPO on Switch should be chaos in the best way.
