**Take-Two finally put a number on it, and it's bigger than anyone's estimate.** Rockstar Games [announced Friday](https://www.take2games.com/ir/news/grand-theft-auto-vi-shatters-entertainment-box-office-records) that Grand Theft Auto VI's day-one retail sales reached **$3.79 billion**, which the company calls "a new record for entertainment across any medium" and "the biggest entertainment launch in history."

## The official numbers

Per the release on [Business Wire](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20261120446139/en/), GTA VI also sold **32.5 million units pre-release**, which Take-Two calls a new record across all platforms. The release leans on the reviews too, pointing to the game's "near perfect score of 99 on Metacritic."

Read the asterisk. The $3.79 billion is "based on company estimates through end of day November 19th worldwide." [AP](https://apnews.com/article/grand-theft-auto-gta-vice-city-playstation-15c69f76548ab5659bc3fe44a69756d8) describes it the same way: a worldwide company estimate through the end of launch day. Take-Two didn't break it out by platform, and it didn't give a unit count for launch day itself, only the pre-release figure.

Sam Houser, Rockstar's president and founder, said: "It is with immense gratitude that we enter a new chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of Grand Theft Auto VI. We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world and we couldn't be more excited to share this moment with you."

## Way past the Sensor Tower estimate

This morning we ran [Sensor Tower's launch-day estimate](/news/gta-6-sensor-tower-2-36-billion-24-6-million-launch-estimate/): about $2.36 billion and 24.59 million units, pre-orders included. The official figure blew past it by roughly **$1.43 billion**, and Take-Two's pre-release units alone top Sensor Tower's full count by close to 8 million. [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-hit-32-5-million-copies-sold-and-almost-4-billion-on-day-one/) makes the same point, noting Take-Two's numbers came in above the roughly 25 million Sensor Tower reported at launch.

For scale, [Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/taketwo-shatters-sales-record-as-gta-vi-launches-to-massive-fanfare-5042505) says GTA V made roughly $800 million on its first day in 2013, so this is nearly five times that. GameSpot adds that GTA V launched at $60, while the cheapest GTA VI is $80, and compares the total to Avengers: Endgame's $2.94 billion across its entire theatrical run.

It's a big swing from Take-Two's usual caution. Reuters recalls CEO Strauss Zelnick on this month's earnings call: "We tend to avoid claiming success before it occurs."

## What's still not official

A PC version and an online mode. Reuters says some analysts expect both in 2027, but Rockstar hasn't announced either. Keep an eye on the [GTA 6 hub](/gta-6/) for anything that changes.
