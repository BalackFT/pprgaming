**Moon Studios** used its latest *Wicked Inside* showcase to lay out what **[No Rest for the Wicked](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1371980/No_Rest_for_the_Wicked/)** looks like when it leaves Early Access, and the biggest change is how you build a character.

According to the [studio's showcase details](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/10/no-rest-for-the-wicked-details-new-class-systems-version-1-0-enhancements), the old attribute system is gone. **Picking up a weapon now changes your class**, out of **24 possible classes**, and you can carry traits from one class into another. Moon says there are **362 traits** to slot across those classes, so mixing builds is the point.

**What else is coming in 1.0:**

- **Six factions** to join, including the assassins of the Severed Hand, the knightly Sacrament Guard and the Unspoken, each with its own questline, traits, vendors and surprises
- **Cerim Cores**, which control how you survive, from simple health, stamina and focus boosts to stranger ones like "Feral" for attack speed and "Crimson" for lifesteal; go over your Core capacity and you take more damage from every hit
- An update that **roughly doubles the size of the game**, with new zones, new enemy factions and bosses, loot and combat refinements, and quality-of-life fixes
- Extra work to make sure **PlayStation 5 performance** runs smoothly

**Release:** No Rest for the Wicked is in Early Access on PC now. Version 1.0 launches on **PlayStation 5 and PC via Steam in March 2027**, with **Xbox Series and Switch 2** coming later.

**PPR take:** Tying your class to the weapon in your hand is a big swing for a game built on careful, Souls-style combat. If the 24 classes really feel different, the 1.0 relaunch could be the moment this one finally clicks for the players who bounced off Early Access.
