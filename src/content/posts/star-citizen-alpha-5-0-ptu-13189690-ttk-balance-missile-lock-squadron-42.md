**Another Alpha 5.0 PTU build is out for Wave 1, and this one is about dogfighting as much as Nyx.** Wakapedia-CIG posted the [patch notes for build 13189690](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/190048/thread/star-citizen-alpha-5-0-ptu-patch-notes/9230254) on Spectrum Friday afternoon, a week after the [first 5.0 PTU](/news/star-citizen-alpha-5-0-ptu-13119881-nyx-guild-halls-mission-boards/). It's still Wave 1 only, on a US-only PTU channel, with long term persistence enabled. The testing focus list is Genesis, Nyx I, Starchitect locations, mission boards, Intraplanetary Server Meshing, the new missile lock interface and, for the weekend, a ship TTK pass.

## The TTK pass (weekend test only)

CIG is clear that the [Time To Kill balance pass](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/190048/thread/star-citizen-alpha-5-0-ptu-patch-notes/9230300) is **"currently not intended for 5.0 release"** and is only in the build for weekend feedback. It builds on the last iteration, and the main moves are:

- **Armor health up 15%**, and ship turret health plus the "overkill" threshold (where one big hit blows a ship up outright) both scaled up by 145%.
- **Size 4 ballistic alpha damage down 20% and size 5 down 10%**, with the AD4B and AD5B taking a further cut on top of that. Mass Driver projectile speed goes up 10%.
- **Laser beam weapons buffed by 145%**, plus bumps for the CF Repeaters, MaxOx Neutron Cannons, the Yeng'tu Repeater, the WAR Cannon and the WRATH Cannon (up 80%).

Per the notes, this pass should leave fighters "settled," so the team can chase individual outliers and then turn to large and multicrew ships. Shark-CIG has a [feedback thread for Update #5](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/4/thread/5-0-ptu-feedback-ship-ttk-balancing-update-5/9226612) if you're testing.

## Missile locks get the Squadron 42 treatment

The old animated pattern around your target is gone. The new interface is the one **built for Squadron 42**: it shows the actual locking cone in the middle of your screen, so you can see where a target has to be before a missile will grab it. Locks now step through four states (idle, acquiring, locked, resolved), "LOCK" pops up next to the target marker, a range bar flags too far or too close, and the whole thing picks up your ship manufacturer's HUD colors.

## Nyx I and the rest

AI in Starchitect locations now spawn inside buildings instead of out in the open, Nyx I gets new orange-tinted grass and lighter forest and swamp foliage for performance, thunder is now audible out to 25km, and there's a new orchestral mix for the planet. Rep gains are running at **10x** on this build for testing. As always, it's PTU....anything can change before LIVE.

**PPR take:** Ok, the missile lock change is the one I'm most curious about. Seeing the actual cone instead of a shrinking pattern just makes sense, and it's kind of cool that Squadron 42 work is bleeding over into the PU like this. The TTK stuff, I'm watching but not judging yet. Armor up, big ballistics down and beams finally getting real love sounds like CIG trying to stop fights from ending in two seconds, and they're saying out loud that fighters are close to done so they can move on to the big ships. That's the part I care about. Turrets getting 145% more health matters for anybody crewing something larger than a fighter. It's a weekend test and not headed to 5.0, so don't panic over any one number....go fly it and leave feedback in the thread. That's what it's there for!
