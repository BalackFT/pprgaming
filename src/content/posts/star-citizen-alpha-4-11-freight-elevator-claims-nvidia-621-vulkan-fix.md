**Lost your gear? Help is coming in pieces.** Community manager Nicou-CIG answered a few long-running backer questions on Spectrum yesterday, and the most useful one is about getting your stuff back. We already noted that the **Item Recovery: Ship Loadout Improvements** card will be revisited in the next Roadmap Update in our [Roadmap Roundup story](/news/star-citizen-roadmap-roundup-intersec-defence-solutions-mission-pack-nyx/). The new part is what Alpha 4.11 gets instead.

**What's confirmed:**

- **Ship loadout recovery needs more time.** In [an Ask The Devs reply](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/50259/thread/item-recovery-ship-loadout-improvements-card-quest/9194552), Nicou-CIG wrote: "This card will be updated with the next Roadmap Update, as we need more time to work on ship loadout changes, expedite fees, and timed claims."
- **Alpha 4.11 refreshes freight elevator claims.** In the same reply: "In the meantime, Alpha 4.11 will introduce a refreshed UI for the existing freight elevator claim system, improving its presentation and clarity while making it more intuitive to recover individual items."
- **The Professions episode isn't cancelled.** Backers had been asking why this week's 10 From the Community is about technology when CIG took questions on professions. Nicou-CIG [replied](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/3/thread/10-not-from-the-community/9194598) that the team "ended up gathering 20 questions from the Professions thread" and that "a Professions-focused 10 From The Community episode is still planned." The [FPS Gameplay question thread](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/3/thread/looking-for-questions-10-from-the-community-fps-ga/9189515) is open now too.
- **Technology episode is today.** As we covered in [this week's schedule](/news/star-citizen-day-of-the-vara-2956-roadmap-update-week-ahead/), the technology-themed 10 From the Community with CIG's Technical Directors is due Friday, October 23. [Open Hangar's calendar](https://openhangar.space/news) still lists it for today.
- **NVIDIA fixed the Vulkan crash.** GeForce Game Ready driver **621.01**, out October 21, lists "Star Citizen: Resolves a crash that may be seen when using Vulkan API [6680590]" under fixed gaming bugs, per [DSOGaming's copy of the release notes](https://www.dsogaming.com/news/nvidia-geforce-game-ready-621-01-whql-driver-released/). [Open Hangar](https://openhangar.space/news) had been tracking a Vulkan crash to desktop on NVIDIA 616.56, with rolling back the driver or switching to DirectX 11 as the workaround.

**Still unknown:** CIG hasn't said when the ship loadout, expedite fee and timed claim changes will land, and it hasn't given a date for the Professions episode.

**PPR take:** A clearer freight elevator screen won't fix losing a whole loadout, but anything that makes pulling out one helmet or rifle less of a fight is welcome. If Vulkan has been crashing you to desktop, update to 621.01 before you go back to the Day of the Vara grind.
