**Busan delivered.** Pearl Abyss's **Crimson Desert** won the grand prize, the Presidential Award, at the 2026 Korea Game Awards on Wednesday, November 18, at BEXCO in Busan, [per Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/business/pearl-abysss-crimson-desert-takes-top-honor-at-2026-korea-game-awards/12980170). It didn't stop at one. The game walked out with eight awards.

## What it won

The Korea Game Awards are hosted by the Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism and organized by the Korea Association of Game Industry, and they're the country's biggest game awards. Twelve titles were up for the main awards this year, NCSOFT's Aion 2 among them. Here's Crimson Desert's haul, going by [The Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/business/tech-science/20261118/crimson-desert-sweeps-2026-korea-game-awards-with-8-honors) and [Inven Global](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/28764/crimson-desert-wins-8-awards-at-2026-korea-game-awards):

- **Grand Prize (Presidential Award)**, which JoongAng says comes with 15 million won (about $11,260).
- **All four technical and creative awards:** planning and scenario, sound, graphics, and character.
- **The Global Game Award**, a brand-new category for Korean games that did well overseas. Crimson Desert is its first winner.
- **Two individual awards:** Cho Yong-hyun, the game's development producer, won Outstanding Developer, and Lee Hyun, the voice of Kliff, won the Popular Voice Actor award.

It's Pearl Abyss's first grand prize since Black Desert Mobile in 2018. The Korea Times calls eight a new record, though Korean outlets don't agree on what the old mark was ([Seoul Economic Daily](https://en.sedaily.com/business/2026/11/18/pearl-abyss-crimson-desert-sweeps-8-awards-at-korea-game) points to Stellar Blade's seven in 2024), so we'll just call it the biggest night anyone's had there in years. Aion 2 took the Prime Minister's Award and the Popular Game Award. The ceremony ran a day before G-STAR 2026 opens at the same venue for four days, Thursday through Sunday.

CEO Heo Jin-young kept it short: the team made the game "with the sole aim of giving players an unforgettable experience."

## The trophy shelf is filling up

This lands a week after Crimson Desert [won PC Game of the Year at the Golden Joysticks](/news/crimson-desert-wins-pc-game-of-the-year-golden-joystick-awards-2026/), and on the same day the official account [asked fans to vote](https://x.com/CrimsonDesert_/status/2123053617573990779) for its Best Community Support nomination at The Game Awards 2026. Per JoongAng, the game sold 2 million copies on day one and passed 6 million within 83 days of its March launch.

**PPR take:** We said Busan was next, and here we are. Eight trophies at home is a big deal for a single-player console and PC game, and Korean coverage today keeps framing it as proof that a Korean studio can build a big console game on its own engine and sell it worldwide. What we like is the new Global Game Award going to it first. That category exists because of games like this one. The Best Community Support nod at The Game Awards fits too. A year of patches, hotfixes and a real DLC in Charting the Unknown is exactly what that award is supposed to reward, and Pearl Abyss kept shipping fixes right up through 2.05.00 last week. Now the question is whether December 10 adds one more to the shelf.
