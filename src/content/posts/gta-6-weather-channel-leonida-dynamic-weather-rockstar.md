Rockstar's last-minute GTA VI marketing took a turn nobody had on their bingo card. [The Weather Channel](https://x.com/weatherchannel/status/2122429589326798848) posted an "exclusive look at the forecast for Leonida" on Monday afternoon, with six new screenshots Rockstar handed over and a statement on how the game's weather works.

## What Rockstar said

The quote comes from Owen Shepherd, Rockstar's VP of Art: Light and Rendering. Leonida is modeled on modern-day Florida, which he called "a landscape with one of the most unpredictable weather systems in the US." To match that, Rockstar says it "had to completely reimagine our in-game weather systems to feel more localized, persistent, and above all dynamic," with skies that range from clear blue to "towering vertiginous cumulonimbus clouds," per the full statement quoted by [TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/114724/gta-6s-dynamic-persistent-weather-exposed-in-new-first-look-from-the-weather-channel/index.html).

## The forecasts

Instead of just dropping the images, The Weather Channel wrote each one up like a real forecast, as [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-has-teamed-up-with-the-weather-channel-to-show-how-dynamic-leonida-can-be/) rounded up:

- Heavy rain cutting visibility across the Keys, and more rain and wind for commuters out of Key Lento.
- A thunderstorm rolling over the swamps of Pelican Valley.
- Sea fog over the beachfront at South Ocean Beach.
- A tropical storm chopping up the water around the Rusty Anchor, and morning mist over Gambit Bay.

The "localized" part is the one to watch: one stretch of coast can be buried in fog while a storm hits the wetlands somewhere else. What Rockstar hasn't spelled out is how much the weather changes actual gameplay, like driving, and nobody should guess at that until people are playing it.

GTA VI launches Thursday, November 19 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with the US unlock at 12:00 AM ET, and [pre-loading](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/pre-load-now) is open now. Unlock times, editions and every story are on our [GTA 6 hub](/gta-6/).

**PPR take:** Honestly, this is a pretty smart bit of marketing. Florida weather is its own character, a sunny afternoon there can turn into a wall of rain in no time, so having an actual weather network do the forecast fits the setting perfectly. The detail I like most is "localized." Weather that changes from one part of the map to another, instead of the whole state flipping to rain at once, goes a long way toward making a world feel lived in. BUT we're still looking at six screenshots and a quote, and Rockstar hasn't said how storms affect the way you play. Two more days and we'll find out. Hope that pre-load is done!
