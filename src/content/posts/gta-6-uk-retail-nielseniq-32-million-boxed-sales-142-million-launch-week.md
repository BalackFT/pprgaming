**Physical games aren't dead yet.** New data from [NielsenIQ](https://nielseniq.com/global/en/news-center/2026/gta-vi-sends-uk-games-retail-sales-soaring-99-to-142m-in-launch-week/) says **GTA VI** made an estimated **£32 million** in UK physical sales over its first three days, November 19 to 21. That's about **74%** of all boxed software money spent in the UK that week, and **88%** of it came on launch day alone.

The wild part is what people were buying. As [The Independent points out](https://www.independent.co.uk/news/business/sales-fifa-b3092369.html), the retail box for GTA VI holds a download code, not a disc. People bought it anyway.

## The bigger picture

GTA VI lifted everything around it. NielsenIQ says total UK games retail revenue hit **£142 million** in launch week, up **99%** on the week before. Boxed software overall reached about **£43 million**, up **409%**, the best week for physical games since Christmas 2018.

Hardware got a bump too. Console hardware was up **38%**, its highest level since late 2025. **PS5 sales rose 46%** week on week and **Xbox Series sales jumped 75%**. Accessories brought in £21 million, up 123%, and a few numbers stand out:

- Controller volumes up 194%, with PS5 pads up 355%
- Headsets up 94%
- External console SSDs up 30%, their biggest week of 2026 so far

## Where it ranks

GTA VI comes in at **No. 17** for first-week boxed revenue in the UK. Only 16 games released since 1995 did better, and the most recent was FIFA 19 in 2018. Its own family still sits above it: GTA V is No. 1 and GTA IV is No. 11. NielsenIQ's Dorian Bloch put that in context. Back in 2008, boxed software was half of all UK games retail revenue. So far in 2026 it's 15%.

Keep in mind these are NielsenIQ estimates based on its retailer panel. They don't include digital store sales like the PlayStation Store or Xbox Store, which is where most copies are sold now.

**PPR take:** No. 17 sounds low for the biggest launch ever until you remember almost nobody buys boxes anymore. Doing these numbers in a market that's 15% physical is crazy. And people paid for a case with a code in it. Some of that is gifting, some is collectors, and some is folks who just like having something on the shelf. I get it. The SSD number jumped out at me too. With a download this big, that one isn't a shock. Rockstar and Take-Two haven't commented on the UK figures, so we're going on NielsenIQ here, but it's one more sign of how big this launch was. Black Friday week is next, so this might not be the last chart GTA tops.
