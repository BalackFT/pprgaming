The final stretch is here. Rockstar Games released the official **[Grand Theft Auto VI Launch Trailer](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/launch-trailer)** on Thursday, October 15, five weeks before the game arrives on **November 19**.

**What's in it:**

- More of **Jason and Lucia** and the story: an easy score goes wrong and drags them into a criminal conspiracy that spreads across the state of **Leonida**.
- New looks at Vice City and the rest of the map, heists and missions, plus a few steamier moments between the two leads.
- The debut of **"Flower in a Hurricane,"** a new single from score composers **Justice** featuring Tame Impala, SZA, and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard. It's also on *Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album*, which you can pre-save now.
- Rockstar says the trailer was captured entirely in-game on PlayStation 5.

It follows Trailer 1 (December 2023), Trailer 2 (May 2025), and the 26-minute Extended Look from this August. GTA VI launches on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

**PPR take:** Launch trailers usually mean the marketing push is on and the date is locked. After all the delays, November 19 finally looks real.
