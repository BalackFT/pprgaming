The final stretch is here. Rockstar Games released the official **[Grand Theft Auto VI Launch Trailer](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/launch-trailer)** on Thursday, October 15, five weeks before the game arrives on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S on **November 19**.

## The story pitch

The [90-second trailer](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/rockstar-has-revealed-the-official-launch-trailer-for-grand-theft-auto-6/) is built to introduce newcomers to **Jason and Lucia**. Rockstar's official description, as quoted by VGC, says the two "have always known the deck is stacked against them," and that when "an easy score goes wrong," they end up "in the middle of a criminal conspiracy stretching across the state of Leonida," forced to rely on each other to make it out. Along the way, the trailer shows new looks at Vice City and the wider map, plus heists and missions. [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-launch-trailer-sex-scene-jason-lucia-shower-youtube-video/) zeroed in on a few steamier moments between the two leads.

## The music

The trailer debuts **"Flower in a Hurricane,"** a new single from **Justice**, the French electronic duo composing GTA VI's original score. It features Tame Impala, SZA, and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard, and it's on *Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album*, which you can pre-save now, per [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/videos/grand-theft-auto-vi-gta6-official-launch-trailer/) and Rockstar. That's a big swing for a game series known for licensed radio stations.

## Captured on PS5

Rockstar says the trailer was captured entirely in-game on PlayStation 5. VGC notes Rockstar didn't say whether that means a base PS5 or a PS5 Pro.

## How we got here

This follows Trailer 1 (December 2023), Trailer 2 (May 2025) and the 26-minute Extended Look from this August. VGC points out GTA VI will be Rockstar's first major new single-player release since *Red Dead Redemption 2*. In a recent Bloomberg interview cited by VGC, studio heads Phil Hooker and Aaron Garbut said there won't be another eight-year gap before Rockstar's next game, because the engine, tools and team built for GTA VI carry over. "We're not going to rip it apart," Garbut said.

**PPR take:** Launch trailers usually mean the marketing push is on and the date is locked. After all the delays, November 19 finally looks real. What stands out to me is how much this one leans on story. It isn't a features list. It's Rockstar selling Jason and Lucia as a duo you'll want to follow, and getting Justice plus that lineup of guests on an original single tells you how big a moment Rockstar thinks this is. The other line worth remembering is Hooker and Garbut's promise that the next game won't take eight years. We'll see. For now, five weeks to go.
