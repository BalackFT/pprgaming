<h2 id="star-citizen-pumpkin-decorating-contest-winners">Star Citizen's Pumpkin Decorating Contest winners are in</h2>

CIG has [named the winners](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/3/thread/2026-pumpkin-decorating-contest) of its 2026 Pumpkin Decorating Contest, the real-life carving contest it ran for Day of the Vara. This year's themes were classic monsters, and the picks are **Astromedic** for Best Vampire, **Subzerowolf76** for Best Werewolf and **Doc_G_** for Best Frankenstein. BigTO-CIG says prizes for the winners and "The Hill Horror" helmet for every qualified entry will arrive on accounts "in the next few days." RSI also [shared the results on X](https://x.com/RobertsSpaceInd/status/2125284974760783882). The rules required real pumpkins, at least one process photo, and no AI, so these were all done by hand. If you entered and you're waiting on that helmet, keep an eye on your hangar this week.

<h2 id="terraria-1-4-6-crossplay-phase-one">Terraria 1.4.6 starts crossplay with local network play</h2>

Re-Logic's [Terraria 1.4.6 update is out](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/105600/view/1849827110446549), and it brings **phase one of crossplay**. For now it's about local play. Devices on the same Wi-Fi or wired network can find each other in the Local section of the new multiplayer menu, across Xbox, PlayStation, Switch, Android, Apple, PC and Mac, with no multiplayer subscription needed. Online crossplay for friends who aren't in the same house is the focus of phase two. Per [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/survival/terraria-devs-make-significant-progress-in-their-quest-to-conquer-the-world-phase-one-of-crossplay-is-now-available-after-years-in-the-making/), PlayStation and Xbox players still need a subscription to join dedicated PC servers, and Switch can't connect to privately hosted dedicated servers at all because of Nintendo's rules. Steam players also get in-game friend invites without the overlay.

<h2 id="where-winds-meet-dlss-5">Where Winds Meet gets DLSS 5 with NVIDIA's new driver</h2>

NVIDIA has added **DLSS 5 with 3D-Guided Neural Rendering** to Everstone Studio's open-world wuxia RPG Where Winds Meet, per its announcement [carried by Worthplaying](https://www.worthplaying.com/article/2026/11/24/news/151498-nvidia-rolls-out-new-game-ready-driver-adds-dlss-5-support-to-where-winds-meet/). NVIDIA says it improves lighting, shadows and material detail, including how light hits foliage, ambient occlusion and reflections. It follows NBA 2K27, Hogwarts Legacy and War Thunder. NVIDIA's own figure is up to 430 FPS at 4K Ultra on an RTX 5090 with DLSS Super Resolution and 6X Multi Frame Generation, so take that as a best case. The same new Game Ready Driver also brings optimizations for Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War IV and adds DLSS 4.5 Dynamic Multi Frame Generation support to Assetto Corsa Rally.

**PPR take:** The Terraria one is my favorite today. Couch and LAN crossplay with no subscription is exactly the kind of thing that gets a family playing together, and phase two is where it gets really interesting. Shoutout to the pumpkin carvers too....doing that by hand with SC flavor takes real work. And DLSS 5 keeps showing up in more games, which is good news if you've got an RTX 50 card.
