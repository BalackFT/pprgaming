**IAE day is here, and so is the free gear.** The Intergalactic Aerospace Expo opens in New Babbage at 11 a.m. ET today, and CIG's [IAE 2956 Twitch Drops](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/transmission/21434-Twitch-Drops-IAE-2956) campaign runs alongside it from November 13 to 27. RSI pitches it as a "flight-ready look" to celebrate the Galaxy finally showing up, and the official art is black armor with orange trim and an orange pack.

## What you get, and when

Watch any Star Citizen stream marked **Drops Enabled**, and the time stacks up:

- **2 hours:** Venture Undersuit Black/Grey
- **4 hours:** Venture Black armor set
- **6 hours:** MacFlex Backpack Orange

Those item names come from the [Star Citizen Wiki's drops page](https://starcitizen.tools/Twitch_drops) and [The Impound's guide](https://theimpound.com/blogs/guides/iae-2956-twitch-drops-rewards-watch-times-and-how-to-claim), and they line up with CIG's undersuit, armor and backpack tiers. The watch time is cumulative, so six hours total over the two weeks gets you all three, and it doesn't have to be one streamer.

## Make sure it actually lands

The step people miss every time is linking. Per the [RSI Knowledge Base](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/42123336848919-Twitch-Drops), go to your RSI Account Dashboard, then Settings, then **Linked Accounts**, and connect the Twitch account you'll actually watch on. Then, when each tier unlocks, you have to hit **Claim** in your Twitch Drops inventory. Watching alone doesn't send it. RSI says it can take up to 72 hours to show up in your hangar, so don't panic if it isn't there right away.

If you're doing the 48-hour rentals and limited-ship waves from the [IAE FAQ](/news/star-citizen-iae-2956-faq-stock-limited-waves-prospector-price/), you're probably going to have a stream up anyway.

**PPR take:** This is the easiest free loot of the year. Most of us are going to have IAE streams running on a second monitor for the next two weeks regardless, between the Galaxy on RSI day, the Marauder, and the Gatac Hyūn on November 21. Six hours is nothing over a fourteen-day expo. Black with orange trim is a sharp look, too, and it'll go with plenty of ships in my hangar. The one thing I'd say: link your accounts BEFORE you start watching. Every drops campaign, someone racks up four hours on a Twitch account that isn't connected to RSI and gets nothing. And it's a nice bonus to toss the new Free Fly folks, since a fresh undersuit and armor beats the starter kit when you're walking New Babbage for the first time. Pick a creator you like with Drops Enabled, claim as you go, and you're set.
