**Starfield modders just got the manual.** Bethesda Game Studios posted on the [Creation Kit's Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/2722710/view/1849827110429641) on Thursday afternoon (2:24 PM ET) that **official Starfield Creation Kit documentation** is now live at [starfield.creationkit.bethesda.net](https://starfield.creationkit.bethesda.net/Main_Page). It's free, and you don't need to log in to read it.

The Creation Kit itself has been a free download on Steam since 2024. Bethesda's pitch hasn't changed: "It's the same tool our own developers used to make Starfield." What's new is a proper wiki explaining how that tool actually works, something modders have mostly had to piece together from Fallout 4 and Skyrim know-how and community guides.

## What's in it

The site is split into four big buckets: **Basics** (setup, the interface, a Form reference, tutorials and a glossary), **Design** (audio, characters, combat, level design, quests and dialogue, worldbuilding and more), **Graphics and Art** (animation, lighting, materials, visual effects, 3D modeling) and **Scripting**, which covers an intro to Papyrus plus the full language reference.

The parts Starfield players will care about most:

- **[Outposts and Shipbuilding](https://starfield.creationkit.bethesda.net/Category:Outposts_and_Shipbuilding)** has 14 pages, including Creating Spaceships, building new outpost modules, ship colors, upgrades and snap templates.
- **[Space](https://starfield.creationkit.bethesda.net/Category:Space)** explains Galaxy View (planet orbit data, overlay locations and biome data), the Planet Content Manager, space cells, and how to make Space Encounters where ship combat happens. There's even a page for the Free Lanes encounters.
- **[Tutorials](https://starfield.creationkit.bethesda.net/Category:Tutorials)** are sorted by who you are: complete beginner, veteran modder (it name-checks Oblivion, Fallout 3, Skyrim and Fallout 4), worldbuilder, storyteller and scripter.

The [Getting Started](https://starfield.creationkit.bethesda.net/Category:Getting_Started) section also has a page on setting up the kit for the **Microsoft Store version** of the game, a "Differences from Fallout 4 to Starfield" page, and Wwise setup for sound designers. Bethesda points people to its official Discord for help. The site is English only, as [2game.info](https://www.2game.info/news/20261121-starfield-creation-kit-docs.html) noted.

**PPR take:** This is the kind of thing that should've shipped with the Creation Kit, BUT I'm glad it's here now. Starfield's best moments on PC lately have come from modders, and the ship and outpost stuff is where the community really goes nuts. Real docs for the Spaceship Editor and the Planet Content Manager lower the bar for new creators, and that's how you get more ship packs, more handmade locations and more reasons to jump back in. What I'd love to see next is a couple of sample projects, like a finished ship mod you can open up and pick apart, because that's how a lot of us actually learn this stuff. Now give us the next big update....please.
