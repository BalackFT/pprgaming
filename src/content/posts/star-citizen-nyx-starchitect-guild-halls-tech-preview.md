Cloud Imperium Games has opened a **third Genesis tech preview** to its Evocati testers, and this one is the first real look at what **Nyx I** will feel like to play rather than just to look at. The build brings the **[Starchitect](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/roadmap/release-view/1591-Genesis-Starchitect)** location tool, a new **Guild Hall** contract system and an early form of **server meshing inside a single planet**.

The test is still limited to Evocati, CIG's NDA testing group, and Live remains on Alpha 4.10.2. But the notes and early impressions, [summarized by Cavalera](https://cavalera.news/editions/2026-10-16) and [StarZen](https://www.starzen.space/t/patch-notes-10-15-2026/39993), lay out how the pieces fit together.

**What Starchitect does:**

- It splits the planet into **sectors** (big areas such as a forest or mountain range, each with a purpose like agriculture, extraction or surveillance), then **clusters** with a visible hub on the star map, then individual **locations** built from modular tiles.
- Each location rolls a **faction**, which changes building tints, loot, NPCs and the contracts on offer.
- Locations get **procedural names** from a prefix, a type and an optional suffix, and large **compounds** are assembled from square tiles.

**Guild Halls and local contracts:** Missions now come from **contract boards inside Guild Halls**, one per regional hub, and completing them earns reputation with that guild. A **local board** at each hub adds short-range jobs. The new board drops the mobiGlas for a larger panel with faction filters. CIG calls the loop **"hub and spoke"**: grab work at the hub, finish it nearby, sometimes on foot or in a ground vehicle, and come back for more. A move to declarative contracts also lets designers build bigger jobs that chain several locations and objective types.

**One planet, three servers:** Until now a whole planet has lived on a single server. In this preview, Nyx I's sectors are **statically split across three servers**. CIG warns testers to expect odd behavior when crossing a boundary and to report it on the Issue Council. It describes this as another step toward full dynamic server meshing.

**Not in yet:** Mineables, harvestables and roaming fauna are switched off until a backend service supports them across server boundaries. DX11 is back for this test alongside Vulkan.

Starchitect and [Planet Tech V5](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/roadmap/release-view/1587-Genesis-Planet-Tech-V5) remain on the RSI roadmap, where Alpha 4.11 is tentatively set for Q4 2026. Watch the [official patch notes forum](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/190048) for wider test waves.

**PPR take:** Guild Halls plus planet-scale meshing is the first time Nyx I sounds like a place you could actually base out of. If the hub-and-spoke loop works, it could keep players planetside for hours instead of bouncing between stations.
