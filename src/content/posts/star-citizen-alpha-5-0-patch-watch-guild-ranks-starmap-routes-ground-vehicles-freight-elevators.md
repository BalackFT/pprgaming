**Now this is the stuff I've been waiting to read about.** Nicou-CIG posted the [first Alpha 5.0 Patch Watch on Spectrum](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/3/thread/alpha-5-0-patch-watch-new-horizons-new-opportuniti/9226412) at about 1:35 PM ET today. Patch Watch posts cover features and fixes that don't always show up on the Public Roadmap, and this one is all about how you'll actually find and run work in 5.0: Guild Halls and Contract Boards, guild reputation, Starmap mission tools, a ground vehicle handling pass and a pile of Freight Elevator fixes. Alpha 5.0 is still on the PTU for Wave 3 testers (latest build [13273298](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/190048/thread/star-citizen-alpha-5-0-ptu-patch-notes-6)), and Live is still 4.10.3 per the [RSI status page](https://status.robertsspaceindustries.com/). CIG says balance and functionality can still change through the PTU, so treat all of this as where things stand right now, not final.

## Guild Halls, Contract Boards and Notice Boards

We first saw Guild Halls show up in [PTU 13119881](/news/star-citizen-alpha-5-0-ptu-13119881-nyx-guild-halls-mission-boards/), and now CIG is explaining the thinking. Guild Halls are physical places with two kinds of boards. **Career Contract Boards** carry work from factions tied to guilds like the Mercenary Guild and the Interstellar Transport Guild, with a tab per faction so you pick who you work for. **Notice Boards** are personal jobs from local settlers, like recovering a lost package. Those pay credits but don't build faction or guild reputation.

CIG calls the idea "hub-and-spoke" gameplay. You land at a settlement, grab a few contracts nearby, run them, maybe on foot or in a Dragonfly, then come back and grab more. Boards also change based on what's around them. A contract that needs two hostile locations, a terminal and a dead drop simply won't show up if those pieces aren't nearby.

## Guild rank is a new gate

This is the big one for anybody grinding rep. Some career contracts now need **both** faction reputation **and** the right guild rank. CIG's example is that even with strong standing at a security contractor, you may still need to climb the Mercenary Guild's ranks to unlock more work. Career contracts raise both at once. Hauling follows the same rules, with higher ranks covering longer runs for bigger pay.

## Starmap, ground vehicles and Freight Elevators

From your ship's Starmap you can now pick a mission marker and hit **Set Route** to the nearest quantum point, and untracked mission markers show without tracking each one. Ground vehicles (except the MULE) get revised suspension and damping, less sliding at low-speed full throttle and better foliage collisions. Haulers get the best news though: you can **hand in cargo for several missions at one Freight Elevator**, elevators now tell you *which* item failed and why, and extra cargo over what a mission needs is **returned instead of eaten**. CIG also lists fixes for stuck transfers and validation errors. The [full post](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/3/thread/alpha-5-0-patch-watch-new-horizons-new-opportuniti/9226412) has the rest, including a note that Nyx I settlements, names and missions may still move around.

**PPR take:** I've been saying it....I log in, run a mission and log out these days, and this hub-and-spoke setup is built for exactly that. Grab three jobs at one settlement, knock them out without flying across the planet, done. That's a real change in how a session feels. The guild rank gate I'm a little more careful about. Layering rank on top of faction rep could be great long-term progression, or it could feel like grinding the same thing twice. We'll see how it plays on the PTU. And the cargo fix? Losing extra boxes into a Freight Elevator has burned plenty of haulers, so getting the excess back is long overdue. Honestly, this Patch Watch makes 5.0 feel less like a tech demo and more like a game loop. Bring on the next build!
