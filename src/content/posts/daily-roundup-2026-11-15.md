Hey everyone, hope your Sunday's been a good one! If you spent Anvil Day parking ships on a Liberator, the first item's for you.

<h2 id="anvil-liberator-rrr-top-pads-clarification">CIG clears up the Liberator's repair, refuel and rearm</h2>

There's been plenty of back and forth on Spectrum about whether the Liberator's top pads give you RRR. Nicou-CIG [answered it directly](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/3/thread/cig-what-s-the-deal-with-the-liberator-rrr-on-top-/9229040): in Alpha 4.10.3, repair, refuel and rearm is **limited to the internal garages** to head off exploits, but CIG is "fast-tracking a potential solve" for 5.0 that would turn it on for all of her landing pads and garages. The same post says the Pledge Store wrongly lists her as a "Dropship," and she's actually a Light Carrier. In a [follow-up](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/3/thread/cig-what-s-the-deal-with-the-liberator-rrr-on-top-/9229557), Nicou also confirmed some ships are sliding off the pads because of her small local grid. Each pad is meant to be about 24 x 32 x 16 meters, and the current height limit isn't meant to be permanent.

<h2 id="rainbow-six-siege-operation-vital-signal-december-1">Rainbow Six Siege reveals Operation Vital Signal for December 1</h2>

Ubisoft laid out Y11S4, [Operation Vital Signal](https://news.ubisoft.com/en-us/article/57b0TcWurSuTMn9oJD4fJ4/rainbow-six-siege-operation-vital-signal-doc-remaster-new-mbz19-weapon-ranked-overhaul-and-more), arriving December 1. Doc gets a remaster, trading his Stim Pistol for an E-HEAL Monitor that remotely triggers medical patches defenders spawn with, so he can heal and revive teammates across the map. Jäger, Warden and Thunderbird get a new MBZ-19 assault rifle, and both the ACS12 and TCSG12 shotguns take damage cuts. Ranked splits into Solo/Duo and Flex queues with separate ranks, and the entry level drops from 50 to 30. Bank gets a new area out back, and Yacht, Favela and Tower are next in line for the Modernized Maps work.

**PPR take:** Good on CIG for just saying it plainly. Top-pad RRR is basically the whole reason a lot of folks want a Liberator, so hearing it's being worked on for 5.0 (and not "someday") is a relief....just remember "potential solve" isn't a promise yet. As for Siege, a Doc who can heal without standing in the doorway is long overdue, and splitting solo players from five-stacks in Ranked should make a lot of people happier.
