**Weather got a brain this build.** CIG pushed **Alpha 5.0 PTU build 13248297** on Friday evening, and the [patch notes are up on Spectrum](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/190048/thread/star-citizen-alpha-5-0-ptu-patch-notes-4). It's still **Wave 3** testers only, on the **US-only** PTU channel, with **long-term persistence** on. It follows [build 13238477](/news/star-citizen-alpha-5-0-ptu-13238477-freight-elevators-loot-scatter-collision/) from Thursday. Live is still Alpha 4.10.3 on the [RSI status page](https://status.robertsspaceindustries.com/).

The notes say a **25x reputation reward multiplier** is active on the PTU right now, and **VOIP reliability** joins the test focus list next to Genesis, Nyx I, Starchitect, mission boards, intraplanetary server meshing, vehicle TTK and the missile lock interface.

## Lightning that reads the sky

Lightning has been "substantially reworked." Instead of a fixed random rate, strike frequency now comes from the **actual cloud coverage and lightning strength** in the weather system, mapped across a grid, so a fully overcast sky behaves differently from patchy cloud. Only one strike at a time can light up the volumetric clouds. Genesis also gets reduced VRAM use, more terrain shader optimization, water and caustics that render properly at a distance, and creatures and harvestables spawning in the right zones.

## New places on Nyx I

Two new Starchitect location types land on Nyx I: **extraction hubs** and **medium settlements**. Landing pads got a lighting pass, surveillance towers have better night lighting, and the Forager's Guide now covers Nyx I. On the contract side, remote locations now read "Unoccupied Territory" instead of a blank address, and CIG lists potential fixes for Eliminate Boss contracts failing on the Vaughn target and for courier deliveries being miscounted.

## Rain on the hull

Audio got a big pass. Cold winds and sandstorms join improved rain, small buildings now muffle the rain a bit, and **rain is now audible inside ship interiors**. Ground ambience fades out as you climb. Voice chat gets two long-standing fixes: releasing push-to-talk no longer clips your last words, and party chat stops replaying speech seconds or minutes later.

Other potential fixes include missiles doing no damage to ships with interiors (STARC-204924), ships failing to stow from instanced hangars (STARC-222180), and a crash tied to the Drake Cutter's rear landing gear. CIG also temporarily disabled the Ursa Medivac alarm that was looping forever.

**PPR take:** Lightning that actually follows the clouds is the kind of thing nobody puts in a trailer....and it's exactly what makes a planet feel alive. Sitting in your ship on the ground while a storm rolls in, and now you can actually hear the rain on the hull? Yes please. That's the 5.0 I keep talking about, the stuff that really shines on the ground. The VOIP fixes are a sneaky big deal too. Anyone who's run an op and had their last word chopped off knows what I mean. A 25x rep multiplier on the PTU tells you CIG wants testers grinding contracts hard. Still no Live date, BUT the builds keep coming.
