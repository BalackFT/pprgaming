**One week out.** S-GAME has dropped the **[official launch trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qgJBFa952mY)** for **Phantom Blade Zero**, and it's packed with weapons and bosses we hadn't seen. The game launches digitally on **PS5 and PC (Steam and Epic Games Store)** on **October 28 at 7 PM PT, which is 10 PM ET** (October 29 in most of the world, which is the date on the [official site](https://pbz.s-game.com/en-US/)). According to [GamingBolt](https://gamingbolt.com/phantom-blade-zero-launch-trailer-teases-new-weapons-and-bosses-physical-edition-announced), **pre-loading opens October 26** at the same time.

**Physical copies come later.** S-GAME also announced that **Standard, Deluxe and Ultimate physical editions** for PS5 will launch in **2027**, per [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/10/phantom-blade-zero-launch-trailer-ps5-physical-editions-launch-in-2027). GamingBolt says the Ultimate Edition comes with a collectible steel case.

**What the trailer shows.** GamingBolt picked out a bladed umbrella that Soul throws like a buzzsaw to finish enemies, dual blades that let him turn into a raven and dive on targets, and a two-handed sword that unleashes a fiery tornado. S-GAME has promised at least 50 bosses (with "a few more" teased), and says enemies won't respawn.

**The setup.** You play Soul, a warrior framed for his master's death and left with a ruined heart and **66 days to live**. S-GAME calls the style "Kungfupunk," a mix of Wuxia, fantasy and folk horror inspired by Hong Kong's golden age of action films, built in Unreal Engine 5 with motion-captured fight choreography. There are more than **30 weapons** and over 20 "Phantom Edges" to mix and match, and beating powerful foes earns you their weapons and signature techniques.

**PPR take:** Phantom Blade Zero was one of the headliners in our [October release roundup](/news/october-2026-game-releases-before-gta-6/), and this trailer makes a strong last pitch before GTA 6 takes over everyone's November. If you're on PC, it's worth setting the pre-load reminder for the 26th.
