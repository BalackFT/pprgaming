**The demons don't stop coming now.** id Software released [Update 5 for DOOM: The Dark Ages | Revelations](https://slayersclub.bethesda.net/en-US/news/doom-the-dark-ages-revelations-slaughter-mode-update-5-release-notes) today, and the headliner is **Slaughter Mode**, the endless horde survival mode Xbox teased at its [25th anniversary event](/news/xbox-25th-anniversary-id-software-new-game-fable-gameplay-halo-firefight/) last week. It costs nothing extra if you own the Revelations DLC, the Premium Edition or the Premium Upgrade.

## How Slaughter Mode works

Each run is a chain of rounds. Every round is either a randomized or a curated encounter, and once you clear the arena you get pulled into the next one. Per [id's announcement](https://slayersclub.bethesda.net/en-US/news/doom-the-dark-ages-revelations-slaughter-mode-release-date), "no two runs through Slaughter Mode are identical," with bosses and arenas mixed in as the heat turns up.

You earn **Skulls** as you go. They raise your Profile Level and unlock rewards like Slaughter Mode-only Slayer skins, weapon skins you can use in every mode, profile icons, and tracks from Andrew Hulshult's IDKFA soundtrack. Weekly challenges add more ways to earn.

The interesting part is the checkpoints. Reach a Round Checkpoint and you can **Secure** your Skulls so a death can't take them, which also unlocks that round as a starting point for future runs. Or you pick **Risk It**, which bumps your Skull multiplier. Make the next checkpoint and you can push your luck again. Die before you get there and every at-risk Skull is gone.

## The rest of Update 5

The [release notes](https://slayersclub.bethesda.net/en-US/news/doom-the-dark-ages-revelations-slaughter-mode-update-5-release-notes) go well beyond the new mode. Standard ammo now refills between levels in both campaigns, the **Combat Shotgun** gets a damage boost (small Blast explosions do double damage with a bigger radius), and there are new UI options to turn off screen-edge damage effects or dial the low-health vignette anywhere from 0 to 100%. On PC, the game now supports **AMD FSR 4**, including FSR Upscaling 4.1 and Frame Generation 4.

**PPR take:** A horde mode in DOOM feels like it should've always been there, and the Risk It system is a smart hook. Banking your Skulls or betting them on one more checkpoint is the exact "one more run" trap that keeps people up way too late. Making the mode free for Revelations and Premium owners is the right call too. Nobody wants to pay twice for content in a game they already own. Plus, the shotgun buff and the ammo refill between levels are good quality of life for the campaign side. With GTA VI landing Thursday, id picked a smart window to give people something to chew on this week....rip and tear while you wait!
