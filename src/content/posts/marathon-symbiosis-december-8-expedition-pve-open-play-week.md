**Marathon is getting a mode where nobody can shoot you in the back.** Bungie's next update, **Symbiosis**, lands **December 8**, and its headline feature is Expedition, the first phase of a permanent PvE-only mode. The details came out Tuesday on the [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/10/marathon-symbiosis-update-launches-december-8-adding-new-pve-and-pvp-modes-weapons-and-more/) alongside a new trailer.

Expedition is built around a three-run series you can play solo or with a crew, with "new combat and environmental challenges without PvP threats." Flash Boosts let you shape your build during a run. For a game that launched as a pure extraction shooter, that's a real change in who Marathon is for.

The rest of the update, in short:

- **Perimeter grows.** Fungal growth has spread across the zone, adding the underground Burrows and the submerged Reservoir, plus a Tick Queen lurking deep below.
- **Deathmatch (Beta)** is a 3v3 team mode where two crews play for points without risking their permanent loadouts.
- **Limbo** is a new social space with firing ranges and a dueling pit.
- **Bishop** is a new Runner shell, a fast, blade-wielding robot, and the V17 Flamethrower and Maverick revolver join the arsenal.

The bigger hook for newcomers is the **Open Play Week**. [Bungie says](https://www.bungie.net/7/en/News/Article/symbiosis_open_play_week_crew_up) that from December 8 to 15, anyone can download and play the full game without buying it, including Expedition, Bishop and the updated Perimeter. If you buy Marathon after the free week, you keep the items and progression you earned. Bungie is pairing that with a **Crew Up** event (December 8 at 12 PM PT through December 22 at 10 AM PT) built around Expedition challenges. Bungie says those tasks can't be done solo, and you'll need to redeem an event code on Bungie.net to take part. The rewards are cosmetics, including a Destroyer skin and an "Exterminator" title. After December 15 you'll need to own the game to keep going.

[The Game Post](https://thegamepost.com/marathon-symbiosis-update-pve-deathmatch-weapons/) notes this is the PvE content Bungie had originally planned for September before delaying it.

## The PPR take

This is the smartest move Bungie has made with Marathon. Extraction shooters are brutal on new players: you lose your gear to someone who's played for 200 hours and you close the game. A PvE mode with no PvP threat gives people a way to learn the guns, the maps and the loop at their own pace, and a Deathmatch mode that doesn't touch your permanent loadout does the same thing for players who just want the shooting.

The timing is the other half. A free week right as the PvE mode drops, with progress carrying over if you buy, is the cleanest pitch Bungie can make to everyone who passed at launch. The catch is that Bungie calls Expedition "Phase 1" and hasn't detailed what comes next, so judge it on what's there on December 8. Either way, it costs nothing to find out.
