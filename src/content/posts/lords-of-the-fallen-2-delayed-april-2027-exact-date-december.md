**Lords of the Fallen II has moved again.** CI Games CEO **Marek Tyminski** kept it short on X on Tuesday: "Lords of the Fallen II: April 2027. Exact date in December," as quoted by [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/action-rpg/lords-of-the-fallen-2-launches-in-april-2027-boss-of-the-soulslike-rpg-announces-and-a-release-date-is-coming-next-month/) and [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/ps5-rpg-lords-of-the-fallen-2-locks-on-to-april-2027-release-date-following-lengthy-delay).

CI Games also made it official in a stock market filing, [current report 39/2026](https://cigames.com/en/ci-games-se-39-2026-update-on-the-development-schedule-of-lords-of-the-fallen-ii/), dated November 24. The management board says the release "is planned for April 2027" and that the exact date "will be publicly announced in December 2026." Neither the post nor the filing gives a reason.

## The second slip

This isn't the first move. Push Square notes the sequel was first aimed at autumn 2026 before it was pushed to a **Q1 2027** window over the summer. April lands just past the end of that window, so it's a short slip on paper, but it's still a delay. GamesRadar+ points out that Tyminski's post doesn't call it one.

The [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4767480/) still just says "Coming soon." CI Games is listed as both developer and publisher there.

**PPR take:** A few extra weeks for a soulslike is not the end of the world, and honestly, getting out of the early-2027 pileup is probably smart. GTA 6 is still eating everyone's free time right now, and the first quarter is already stacked. If the extra time goes into polish and performance, players win. The part I'd watch is the December date reveal. A filing that commits to April and a date announcement next month is a lot firmer than "Q1." If December comes and goes without that date, that's when I'd start worrying.
