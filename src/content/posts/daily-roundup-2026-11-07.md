Hey all - quick Saturday catch-up on two stories that landed around the edges of today's full posts.

<h2 id="gears-of-war-20th-free-raam">Gears of War turns 20, and every E-Day player gets free RAAM</h2>

Today is the exact 20th anniversary of the original *Gears of War* on Xbox 360 - November 7, 2006 - and The Coalition is marking it inside [Gears of War: E-Day](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/06/gears-of-war-e-day-season-2-20th-anniversary/). Studio brand director Nicole Fawcette's [Xbox Wire post](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/06/gears-of-war-e-day-season-2-20th-anniversary/) says every E-Day player can claim **RAAM** as a free multiplayer character by logging in. No purchase, match, or challenge is listed. A two-week anniversary event is already running with limited-time modes: **Guardian** in Versus (only one leader makes it out alive) and **For the Queen** in Horde Siege (supply cache disabled, so squads lean on loadouts). Season 2, titled **Legacy**, arrives December 1 with returning maps, modes, and skins, including Solemn Memories Dom from the famous Dom and Maria photo in *Gears of War 2*. The post does not spell out a hard claim cutoff after today, so logging in on the anniversary itself is the safe play.

<h2 id="wow-forever-record-launch">Blizzard: WoW Forever posts the biggest concurrent launch in WoW history</h2>

Three days after [World of Warcraft: Forever went live](/news/world-of-warcraft-forever-launches-today-november-4/), Blizzard is celebrating the numbers it will share. The official [@Warcraft account](https://x.com/Warcraft/status/2118893768984162668) posted late Friday night (ET): Forever achieved "the biggest launch in WoW history by peak concurrent players" and "the highest number of returning players in WoW history." No headcount was published. Executive producer Holly Longdale [added](https://x.com/TheWindstalker/status/2118894053094035457) that Forever is also seeing "the most NEW players for a WoW launch in 15 years." [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/world-of-warcraft/world-of-warcraft-forever-brought-back-a-record-number-of-lapsed-players-and-has-the-biggest-concurrent-player-launch-in-the-mmos-history/) and other outlets carried the same claims. Forever remains the permanent third branch alongside modern WoW and Classic, included with a regular subscription, so the open question is how long the comeback crowd sticks once the launch week glow fades.

**PPR take:** A free Locust general on the day Gears turns 20 is the kind of anniversary gift that actually gets people to boot the game, and Season 2: Legacy in December keeps the nostalgia train rolling with maps and skins pulled from the whole franchise. On the WoW side, Blizzard saying "biggest ever" without publishing a headcount is classic Blizzard - still, peak concurrent, most returning players, and most new players in 15 years is a hell of a launch week for a 2004-shaped Azeroth. Two very different franchises having a good Saturday for different reasons, and both of them earned the win by giving players a reason to show up.
