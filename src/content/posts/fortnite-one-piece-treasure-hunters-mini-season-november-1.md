**The Straw Hats are on their way.** After days of pirate-themed teasers, Epic used its 2 PM ET reveal on Saturday to confirm what fans had been hoping for. The [official Fortnite account](https://x.com/Fortnite/status/2114055025161064570) posted a seven-second clip of the **Going Merry**, the Straw Hat Pirates' first ship, rising out of moonlit fog, followed by the Fortnite and One Piece logos. The caption was just three straw hats: "👒👒👒".

**The details so far**

- **When:** the **Treasure Hunters** mini-season starts **Sunday, November 1**, after downtime, when Chapter 7 Season 4 ends. Epic's [earlier teaser](https://x.com/Fortnite/status/2113979023504351609) called it "The greatest adventure of all! 11.1."
- **Which One Piece:** the anime. [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/fortnite-one-piece-confirmed-mini-season-teaser/) notes the Merry's figurehead matches the animated series rather than Netflix's live-action version.
- **The other crews:** an in-game news image ahead of the reveal showed several pirate crew logos, including Peely, A$AP Rocky, Hatsune Miku, Kasane Teto and Lil Uzi Vert.
- **How long:** Polygon expects the mini-season to run about a month before a new chapter begins.

**Still unknown:** Epic hasn't said which Straw Hats will appear as skins, whether they're in the Battle Pass or the Item Shop, or what they'll cost.

**PPR take:** One Piece has been near the top of Fortnite collab wish lists for years, and launching it inside a treasure-hunting season is a natural fit. If you're behind on the current Battle Pass, you've got one week.
