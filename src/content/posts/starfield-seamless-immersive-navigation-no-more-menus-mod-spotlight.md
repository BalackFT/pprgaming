**Sit down, look left, pick a star, hold a key....and you're gone.** That's the whole pitch for **[Seamless Immersive Navigation - No More Menus](https://www.nexusmods.com/starfield/mods/18779)** by modder **AhayOziel**. It moves Starfield's travel out of the star map and onto your ship's HUD, so you never have to leave the pilot seat. It went up on Nexus Mods on **November 9**, version **0.3.1** followed on **November 15**, and it's already at 45 endorsements and in Nexus's mods of the month voting.

**What it does.** Once you're in the seat, looking left or right opens a list drawn in the game's own HUD style. According to the [mod page](https://www.nexusmods.com/starfield/mods/18779):

- **Left list:** systems one jump away, with a little star globe. Systems you can't reach show up red, and a diamond marks one with a quest.
- **Right list:** landing sites on the planet you're orbiting, plus an in-orbit tab with every planet, moon and station in the system.
- **Hold to go:** hold the jump key and you grav jump, pick a landing site and you land, or pick a body and the ship cruises there on the game's own autopilot.
- **Fuel gauge** above the power bars, built for the Real Fuel Creation (or always on, if you set it).
- **Settings in the game** under Settings > Gameplay: colors, keys, hold time and the fuel gauge.

In version 0.3.1, if your tracked quest's system is out of reach, it gets pinned to the top of the list, and holding jump takes you as far toward it as your fuel allows. AhayOziel says controller support and third-person fixes are next.

**Platforms, price and requirements:**

- **Platform:** PC only. It's an SFSE plugin on Nexus Mods, so there's no Xbox version.
- **Price:** free.
- **Required:** Starfield 1.16.244, Starfield Script Extender (SFSE) and Address Library for SFSE Plugins. After a game update the mod switches itself off until it's updated. The game's Free Look action also needs a key bound (Q, the default, works).
- **Recommended:** 0xBobby's [True Seamless Grav Jumps SFSE](https://www.nexusmods.com/starfield/mods/17159), which removes the grav jump loading screen. If you use AstroNAV Ship HUD, set `HideInFreeLook=false` in its ini or the lists get hidden.
- **Heads up:** the mod is tagged as AI-generated content on Nexus, and it's early. The [comments](https://www.nexusmods.com/starfield/mods/18779?tab=posts) have some bug reports, and the author has been answering them.

**PPR take:** Starfield's ships look great, but getting anywhere has always meant ducking out to a map screen, and that kills the feeling of actually flying the thing. Pair this with 0xBobby's seamless grav jumps and the [takeoff mod we covered last month](/news/starfield-seamless-planet-takeoffs-mod-spotlight/), and your ship starts to feel like a ship instead of a menu with a cockpit view. It's version 0.3, so expect some rough edges....BUT this is the direction Starfield's flying should have gone from day one.
