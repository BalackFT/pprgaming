**Now Sony has a number too, and it's a big one.** In a [PlayStation.Blog post](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/23/thank-you-for-making-grand-theft-auto-vi-the-biggest-launch-in-playstation-history/) Monday, Sony Interactive Entertainment's Tim Turi says Grand Theft Auto VI is "the biggest game launch in PlayStation history, across three decades and five console generations." [@PlayStation](https://x.com/PlayStation/status/2125009125322957131) followed at 6:30 PM ET: more than **39 million unique players** have jumped into GTA VI on PS5.

## What Sony shared

All of it is SIE internal data from **November 19 to November 22**, launch day through the opening weekend:

- **39 million+ unique players** on PS5
- A **new record for the most concurrent players** of any game on PlayStation, beating multiplayer, free-to-play and single-player titles. Sony didn't give the number.
- **683 million hours** played across PS5, or an average of **17.4 hours per player** in the opening days
- The **most newly connected PS5 consoles on a single day** since the PS5 launched, not counting end-of-year holidays
- GTA VI was the **first game played on 70%** of new PS5 consoles since it came out

Sony also pointed to its two limited-edition Vice City DualSense controllers and Grand Theft Auto VI: The Festival in Miami this past weekend.

## Players, not sales

Keep the labels straight. Sony is counting people who played on PS5, not copies sold, and it's PlayStation only. The only official sales numbers are still Take-Two's: [**$3.79 billion** in day-one retail sales and **32.5 million** units before launch](https://pprgaming.com/news/gta-6-3-79-billion-day-one-sales-32-5-million-pre-release-take-two/), both company estimates. It also fits what Circana saw on launch day, when [61% of active US PS5 players were in the game](https://pprgaming.com/news/gta-6-comcast-record-traffic-circana-61-percent-launch-day-engagement/). Rockstar still hasn't shared a week-one count of its own. Everything else we have is on the [GTA 6 hub](/gta-6/).

## PPR take

So yeah, the victory laps have started. Take-Two had its billions on Friday, Comcast had its traffic record, and now Sony gets its turn. Everybody wants a piece of this one, and honestly I get it - they've been waiting over a decade to brag about this one.

But the number I actually care about isn't the 39 million. It's 17.4 hours per player in four days. That's not a hype number, that's people sitting down and playing the thing. Same goes for 70% of new PS5s starting with GTA. There were a lot of fresh consoles under the TV this weekend, and most of them went straight to Leonida.

Sony keeping the concurrent record number to itself is a little funny, though. "Trust us, it's a record." Sure. Still, millions of people are clearly having a blast, and that's the part that matters. Sony gets the press release, Rockstar gets the money, and players get the game. Not a bad weekend for anybody.
