**Pywel got a Thanksgiving patch.** Pearl Abyss released [Crimson Desert patch 2.06.00](https://crimsondesert.pearlabyss.com/EN-US/News/Notice/Detail?_boardNo=163) Thursday morning at 7:20 AM ET. It's live now on Steam (PC and Mac), PlayStation and the Epic Games Store. Xbox and the Mac App Store are still listed as "in-progress," so console players on Xbox will have to wait a little longer.

This one is mostly about housing and camp management, which has been a sore spot since [the 2.05.00 update](/news/crimson-desert-patch-2-05-00-charting-the-unknown-mac-boss-retry/). Here's what most players will notice.

## The changes that matter most

- **You don't need a stack of copies to decorate anymore.** Once you register an installable item, you can place more of it by spending Camp Resources. Anything you've already placed gets registered automatically, and if you placed two or more of the same item, you're refunded for all but one. Rare items that used to be limited to one can now go down multiple times too.
- **Facility numbers are getting reset.** Pearl Abyss raised the requirements for facility grades tenfold and scaled up the Management stats on installables to match. The notes warn that your Placement Score, Facility Grade and Daily Upkeep Cost may change after the update even if you didn't touch anything.
- **Unplaced furniture can be sold** at furniture shops, and add-on content items can finally go into storage.
- **The Gigantified Titan's attack pattern has been adjusted** for difficulty.
- **A sharpness toggle for Frame Generation** is now in the graphics settings.

There are smaller quality-of-life bits too. Cooking tools can be enhanced to the next level, the Dewhaven Shotgun is now sold by Forrex the Cannonwright at Rustfield Scrapyard, and, per [VULKK's rundown](https://vulkk.com/2026/11/26/crimson-desert-update-2-06-changes-overview/), the A.T.A.G. no longer sinks when you hop off it on the water's surface. The [full notes](https://crimsondesert.pearlabyss.com/EN-US/News/Notice/Detail?_boardNo=163) also cover a long list of quest, visual and localization fixes.

**PPR take:** This is the housing fix people have been asking for. Having to own a separate copy of every single lamp and table just to decorate a camp was a grind that added nothing, so register-once-and-place is the right call. The part I'd keep an eye on is the tenfold grade change. Pearl Abyss is being upfront that your numbers will move, but if your upkeep jumps overnight, now you know why before you go blaming a bug. The sharpness toggle is a small one that PC players will appreciate, since Frame Generation can make the image look a little over-processed. I've put a lot of hours into this game, and Pearl Abyss keeps patching it at a steady clip. Xbox players, hang tight....yours is on the way.
