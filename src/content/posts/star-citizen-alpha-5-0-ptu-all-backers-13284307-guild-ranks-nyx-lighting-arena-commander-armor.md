**Alpha 5.0 is open to everybody now.** CIG posted the [All Waves patch notes for PTU build 13284307](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/190048/thread/star-citizen-alpha-5-0-ptu-patch-notes-6-1) on Spectrum Wednesday afternoon, and the audience line reads "All Backers." Until now 5.0 testing was limited to Wave 1 and then Wave 3 testers, with the last of those builds being [13273298](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/190048/thread/star-citizen-alpha-5-0-ptu-patch-notes-6) on Tuesday.

A few things to know before you download. The notes say the server is on the **PTU channel, US only**, and long-term persistence is enabled. A **50x reputation reward multiplier** is active on the PTU, so rep climbs fast while you test. CIG's testing focus list covers stability, Genesis (Planet Tech V5), Nyx I, Starchitect points of interest, mission boards and local missions, intraplanetary server meshing, vehicle TTK balance, the missile lock interface and VOIP reliability. Live is still Alpha 4.10.3, per the [RSI status page](https://status.robertsspaceindustries.com/), and there's no Live date for 5.0 yet.

## The key changes in 13284307

- **Guild ranks have names now.** The guild reputation pages in the mobiGlas were blank before. Now the first six ranks for each guild show names and icons, which ties right into the guild rank system CIG explained in [yesterday's Patch Watch](/news/star-citizen-alpha-5-0-patch-watch-guild-ranks-starmap-routes-ground-vehicles-freight-elevators/).
- **Starchitect locations get a lighting and performance pass.** Light leaks are sealed across guild halls, compounds and interiors, and CIG says the imposter shader is around 25% faster, with the planet texture bake "substantially sped up."
- **Weather looks right on Genesis planets.** Sky light now dims properly under ground fog and rain, ground cover stops floating near bedrock, and sunset clouds get optional atmospheric reddening so they don't wash out.
- **Arena Commander armor works again.** A potential fix targets armor giving no protection in AC, which had most weapons killing in one or two shots.
- **Hauling and travel fixes.** Quantum routes should stop resetting right after you set them, and the "Failed Awaiting Manual Delivery to Freight Elevator" error at commodity kiosks has a potential fix.

There's plenty more, including contract generation fixes, audio polish and a batch of crash fixes, so check the [full patch notes](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/190048/thread/star-citizen-alpha-5-0-ptu-patch-notes-6-1) before you jump in.

**PPR take:** Opening the PTU to all backers is the step I've been watching for. It doesn't mean 5.0 is coming to Live tomorrow....but it does mean CIG wants a whole lot more people hammering on Nyx I and the server meshing before it goes out. And with that 50x rep boost on the PTU, it's a great time to go poke at the guild halls and see how the new rank layer actually feels. I'll be honest, the Arena Commander armor fix made me laugh a little. One or two shots and you're done is not "TTK balance." The lighting work matters too. Those Starchitect locations looked a bit flat and white everywhere, so seeing CIG tint them and tune them is good news. Thanksgiving weekend on the PTU? Sounds about right to me!
