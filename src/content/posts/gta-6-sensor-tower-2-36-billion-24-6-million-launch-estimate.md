**The first real sales picture for GTA 6 is in, and it's an estimate, not an official count.** Market research firm Sensor Tower says Grand Theft Auto VI brought in **more than $2.36 billion** in less than 24 hours after its midnight launch, [Deadline reported](https://deadline.com/2026/11/gta-6-sales-launch-day-surge-videogame-1237229514/) Thursday afternoon. That figure includes pre-orders.

## What Sensor Tower is estimating

Per Deadline, Sensor Tower puts GTA VI at **24.59 million units**, with about **75% on PlayStation** and the rest on Xbox. Sales rose about **$710 million** from early Wednesday, when pre-sales stood at $1.65 billion. Karl Kontus, Sensor Tower's SVP of Video Game Insights, told Deadline that pre-order revenue alone passed $2 billion through launch day.

The number climbed fast through Thursday. [Reuters](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/taketwo-set-to-shatter-sales-records-as-gta-vi-launches-to-massive-fanfare-5039603) reported Sensor Tower's morning estimate as 19.1 million units and about $1.84 billion. [USA Today](https://www.usatoday.com/story/tech/gaming/2026/11/19/gta-6-major-takeaways-release-day/92862268007/) broke that earlier figure down as about $1.38 billion on PlayStation and around $464 million on Xbox.

For comparison, Reuters says GTA V sold roughly **$800 million** worth of copies on its first day in 2013, long seen as the 24-hour record.

## How the estimate works, and what's official

The [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c9zr5nyv5dj2o) says Sensor Tower's estimates come from email receipt data and other statistical sources, plus public profiles on PlayStation and Xbox. Rockstar and Take-Two haven't released sales figures. They've only said demand is "unprecedented," and the BBC says it has asked both for comment. Niko Partners told the BBC it expects more than $3 billion worldwide this year.

People are watching it too. [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/20/gta-vi-launch-streaming-content-economy.html) cites Streams Charts at **125.5 million hours** of GTA VI viewing across Twitch, YouTube and Kick in the first 24 hours.

Take-Two hasn't said when it will share its own numbers, so treat every figure here as an estimate. The PC version and an online mode are still unannounced.

**PPR take:** Honestly, nobody is shocked by this. Thirteen years of waiting, a 99 on Metacritic and a marketing machine running in every direction - of course the money showed up. What I like is the gap between the noise and what's actually confirmed. Every number floating around is Sensor Tower working off receipts, and Take-Two is sitting there saying "unprecedented" and nothing else. Let the estimates do the bragging for you, right? BUT I'd still wait for Take-Two's own numbers before anyone crowns it the biggest launch ever. Estimates moved by half a billion dollars in a single day....so the final count could look different. Either way, $2 billion plus for a single-player game on two consoles? That's wild.
