**Grand Theft Auto VI went live in the US at 12:00 AM ET Thursday, right on schedule.** New Zealand, Australia, Japan and Europe had already been in since Wednesday, and the US was one of the last big markets to unlock.

## How the unlock went

Quiet, from what the official channels showed. The US unlock hit at midnight Eastern with no delay, and Rockstar's service status page listed all services as operational when we checked around 12:30 AM ET. [Xbox posted "Grand Theft Auto VI is out now"](https://x.com/XBOX/status/2123280111315386533) right at midnight, and Xbox Support had no outage or delay post when we looked again at 1:20 AM ET. The [Rockstar Store](https://store.rockstargames.com/) now says "Out now on PS5 and XBOX Series X|S."

There's no official day-one patch to talk about either. Rockstar hasn't published patch notes for launch, and its Newswire hasn't posted anything since the November 10 pre-load announcement.

## Xbox Cloud Gaming is live

[Xbox Wire said](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/10/grand-theft-auto-6-comes-to-xbox-cloud-gaming-november-19/) streaming would start at 1:00 AM ET, an hour after the console unlock. Checked after that time, the [official Xbox game page](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/games/grand-theft-auto-vi) reads "Available Now" and says Xbox One owners can play through Xbox Cloud Gaming at Series S settings. The [Xbox Store listing](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/games/store/grand-theft-auto-vi/9nl3wwnzlzzn) marks it as cloud playable with Game Pass Essential, Premium or Ultimate. You still have to buy the game.

## Still waiting on the poster and map

Rockstar said a printable cover poster and a map of Leonida would be free downloads on launch day. As of this morning, neither is listed on the [official downloads page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/downloads). It has the cover art, wallpapers, region postcards and a few new launch images, but not those two.

## What the critics said

Reviews landed Wednesday, and Metacritic now shows a 99 Metascore from 156 critic reviews. [Our reviews story](/news/gta-6-reviews-score-metacritic-99-opencritic-98/) has the breakdown, with no plot talk.

## Still not announced

PC, and any online mode for GTA VI. Rockstar hasn't said a word on either.

**PPR take:** Honestly, the best launch news is no news. A game this big unlocking at midnight with no delay and no outage posts from Rockstar or Xbox is not a given. Ask anybody who's sat through a big online launch. The cloud part is still the story I keep coming back to. Somebody on an old Xbox One can stream the biggest game of the decade on day one, at Series S settings, and that's a real door opening for a lot of people who couldn't afford a new console this year. The missing poster and map are a small thing BUT Rockstar promised them for today....so we'll keep an eye on the downloads page and update when they show.
