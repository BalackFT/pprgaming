**New patch? Time to go to work!** CIG pushed **Alpha 5.0 PTU build 13260598** just before 1 PM ET today, and the [patch notes are up on Spectrum](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/190048/thread/star-citizen-alpha-5-0-ptu-patch-notes-5). It's still **Wave 3** testers only, on the **US-only** PTU channel, with **long-term persistence** on. It follows [build 13248297](/news/star-citizen-alpha-5-0-ptu-13248297-lightning-rework-nyx-settlements-rain-audio-voip/) from Friday, and the **25x reputation multiplier** is still running. Live is still Alpha 4.10.3 on the [RSI status page](https://status.robertsspaceindustries.com/).

The test focus list didn't change: Genesis, Nyx I, Starchitect locations, mission boards, intraplanetary server meshing, vehicle TTK, the missile lock interface and VOIP.

## Armor you can actually see

This is the big one for me. CIG says **armor values across all ships** have been brought in line with the recent TTK balancing, and armor now shows up on the **engineering MFD**, so you can check its condition right next to your other systems. On the Starmap, the objective you're tracking now always draws on top when markers merge together. Before, the order was random, so an untracked marker could cover the one you were following.

## Wet ground that looks right

Genesis keeps getting polish. **Wet terrain shading** has been reworked, puddles no longer form on bare bedrock, and **rain reflections** now line up with the horizon instead of sitting at a weird angle. There are more VRAM savings too, with ground albedo detail only loading near the camera, plus a potential fix for puddles not rendering right on parts of Nyx I. Starchitect storage and habitation sets got a lighting pass.

## Contracts and crashes

Potential fixes include **Very Hard and Super Bounty Hunter contracts** not showing on mobiGlas or mission boards, a pirate showing up in an **890 Jump**, targets spawning far from their escorts, and "PLACEHOLDER" mission boxes. There's also a fix for PvP bounties sticking around after hacking Security Post Kareah (STARC-214658). On the crash side, CIG lists fixes for the client freezing while planet textures load and crashing when you hover over inventory items.

Over on Spectrum, CIG's audio team is replying to testers in the [previous build's thread](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/190048/thread/star-citizen-alpha-5-0-ptu-patch-notes-4) and says it's still tuning the weather mix and looking into loud beam and laser sounds.

**PPR take:** Armor on the engineering MFD is one of those things that makes total sense the second you read it. If CIG is going to rebalance how long ships take to die, I want to see how much armor I've got left without guessing. That's real feedback, not a number buried in a spreadsheet. The bounty fix matters too. Very Hard and Super bounties not showing up is a big hole when CIG is asking testers to grind rep at 25x. And the wet terrain work? That's 5.0 on the ground again....which is exactly where it shines. An 890 Jump as a random pirate spawn is a little funny though..lol. Still no Live date, BUT three Wave 3 builds since Thursday says they're pushing hard.
