Hey all! Sunday's a slower news day, but there's a busy week coming for a few games, so here's what to have on your radar.

<h2 id="dune-awakening-double-mining-blood-and-ice-ptc">Dune: Awakening's double mining weekend ends Monday, and the Blood and Ice test opens Nov 26</h2>

If you're playing Dune: Awakening, you've got until tomorrow morning to cash in. Funcom's [Communinet Signal #23](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1172710/view/1849827110428710) says the Double Mining Output weekend doubles resource gain from cutteray and vehicle mining until Monday, Nov. 23, at 9:00 UTC, which is 4 AM ET. After that, the focus is the free Blood and Ice expansion, which takes players to the Polar Cap on Dec. 1. Funcom says it's keeping the Public Test Client short to limit spoilers and only hunt critical bugs. The PTC runs Nov. 26 to 30, with servers coming up around 13:00 UTC (8 AM ET). A new in-game event, the Water Crisis, follows "several days after" the expansion, with players fighting over scarce water.

<h2 id="aion-2-world-market-world-exchange-november-25">AION 2 opens cross-server trading with a World Market on Nov 25</h2>

NCSoft [posted on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3393110/view/1849827110434764) that AION 2's World Market and World Exchange go live on Wednesday, Nov. 25, after maintenance. Players will be able to trade with people on other servers in the same faction and region, and each server's own market and exchange stay as they are. There are strings attached, though. Sales through the new World Market and World Exchange carry a 20% tax, each one is capped at 10 sale listings per day, and both are for Membership users only. The notice lands as the team keeps posting near-daily account restriction lists aimed at botting, gold farming and real-money trading, so a tight cap on cross-server listings makes some sense.

<h2 id="company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch-november-25">Company of Heroes 3's Operation Torch campaign lands Nov 25</h2>

Relic's [Operation Torch overview](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1677280/view/1849827110433135) is up ahead of the DLC's launch on Wednesday, Nov. 25. It's a narrative-driven, single-player campaign about the American Army's first major fight against German forces in North Africa. You rebuild and shape your army into a battle-hardened force as you go. [Strategy and Wargaming](https://strategyandwargaming.com/2026/11/22/strategy-and-wargaming-news-22-november-2026-total-war-40k-specialisations-and-space-marines-dawn-of-war-4-specs-company-of-heroes-3-operation-torch-and-much-more/) says it runs 15 missions, from the first landings through Kasserine Pass to Hill 609, told through three soldiers' eyes, with upgrades earned from missions and optional objectives carrying between battles. It's the first new single-player mission content since Company of Heroes 3 launched, and it arrives after the 3.0 update shook up multiplayer balance.

**PPR take:** Funcom keeping the Blood and Ice test to five days is smart. Nobody wants the whole expansion spoiled before it even launches. AION 2's World Market sounds great until you hit the Membership wall. Cross-server trading should be for everyone, and a 20% tax plus a paywall feels like a lot. Still, if it slows down the gold farmers, I'll take it. And Operation Torch is the one I'm curious about. Company of Heroes has always been at its best with a real campaign, and 15 missions with an army you build up over time sounds like a proper return to that.
