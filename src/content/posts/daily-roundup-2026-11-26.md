Hello all, and Happy Thanksgiving! Here are a couple more stories from today that are worth a look between plates.

<h2 id="hitman-the-mutiny-bawma-lenny-kravitz">Lenny Kravitz's Bawma crosses over into HITMAN</h2>

IO Interactive is mixing its two worlds. [The Mutiny](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/26/hitman-bawma-elusive-target/), a new Elusive Target in HITMAN World of Assassination, brings in Bawma, the Pirate King of Aleph from 007 First Light, with Lenny Kravitz lending both his likeness and his voice. It's live now through December 24. For once the client briefs Agent 47 in person, as Bawma walks him across Ambrose Island to take out three rogue lieutenants. IOI's favorite trick this time is a set of Voodoo Dolls from Madame Serafine that can handle all three targets. The mission is free for all World of Assassination players and in the Free Demo. Like every Elusive Target it disappears after December 24, but owners keep an Arcade version called The Retaliator plus its Special Assignment.

<h2 id="no-mans-sky-holiday-expeditions-2026">No Man's Sky brings back four expeditions for the holidays</h2>

Hello Games has started [Holiday Expeditions 2026](https://www.nomanssky.com/2026/11/holiday-expeditions-2026/), re-running four of this year's community expeditions in streamlined form, two weeks each. Aquarius, the fishing expedition, runs now through December 9. Remnant follows from December 9 to 23, then Our Journey Continues, the 10th anniversary expedition with a golden Rasamama S36 and a diplodocus companion, runs December 23 to January 6. Swarm closes it out from January 6 to 20. Update 7.15 came with it, adding AMD FSR upscaling on PC and fixing Corvette tech getting shuffled or lost when warping to a new galaxy. A [7.15.1 hotfix](https://www.nomanssky.com/2026/11/holiday-expeditions-2026-7-15-1/) is already out on Steam for a Steam Deck crash.

**PPR take:** Kravitz playing a pirate king who hires 47 personally is exactly the kind of weird crossover HITMAN does best, and voodoo dolls as the kill method is peak IOI. On the No Man's Sky side, if you missed that golden Rasamama back in September, mark December 23 on the calendar. Hello Games never really takes a holiday.
