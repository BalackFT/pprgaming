**Anyone waiting on that Squadron 42 behind-the-scenes video will have to hang on a little longer.**

In this week's [This Week in Star Citizen](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/transmission/21421-This-Week-In-Star-Citizen), CIG wrote: "Later this week, we plan to release a Behind the Scenes video of the Squadron 42 October Event, giving you a peek behind the curtain at how things went."

On Friday, backers on Spectrum started asking [when "later this week" actually was](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/3/thread/when-is-later-this-week/9206556). Community manager Zyloh-CIG answered on the thread, which also shows up on the [RSI Dev Tracker](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/community/devtracker):

> "Quick note: A few unexpected things came up, so the video won't be going out this week. We're looking forward to sharing it with you soon!"

CIG didn't give a new date or say what came up, so for now it's simply "soon." We'll post it here when it lands.

**PPR take:** Bummer, but I'd rather they get it right. I'm a sucker for the behind-the-scenes stuff, so I'll be watching for it.
