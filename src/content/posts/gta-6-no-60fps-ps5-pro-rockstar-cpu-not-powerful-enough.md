**If you were holding out for a 60fps patch on PS5 Pro, Rockstar just closed that door.** The studio told [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/gta-6-wont-get-a-60-fps-mode-even-on-ps5-pro-because-its-not-powerful-enough-2000755979) that Grand Theft Auto VI won't get a 60fps mode, or even a 40fps one, on the Pro. [IGN picked it up](https://www.ign.com/articles/gta-6-will-never-get-a-60fps-update-on-playstation-pro-as-its-not-powerful-enough) Wednesday evening.

## What Rockstar said

The explanation comes from Marc Chambers, Rockstar's technology production director. He says the Pro's upgraded graphics chip and upscaling go into how the game looks, not how fast it runs:

"While the PS5 Pro's central processor is slightly better than the PS5 base console, it is not powerful enough to run the game's complex simulation at higher frame rates."

In other words, the bottleneck is the CPU. All the traffic, crowds and systems running at once in Leonida eat up the processor, and turning down graphics wouldn't free that up.

## What the Pro gets instead

Chambers says the base game already uses ray-traced reflections and global illumination. On PS5 Pro, that's "enhanced with the addition of ray-traced shadows," plus sharper image quality through Sony's PSSR upscaling. That lines up with what Rockstar said on November 10, when it confirmed GTA VI targets 30 frames per second on every console, as [Game Informer reported](https://gameinformer.com/2026/11/10/grand-theft-auto-vi-will-run-at-30-fps-on-all-consoles-but-ps5-pro-will-feature-improved). [We covered that announcement here](/news/gta-6-30-fps-ps5-pro-pssr-ray-traced-shadows-xbox-cloud-gaming/).

[Engadget points out](https://www.engadget.com/2321134/rockstar-just-settled-a-major-question-about-gta-6s-controversial-fps-decision/) that Digital Foundry's testing found the Pro holds a locked 30 with steady frame pacing, while the base PS5 can dip below 30 in heavy scenes. So the Pro's edge is a cleaner image and a steadier 30.

Rockstar still hasn't announced a PC version, which is where higher frame rates would most likely show up.

**PPR take:** I get why the frame rate crowd is mad. People bought a PS5 Pro hoping for exactly this game at 60, and they just got told "never" on launch day. BUT at least Rockstar gave a real reason instead of a vague "we'll see." If the CPU is the wall, no settings menu fixes that. Think about it, a world this busy has to be simulated every frame, and the Pro's processor isn't much faster than the base PS5's. I'd rather have a locked, smooth 30 than a 60 mode that falls apart in a big chase. If 60 is a must for you, the honest answer is to wait for a PC version that hasn't even been announced yet....and that could be a long wait.
