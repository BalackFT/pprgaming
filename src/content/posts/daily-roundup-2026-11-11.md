Hello all! Two days out from IAE and the news didn't slow down, so here are a few more from today worth your time.

<h2 id="star-citizen-drake-marauder-introduced">RSI officially introduces the Drake Marauder</h2>

The ship that showed up in the IAE promo now has a name tag on it. [RSI posted the reveal](https://x.com/RobertsSpaceInd/status/2120577505903976691) this afternoon: "Drake Interplanetary is storming #IAE2956 with one of the most armed medium fighters on the market. Introducing: Drake Marauder." That's the official line so far: a medium fighter, and a heavily gunned one. Anything you've seen floating around about exact gun sizes, hardpoint counts or the cockpit layout is still community reads off the teaser until CIG puts up a pledge page or a ship brochure, so I'm not repeating numbers here. What we do know is when to go look. Drake Interplanetary has its rental window on **November 17–18** at the Tobin Expo Center in New Babbage, per the [IAE schedule](/news/star-citizen-iae-2956-official-november-13-27-new-babbage-free-fly/), and the whole show runs November 13 through 27 with Free Fly the entire time.

<h2 id="zelda-ocarina-of-time-5-2-million">Zelda: Ocarina of Time sells 5.2 million in four days on Switch 2</h2>

Nintendo [announced today](https://www.nintendo.com/us/whatsnew/the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time-for-nintendo-switch-2-sells-over-5-2-million-units-worldwide-in-first-four-days/) that the full remake of *The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time* sold over 5.2 million units worldwide in the first four days after its November 5 launch, counting physical and digital. That makes it the fastest-selling Nintendo-published Switch 2 game globally, and in the U.S., Canada and Latin America. [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/11/its-official-zelda-ocarina-of-time-has-sold-over-5-million-copies-in-4-days) notes 810,000 of those were in Japan, and that four days of sales already put it past the 5.05 million Nintendo last reported for Donkey Kong Bananza, behind only Mario Kart World on Switch 2. [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time-remake-sold-5-2-million-in-its-first-four-days-nintendo-says/) points out the "first-party" wording matters, since not every third-party publisher shares per-console numbers. The remake is part of Zelda's 40th anniversary, with the live-action movie due April 30, 2027.

<h2 id="ps5-100-million-shipped">Sony says the PS5 has shipped more than 100 million units</h2>

Sony quietly updated its [business data page](https://sonyinteractive.com/en/our-company/business-data-sales/) today: the PlayStation 5 has passed **100 million** units shipped worldwide as of November 10, 2026. That's sell-in to retailers, not consoles in players' hands, but it's still a big line to cross. [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/ps5-has-officially-crossed-100-million-sales-sony-confirms) worked out that Sony moved about 2.1 million units since the end of September. [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/hardware/sony-confirms-the-ps5-is-closing-in-on-the-ps4-with-100-million-consoles-shipped-and-thats-despite-the-shortages-and-price-hikes/) notes it lands just before the console's sixth birthday on November 12, a little slower than the PS4 and PS2 got there, and it makes the PS5 the fourth Sony console past 100 million after the PS1, PS2 and PS4. The PS4 still sits ahead at more than 117 million, and with GTA VI arriving November 19, the gap probably won't last forever.

**PPR take:** The Marauder is the one I'm watching. Drake building "one of the most armed medium fighters" sounds exactly like Drake....lots of guns, and I'd bet the trade-off shows up somewhere else. I want to fly it on Drake days before I believe any spreadsheet. Zelda moving 5.2 million in four days shows a remake done right still sells like a brand-new game, and the PS5 at 100 million right before GTA VI lands is good timing for Sony. Big numbers all around today.
