**Warhammer 40,000: Dark Heresy finally has a date: January 28, 2027.** Owlcat Games [announced it today](https://x.com/OwlcatGames/status/2119822518970089950) alongside a release date trailer, and it's a simultaneous launch on PC (Steam, GOG and the Epic Games Store), PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. A Nintendo Switch 2 version is still coming, but Owlcat says that date will be shared later.

## What you're signing up for

If you played *Rogue Trader*, the shape will feel familiar, but the job is very different. According to [Owlcat's announcement](https://www.gamespress.com/Warhammer-40000-Dark-Heresy-Launches-on-January-28th-2027), you start as a lowly acolyte of the Inquisition in the Calixis Sector and build a warband of "zealots and outcasts" to investigate crimes and hunt heresy, from the slums up to the spires where the elites live. The studio's own post teases the Night Lords as a threat waiting out there.

The companion list is a proper 40K grab bag. Owlcat names abhumans like Cogg, the psyker Saro, the Pariah Discordance, the Aeldari Corsair Laarthyr and the Kroot Ra'akhti, plus human recruits like the Catachan fighter Haymar, the bored Rogue Trader Chorda and Touma. Each one feeds into either the "revamped tactical combat" or the investigations, where companions chip in with advice. Owlcat is also leaning hard on consequences: you can chase the truth at any cost or sacrifice innocents for your own gain, and either way it's on you.

## The dates that matter before launch

The [news post on Owlcat's site](https://darkheresy.owlcat.games/news/en/29) has a few practical dates that are easy to miss:

- **Beta closes January 7, 2027.** It's currently open to founders and goes offline three weeks before launch so the team can prepare the release build.
- **Founder's Packs stay on sale until early January.** Those come with a credits listing and in-game rewards, and the Collector's Edition is sold the same way.
- **Collector's Edition buyers should check their shipping address** on Owlcat's site now. Shipping is planned to start shortly after release.

For context, this has been a fairly quick run by Owlcat standards. The game was revealed during the Warhammer Skulls 2025 showcase in May 2025, went into an open alpha in December 2025 and moved to beta in May 2026, as [Wccftech recapped](https://wccftech.com/warhammer-40000-dark-heresy-release-date-owlcat-games/). It's on [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3710600/) for wishlists now.

**PPR take:** Owlcat has quietly become the best home Games Workshop has for big, choice-heavy RPGs, and an Inquisition game is the natural next step after *Rogue Trader*. Detective work in 40K is a great fit, because nobody in that setting is clean and every lead is half a trap. The thing I'm watching is the combat. "Revamped" is a promise, not a result, and a year-plus of alpha and beta feedback is exactly what should make it land. A late-January date is smart too. It gets out of the way of the holiday rush and *GTA 6*, and it gives cRPG fans something meaty for the dead weeks after the holidays. If you're a founder, play the beta while you can, because it goes dark on January 7.
