**The race for Game of the Year is officially on.** Geoff Keighley revealed the nominees for The Game Awards 2026 in a livestream today across YouTube and Twitch, covering all of the show's categories. Winners get handed out at the live show on **Thursday, December 10 at 7:30 PM ET**, which you can already [catch on Disney+](/news/the-game-awards-2026-disney-plus-december-10/) on top of the usual streams. Full lists are up at [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/game-awards-2026-nominees-list-game-of-the-year/) and [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/gaming/news/game-awards-nominations-2026-list-1236949480/).

## Game of the Year

Six games made the cut:

- 007 First Light (IO Interactive)
- Control Resonant (Remedy/Annapurna)
- Grand Theft Auto VI (Rockstar Games)
- Phantom Blade Zero (S-GAME)
- Resident Evil Requiem (Capcom)
- The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time (Nintendo)

## GTA 6 leads, and it isn't even out yet

The headline is Rockstar. GTA 6 doesn't launch until Thursday, November 19, but it lands just inside the eligibility window, which Variety says closes this Friday. Keighley pushed back on fans who claimed the window was moved to fit it in. By our count of the published lists, GTA 6 picked up **eight nominations**: Game of the Year, Game Direction, Narrative, Art Direction, Score and Music, Audio Design, Performance (Manni L. Perez) and Action/Adventure. Control Resonant is right behind with six. Polygon also flags Big Walk, a critics' favorite up for Best Independent Game and Best Multiplayer, as one to watch after a recent AI-related controversy.

## Games we follow

Pearl Abyss gets a nod: **Crimson Desert** is up for Best Community Support next to Helldivers 2, No Man's Sky, The Witcher 3 and Warframe. That fits a year of steady patches after launch. Star Citizen, Mortal Online 2 and Starfield aren't on any of the lists we've seen.

**PPR take:** Nominating a game nobody outside of reviewers has played yet is going to start some fights, and honestly, I get both sides. GTA 6 is going to be enormous. BUT handing it eight nods two days before launch tells you how much weight the hype carries. I'd love to see Control Resonant or Requiem walk off with the big one, just to keep it interesting. The Crimson Desert pick is the one that makes me smile. That team has kept the patches coming since launch, and it's nice to see that work get noticed. Voting is open now, so go make your picks....then let's argue about it on December 10.
