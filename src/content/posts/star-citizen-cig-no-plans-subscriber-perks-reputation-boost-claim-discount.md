**CIG has answered the subscriber perk drama, and the answer is short.** On Friday night at about 8:19 PM ET, community team member **Big T-O** posted in one of the busiest threads on Star Citizen's General forum: "There are no plans to introduce these subscriber benefits." The [reply is on Spectrum](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/3/thread/sorry-1/9250405), and the same message went into a second thread titled "Now a pay2win ANTI MMO?". Both show up on the [RSI Devtracker](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/community/devtracker).

## What set it off

The thread, titled "Sorry ????", went up Thursday evening. A backer pasted a list from a third-party source saying subscribers "will receive some in game perks (this could be active already)":

- **Reputation boost:** +10% for Centurion, +15% for Imperator, plus a +5% "group" boost for both tiers
- **Vehicle claim cost:** 10% cheaper for Centurion, 15% for Imperator
- **Vehicle claim time:** 10% faster for Centurion, 15% for Imperator

The French Star Citizen news site [Cavalera](https://cavalera.news/articles/2026-11-20/alpha-5-0-ptu-wave-3-primes-d-abonnement-ttk-chiffre-et-telemetrie-dat) reported on Friday that dataminers found these values in the files of the current **Alpha 5.0 PTU Wave 3** build, and labeled it unofficial. Centurion and Imperator are RSI's two monthly [subscription tiers](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/pledge/subscriptions). The thread blew past 390 replies, and "pay to win" was all over X by Friday afternoon.

## What CIG said, and what it didn't

Here's Big T-O's full reply: "Hi everyone, jumping in here to address some of the speculation. There are no plans to introduce these subscriber benefits. I also think it's worth noting that information from third-party sources should be taken with a pinch of salt, especially when it comes to confirming CIG plans and decisions."

That's a community team post on Spectrum, not a Comm-Link or a design doc. It says CIG doesn't plan to ship these benefits. It **doesn't** say whether the values are really in the build or explain why they'd be there, and it doesn't rule out other subscriber perks down the road. [RubenSaurus asked exactly that on X](https://x.com/TheRubenSaurus/status/2124057147927408853): "If it's not planned, then why is it in the PTU?" CIG hasn't answered that part.

For the record, the official patch notes for the last two Wave 3 builds, including [13248297](/news/star-citizen-alpha-5-0-ptu-13248297-lightning-rework-nyx-settlements-rain-audio-voip/), don't mention subscribers, Centurion or Imperator anywhere.

**PPR take:** I've been an Imperator subscriber pretty much since I backed in 2013, so I'll say it again....I do NOT want a reputation boost tied to a subscription. Ever. Reputation is progression, and progression should be earned in the 'verse, not on the pledge store. Claim cost and claim time? I could live with that AFTER 1.0, when the game is actually out and subs help keep the servers running. BUT not during the alpha. Now, "no plans" is a good answer and I'll take CIG at its word. Code sitting in a build doesn't mean it's shipping, and anyone who's watched this project for a while knows how much gets prototyped and shelved. Still, a little more on why it was there would go a long way, and it would shut down the doom videos fast.
