**Start building your crew now.** The **Daymar Rally**, the community's long-running endurance race across Daymar's dunes, returns on **Saturday, January 23, 2027**, and registration is already open. RSI flagged it in last night's [This Week in Star Citizen](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/transmission/21421-This-Week-In-Star-Citizen), and [The Impound's rundown](https://theimpound.com/blogs/star-citizen-news/this-week-in-star-citizen-october-26-2026) says the course promises more than 510 kilometres of terrain across three divisions.

**The details so far.** Organizer [ATMO Esports](https://atmo.gg/events/daymar-rally) lists the race as team racing for **2–4 person crews**, starting from the Shubin Mining Facility on Daymar in Stanton. Before race day there are two open practice sessions: **December 12, 2026** and **January 9, 2027**. Last season's winners give you an idea of the scale. FAR OUT took the Bike Division in 44:37.9, while XGR won Trucks in 4:39:23.3 and 1ER CORPS COLONIAL won Buggies in 5:13:08.4. The current rulebook on the event page covers entry requirements and eligible vehicles, so read it before you pick a ride.

**Also this week: a Squadron 42 film.** CIG plans to release a behind-the-scenes video from the October [Squadron 42 Manchester event](/news/squadron-42-manchester-hands-on-event/) later this week. No exact day has been given. It's the first official look back at the play sessions where invited creators got real hands-on time with the campaign.

**The rest of the week**

- **Friday, October 30:** the RSI Weekly Newsletter, usually with a Sneak Peek, and the October issue of **Jump Point** for subscribers.
- **PTU:** Alpha 4.10.3 is still in [Wave 1](/news/star-citizen-alpha-4-10-3-ptu-wave-1-vanduul-incursion-skarnov-rifle/) with the Nyx Vanduul Incursion missions and the Skarnov rifle. RSI says more waves are coming "soon" but hasn't given dates.
- **Day of the Vara** is still running, including Arena Commander's Kill Collector FPS mode and its free Vanduul mask, plus the pumpkin-decorating contest.
- **Community MVP:** HEBRAiST's cinematic trip aboard the Constellation Mk V Centaurus and its Skylark, [Is This the Most Star Citizen Ship Ever Made?](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/community-hub/post/is-this-the-most-star-citizen-ship-ever-made-9yWYLDumKGZLo)

**PPR take:** January sounds far off, but a 500-plus-kilometre race across Daymar punishes crews that don't practise. Lock in your drivers and navigators now and use the December session as a shakedown. And if CIG drops that Squadron 42 film this week, we'll have it here.
