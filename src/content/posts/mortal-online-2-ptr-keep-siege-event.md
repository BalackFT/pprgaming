Star Vault wants a full siege on the test branch before Era of Refinement Patch 1 goes live.

In an [October 15 announcement](https://www.mortalonline2.com/eor-patch-1-ptr-event-keep-siege), the studio invited players to a **hosted Keep Siege on the Mortal Online 2 PTR** on **Saturday, October 17 at 9:00 PM CEST** (3:00 PM ET / 12:00 PM PT).

**How the event works:**

- Fight at a designated keep on **Sarducaa** (map posted on the official page)
- Once you're on the PTR, ask for a **teleport to the event** in Nave chat
- Attendees split into **Attackers** and **Defenders** — one side storms the keep, the other holds it
- **Banks**, banker NPCs from every Sarducaa town, crafting buildings, and free siege materials are provided on site
- **Everyone is welcome**; Star Vault says everything required for the siege will be supplied

**Why it matters for the patch:** The [Era of Refinement Patch 1 PTR](https://www.mortalonline2.com/news/) opened **October 14** with the combat and balance pass we covered earlier — hold-to-parry blocking, the Endurance stamina buff, Mental Fortitude, and more. Star Vault is using this siege as a large-scale stress test of those changes and asks players to send feedback through the **in-game reporting tool**.

**How to get on the PTR (per Star Vault):** In Steam, right-click Mortal Online 2 → Properties → Game Versions & Betas → select **Mortal Online Public Test Realm**. Characters reflect the PTR snapshot taken **October 14**.

**PPR take:** A scheduled keep fight is the right way to prove the parry and stamina rework isn't just patch-notes theory. If you're already on the PTR, this is the weekend to show up with a side and a bug report.
