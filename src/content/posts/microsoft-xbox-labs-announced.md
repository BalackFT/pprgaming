Xbox has a new skunkworks. In a memo to employees on Tuesday, Xbox CEO **Asha Sharma** announced **[Xbox Labs](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/microsoft-forms-xbox-labs-to-explore-game-remixes-ports-and-other-new-frontiers/)**, which she described as "a small group of game makers, engineers and scientists" turning emerging ideas into things players and developers can actually use.

**The key points, per GeekWire:**

- It's led by **Scott Van Vliet**, who has been Xbox's CTO since May.
- The team will build in the open, and Sharma says success will be measured "by whether more people are playing and creating."
- She pointed to games being decompiled and rebuilt for hardware they were never made for, and PC games running on Apple Silicon or in a web browser. She called these good outcomes for players, "even when (and sometimes because) they challenge our existing business models."

It comes during a tough stretch for Xbox. Last month it cut 268 roles in Xbox Game Studios as part of a restructuring announced in July, which calls for about 3,200 job cuts this fiscal year. Sharma says Labs won't change Xbox's existing priorities.

**PPR take:** It's strange to hear a platform holder praise fan ports and decompilation projects. Whether that turns into real products or just a nice memo is still unknown, but it's worth watching.
