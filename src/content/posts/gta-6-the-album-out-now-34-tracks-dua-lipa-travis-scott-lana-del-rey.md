**The game came out Thursday. The record came out Friday.** Rockstar posted "GRAND THEFT AUTO VI: THE ALBUM NOW AVAILABLE" [on X](https://x.com/RockstarGames/status/2123778388296888354) at 9:00 AM ET, and *Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album* is now on all the major streaming services, with vinyl and CD on the [official album store](https://www.gtavi-thealbum.com/) and at select retailers.

## What's on it

It's a big one. The album has **34 tracks** and runs about **1 hour 37 minutes** on [Spotify](https://open.spotify.com/album/5BYKG4MHfySZthe2W6r7n4), where it's credited to Atlantic Recording Corporation and Take-Two Interactive. [Complex](https://www.complex.com/music/a/shawn-setaro/grand-theft-auto-vi-the-album-released) says it arrived through Atlantic Records and Rockstar Games.

The lineup is all over the map on purpose. It opens with Justice's "Starting From Zero" featuring Michael McDonald, then runs through Yung Lean with Future and Metro Boomin, Playboi Carti, Travis Scott's "RHYNO," Dua Lipa's "Go Easy," Morgan Wallen, Burna Boy, Gunna, Cardi B, JENNIE, Tove Lo, Zara Larsson, Camila Cabello and Lana Del Rey's "Crazy For You." There's a Latin run with JHAYCO, Fuerza Regida and Rauw Alejandro, plus Kali Uchis and Helado Negro, and Omar Apollo. Track 32 is "Bright Lights, Big City" by **Keith Richards**, and it closes on "Overtown Lights" from Stanley Clarke, Yussef Dayes and Robert Glasper.

Justice shows up twice. The second is "Flower In A Hurricane" with Tame Impala, SZA and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard, the song that debuted in the launch trailer. [Consequence](https://consequence.net/2026/11/gta-6-soundtrack-album-stream/) notes a batch of these songs were already out as singles before the game launched, so some of it will sound familiar.

## Vinyl and CD

The [official store](https://www.gtavi-thealbum.com/) sells the album on CD and on vinyl, including retailer-exclusive colors. An Amazon-exclusive pink vinyl with purple splatter is listed at $49.98 and was showing "limited quantity remaining" when we checked.

Don't mix this up with the game's **original score**. That's a separate Justice album, and it's due December 11. We covered it [here](/news/gta-6-original-score-justice-december-11-pusher-gesaffelstein/).

## PPR take

Honestly, this is the most Rockstar thing ever - the game is out and they've already made their money, so now here's a 34-track album with an Atlantic label deal and five flavors of vinyl. Smart marketing! BUT I'm not gonna pretend the lineup isn't wild. Dua Lipa, Travis Scott, Lana Del Rey and Keith Richards on the same tracklist? Morgan Wallen next to Arca? Somebody at Rockstar had fun with this. The radio has always been half the personality of a GTA game, and a soundtrack this wide tells you Vice City is going to sound like Vice City. Spin it on a stream before you drop fifty bucks on the splatter vinyl though....that's the free part.
