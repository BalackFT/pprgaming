**The Witcher 4 has a marketing partner, and it's PlayStation.** CD PROJEKT RED posted its [third-quarter 2026 results](https://www.cdprojekt.com/en/media/news/cd-projekt-red-wraps-up-the-third-quarter-of-2026/) today, and the biggest news wasn't in the numbers. Per the company's report, quoted by [Wccftech](https://wccftech.com/cd-projekt-red-sony-deal-the-witcher-4-marketing-playstation-plus/), on November 24 it "signed new partnership agreements with Sony Interactive Entertainment LLC" covering the availability of its games on PlayStation platforms and through **PlayStation Plus**, plus collaboration on the **marketing campaign for The Witcher 4**.

CFO Piotr Nielubowicz brought it up on the earnings call, per the [Investing.com transcript](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-cd-projekt-red-posts-solid-q3-2026-profit-stock-rises-93CH-5048788), saying the cooperation "will support our business and financial outlook in this and future years." CDPR didn't share the terms, which games are headed to PlayStation Plus, or what the marketing work will actually look like.

## Still 2028, still multiplatform

Nothing here changes the release plan. CDPR's results again list **The Witcher 4 for 2028**, after The Witcher 3: Songs of the Past in 2027. As [Wccftech](https://wccftech.com/cd-projekt-red-sony-deal-the-witcher-4-marketing-playstation-plus/) notes, the game is still planned for PC, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. This is a marketing deal, not an exclusivity deal.

[VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/cdpr-has-entered-a-marketing-agreement-with-sony-for-the-witcher-4/) points out the obvious comparison: Sony also has the marketing deal for GTA 6, which is why that launch has been all over the PS Store and PlayStation's own channels. It's also worth remembering CDPR has worked with Xbox before on Cyberpunk 2077 marketing.

## The quarter itself

The numbers were solid. CDPR reported **208 million PLN in revenue** and **109 million PLN in net profit** for Q3, and 643 million PLN in revenue for the first nine months. Cyberpunk 2077 and Phantom Liberty are still the main earners, and The Witcher 3's Remastered edition launched September 29. The board also adopted a conditional advance dividend of 1.20 PLN per share.

**PPR take:** If you play on Xbox or PC, don't panic. Nothing CDPR said today takes The Witcher 4 off any platform. What it probably means is that the big Witcher 4 showings over the next couple of years lean toward PlayStation events, and the PS5 version gets the spotlight in trailers, the same way GTA 6 did. That's how these deals tend to work. The PlayStation Plus part is the piece I'm more curious about. CDPR's back catalog is strong, and with Songs of the Past coming in 2027, putting The Witcher 3 or Cyberpunk in front of more PS Plus players would make sense. But that's my guess, not something CDPR announced. Until we hear specifics, the only hard facts are a Sony deal, a 2028 target, and the same three platforms.
