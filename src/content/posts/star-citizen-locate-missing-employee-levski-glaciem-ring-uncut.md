Sometimes the best way to show what Star Citizen is like is to just hit record and play. Today we took a **"Locate Missing Employee"** investigation contract out of **[Levski](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/transmission/20947-Alpha-46-Lifeline-For-Levski)** and filmed the whole thing, [posted in full on @PPRGamers](https://x.com/PPRGamers/status/2111506715573154075). It runs **1 hour and 16 minutes**, from leaving Levski to the end of the job, with **no cuts, no edits and no commentary**.

**The contract:**

- **Issued by:** Hockrow Agency, Case File **#MPS1132**, lead investigator **Arkan Mallor**
- **The job:** Travel to a **QV Breaker Station in the Glaciem Ring** in the Nyx system and find **Cygne Danko** at the station's **Central Core**. Confirm their status, and if they're dead, examine the body with your **mobiGlas**.
- **Reward:** **32,750 aUEC**
- **Warning:** The contract is flagged as **likely to involve combat**

**The run:** The video follows the contract from start to finish, beginning at [Levski](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/spectrum-dispatch/14953-Observist-Dark-Levski-Nyx), the People's Alliance hub carved into Delamar, and heading out into the Glaciem Ring. It took over an hour because we stopped to loot along the way, and that's left in too. Nothing is trimmed, so you see the travel, the search and every stop in real time.
