**Start clearing space.** **Grand Theft Auto VI** will be a **210GB download on PS5**, according to the PlayStation Store tracking account **PlayStation Game Size**, [Engadget reports](https://www.engadget.com/2298872/gta-6-download-size-reportedly-210-gb/). Insider Gaming corroborated the figure, per Engadget. Rockstar hasn't confirmed the size itself, and [GamesRadar+ notes](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gta-6-is-a-210gb-download-says-playstation-store-sleuth/) that download size and final install size can differ.

**How big is that?** It's 61GB more than the 149GB Red Dead Redemption 2 needed on PS4 in 2018, and more than half of the roughly 362GB of usable storage on a standard 512GB **Xbox Series S**, according to Engadget. The Xbox download size hasn't been listed yet.

**Pre-load opens November 12.** That part is official. Take-Two's [pre-order announcement](https://ir.take2games.com/node/32311/pdf) says digital pre-orders can start pre-loading on **November 12**, a week before launch on **November 19** for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Physical copies come with a **download code** in the box and go on sale November 12 so those players can pre-load too. The standard edition is **$79.99** and the Ultimate Edition is $99.99. PlayStation Game Size also lists the release time as **midnight**.

**PPR take:** 210GB is a lot of drive for one game, especially on a Series S. If you've pre-ordered, plan to start that download on the 12th instead of launch night.
