**New Eden just got a lot bigger, on the ground and in space.** Fenris Creations [launched EVE Online: Fractured Skies](https://fenris.com/news/2026/fenris-creations-launches-eve-online-fractured-skies-and-opens-eve-vanguards-live-alpha) today, its winter expansion, and on the same day opened a persistent, around-the-clock paid alpha for EVE Vanguard, the extraction-adventure shooter set in the same universe. Fractured Skies is out now on PC and Mac through eveonline.com, Steam and the Epic Games Store, and [MMODude notes](https://www.mmodude.com/news/november-10-2026-roundup/) it went live at 11:00 UTC.

The heart of the expansion is Factional Warfare. There are four new Military Campaigns, one for each empire, running into January, and any pilot can join in through combat, mining or exploration, whether or not they've enlisted. Contributions pay out ISK, Loyalty Points and faction gear, and finished objectives unlock system-wide bonuses that switch on January 5. The first empire to complete its objectives picks where a mid-January four-way battle happens, and only that empire's bonuses apply there. In February 2027, seasonal "Conquests" start, with definite winners and lowsec sieges that can destroy the empires' Conquest Engines.

There's new hardware too. The Naga Navy Issue, Oracle Navy Issue, Talos Navy Issue and Tornado Fleet Issue battlecruisers are sold through Factional Warfare Loyalty Point stores, alongside nine sets of new synchronizer rigs that get stronger with each matching faction module. A redesigned HUD arrives as an opt-in Feature Preview. New players get 13 free, pre-fitted AIR Academy ships for combat, exploration and mining, though they can only be flown in Exordium, the PvP-free starter region.

EVE Vanguard is the other half. Instead of waiting for timed playtests, players can now drop in whenever they want. Access is separate from EVE Online's Omega subscription and starts at $15, which Fenris says covers the alpha, full launch and beyond. Completed Vanguard contracts spawn extra combat, mining and exploration sites in EVE Online's highsec and lowsec. The alpha brings three new maps, one per security level, with High Castle as PvE, Vorago as PvPvE and The Vault as full PvP. Controller support is planned for December, Factional Warfare enlistment for Vanguard players in Q2 2027, and a Steam Early Access launch in Q3 2027.

**PPR take:** This is the thing CCP promised back in the DUST 514 days, and Fenris says as much in its own announcement. Shooters on the ground nudging what happens in space is exactly the kind of connected sandbox a lot of us want from Star Citizen too, so it's fun to watch a different studio take a run at it. The link is still modest. Vanguard adds sites to EVE rather than flipping systems, and real Factional Warfare enlistment is a 2027 item. The free Academy ships are the smartest part for newcomers, since learning to fit a ship has scared off plenty of people. I'm curious whether $15 for permanent access turns into a healthy alpha population or a quiet one. Either way, it's a big day for space game fans.
