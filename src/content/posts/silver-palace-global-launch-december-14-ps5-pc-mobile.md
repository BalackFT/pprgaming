**Silver Palace finally has a date.** Publisher Elementa [announced](https://www.gamespress.com/Silver-Palace-Sets-Global-Launch-Date-for-December-14-2026) that its free-to-play open-world detective action RPG launches worldwide on **December 14, 2026** (UTC+8). Elementa hasn't given an exact launch time yet.

It's coming to PlayStation 5, PC, iOS and Android at the same time. [Gematsu notes](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/10/silver-palace-launches-december-14) the PC version will be available both through its own client and the Epic Games Store, and the game is developed by Silver Studio.

## What it is

Per Elementa's press release, Silver Palace is built in Unreal Engine 5 and set in a Victorian-inspired city powered by a mysterious substance called Silverium. You play a detective, investigating cases with a cast of companions, and combat is real-time with character switching and skill synergies.

## Pre-registration

Elementa says pre-registrations have passed **15 million** worldwide. Pre-registration is open now on the [official site](https://silverpalace.elementagames.com/en-us/), the App Store and Google Play, and the game is listed on the PlayStation Store and Epic Games Store. A milestone campaign is running alongside it, with rewards including a new 4-star character and 20 Simulation Shots unlocked as pre-registration and social follower targets are hit.

At launch it will support 12 languages: Simplified and Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, Korean, Indonesian, Thai, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Russian.

**PPR take:** December 14 lands less than a month after GTA VI's November 19 launch, which is a bold spot for any game, free-to-play or not. Fifteen million pre-registrations says plenty of people are curious, though.
