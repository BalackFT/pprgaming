**Agent 47 has a new client, and he's a rock star.** IO Interactive launched [The Mutiny](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/26/hitman-bawma-elusive-target/), a new Elusive Target for HITMAN World of Assassination, on Thursday. It stars Lenny Kravitz as Bawma, the self-styled Pirate King of Aleph who first showed up in IOI's 007 First Light. Kravitz provides both his likeness and his voice, and it's the first time the character has crossed over into the World of Assassination.

## When and where you can play it

The Mutiny is live now and runs **through Thursday, December 24, 2026**. IOI's post on Xbox Wire lists it on Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One, the ROG Xbox Ally handhelds and PC. Like every Elusive Target, it's a limited-time contract that disappears when the window closes.

The good news for anyone on the fence: it's **free**. IOI says the mission is open to every HITMAN World of Assassination owner and to new players through the game's Free Demo. Once it expires, owners of the full game or the Ambrose Island location keep The Retaliator, the Arcade version of the mission, plus its Special Assignment, at no extra cost.

## What's different about this one

IOI pitches The Mutiny as one of the most laid-back contracts in the series, with a bright, holiday tone and Bawma talking in 47's ear for the whole job, which is unusual since the Agency normally does the briefing. The standout tool is a set of **Voodoo Dolls**. You gather ingredients for a ritual, collect the doll that gets made, and then "kill" the doll however you like, with a knife, water, fire, electricity, a gun or explosives. IOI says all three of the mission's targets can be taken out that way.

Finish it with an IOI Account while it's live and you unlock the **Rock Royalty Duck**, a sunglasses-wearing rubber duck that explodes when it senses movement nearby. Alongside the mission, IOI released the **Rockstar Pack** for $5.99/€5.99/£4.99, with rock-themed suits, a guitar-pick throwable, drumstick and guitar items, a pyrotechnic device, a Baller R0-AR Wildcat and new Freelancer Safehouse cosmetics.

**PPR take:** This is a smart crossover. IOI gets to show off 007 First Light's cast inside the game that made the studio's name, and HITMAN players get a free, month-long contract to play over the holidays. Making it free through the demo matters too. Elusive Targets are one of the best hooks in the series, and letting brand-new players try one without paying is an easy way to pull people in. The Voodoo Doll trick sounds like exactly the kind of goofy, creative kill HITMAN does best. The duck is the real prize though....an exploding rubber duck in shades is about as HITMAN as it gets. If you've got a free evening between now and Christmas Eve, it costs you nothing to give it a run.
