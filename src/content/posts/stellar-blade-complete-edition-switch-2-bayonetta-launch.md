**EVE is on Switch 2.** [SHIFT UP](https://www.gamespress.com/en-US/STELLAR-BLADE-COMPLETE-EDITION-NOW-AVAILABLE-GLOBALLY-ON-NINTENDO-SWIT) launched *Stellar Blade Complete Edition* globally on Nintendo Switch 2 today, November 5, 2026. It's the action RPG's first appearance on a Nintendo console. The [Nintendo eShop](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/stellar-blade-complete-edition-switch-2/) has it for $59.99, marked in stock, with TV, tabletop and handheld play and a Mature rating.

## What's in the Complete Edition

According to SHIFT UP's launch release, the Complete Edition bundles the base game with the previously released **NieR:Automata** and **GODDESS OF VICTORY: NIKKE** DLC, plus the new **Bayonetta** collaboration. The NieR pack brings Emil's Shop into the world, with 2B and A2 outfits for EVE, a 9S outfit for Lily, Emil headwear for Adam, and a Pod 042 drone skin. The NIKKE pack adds a fight against Scarlet for a costume, hairstyle and song for EVE, plus shooting-range mini-games, a shop and fishing for NIKKE collectibles. SHIFT UP's earlier [date confirmation](https://www.gamespress.com/STELLAR-BLADE-COMPLETE-EDITION-CONFIRMS-NOVEMBER-5-RELEASE-DATE-ON-NIN) also lists Complete Edition bonuses such as the Stargazer outfit set, extra cosmetics, 2,000 SP EXP and 5,000 Gold.

## Bayonetta outfits are free across platforms

The Bayonetta collaboration adds four couture outfits for EVE: Bayonetta, Jeanne, Nun and Black Tresses. SHIFT UP says that update is free on Switch 2, PlayStation 5 and PC as of today's launch window. A [public free demo](https://www.gamespress.com/en-US/STELLAR-BLADE-COMPLETE-EDITION-NOW-AVAILABLE-GLOBALLY-ON-NINTENDO-SWIT) of the Complete Edition is also live on the eShop, covering the opening section so you can try the combat before you buy. The launch notes also call out Joy-Con 2 motion support for non-combat activities.

## Full game on the cartridge

Physical buyers get the whole game on the cart. SHIFT UP and [GoNintendo](https://www.gonintendo.com/contents/66927-stellar-blade-complete-edition-cuts-a-path-to-switch-2-today) both stress there is no game-key card and no required download just to start playing. [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/09/stellar-blade-launches-on-switch-2-this-november-with-a-proper-physical-release) previously reported the cart is a 32 GB card, one of the smaller Switch 2 sizes, and that a Limited Edition SteelBook was offered through participating retailers and Panax.

**PPR take:** I'm glad this one showed up on Switch 2 as a real cartridge instead of another key-card download. Stellar Blade already proved itself on PS5 and PC as a sharp, stylish action game, and handing Nintendo players the Complete Edition with NieR, NIKKE and a free Bayonetta drop on day one is the right way to do a late-platform launch. The free demo helps too. If you've been waiting for a portable version, or you just want Bayonetta's fashion sense on EVE while you're on the couch, today's the day. At $59.99 with the DLC already in the box, it's an easy recommendation for anyone who likes tight combat and big boss fights.
