**Phantom Blade Zero's launch just got a lot bigger.** S-Game says its wuxia action RPG has sold **more than 4 million copies in its first 72 hours**, as [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/11/phantom-blade-zero-sales-top-four-million-in-72-hours) reports. The studio celebrated on X with a short thank-you to players. The game launched digitally on PS5 and PC (Steam and Epic Games Store) on October 29, and that 4 million figure covers both platforms. It isn't on Xbox.

## The numbers so far

When we covered launch day, Phantom Blade Zero had just passed [600,000 concurrent players on Steam](/news/phantom-blade-zero-launch-600000-steam-players-day-one-patch-nvidia-driver/). It kept climbing over the weekend. According to [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/phantom-blade-zero-put-up-some-massive-sales-numbers/), it hit a record **833,921 peak concurrent players on Steam on November 1**, and that's before you count anyone playing on PS5, since Sony doesn't share player counts. GameSpot also notes most pre-orders came from China, Taiwan and Hong Kong, which makes sense for a Beijing studio's game with this much buzz at home.

[Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/phantom-blade-zero-4-million-copies-opening-weekend/) calls it one of 2026's best-selling games already, and says the concurrent peak puts it behind only Palworld among this year's single-player games on Steam. For scale, Polygon points out that Black Myth: Wukong, the obvious comparison, sold 10 million in its opening weekend, so this isn't a Wukong-sized debut. It's still a huge one.

The reviews helped. Our [reviews roundup](/news/phantom-blade-zero-reviews-88-metacritic-91-opencritic/) had it at 88 on Metacritic and 91 on OpenCritic, and the combat was the big talking point. [RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21678-phantom-blade-zero-ps5-pc-sales-numbers-new-4-million) lists the game at $59.99 for the Standard Edition and $69.99 for the Deluxe Edition, and a PS5 physical release is still planned for 2027.

**PPR take:** I love seeing a studio's first big console and PC game land like this. As [Insider Gaming](https://insider-gaming.com/phantom-blade-zero-sells-4-million-copies-in-72-hours/) points out, this is S-Game's first major release outside mobile, and that's a big jump to make on your first try. Four million in three days answers that. The comparison to Wukong is going to come up all week, but I don't think it's the right yardstick. Wukong was a once-in-a-generation launch, and this is a new IP with no Journey to the West name behind it. The real story is that action fans clearly wanted a great-looking game built around flashy martial arts combat, and they showed up for it on day one. If S-Game keeps the patches coming and the PS5 physical edition lands in 2027, this could keep selling for a long time.
