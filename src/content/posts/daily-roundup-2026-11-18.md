<h2 id="star-citizen-5-0-ptu-wave-3">Star Citizen 5.0 PTU opens up to Wave 3</h2>

Alpha 5.0 just got a lot more crowded. Wakapedia-CIG posted the [Wave 3 patch notes for PTU build 13228337](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/190048/thread/star-citizen-alpha-5-0-ptu-patch-notes-2) on Spectrum around 1:21 PM ET, one day after the last Wave 1 build. It's still a US-only PTU channel with long term persistence on, and the focus list stays the same: Genesis, Nyx I, Starchitect locations, mission boards, Intraplanetary Server Meshing, the TTK vehicle balance and the new missile lock interface. The fun bits this time: contracts now carry an icon and color showing whether a job is local, planetary, system-wide or universal, hauling markers point you to the nearest freight elevator kiosk, and ships finally make takeoff and landing sounds you can hear from outside. CIG also lists four server crash fixes. Still no Live date beyond the Q4 commitment.

<h2 id="sony-wins-playstation-store-case">Sony beats the £2 billion PlayStation Store claim</h2>

The UK class action over PlayStation Store prices is done, at least for now. London's Competition Appeal Tribunal [dismissed the claim "in its entirety"](https://www.catribunal.org.uk/sites/cat/files/2026-11/15277722%20Alex%20Neill%20Class%20Representative%20Limited%20v%20Sony%20Interactive%20Entertainment%20Europe%20Limited;%20Sony%20Interactive%20Entertainment%20Network%20Europe%20Limited;%20and%20Sony%20Interac.pdf) on November 17. Brought by campaigner Alex Neill for about 12.2 million UK players, it argued Sony's locked-down store pushed digital prices around 20% higher, with damages and interest coming to roughly £1.97 billion. The tribunal didn't buy the market definition: players could buy physical games or pick another console, and competition between systems keeps prices in check, as [CNA's Reuters report](https://www.channelnewsasia.com/business/sony-defeats-ps2-billion-uk-claim-over-playstation-store-pricing-6559261) explains. Neill said she was "disappointed" in a statement to [Consumer Voice](https://consumervoice.uk/gaming/sony-playstation-claim-dismissed/). A hearing on costs and any appeal requests is set for the week of December 14.

<h2 id="spyro-a-realm-beyond-the-game-awards">Spyro: A Realm Beyond is coming to The Game Awards</h2>

Our purple buddy is getting some stage time. Activision and Toys for Bob say they'll share more on Spyro: A Realm Beyond at The Game Awards on December 10, according to [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/11/spyro-a-realm-beyond-teased-for-the-game-awards-2026), and they sent out new screenshots and concept art of the Painted Plains area to get things going. Activision calls it "the first all-new Spyro adventure in nearly 20 years" and ended the email with "Sheep beware." [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/spyros-new-ps5-game-teases-appearance-at-this-years-game-awards) notes this would be the game's first public showing since its June reveal, and the official Spyro account has started teasing it too. Nobody has said what we'll actually see, whether that's a gameplay trailer or a date. It's still due in spring 2027 on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Switch 2 and PC.

**PPR take:** Wave 3 is the one I'm watching. Opening the doors to more testers usually means CIG thinks the build can handle the load....or they want to find out the hard way..lol. Either way, contracts that tell you up front how far away they'll send you is a small change that'll save a ton of "wait, it's WHERE?" moments, and hearing a ship lift off a pad next to you is going to make New Babbage feel a lot more alive. The Sony ruling is a big win for closed console stores, BUT there's still that December hearing and a possible appeal, so it's not quite over yet. And Spyro at TGA? Sheep beware is right!
