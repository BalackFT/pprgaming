Three weeks after digital launch, **[CONTROL Resonant](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3669870/CONTROL_Resonant/)** has cleared a million copies.

In an [October 15 company press release](https://www.placera.se/pressmeddelanden/remedy-entertainment-remedy-entertainment-plc-control-resonant-over-a-million-copies-sold-20261015) (also listed on Remedy’s [media page](https://www.remedygames.com/media-and-influencers)), Remedy Entertainment said worldwide sales **exceeded 1 million copies**, making Resonant **the fastest-selling game in the studio’s 31-year history** — ahead of Alan Wake 2’s roughly two-month climb to the same mark.

**Key facts from Remedy:**

- Digital launch was **September 24, 2026** on **PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC** (Steam and Epic Games Store), with GeForce NOW support
- It is Remedy’s **first self-published release of this scale**
- **Physical editions** for PS5 and Xbox Series X arrived in stores around the same announcement window
- A **Mac** version via Steam and the App Store is planned for later in 2026

CEO **Jean-Charles Gaudechon** called the milestone “a very strong start” in a packed release window, and said the game is pulling in both long-time Control fans and new players — important as Remedy pushes for profitable growth across the Control and Alan Wake franchises.

**PPR take:** A million in three weeks will not rewrite industry charts, but for a self-published Remedy sequel it is the proof point the studio needed. Watching the November 3 Q3 business review for whether that velocity turns into actual profit is the next checkpoint.
