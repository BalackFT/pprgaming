**The first real Switch 2 hardware deal of the holidays started this morning.** Nintendo's [Black Friday offers](https://www.nintendo.com/us/whatsnew/nintendo-announces-black-friday-offers-to-help-spread-joy-this-holiday-season/), announced on Nov. 12, kicked in today, Nov. 22, and the headline is the **Nintendo Switch 2 + Mario Kart World Bundle at $499.99**, which Nintendo calls a $50 savings off its suggested price. The bundle price runs **through Dec. 5**, while supplies last.

## What you get for $499.99

Per [Nintendo's bundle listing](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/nintendo-switch-2-mario-kart-world-bundle-128067/), the box has the Switch 2 console and dock, a pair of Joy-Con 2 controllers with the grip and straps, the AC adapter and HDMI cable, a **digital download of Mario Kart World**, and a code for **three months of Nintendo Switch Online** (Individual). That membership auto-renews at the regular price after the first term unless you cancel, so set a reminder if you don't want it.

Nintendo says the deal is at participating retailers, its Nintendo New York and Nintendo San Francisco stores, and on the [Nintendo Store website](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/showroom/black-friday-cyber-monday/). The Verge [lists](https://www.theverge.com/gadgets/1038181/nintendo-switch-2-black-friday-deal-mario-kart-world-bundle-2026-sale) Amazon, Best Buy and Walmart among the stores carrying it.

There's a second bundle in the announcement too: **Switch 2 + Nintendo Switch Sports Resort for $529.99**, which Nintendo says saves $27.98.

## The game deals

From **Nov. 22 through Nov. 29**, select physical games are **$20 off** at retailers including Amazon, Best Buy, GameStop, Target and Walmart. Nintendo's list:

- Super Mario Party Jamboree, Nintendo Switch 2 Edition + Jamboree TV
- Metroid Prime 4: Beyond (Switch 2 Edition), Donkey Kong Bananza and Kirby Air Riders
- Super Mario Galaxy + Super Mario Galaxy 2
- The original Switch versions of Super Mario Party Jamboree and Metroid Prime 4: Beyond

Separately, a digital sale on the eShop and Nintendo Store has been running since Nov. 19 and ends **Dec. 2 at 11:59 p.m. PT**.

## Context: this mostly undoes September's price bump

The Verge points out that the $50 off [temporarily rolls back](https://www.theverge.com/gadgets/1038181/nintendo-switch-2-black-friday-deal-mario-kart-world-bundle-2026-sale) the Switch 2 price increase that took effect in early September. The Mario Kart World bundle had been $549.99. So this is a real discount from where the bundle sits today, but it lands right back at a price that used to be normal.

**PPR take:** If you've been holding out on a Switch 2 for a kid, a spouse or yourself, this is the one to grab, and honestly the math is simple: $499.99 is what Nintendo now charges for the console by itself, so Mario Kart World and three months of online basically come free. I wouldn't call it a huge sale, though. It's Nintendo handing back the September increase for two weeks and calling it Black Friday, which, fine, that's how this works now. The thing I'd actually watch is the "while supplies last" line. Every retailer's stock is different, and Nintendo hasn't said how many bundles are out there, so if you know you want one, don't wait for Cyber Monday to think it over. The $20 game deals are nice but not wild. Metroid Prime 4 and Donkey Kong Bananza at $20 off are the picks if they're on your list. And cancel that auto-renew if you don't plan to keep Switch Online.
