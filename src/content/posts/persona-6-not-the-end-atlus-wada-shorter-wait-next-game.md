**Atlus says Persona 6 isn't the last stop, and it doesn't want the next wait to be another decade.** Persona general producer **Kazuhisa Wada** said it plainly in part 2 of Sega Sammy's [SegaSammyMania interview series](https://www.segasammy.co.jp/ja/ir/individual/interviews/segasammania002_part2/), published this week and translated by [Persona Central](https://personacentral.com/persona-reducing-time-releases/) and [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/persona/persona-6-is-not-the-end-for-the-series-lead-hints-and-atlus-is-working-to-ensure-fans-wont-have-to-wait-as-long-for-the-next-game/).

## What Wada said

Wada confirmed P-Studio's current project is Persona 6. He said he "can't say much at the moment," but that the team is "creating something better than anything we've made before," per GamesRadar's translation.

Then came the part fans will hold him to. "Persona 6 is not the end," Wada said. Atlus is "working on various things" so people "won't have to wait as long for the next game as they have previously."

He also explained why the gaps have been so long. "Large-scale RPGs like the Persona series take five years or more to develop," he said. Persona Central's translation adds how Atlus plans to speed things up: "strengthening collaboration between [internal] departments and improving the development process by utilizing management and sharing tools."

## What we still don't know

Wada didn't give a release date, a window or platforms. The [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2649560/) lists Persona 6 as developed by ATLUS and published by SEGA, with the date "To be announced." You'll see 2027 floating around, but that comes from leaks, not from Atlus, so don't count on it yet. Wada also didn't say whether work on the game after Persona 6 has started, or if the faster pace means shared tools and tech across games.

For context, Persona 5 came out in Japan in 2016. GamesRadar notes Persona 6 has been in development since 2021, after Persona 5 Royal's global launch.

**PPR take:** I appreciate the honesty here. "Five years or more" for a big RPG is just the reality now, and Wada isn't pretending otherwise. What I like is that he's talking about process, not hype....better tools, departments working together, less wheel reinventing. That's how you actually close the gap between games without burning out your team or shipping something half-baked. BUT "we're working on various things" is still a promise with no numbers on it. Persona fans have waited a long, long time, and a decade between numbered games is rough for any series. I'd rather Atlus take the time and nail Persona 6 than rush it to hit a date. Show us the game, then show us the faster pipeline actually works with whatever comes next.
