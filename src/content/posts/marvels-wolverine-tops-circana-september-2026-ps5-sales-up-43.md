**Mixed reviews, big sales.** Insomniac's **Marvel's Wolverine** was the best-selling game in the US in September by dollar sales, according to Circana's latest chart, as reported by [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/wolverine-was-uss-best-selling-game-in-september-with-ps5-sales-up-43/). Circana's Mat Piscatella said it posted the highest launch-month dollar sales for a Sony-published game since **Helldivers 2** in February 2024, and it's already 2026's 11th best-selling game in the US.

**The rest of the picture:**

- **PS5 hardware:** dollar sales were up **43%** from September 2025, giving Sony its best hardware dollar share in a month since May 2025, per [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/marvels-wolverine-tops-september-sales-drives-ps5-numbers-up-circana/).
- **A crowded month:** eight of the top 10 were new. NBA 2K27 came in second, followed by EA Sports FC 27, Fire Emblem: Fortune's Weave, The Blood of Dawnwalker, Wardogs, Onimusha: Way of the Sword, Halloween: The Game, Ace Combat 8: Wings of Theve and Madden NFL 27.
- **Overall spending:** total US spending on games, hardware and accessories slipped **1%** to about **$4.8 billion**. Hardware rose 12% and accessories 10%, while game content fell 2%.

Circana's September covers August 30 through October 3. As [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/marvels-wolverine-proves-you-shouldnt-always-trust-what-you-see-on-social-media/) put it, the sales tell a different story than the game's mixed reception online. GamesBeat's own review knocked its linear design and repetitive combat.

**PPR take:** Loud complaints online and sales don't always match. A PS5 exclusive topping a month this packed, weeks before GTA 6, is a strong result for Sony.
