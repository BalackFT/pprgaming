**Six and a half million and counting.** Grand Theft Auto VI has an estimated **6.45 million digital pre-orders** worldwide, worth about **$630 million** net of returns, according to the latest weekly update to [Sensor Tower's GTA VI sales tracker](https://sensortower.com/grand-theft-auto-VI/sales-tracker). The snapshot runs to October 18, a month before the game's November 19 launch on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

**PlayStation is carrying it.** Sony's platform accounts for roughly 4.80 million copies and $468 million, or 74% of the total, while Xbox sits at about 1.66 million copies and $162 million, as broken down by [TechPowerUp](https://www.techpowerup.com/353934/gta-6-digital-pre-orders-climb-to-6-45-million-playstation-holds-74-share). The October 13 snapshot had the game at about 6.08 million pre-orders and $594 million, so roughly 370,000 more people locked in a copy in under a week. [TweakTown](https://www.tweaktown.com/news/114187/gta-6-has-already-made-rockstar-more-than-half-a-billion-dollars/index.html) points to the recent launch trailer as a likely driver. In the US, Sensor Tower says digital pre-orders are running 148% ahead of 2022's Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 2 at the same point before release, the best of any big launch it has tracked in six years.

**Read the fine print.** These are third-party estimates, not sales reports. They count digital pre-orders only, so physical boxes (which for GTA VI hold a download code rather than a disc) aren't included, and the revenue is what buyers paid before Sony and Microsoft take their cut. [GTA Intel](https://gtaintel.com/news/gta-6-preorders-6-million-sensor-tower) also notes that Sensor Tower tweaked its estimation model on October 5, so older numbers aren't a perfect comparison. Take-Two hasn't published a pre-order figure, though CEO Strauss Zelnick has called demand "unprecedented and astonishing" and said buyers are skewing toward the $100 Ultimate Edition. The next chance for an official number is Take-Two's second-quarter earnings on November 9, its last call before launch.

**PPR take:** Nobody expected GTA VI to struggle, but $630 million before a single copy has unlocked is still a staggering number, and it's only the digital slice. The more interesting figure is that 74/26 split: it says a lot about where console players are right now, and it's a big reason Sony is happy to keep the marketing deal. Expect this tracker to cross 7 million well before November 19.
