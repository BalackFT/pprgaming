**Patch 2 is out of testing.** Star Vault posted [Patch Notes 3.1.21](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1170950/view/1850460852980278) on Steam early this morning, putting the second **Era of Refinement** update on Mortal Online 2's live servers. It spent a week on the Public Test Realm, which [opened on November 18](/news/mortal-online-2-era-of-refinement-patch-2-ptr-live-patch-notes/), and most of what we covered from the [PTR notes](https://www.mortalonline2.com/era-of-refinement-patch-2-ptr-opens-today-patch-notes) made it through.

## The Aggressive Flag is now how PvP works

The headline change replaces the old **criminal actions toggle** with an **Aggressive Flag**. If you aren't flagged, you simply can't damage innocent players or commit criminal acts. Swings and spells still play out, they just do nothing on an invalid target. Once you flag, anyone can legally attack and loot you, and other players see a **crossed swords icon** under your name. The Criminal targeting icon is now a hooded face. One nice touch from the live notes: you can now cancel the flag by pressing the keybind again before it finishes toggling on. A side effect is that Elementalism spells can now be cast without flagging, since AoE only hits valid targets.

## Group fights hit differently

A new combat mechanic tracks how much health you lost in the last second and gives you a **defensive bonus against further damage** that scales with it. Star Vault says it matters most when several players hit one target at once. It doesn't apply to normal AI damage, but it does apply to pets.

## The world got busier

**Tagmaton invasions** are switched back on, and they no longer march into cities. They now grow slowly from where they start, and they can show up on Sarducaa too. Over **100 structure skins** can be found in the world to change the walls, floors, roofs and trim of houses, strongholds and keeps. Six more creatures are mountable, eight rare mount variants were added, and the core AI navigation system was replaced, with Star Vault warning there's more fixing to come.

One change from the PTR is worth flagging: the test notes cut **Alvarin Sight** to 4 seconds, while the live notes say it now lasts **15 seconds**, fading in over the first 10. The [full notes](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1170950/view/1850460852980278) cover the new spells, Concentration and weapon skill reworks, and weight class rebalancing.

**PPR take:** The Aggressive Flag is the change that will decide how this patch is remembered. Making criminal play an opt-in state that everyone can see should cut down on accidental flags and on new players getting jumped by someone who looked harmless. The catch is it also makes intent obvious, so the gankers who relied on surprise will have to adjust. The anti-burst mechanic is the quiet big one. If it works, a small group has a real shot against a zerg instead of getting deleted in one volley. And Star Vault keeps hitting its monthly cadence, with [Table Games set for December](https://www.mortalonline2.com/development-livestream-recap-table-games-and-ships-announcements). That consistency is how you win back lapsed players.
