**Final Fantasy XIV's next expansion has a date.** During the Fan Festival 2026 keynote in Tokyo, Square Enix [announced](https://press.it.square-enix.com/en-GB/ZEPHYR-UNVEILED-AS-SECOND-NEW-JOB-FOR-FINAL-FANTASY-XIV-EVERCOLD-SET-T) that **Evercold launches Tuesday, February 23, 2027**. Players who pre-order get early access, which is tentatively set to start **Friday, February 19**.

**Meet the Zephyr**

Producer and director Naoki Yoshida revealed **Zephyr**, the second of Evercold's two new jobs. It's a physical ranged DPS that fights with a crossbow, backed up by a bird familiar called Nephele. Per [Noisy Pixel](https://noisypixel.net/ffxiv-zephyr-job-guide/), like the new Bastion tank it's only playable in the new Evolved Mode control scheme, and it starts at level 90 in Gridania.

**35 million accounts and pre-orders**

Square Enix also said Final Fantasy XIV has passed **35 million registered accounts** worldwide. Pre-orders for Evercold and the Collector's Box start **Wednesday, November 4**, with the physical Collector's Bundle sold in limited quantities through the Square Enix Store. The Digital Collector's Edition includes a Valhallar Odin mount, an Entreat Crystal emote and a Crystal Skyltborg weapon for Bastion. Pre-ordering also grants items right away, including the Halone's Earring EXP bonus.

**What else is in Evercold**

The [official Evercold site](https://na.finalfantasyxiv.com/evercold/) lists a level cap increase from 100 to 110, new areas aboard the Fourth's massive realmships, new cities, an EVANGELION alliance raid series called Ghosts of Desire, a new raid series called Beyond the Lifestream, a new Fierce raid difficulty between Normal and Savage, a new Ultimate raid, chocobo companions that can join you in dungeons, and housing updates through the 8.x patches.

**PPR take:** A crossbow plus a pet bird is a fun pick for a new ranged job, and February isn't that far off.
