**The finish line is in sight.** Publisher Kwalee and developer Deep Denizens announced on Thursday that [HARK THE GHOUL](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3058660/HARK_THE_GHOUL/) will launch on **January 21, 2027**. It's coming day and date to PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch and Switch 2. [Gematsu notes](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/11/hark-the-ghoul-launches-january-21-2027-for-ps5-xbox-series-switch-2-switch-and-pc) that the console versions are newly announced, and the date came with a new release trailer.

The team had already pushed the game from 2026 to Q1 2027 in an earlier Steam update, saying it wanted the game "super polished and super complete day one" and promising an exact date at the end of November. They delivered right on time. In the [announcement post on Steam](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3058660), the developers say it has been four years of work for the three of them.

## What kind of game it is

HARK THE GHOUL is a first-person dungeon crawler built around freeform exploration. Per its Steam page, you wake from a cocoon under the light of an ominous star in a rain-soaked city full of shortcuts and secrets, and you're free to wander wherever you want. The arsenal covers swords, whips, pistols, cannons, magick and kicks, and each tool has its own tricks: you can grapple enemies with a harpoon gun, plant a spear in a wall and stand on it, or make boxes appear to climb. Kwalee calls it a "neo-gothic love letter" to older games, and it ships with customizable visual filters and a 72-page in-game artbook with developer commentary.

## The demo got one last update

There's also a final update to the [two-hour Steam demo](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3132850/HARK_THE_GHOUL_Demo/). The developers say it adds an optional new area, the Cerulean Sluice, along with more environmental detail, combat and platforming tweaks, bug fixes and better performance on lower-end PCs. Demo saves still carry over to the full game. The demo already sits at Very Positive on Steam.

**PPR take:** Three people, four years, and a real date. That's worth a nod. The demo has been well liked on Steam for a while, so getting console versions on the same day is a big deal for anybody who doesn't game on PC. January is also a smart window. It's usually a quiet month after the holiday rush, which gives a small team a better shot at being noticed. If you like the old-school, figure-it-out-yourself style of dungeon crawler, the demo is free and your progress comes with you, so there's no reason not to try it now. Just know the devs themselves say you might want to wait so you don't spoil your first run.
