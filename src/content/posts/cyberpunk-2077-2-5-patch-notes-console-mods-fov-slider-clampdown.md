**The full list is out.** After today's REDstreams with Paweł Sasko and Natalia Pych, CD PROJEKT RED [posted the patch notes](https://www.cyberpunk.net/en/news/52120/check-out-whats-coming-to-night-city-in-the-edgerunners-2-update-for-cyberpunk-2077) for **Cyberpunk 2077's Edgerunners 2 Update (2.5)**, which [we first covered yesterday](/news/cyberpunk-2077-update-2-5-edgerunners-2-october-22/). It goes live **tomorrow, October 22**, on PC and Mac (Steam, GOG, Epic), PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, with Switch 2 and the Mac App Store version "in the days to follow" ([X announcement](https://x.com/CyberpunkGame/status/2112933739189928409)).

**Mods on consoles.** The biggest surprise is **cross-platform mod support powered by mod.io**: you'll be able to browse and install a curated selection of player-made mods from the in-game menu on PC, Mac, PS5, PS5 Pro, Xbox Series X|S and Switch 2. CDPR calls it "a first step" and says more mods will be added over time. One catch for console players: saves that use mods get a special icon, and **achievements and trophies are disabled** on a modded save.

**The Edgerunners content.**

- **Clampdown**, a new side quest set before the events of *Edgerunners 2*. To unlock it, finish The Heist and two gigs for Wakako, then wait for her message.
- A **5-piece gear set based on King**, earned during Clampdown.
- **HKGN 102.0 FM**, a new radio station with tracks from the Edgerunners series.

**New ways to play and show off.**

- The **Double Trouble** perk in the Cool tree, plus four supporting perks, lets you **dual-wield Power pistols and revolvers**, with new Photo Mode poses to match.
- **Cyberware skins:** five **Glamdocs**, each in its own shop with an intro scene, let you restyle Mantis Blades, Gorilla Arms, Monowire and the Projectile Launch System with custom colors.
- Five new outfit sets from Maxiwear, Jinguji, NetWatch and IconAmerica (with a fifth coming later), the NUS Infiltrator set, and some NPC-only hairstyles for V.
- Cyberpunk Trading Card Game frames in Photo Mode, and four TWINTONE skins for the Quadra Turbo-R V-Tech through Twitch Drops.

**More to do.** A second new side quest has Richard Night narrating a guided tour of the city (unlocks after The Heist at Street Cred 15), and **12 Minor Arcana tarot cards** extend the Fool on the Hill quest with a reward for collecting them all.

**Quality of life and tech.** Consoles finally get an **FOV slider**. Saves are now grouped by playthrough, and stashes get item filters. On PC, 2.5 adds **DLSS 4.5** with up to 6X Multi Frame Generation, drops FSR 3 upscaling (FSR 2.1, FSR 4 and FSR 3.1 Frame Generation stay), and adds an FSR Anti-Lag 2 toggle. CDPR also lists ray tracing and PS5 Pro optimizations, better HDR, and fixes such as the Switch 2 "Save failed, insufficient space" error and Film Grain doing nothing.

**PPR take:** This is a much bigger update than "anime tie-in" suggested. Console mods and an FOV slider are the headline for most players, and a new Wakako-linked quest is a good excuse to reinstall. Just remember that if you want trophies or achievements on console, keep a clean save separate from your modded one.
