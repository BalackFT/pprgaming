**Update (Nov 23, 10:45 AM ET):** Rockstar has now posted notes. At 9:42 AM ET [@RockstarSupport said](https://x.com/RockstarSupport/status/2124876263357648915) "a new update is now available" on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, linking its [GTA VI Launch Updates page](https://support.rockstargames.com/articles/sN5pEdFYZdlvijKXd6xpJ/). Rockstar's notes don't list a version number. The November 23 entry says it:

- Fixed several issues that caused the game to crash
- Fixed weather not changing for extended periods
- Fixed the game not autosaving correctly
- Fixed several issues with phone calls not triggering, including ones that progress the story or unlock mission content
- Fixed players being unable to lose their Wanted Level
- Fixed players being unable to use the deadlift bar in the gym
- Fixed partners not handing over bags to sell to fences

Rockstar doesn't name error CE-108255-1 specifically, so we still can't say whether that crash is fixed. The rest of this story is as published at 7:55 AM.

**Four days after launch, GTA 6 has its first real update.** Rockstar dataminer Tex2 [flagged it on X](https://x.com/TexFunz2/status/2124804109022937382) just before 5 AM ET this morning: version **01.002.002** on PS5 and **1.0.2.2** on Xbox Series X|S. A few minutes later [he confirmed it was live](https://x.com/TexFunz2/status/2124806178047721848), at **603.8 MB on PS5 and 1.6 GB on Xbox**. [VICE](https://www.vice.com/en/article/gta-6-update-01-002-002-patch-notes/) picked it up and calls it the first patch since the November 19 launch. The two before it, 01.002.000 and 01.002.001, both landed before release day.

## PS5 gets it in waves

If your PS5 isn't showing it yet, that's expected. Per [Tex2](https://x.com/TexFunz2/status/2124808147848020308), the PS5 rollout started with 33% of players, moves to 66% about three hours later, and reaches everyone about six hours after it began. On Xbox it went out to everyone at once.

## No patch notes

Here's the catch. Rockstar hasn't published notes for this one, and VICE says the same. We didn't find a patch post from Rockstar on X this morning either. So we can't tell you if it touches the PS5 crash a lot of players have been stuck on, error [CE-108255-1](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/error-codes/ps5/ce-108255-1/), which we [wrote about yesterday](/news/gta-6-metacritic-user-score-5-6-review-bombing-critics-99-ps5-crashes/). If notes show up, we'll add them here and on the [GTA 6 hub](/gta-6/).

**PPR take:** A 600 MB patch on day four is pretty normal for a game this size. What isn't great is the silence. If you're one of the people whose game crashes in the opening hours, "an update came out, good luck" isn't much to go on. A single line saying "we're aware of CE-108255-1 and we're working on it" would go a long way, and Rockstar hasn't even given players that. Honestly, after $3.79 billion on day one, they can afford a changelog. My advice: let it install, jump back in, and if you were crashing before, see if you still are. Don't go hunting for "fixed" claims on social media. Until Rockstar says what's in the patch, nobody knows for sure, no matter how confident the post sounds.
