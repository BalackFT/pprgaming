Hey everyone, hope the Black Friday deals are treating you right! A couple more things worth knowing about before the weekend.

<h2 id="mortal-online-2-roadmap-patch-3-krampos">Mortal Online 2's new roadmap puts Krampos and Table Games next</h2>

With Patch 3.1.21 settled in, Star Vault has [revealed a new roadmap](https://www.gamespress.com/en-US/Mortal-Online-2-Releases-Second-Era-of-Refinement-Update-and-Reveals-R) for Mortal Online 2. The third Era of Refinement update is currently planned to bring Table Games, which the studio says will give players a new reason to gather in taverns, along with more balance and quality-of-life work, more rideable animals and the return of the Krampos event. Star Vault notes the roadmap can change as development goes on. Today also brought [Hotfix 3.1.21.52](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1170950/view/1850460852989801), which fixes AI giving up on targets mid-fight, gets relics spawning again and changes Alvarin Sight to a teal color to help colorblind players.

<h2 id="dawn-of-war-iv-painter-demo">Dawn of War IV's free Painter Demo is out on Steam</h2>

Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War IV doesn't launch until December 3, but you can start on your army's colors now. Deep Silver and KING Art released a free [Painter Demo](https://www.deepsilver.com/games/dawn-of-war-iv/news/painter-demo-available-now) on Steam that covers units from all four factions. The Basic Painter recolors a whole army quickly, while the Advanced Painter lets you work individual armor and body parts with colors, materials, patterns, decals and weathering. Schemes you save carry over to the full game, and sharing codes let friends import your designs. Per [Warhammer Community](https://www.warhammer-community.com/en-gb/articles/zpan8d3y/show-your-true-colours-in-dawn-of-war-iv/), Commander edition owners start playing Monday.

**PPR take:** Krampos coming back is a nice holiday touch for Nave, and tavern games are the kind of small social feature a sandbox MMO really needs. As for the Painter Demo, anyone who has spent hours on a real miniature knows exactly where their weekend is going.
