**The datamines got ahead of themselves.** Alongside today's pre-load kickoff, Rockstar finally laid out how Grand Theft Auto VI will run, and the headline is 30 frames per second on every console, PS5 Pro included. "Grand Theft Auto VI has been fully developed from the ground up to provide the best possible experience for the current generation of consoles, targeting 30 frames per second," Rockstar wrote, per [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/2026/11/10/grand-theft-auto-vi-will-run-at-30-fps-on-all-consoles-but-ps5-pro-will-feature-improved). The studio says the target lets it keep the density and detail of Leonida's environments and crowds.

That cools off the 120Hz PS5 Pro references dataminers spotted in the pre-load files, which [we flagged yesterday](/news/gta-6-pre-load-tonight-212gb-multiplayer-files-the-festival-miami/) with a healthy dose of salt. [GameSpot notes](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-ps5-pro-features-frame-rates-and-xbox-cloud-gaming-details-confirmed-by-rockstar/) Rockstar made no mention of extra performance modes.

PS5 Pro owners do get something for their money, just not frames. Rockstar confirmed Sony's PSSR upscaling for "improved clarity" and ray-traced shadows that are exclusive to the Pro. On both PS5 models, the game supports DualSense haptics you can tune in the accessibility settings, Tempest 3D audio and faster load times, which Rockstar says make GTA VI "play best on PlayStation 5 consoles." Remote Play is supported on PlayStation and Xbox, [The Verge reports](https://www.theverge.com/games/1030749/rockstar-gta-6-30fps-ps5-pro-pssr-ray-tracing).

The bigger surprise is on Xbox. [Xbox Wire confirmed](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/10/grand-theft-auto-6-comes-to-xbox-cloud-gaming-november-19/) GTA VI will be playable on day one through Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox One and Series X|S, starting at 1 a.m. ET on November 19, using Xbox Series S graphics settings. Microsoft also called it the most pre-ordered title of all time on the Microsoft Store, and says GTA V, Red Dead Redemption 2 and more Rockstar games will join the cloud library in the coming months. You still have to buy the game, and starting November 11 players in supported markets can buy cloud playtime without Game Pass, [as we covered](/news/xbox-cloud-playtime-packs-pay-as-you-go-without-game-pass/). There's no cloud streaming on PlayStation.

**PPR take:** A 30fps lock on the PS5 Pro is going to sting for people who bought that console for exactly this game, and I get it. But Rockstar has never chased frame rate on consoles. Its console games have always leaned on detail over speed. Spending the Pro's extra power on image quality and ray-traced shadows instead of a shaky 60 is the safer call for a world this dense. The cloud news is the real win. Letting someone on a 13-year-old Xbox One play GTA VI on launch night is a big deal, even at Series S settings, though your internet connection will decide how good it feels. Nine days left.
