**The look back from Manchester is finally here.** The official Squadron 42 channel posted an 18-minute [Inside CIG: Community Gameplay Event](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6JHK6KPap-M) video today, and [@squadron_42](https://x.com/squadron_42/status/2120548524144152802) put it up this morning with a simple pitch: a look back at the recent community event in Manchester, "featuring gameplay captured directly from attendees' play sessions and a glimpse behind the scenes."

CIG first flagged a behind-the-scenes film from the October event in late October, then [slipped it](/news/squadron-42-october-event-behind-the-scenes-video-delayed/) when community manager Zyloh-CIG said "a few unexpected things came up" and that the video would come "soon." This is that look back.

## What's in the video

It opens in the room before people sit down to play. The staff are buzzing, and **Chris Roberts** tells the attendees that Squadron 42 is "really very much the Wing Commander that I dreamed I could have made back when I was a 20-year-old kid." He talks about wanting a fully immersive game, and says you're "the main character of the biggest summer blockbuster you've ever experienced." Another speaker, standing with him, calls what they're about to play "a really good chunk of the game," and tells everyone not to rush it: sit down, put the headphones on, tune everyone out, and enjoy it.

Then it cuts into the play sessions themselves. You can hear people in the room reacting as someone plays, which is exactly what CIG promised: footage captured from the attendees' own sessions. The gameplay walks through life aboard the **UEES Stanton**, with chief engineer **Eugene Morrow** welcoming the new pilot, master-at-arms **Duncan Chakma** getting you registered for the weapon lockers, a stop in medbay for first-aid gear, and a meal from the ship's dispensers. **Captain Noah White** welcomes you aboard, mentions your actions in the Battle of Vega 2, and briefs a run in the **Odin system**, which he describes as mostly mining and corporate research, with outlaws, illegal salvage, and the occasional slavers in the mix. There's a quick primer on ballistic weapons versus energy weapons, then a hangar launch under the callsign **Baron 2**, and a UEE Navy challenge to unidentified vessels that doesn't stay polite for long.

The last stretch is the behind-the-scenes glimpse, including **Mark Hamill** talking about his character, Lt. Cdr. Steve "Old Man" Colton. Attendees talk about how emotional the weekend was, and one says the favorite part was getting to chat with Chris, and how unexpected it was to see him spend so much time with every person there. A developer says the team has "achieved a level of stability and speed and performance in the game that allows Chris's vision to come to life. Finally," and thanks the people in the room and the rest of the community.

## The event this looks back on

This is the invite-only Squadron 42 hands-on at CIG's Manchester studio, October 9-11 ([our story](/news/squadron-42-manchester-hands-on-event/)). In its October 12 [This Week in Star Citizen](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/transmission/21374-This-Week-In-Star-Citizen), CIG said it "welcomed around 140 community members to Manchester for the first hands-on experience." Attendees spent five hours playing Squadron 42, plus an extra hour in a unique story chapter aboard an adrift Xi'an ship.

The video itself is a look back at that weekend, not a date announcement. In the August 27 [Letter from the Chairman](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/transmission/21301-Letter-From-The-Chairman), Chris Roberts wrote that CIG would make "further announcements about our release plans during this time and a specific release date shortly afterwards." The [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4045720/Squadron_42/) still lists **Q2 2027**.

**PPR take:** I've been waiting on this one since they slipped it at the end of October, and I'm glad they finally put it out. Hearing Chris talk about the Wing Commander he dreamed of making, then watching people actually play it from their own sessions....that's the look I've wanted. Still no date in this video, and that "specific release date shortly afterwards" promise from August is still hanging. But for those of us who weren't in that room, this is the next best thing. o7
