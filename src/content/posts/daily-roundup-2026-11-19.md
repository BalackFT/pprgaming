Hello all! Vice City is eating the whole internet today, but a few other things popped up that are worth a quick look.

<h2 id="gta-6-streaming-records">GTA 6 breaks livestreaming and internet traffic records</h2>

GTA 6 didn't just launch, it took over the streams. Preliminary Streams Charts data cited by [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/gaming/news/gta-6-launch-livestream-internet-traffic-records-1236953145/) has the game peaking at 7.91 million concurrent viewers across platforms, with about 3.31 million on Twitch, 3.22 million on YouTube and 1.5 million on Kick. [Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/gta/news/gta-6-twitch-viewership-record), using SullyGnome, has the Twitch peak at 3,293,515, just edging Fortnite's December 2023 record by under 2,000 viewers. Twitch itself told Variety the game passed Valorant's 1.7 million launch record within six hours of the New Zealand unlock, and [Riot Games congratulated Rockstar](https://x.com/riotgames/status/2123210073644446049) on X. Internet exchange operator DE-CIX also reported a record 33.03 terabits per second across its exchanges Wednesday evening, which Variety ties to the launch window. The trackers count it a little differently, so treat the exact numbers as preliminary.

<h2 id="gta-6-ign-official-guide">Rockstar and IGN team up on an official GTA VI map and guide</h2>

If you'd rather have a map open on your phone than the printable one on your wall, there's an official option. IGN says it [collaborated with Rockstar Games](https://x.com/IGN/status/2123431503157940488) on a big GTA VI guide with a detailed interactive map of Vice City and the rest of Leonida, plus checklist trackers, and Rockstar retweeted it on launch morning. The guide lives on [IGN's GTA hub](https://www.ign.com/gta). Fair warning: guides and maps naturally show locations and collectibles, so if you want to find everything yourself, maybe hold off a few days.

<h2 id="star-citizen-neonwave-bundle">Star Citizen gets a Miami-flavored Neonwave bundle</h2>

CIG picked a fun day for this one. The [official Star Citizen account](https://x.com/RobertsSpaceInd/status/2123513561045450868) posted "Dress for the occasion. 😎🌴" Thursday afternoon with a link to the new [Neonwave Bundle](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/pledge/Gear/Neonwave-Bundle-Warbond) on the RSI Store. RSI describes it as a collection of seven items "inspired by the Sol system's famous city of vice, Miami." It's listed as a Warbond bundle, and RSI's Warbond offers are paid with new money rather than store credit. It's cosmetic gear, so nothing here changes how the 'verse plays.
