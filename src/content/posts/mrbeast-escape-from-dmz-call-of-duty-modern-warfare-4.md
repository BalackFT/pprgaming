**MrBeast is going back to where he started.** Yesterday he [posted on X](https://x.com/MrBeast/status/2111860420692803970): "I started on YouTube making COD videos and tomorrow I'm dropping one of the biggest COD videos of all time to celebrate the release of Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4! IM SO EXCITED!" That post has passed 2.3 million views, and the video is due on his channel today, October 19.

**What we know about it.** [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/mr-beast-teases-a-huge-call-of-duty-video-focused-on-dmz-with-real-life-content-creators/) reports that a title screen in the teaser reads **"Escape from DMZ,"** so it looks like a real-life version of Modern Warfare 4's extraction mode. Other creators in the video have started posting their own teasers, and one of them, SnowBikeMike, billed it as "Call of Duty x Mr. Beast x 19 Incredible Creators and ME." [GameRiv](https://gameriv.com/mrbeast-teases-massive-call-of-duty-video-to-celebrate-modern-warfare-4-launch/) notes the announcement didn't spell out the challenges or the full cast, so the exact format is still a surprise until it goes live.

**The timing isn't an accident.** The video lands one day before **DMZ Early Access opens on Tuesday, October 20** for anyone who digitally pre-purchased the Standard Digital or Vault Edition, per Activision's [DMZ FOB Intel Guide](https://news.blizzard.com/en-us/article/24306097/dmz-deep-dive-part-2-fob-intel-guide). The full game, with multiplayer, launches **Friday, October 23** on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, PC and Nintendo Switch 2. We covered the campaign's early access window [here](/news/modern-warfare-4-campaign-early-access/).

**Quick DMZ refresher.** DMZ is Call of Duty's extraction mode: you drop into the Hajin zone solo or with a squad, fight AI forces and rival players, loot and craft gear, and try to get out alive with what you found. Between runs you build up a Forward Operating Base with more than a dozen stations, as the [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/10/05/everything-you-need-to-know-about-dmz-mode-in-call-of-duty-modern-warfare-4/) explains.

**PPR take:** A creator with over 500 million subscribers making a COD video the day before DMZ opens is about the biggest marketing push a game mode can get. Whether the mode holds onto those players once GTA VI arrives on November 19 is the real test.
