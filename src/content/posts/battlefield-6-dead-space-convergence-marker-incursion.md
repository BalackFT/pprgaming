**Something woke up under Area 51.** Battlefield 6's limited-time **Dead Space: Convergence** event went live today, October 20, and it runs through **November 10** in both Battlefield 6 and the free-to-play REDSEC, [per EA's event guide](https://www.ea.com/games/battlefield/battlefield-6/news/battlefield-dead-space-event-guide). It's playable on PS5, Xbox Series X|S and PC.

The setup: Pax Armata forces digging through the vaults of Area 51 for new weapons tech find an ancient alien **Marker**. Anyone who gets too close slides into violent madness, and the spread threatens to become an extinction-level event. Area 51 is one of the two maps that arrived with [Season 5: Siege of Vegas](https://www.ea.com/games/battlefield/battlefield-6/news/battlefield-6-game-update-2-1-1-0) on October 13.

**Marker Incursion** is the headline mode, a new **16-player PvE** experience. Squads push through corrupted ground against hordes of AI-controlled **Marked Soldiers**, troops who now treat their old allies as enemies and defend the Marker at any cost. Along the way you destroy **Marker Shrines**, smaller replicas that strengthen the Marker's grip and pull in more resistance, before making a final push to stop the **Convergence**. The deeper you go, the harder it gets.

No Dead Space crossover would be complete without the **DS-23 Pulse Rifle**, and it makes its Battlefield debut as a Battle Pickup inside the mode. [Gamerable](https://gamerable.com/battlefield-6-dead-space-convergence-event-is-live-with-marker-incursion) notes the event shipped with Update 2.1.1.0.

**Free rewards** from event challenges include:

- The **"Aegis VII" soldier skin**, modeled on the series' industrial engineering suits
- The **"RIG-Certified" SMG** weapon package
- Two weapon stickers, soldier patches and a player card background

EA is also selling two Dead Space-themed store bundles, and it says hidden transmissions and environmental storytelling are scattered through the event for longtime fans to find.

**PPR take:** A co-op horde mode with the Pulse Rifle is a smart Halloween hook, and 16 players is a big enough squad to make the Marker feel like a real raid. You've got three weeks to grind out the Aegis VII skin before it's gone on November 10.
