Evening, everybody! A few more from today that are worth a look before you log off.

<h2 id="crystal-dynamics-eight-layoffs">Crystal Dynamics confirms eight more layoffs</h2>

The Tomb Raider studio has cut staff again. A Crystal Dynamics spokesperson told [Vertical Slice](https://www.verticalslice.news/report-embracer-studio-crystal-dynamics-has-seemingly-begun-its-fifth-layoff-round-in-two-years/) it made a "difficult, but necessary business decision" to reduce the team by eight people, with full severance and job placement help for everyone affected, and said its current projects aren't impacted. Developers who posted about it on LinkedIn listed Tomb Raider: Catalyst among their credits. [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/tomb-raider-developer-makes-fifth-round-of-layoffs-in-less-than-2-years/) counts this as at least the fifth round in under two years, about 70 people total. Legacy of Atlantis is still set for February 2027, and Catalyst slipped to 2028.

<h2 id="netflix-games-layoffs">Netflix Games lets more people go</h2>

Netflix confirmed to [Vertical Slice](https://www.verticalslice.news/report-netflix-games-workers-are-being-laid-off/) that a "small number" of Netflix Games employees were laid off, while saying its overall games strategy hasn't changed and it's still hiring and launching titles like Scrabble Shake-Up and Scattergories Party, both out today. Game director Andrew Brownell wrote on LinkedIn that the team is shifting "how it approaches making games," and per [Mobilegamer.biz](https://mobilegamer.biz/netflix-games-lays-off-staff-as-team-shifts-how-it-approaches-making-games/) his group had been working on cloud-streamed action games and phone-as-controller play. No headcount was given. Next Games in Helsinki is now Netflix's only first-party studio.

<h2 id="studio-bravarda-closes">Studio Bravarda shuts down</h2>

The six-person Brazilian indie behind Extremely Powerful Capybaras and Sky Caravan is closing. In a farewell note on November 16, the team said Capybaras never earned back its development costs after launch-day online play wasn't stable in every region, "especially in China," which hurt its Steam reviews, as reported by [Gaming.net](https://www.gaming.net/studio-bravarda-closes-after-latest-game-fails-to-recoup-costs/). New projects built in Godot couldn't find funding, and work-for-hire jobs kept the lights on until they couldn't, per [Infobae](https://www.infobae.com/malditos-nerds/2026/11/17/studio-bravarda-cierra-tras-las-perdidas-de-extremely-powerful-capybaras/). The good news for players is that both games stay up for sale on their platforms.

**PPR take:** Rough Tuesday for a lot of folks. Between Firaxis this afternoon, Crystal Dynamics, Netflix and a small indie team saying goodbye, that's a lot of talented people looking for work right before the holidays. Crystal Dynamics calling it "necessary" for the fifth time in two years is starting to wear thin..lol. Hats off to the Bravarda crew for owning their launch problems instead of pointing fingers. Go grab Capybaras with some friends if you've never tried it.
