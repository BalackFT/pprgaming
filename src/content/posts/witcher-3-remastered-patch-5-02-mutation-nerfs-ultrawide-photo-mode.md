**Geralt just got a balance pass.** CD PROJEKT RED put out [Patch 5.02 for The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt — Remastered](https://www.thewitcher.com/us/en/news/52162/patch-5-02-for-the-witcher-3-wild-hunt-remastered-is-live) Thursday on PC, PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and Nintendo Switch 2. It's mostly fixes, but a few changes stand out for anyone mid-playthrough.

## The changes that matter most

- **Mutations and Sign builds take a hit.** Adrenaline Rush loses 20% of its Attack Power and Sign Intensity bonus, Magic Sensibilities loses 15% crit chance and 50% crit damage, and Catalyst and Sustained Glyphs are trimmed too. Sunder Armor now reduces resistance on all attacks, not just Strong Attacks.
- **The mod inventory wipe is fixed.** Installing or uninstalling mods could empty merchant inventories, Geralt's inventory and storage. That's patched.
- **Werewolves can die again.** They could regenerate health so fast on higher difficulties that they were impossible to beat.
- **32:9 super-ultrawide support** is in, plus an option to hide the UI when paused.
- **Photo Mode upgrades**, including hiding Geralt and NPCs individually and having the camera track an NPC.

There's also a pile of quest fixes, including an infinite load after giving Philippa's crystal to Yennefer in The Last Wish, plus Path Tracing optimizations on PC and improved V-Sync and VRR behavior on PS5. Switch 2 players get several mod fixes and a higher mod size limit. The [full notes](https://www.thewitcher.com/us/en/news/52162/patch-5-02-for-the-witcher-3-wild-hunt-remastered-is-live) list everything.

**PPR take:** The mod fix is the big one for me. Losing your whole stash because you swapped a mod is the kind of bug that makes people quit a playthrough on the spot, so good on CDPR for getting it handled. The Mutation nerfs are going to sting for anyone running a Sign-heavy, crit-stacked Geralt that melts everything, but honestly some of those numbers were silly. And the werewolf fix made me laugh....an unkillable werewolf on Death March is a nightmare nobody signed up for. Ultrawide folks finally get their 32:9 too. It's wild that a game this old is still getting this much love, and it's a good sign for how CDPR will treat The Witcher 4 down the road.
