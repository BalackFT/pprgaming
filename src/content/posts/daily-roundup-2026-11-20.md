<h2 id="star-citizen-ttk-balancing-update-6">Star Citizen's sixth ship TTK pass doubles turret health and trims Size 2 shields</h2>

If you've been dogfighting on the 5.0 PTU, the time-to-kill numbers moved again. Shark-CIG posted [Ship TTK Balancing Update #6](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/4/thread/5-0-ptu-feedback-ship-ttk-balancing-update-6) on Spectrum Thursday. Based on the last round of testing, CIG says heavy fighters felt slow with shields that were too tanky, shields took too long to knock down, and turrets died too fast. So version 6 doubles ship turret HP, cuts Size 2 shields by 20%, shortens shield "slap down" time by 25%, and adjusts heavy fighter flight. A few guns got tuned too: the Supremacy-10T laser beam and Revenant Gatling are down 30%, while the Relentless-L21 Gatling and Axiom L-22 repeater are up 10%. CIG wants PvP and PvE ratings for each ship size plus a survey. Remember, CIG has said this balance pass isn't currently planned for the 5.0 release.

<h2 id="iae-2956-day-8-aegis">IAE 2956 Day 8 is Aegis day</h2>

The show floor in New Babbage changed hands again. The [official Star Citizen account](https://x.com/RobertsSpaceInd/status/2123810971189723313) posted "Dedicated To Excellence. 🎖️" Friday, saying Aegis Dynamics "brings its storied military heritage to the forefront of Day 8 of #IAE2956," with a link to the [IAE 2956 page](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/iae2956). So it's Aegis ships front and center for the day. Free Fly is still running for the whole event, and IAE wraps on November 27, so there's still a week to try ships before you buy. Or before you talk yourself into a CCU you didn't plan on.

<h2 id="crimson-desert-2-05-01-hotfix">Crimson Desert hotfix 2.05.01 lets you read treasure maps from your inventory</h2>

Pearl Abyss pushed [Crimson Desert patch 2.05.01](https://crimsondesert.pearlabyss.com/en-US/News/Notice/Detail?_boardNo=161) to Steam, PlayStation, Xbox and Epic, with the Mac App Store version coming later. The handiest change: Treasure Maps, Wanted Posters and books can now be examined straight from the inventory through View Details, though a book stays locked until you've learned its knowledge. The camera on the dye and haircut screens can now be dragged from anywhere on screen, and the character's upper body moves more naturally when you start walking. The rest is fixes, including Kutum not attacking, quest items you couldn't get while building the Flame Knights' Castle and Drakesfall Castle, and a Kuku Pot bug that deleted extra copies when you discarded several at once, per the patch notes and [PlayStation Universe](https://www.psu.com/news/crimson-desert-update-2-05-01-improves-inventory-so-you-can-now-examine-treasure-maps-directly-from-it-full-patch-notes-listed/).

**PPR take:** Doubling turret HP is the right call. Crewed ships should feel like crewed ships, not a turret that pops in three seconds. Six passes in and CIG is still asking for feedback....that's how it should work. As for Aegis day, the military look is always fun to walk around in....even if the Lib's not going anywhere..lol. And a Kuku Pot that eats your stuff? That's one sneaky pot. 😆
