**"Meditate on this, I will."** That's the whole caption on this week's Star Citizen Sneak Peek, the teaser image that closes every Friday newsletter. [CIG's Oct 23 newsletter](https://mailchi.mp/cloudimperiumgames/weekly-friday-newsletter-10232026) ends on a [top-down render](https://media.robertsspaceindustries.com/y2rdew8h1x4if/source.png) of a square bronze-and-red device. It has a glowing blue sphere at the centre, cyan light lines running around it and four small pods at the corners. There's no name and no explanation.

**What is it?** Nobody outside CIG knows yet. The [Cavalera daily brief](https://www.cavalera.news/editions/2026-10-24) says the image goes by the title "Hyaoklo Meditation Focus" and reads it as a hint at something contemplative rather than combat. Whether that's an item, a location or a character is still anyone's guess. Sneak Peeks are meant to start arguments, and this one is working.

**The ATMO Social: extinguishers as engines**

The same newsletter has a more concrete invite. ATMO esports is hosting an "in-game extinguisher propulsion race" on **Saturday, November 14 at 19:00 UTC** (2 PM ET, since clocks fall back on November 1). The race runs on an **unreleased track at Levski, Nyx**. CIG's pitch: "Extinguishers: not just for emergencies." Sign-ups are in the event calendar on the [ATMO esports Discord](https://discord.com/invite/ATMOesports).

- **Day of the Vara** is still live, with limited-time monster paints for the Drake Vulture, Aegis Eclipse and Kruger Wolf, a pumpkin decorating contest, and a Vanduul mask you can claim in Arena Commander.

- **Wallpapers:** Mordvinov's [Constellation Mk V Centaurus gallery](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/community-hub/post/constellation-mk-v-centaurus-gallery-YTpBMeXQLd89r) is up on the Community Hub.
- **Recaps** of the Roadmap Update, the latest 10 From the Community and the Squadron 42 wishlist push on RSI and Steam.

**What's next:** [Open Hangar](https://openhangar.space/news) lists This Week in Star Citizen for Monday, and Alpha 4.10.3 is still on the Wave 1 PTU with the [Vanduul Incursion missions](/news/star-citizen-alpha-4-10-3-ptu-wave-1-vanduul-incursion-skarnov-rifle/).

**PPR take:** A glowing blue mystery box and a race where your only engine is a fire extinguisher. Peak Star Citizen weekend. Our money says the device is something you'll want sitting in your hangar.
