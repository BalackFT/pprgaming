**A joke mode built to troll GTA 6's launch turned into an AI mess, and it took about a day to undo.** The Binding of Isaac: Repentance+ hit PS5, Xbox Series X|S and Switch 2 on Nov. 19, the same day as GTA 6, and the Steam version got a free update with a special daily run to match. Players found that the run's music was made with the AI tool Suno. Creator Edmund McMillen says he didn't know, and publisher Nicalis has now pushed a PC patch that pulls the tracks.

## What players found

The parody run, which you can still start with the seed **"V1CE BSMT"**, lets you drive a cart around the basement and racks up GTA-style wanted stars and cop chases as you kill enemies, [Polygon reports](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-parody-binding-of-isaac-ai-use/). It also had its own soundtrack.

On Reddit, user ShweetsStuff posted a breakdown showing the update added **14 music files**, seven full songs plus intros and outros, and that some still carried a metadata comment reading "made with suno," with a creation date of Oct. 30, [according to Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/binding-of-isaac-update-review-bombed-over-ai-generated-music-in-gta-6-mode-woke-up-to-a-sht-show-obviously-i-had-no-idea-2000759213). Other files had that comment scrubbed. Steam reviews tanked fast, and [The Verge](https://www.theverge.com/games/1039770/binding-of-isaac-ai-music) notes the game's recent reviews were still "overwhelmingly negative" as of today. It also notes Valve requires store-page disclosure of generative AI content, and Repentance+ didn't have one.

## McMillen's response and the patch

McMillen posted on X on Nov. 21 that he "woke up to a shit show," adding: "obviously I had no idea it featured generated music in the daily run, looking into how that happened and getting it removed, stay tuned." Kotaku points out he had told Eurogamer the GTA tie-in was Nicalis's idea.

Nicalis then said it had pushed a new build with "critical fixes," per [Noisy Pixel](https://noisypixel.net/binding-of-isaac-repentance-plus-update-generated-music-files-larynx-car-battery-fix/):

- Removal of the generated music files
- A fix for the Larynx + Car Battery bug
- The PC patch is live, and the build is submitted to Microsoft, Nintendo and Sony

There's no date yet for the console patch, and Nicalis has not explained how the AI tracks got into the game.

**PPR take:** The fix was quick, I'll give them that. But "we removed it" isn't the same as "here's how it got there," and that second part is what Isaac players actually want to hear. This is a game that's lived on its handmade feel for over a decade, and McMillen has been loud about not liking AI in games, so finding Suno tags in the files of a marketing stunt is about the worst look possible. The part that bugs me most is the Steam disclosure. If nobody flagged it, either the process failed or nobody thought it mattered, and neither one is great. On PC, the run is still there to play, minus the Suno tracks. I'd just like Nicalis to own the explanation instead of letting McMillen take the heat for it.
