**I already put my Game of the Year nomination in.** The [2026 Steam Awards](https://store.steampowered.com/steamawards) opened today, and I didn't have to think about it. **Crimson Desert** is my pick.

Nominations are open now through **November 30 at 1:00 PM ET** (10 a.m. Pacific), [Valve said this afternoon](https://steamcommunity.com/ogg/593110/announcements/detail/685271070563893998). Final voting starts **December 17 at 1:00 PM ET**, during the Steam Winter Sale, and runs through **January 2 at 1:00 PM ET**, when the winners get announced right away, [per the official awards page](https://store.steampowered.com/steamawards).

## The 10th anniversary ballot

This year marks 10 years of the Steam Awards. The ballot has **14 categories**. Valve added three throwbacks inspired by earlier years: **Just Five More Minutes**, **Defies Description**, and **This Is My Job Now**. There's also a **Hall of Fame Award**, where previous Steam Game of the Year winners compete for the top game of the past 10 years.

The full list:

- Hall of Fame
- Game of the Year
- VR Game of the Year
- Labor of Love
- Best Handheld Game
- Just Five More Minutes
- Defies Description
- This Is My Job Now
- Better With Friends
- Outstanding Visual Style
- Best Game You Suck At
- Best Soundtrack
- Outstanding Story-Rich Game
- Sit Back and Relax

Any non-limited Steam account can nominate. You pick one game per category. Steam shows suggestions from games you've played, plus recommended titles, or you can search. You can put the same game in as many categories as you want, and you can change your picks later. For most categories, only games that released on Steam after last year's Autumn Sale are eligible. Labor of Love is the exception, for older games that are still getting support.

Nominate in at least one category and you earn level one of the 2026 Nomination Committee Badge. There are more tasks to level it up, and each level is worth 25 XP. If you're writing a review to help the badge, it has to be a new or updated review of a game you've already nominated.

## Pearl Abyss is asking for the GOTY nod

Pearl Abyss posted a [Steam Awards 2026 Nomination Guide](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3321460/view/694278269818635229) today at 1:00 PM ET for Crimson Desert Enhanced, asking players to put the game in for Game of the Year. They also point to Outstanding Visual Style and Best Soundtrack as other fits. The post thanks players for the [PC Game of the Year win at the 2026 Golden Joystick Awards](/news/crimson-desert-wins-pc-game-of-the-year-golden-joystick-awards-2026/), which was decided by votes from players around the world. The game also [took the grand prize at the 2026 Korea Game Awards](/news/crimson-desert-korea-game-awards-2026-grand-prize-eight-awards/) earlier this month.

## PPR take

I've got about 95 hours in Crimson Desert on PC, and I'm maybe a quarter of the way through the main story. That's why this ballot matters to me. The Steam Awards are a vote by people who actually played the games. That's a nicer setup than awards that hand out Game of the Year nominations before a game even comes out. And no, GTA 6 isn't on this one. It's not on Steam. It's not on PC at all.

Pearl Abyss is asking for the nomination, and I already gave them mine. If you've been in Pywel this year, you've got until November 30 at 1:00 PM ET to do the same.
