Less than two weeks after [RuneFest 2026](https://www.jagex.com/news/runefest-2026-jagex-unveils-fourth-mmo-in-the-runescape-franchise-plus-runescape-reignited-old-school-menaphos-and-more-at-biggest-runefest-ever) put **RuneScape Reignited** on the calendar for December 2, Jagex has killed the project.

In a [October 15 blog and video from Mod Ryan](https://secure.runescape.com/m=news/reignited-an-update-from-mod-ryan) (associate creative director Ryan Philpott), the studio says player feedback made the call clear: **"It is clear that we missed the mark on this, and so we are not moving forward with it."**

**Why it died:** Reignited was pitched as a fresh-start experience with its own character track, economy, and hiscores. A chunk of the existing community pushed back hard over a split world, limits on how new and veteran characters would interact, and extra membership friction. Jagex had already apologized and floated heavy revisions earlier in the week; after another round of feedback, it cancelled instead of shipping a half-fixed reset.

Ryan frames the choice against Jagex's history: "the last time we didn't listen to player feedback, combat changed forever. And it has taken us over a decade to recover from that." The goal is still growth with returners and new players — **"but never at the expense of our existing community."**

**What still happens December 2:**

- The **Adventure Guide** and the first part of the **Remastered Journey** still launch
- A **10-day free members trial** runs December 2–12 for new characters, or any character that has not been in membership since June 2, 2026
- **Old School RuneScape** members worlds are **not** included in that trial
- Jagex says the **2027 roadmap** (combat and skilling, a grandmaster quest, an Agility rework, Road to Restoration) remains on track

Plugins are also still due **Monday, October 19**, with more detail promised then.

**PPR take:** Scrapping a RuneFest headline this fast is messy PR, but it's better than forcing a community split. Watching whether December's onboarding push actually brings people back — without a separate Reignited silo — is the real test.
