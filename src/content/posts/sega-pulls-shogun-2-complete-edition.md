Two days after launch, **Total War: Shogun 2 – Complete Edition** is being yanked from sale.

In an [October 15 update on the Creative Assembly forums](https://community.creative-assembly.com/total-war/total-war/forums/37-total-war/threads/16254-an-update-on-shogun-2-complete-edition), CA and SEGA wrote that the remaster **"has fallen short of both your expectations and our own standards"** and that **"the current experience is not where it needs to be."**

**What they're doing:**

- Working with platform holders to **temporarily remove Complete Edition from sale**
- **Reinstating the original 2011 Shogun 2** while they investigate
- Confirming that **existing Complete Edition owners can still play** what they bought
- Working with their development partner to find causes, ship improvements, and meet Total War quality standards

**How we got here:** Complete Edition launched **October 13, 2026**, bundling the base game and DLC with promised upgrades like improved visuals, 1440p/4K UI scaling, and modernized controls. Steam reviews cratered to **Overwhelmingly Negative** (VGC cited about 19% positive from more than 2,000 reviews before the pull). Players called out lingering bugs and unit-card/artwork issues that look like sloppy AI upscaling — including hands that don't have the right number of fingers.

Steam also lists **Mataboo** alongside Creative Assembly as a developer; SEGA's statement does not name the partner in the forum post.

**Where it stands:** There's **no timeline** yet for when Complete Edition returns, or what "improvements" will include. Unconfirmed whether SEGA will address the AI-art allegations head-on beyond the general quality investigation.

**PPR take:** Delisting your own remaster in under 48 hours is rare and ugly — and the right move if the store page can't stand behind the product. Reinstating the 2011 build is a life raft for the series; the hard part is proving the "definitive" edition was more than a pass through an upscaler.
