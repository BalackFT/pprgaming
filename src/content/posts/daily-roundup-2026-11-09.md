Hello all! A couple more things from today that didn't get their own full write-up but are still worth your time.

<h2 id="star-citizen-galaxy-twitch-drops">Star Citizen's new Twitch Drops dress you for the Galaxy</h2>

The Galaxy's big debut is a few days out, and CIG is getting the outfit sorted first. The [@RobertsSpaceInd account](https://x.com/RobertsSpaceInd/status/2119897865417826341) announced a new set of Twitch Drops this afternoon that celebrates "the long-awaited arrival of the RSI Galaxy." It's a three-piece look earned by watching any Drops-enabled Star Citizen stream: **two hours** gets the undersuit, **four hours** adds the matching armor, and **six hours** finishes it with the backpack. The [This Week in Star Citizen Comm-Link](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/21352-This-Week-In-Star-Citizen) files it under IAE 2956, which opens in New Babbage on November 13. Usual drill per [RSI's Twitch Drops guide](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/42123336848919-Twitch-Drops): link your Twitch account to your RSI account, make sure the stream has the Drops Enabled tag, then claim each piece from your Twitch inventory. RSI says items can take up to 72 hours to show up on your account. Check the Drops page on Twitch for the exact window.

<h2 id="casualties-unknown-1-0">Casualties: Unknown leaves the demo behind with its full 1.0 release</h2>

Indie cave-crawler *Casualties: Unknown* launched its full version on [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4576490/Casualties_Unknown/) today. You play a disposable test subject sent down to recover lost cargo on an alien planet, and the game is unforgiving about wounds, infection and losing limbs. Developer Orsoniks says in its [launch post](https://steamdb.info/patchnotes/25545677/) that the full game has twice as many layers as the demo, 40 achievements, multiple characters, endings and a pile of quality-of-life fixes. The [1.0 patch notes](https://steampeaks.com/news/683018108282077545) fill that in: layers 6 through 11, two new playable characters, an endless mode, prosthetics and item customization, plus rebalanced guns and a less punishing amputation minigame. The free demo stays up but won't get new updates beyond a small text fix. It's launching with a 15% discount, and at the time of writing its Steam reviews sit at Overwhelmingly Positive.

**PPR take:** Free gear for watching streams is about the easiest win CIG can hand out, and tying a full matching set to the Galaxy is a fun way to build hype before people can actually fly her at the Expo. Six hours is a real ask, but with IAE streams about to be everywhere, most of us will clear that without trying. Casualties: Unknown is the opposite kind of story: a small team turning a demo that already had a loyal following into a complete game, and doubling the depth instead of just polishing what was there. If you like your survival games grim and a little funny, that one's worth a look.
