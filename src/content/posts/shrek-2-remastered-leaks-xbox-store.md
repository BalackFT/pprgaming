The worst-kept secret in remasters just got a little less secret. A **Microsoft Store page for Shrek 2 Remastered** went live early on October 16, [as Gematsu spotted](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/10/shrek-2-remastered-microsoft-store-page-appears), complete with screenshots, a feature list and a pre-order trailer, before it was taken down.

The listing names **Aspyr**, the studio behind recent classic re-releases, as the developer. A *Shrek 2* remaster first surfaced in a big Steam achievements leak in September, and Shrek briefly appears in the teaser for the **ID@Xbox Showcase on Tuesday, October 20**, so the official reveal is likely days away.

**What the store page promised:**

- **Rebuilt visuals** with better textures, an enhanced UI, 4K resolution and updated cutscenes
- A new **Photo Mode** and a **Cheat Mode** for unlocking cheats during a run
- **Up to four players** in couch co-op, online play, or a mix of both
- The same levels from Shrek's swamp to the castle of Far Far Away, with the option to **swap heroes at any time** to use their abilities

According to [TheGamer](https://www.thegamer.com/shrek-2-remastered-aspyr-xbox-official-leak-trailer-gta-6-parody/), the pre-order trailer opens with a **GTA VI parody** before revealing what it's actually for. [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/shrek-2-remaster-announcement-trailer-screenshots-2000746791) and [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/shrek-2-remaster-leaks-looks-amazing-and-i-need-it-now/) both note the page listed **no release date or price**, and other platforms haven't been confirmed yet.

**PPR take:** The 2004 original was a surprisingly solid co-op brawler, and adding online play fixes the one thing that kept it on the couch. Watch the ID@Xbox Showcase on Tuesday, October 20 at 1 p.m. ET for the date.
