**Exoprimal is going dark.** Capcom announced that its online dinosaur shooter will end service at **8:00 PM PDT on Wednesday, September 29, 2027** (11 PM ET), and the game will **no longer be playable** after that. The notice is on the official [Exoprimal information site](https://info.exoprimal.com/en/pages/important_261125_001/) and covers every version: PS5, PS4, Xbox Series X|S, Xbox One, Windows and Steam.

"We cannot thank you enough for the love and support you have shown over the years, and we sincerely apologize for the gravity of this announcement," Capcom wrote.

## The shutdown schedule

Capcom is winding things down in three steps:

- **November 24, 2026, 5 PM PST (8 PM ET):** Survival Pass Premium Tiers for Seasons 1 through 4 were pulled from stores. That step has already happened.
- **March 30, 2027, 6 PM PDT (9 PM ET):** the game, its DLC and everything else leave digital stores.
- **September 29, 2027, 8 PM PDT (11 PM ET):** servers go offline for good.

If you already own it, Capcom says you can **still re-download the game** after it's delisted, right up until the end of service. As [Game Informer](https://gameinformer.com/2026/11/25/exoprimal-capcoms-dinosaur-hero-shooter-will-go-offline-and-become-unplayable-next-year) points out, Exoprimal needs an internet connection to play, so once the servers close, that's the end of it.

[Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/11/exoprimal-to-end-service-on-september-29-2027) notes the game launched on July 14, 2023 at $59.99, with a $69.99 Digital Deluxe Edition. That gives it a little over four years of life by the time the lights go out.

**PPR take:** Credit where it's due: almost a year of notice, plus re-downloads after the delisting, is the decent way to do a shutdown. Plenty of live-service games get a few weeks' warning. Still, this is the part of online-only design that bugs me. People paid full price for a game that just stops existing on a date the publisher picks. Exoprimal had a genuinely weird, fun hook, fighting dinosaur hordes in exosuits, and it deserved a bigger crowd than it got. If there's a lesson here, it's that a $60 online-only game should have an offline or private-server plan before it launches, not a goodbye letter four years later. If you own it, make the most of the next ten months.
