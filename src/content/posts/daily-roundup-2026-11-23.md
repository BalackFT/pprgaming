Hello all! Monday's wrapping up, and here are a few more things worth a look before you call it a night.

<h2 id="iae-2956-finale-daily-deals-free-fly">IAE 2956 hits the finale with four days of daily deals</h2>

IAE is in its last week, and CIG put up the [IAE 2956 Finale](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/comm-link/transmission/21392-IAE-2956-Finale) today. Per [RSI on X](https://x.com/RobertsSpaceInd/status/2124926423529906441), bundles from earlier in the show are back so you can "complete the set," and there are **four days of daily deals**: a new Warbond Daily Deal and a stock-limited ship each day, each up for 24 hours starting at 16:00 UTC (11 AM ET). Free Fly is still on too. RSI says [more than 200 ships and ground vehicles](https://x.com/RobertsSpaceInd/status/2124896720353955964) can be tried for free from any show floor kiosk in New Babbage. The event and Free Fly run through 3:00 PM ET on Friday, Nov. 27.

<h2 id="plug-in-digital-acquires-twin-sails-interactive">Plug In Digital buys News Tower publisher Twin Sails</h2>

French distributor and publisher Plug In Digital has [acquired Twin Sails Interactive](https://gamesbeat.com/plug-in-digital-acquires-twin-sails-interactive-and-names-new-ceo/), the Paris indie publisher behind News Tower. The two now operate as Plug In Digital Group. Twin Sails CEO Nicolas Godement becomes Group CEO, and Plug In Digital founder Francis Ingrand moves to Group Chairman. Per [Quarterstack](https://quarterstack.online/industry-business/plug-digital-acquires-twin-sails-interactive/), Twin Sails stays a publishing brand next to Dear Villagers (Caravan SandWitch, Fabledom), alongside porting studio Pixel Ratio and developer Celsius Online. Godement says both companies are financially sound and that "the economics of publishing indie games have changed dramatically." The group plans to focus on a smaller number of games, including Ascenders: Beyond the Peak, Wind Runners and Sky Dust.

<h2 id="riot-games-acquires-mudstack">Riot picks up Mudstack, two months after it said it was winding down</h2>

Riot Games has [acquired Mudstack](https://www.verticalslice.news/riot-games-acquires-workflow-and-asset-management-platform-mudstack/), a digital asset and workflow management platform for game art teams, for an undisclosed fee. Riot says the deal is part of its goal to be the "best place to make the best games." The odd part is the timing. Mudstack told users a couple of months ago that it was shutting down, without saying why, and it's not clear whether Riot stepped in to save it or the shutdown was tied to the deal. Either way, the team is now at Riot, including founder and CEO Jordan Stevens, who becomes the studio's tools and pipeline technical art director.

**PPR take:** If you haven't jumped into Free Fly yet, this is the week. 200-plus ships for free is the best way to figure out what you actually like before spending a dime....and with daily deals running, that's exactly what I'd do first. The Plug In Digital deal is the kind of consolidation that at least sounds like it's for the right reasons, two healthy companies teaming up instead of one picking up the pieces. And Riot buying a tools team is a quiet one, but good pipeline tools are how great games get made.
