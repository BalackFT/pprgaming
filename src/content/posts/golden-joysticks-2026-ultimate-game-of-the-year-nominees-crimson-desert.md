**Crimson Desert is in the running for the big one.** The 44th Golden Joystick Awards revealed its **Ultimate Game of the Year** shortlist today, and Pearl Abyss's open-world action game made the cut, [GamesRadar+ reports](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/events-conferences/alix-wilton-regan-announced-as-golden-joystick-awards-host-as-ultimate-game-of-the-year-voting-goes-live/). That's a nice turnaround after fans were upset it [missed the Console Game of the Year list](/news/golden-joysticks-2026-voting-closes-crimson-desert/) earlier this month. It was already up for PC Game of the Year and Best Soundtrack, and [Inven Global notes](https://www.invenglobal.com/articles/27715/golden-joystick-awards-ultimate-goty-voting-begins-crimson-desert-nominated) it's the only Korean game on the shortlist.

**The 12 Ultimate Game of the Year nominees:**

- 007 First Light
- Big Walk
- CONTROL Resonant
- Crimson Desert
- Dispatch
- Forza Horizon 6
- Phantom Blade Zero
- Pokémon Pokopia
- PRAGMATA
- Resident Evil Requiem
- STAR WARS: Galactic Racer
- The Blood of Dawnwalker

**How to vote.** Voting is open now and closes at **8 PM ET on Friday, October 30** (midnight GMT), per [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/events-conferences/the-golden-joystick-awards-ultimate-game-of-the-year-voting-is-a-go-with-games-like-crimson-desert-the-blood-of-dawnwalker-and-big-walk-all-on-the-docket/). Voting in every other category has already closed. [TheGamer points out](https://www.thegamer.com/golden-joysticks-2026-game-of-the-year-nominees/) that 007 First Light and Big Walk lead the field with six nominations each.

**The show.** Actor Alix Wilton Regan, whose credits include Tomb Raider: Legacy of Atlantis, Cyberpunk 2077 and Dragon Age: Inquisition, will host the ceremony on **November 11** at the Troxy in London, streamed live on Twitch.

**PPR take:** Some fans are already grumbling that Phantom Blade Zero (out this week) and Star Wars: Galactic Racer made a fan-voted GOTY list before most people have played them. For Crimson Desert, a game that kept shipping big updates all year, this is a deserved nod, and its DLC drops on October 29, right before voting closes.
