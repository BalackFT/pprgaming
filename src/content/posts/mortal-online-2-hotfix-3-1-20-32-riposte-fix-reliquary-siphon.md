**First cleanup pass on the new combat.** Star Vault pushed **Hotfix 3.1.20.32** for **Mortal Online 2** in the early hours of Wednesday, October 28 (ET), per its [Steam announcement](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1170950/view/1847920302229934). It's the first fix-up since the [Era of Refinement Patch 1](/news/mortal-online-2-era-of-refinement-patch-1-live/) went live on October 21 with the parry and bandage reworks.

**Combat fix**

- Ripostes (attacks after parrying) were charging **longer than intended**. Star Vault says this was especially noticeable on heavier weapons after the last patch. Now fixed.

**Reliquary Siphon balance**

- A Reliquary Siphon that's turned on and not interrupted now drains a Reliquary Shield in **30 minutes**, down from 45.
- A siphon that's turned off and not interrupted now deteriorates in **20 minutes**.

**Other fixes**

- Pet bandages were ignoring the Corrupt debuff. Fixed.
- A UI issue with claiming account items. Fixed.
- A minor issue in the Sator Vessel boss room. Fixed.
- More analytics were added.

Full notes are also on [SteamDB](https://steamdb.info/patchnotes/26236003/).

**PPR take:** Quick turnaround on the riposte timing is good to see, since that's exactly the kind of thing the new parry system lives or dies on. Heavy-weapon folks, go see if it feels right again.
