[GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/the-biggest-new-game-releases-of-october-2026/) calls October 2026 "absolutely stacked," as publishers try to avoid the blast radius of **Grand Theft Auto VI**, which launches **November 19**.

## Already out
- **Gears of War: E-Day** — Oct 6 (PC, Xbox Series X|S)
- **Star Wars: Galactic Racer** — Oct 6 (PC, PS5, Xbox)
- **Dragon's Dogma 2: Dark Arisen** — Oct 9 (PC, PS5, Xbox, Switch 2)
- **Valor Mortis** and **Planet Zoo 2** — Oct 13

## Still to come
- **Final Fantasy Resonance** — Oct 22: the first HD-2D Final Fantasy (Square Enix). A Steam demo is available.
- **Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4** — Oct 23 (PC, PS5, Xbox, Switch 2), the first new CoD on a Nintendo console in years.
- **Minecraft (native Switch 2)** — Oct 27
- **Phantom Blade Zero** — Oct 29 (PS5, PC)
- **The Wolf Among Us Remastered** — Oct 29

> **Note:** PlayStation's "This Month on PlayStation" page lists Modern Warfare 4 as 10/15/26, while GameSpot, Nintendo and Metacritic list October 23. We're going with October 23 until Activision clarifies.
