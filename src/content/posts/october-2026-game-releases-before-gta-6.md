[GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/the-biggest-new-game-releases-of-october-2026/) calls October 2026 "absolutely stacked," and the reason is simple: publishers are trying to stay out of the blast radius of **Grand Theft Auto VI**, which launches **November 19**. That leaves a crowded few weeks for everyone else, so here's how the month shakes out from our halfway point.

## What's already out

The month opened with Xbox's big one. **Gears of War: E-Day** landed October 6 on Xbox Series X|S and PC, the series' first new game in seven years and a prequel about humanity's first encounter with the Locust. It's also on Game Pass from day one, per [Pixel Herald](https://pixelherald.com/october-2026-game-releases/). **Star Wars: Galactic Racer** arrived the same day, **Dragon's Dogma 2: Dark Arisen** added a new region on October 9, and **Valor Mortis**, a first-person soulslike from the Ghostrunner studio, launched October 13 at $40 alongside **Planet Zoo 2**. Today brings a new 2D Castlevania, [Belmont's Curse](/news/castlevania-belmonts-curse-launches/).

## The big three still to come

**Final Fantasy Resonance (October 22)** is the month's big RPG. It's an HD-2D game from Square Enix that GameSpot says revives a shut-down mobile game as a traditional RPG, with characters from across the series, from Final Fantasy VI's Terra to Final Fantasy XVI's Clive, joining as "Visions." A Steam demo is out now.

**Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 (October 23)** is this year's Call of Duty, coming to PC, PS5, Xbox and Switch 2. Pixel Herald notes it's the first new CoD on a Nintendo console since the Wii U era, and GameSpot points out it won't be on Game Pass at launch.

**Phantom Blade Zero (October 29)** closes the month on PS5 and PC. It's a fast, Chinese-mythology action RPG whose martial arts are overseen by Donnie Yen, according to GameSpot.

The last week also brings a native Switch 2 version of **Minecraft** on October 27 and **The Wolf Among Us Remastered** on October 29.

> **Note:** PlayStation's "This Month on PlayStation" page lists Modern Warfare 4 as 10/15/26, while GameSpot, Nintendo and Metacritic list October 23. We're going with October 23 until Activision clarifies.

**PPR take:** GTA VI is shaping the calendar without even being out yet, and players win from that, at least for October. If you're only buying one game this month, it really comes down to your platform: Gears on Xbox and PC (and it's on Game Pass), Phantom Blade Zero on PlayStation, or Castlevania if you want something you can play anywhere. Final Fantasy Resonance is the one I'd try first thanks to the free demo. Just remember that November 19 is coming fast, so don't build a backlog you'll abandon the second Vice City opens up.
