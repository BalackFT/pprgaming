**More Chaos to purge.** Saber Interactive and Focus Entertainment released the [Decimation Update](https://www.focus-entmt.com/en/news/warhammer-40000-space-marine-2s-decimation-update-is-out) for Warhammer 40,000: Space Marine 2 on Thursday. It's patch 16.0, it's free, and it's live now on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S and PC.

## What's new

- **Weekly Clash, a new PvP mode.** It puts special rules on top of Eternal War, like matches locked to a single class or a single weapon, or near-unlimited grenades. Focus says more modifiers are coming.
- **Chaos Siege Sector 3.** The Chaos Siege mode from last month's Anniversary Update gets its third sector, covering waves 11 to 15. The [patch notes](https://community.focus-entmt.com/focus-entertainment/space-marine-2/blogs/482-patch-notes-16-0) call it the final part of the "Hive" map, where loyalists try to stop a Daemon Host ritual in the hive city of Avarax.
- **A big Vanguard rework.** The Grapnel Launcher now works at close range, the perk tree has been reshuffled, and a new perk lets the grapnel apply Stun and a new Revenge effect. Hitting an enemy marked with Revenge also damages enemies nearby. The Vanguard also gets the Plasma Pistol as a secondary.
- **New Heroic gear.** Six Heroic weapons, including the Bolt Rifle, Heavy Bolter, Las Fusil and Omnissian Axe, plus four new helmets, all unlocked with Accolades.

Bulwark players can now put robes and tabards in their own Wargear slot and equip a cloak, and the Inferno Pistol gets a 30% base damage boost in PvE. Season Pass III owners also get two cosmetic DLCs today: the Deathwatch Champion Pack for the Vanguard, with an Occulus Bolt Carbine skin, and the Mortifactors Cosmetic Pack. The [full 16.0 notes](https://community.focus-entmt.com/focus-entertainment/space-marine-2/blogs/482-patch-notes-16-0) have all the balance numbers.

**PPR take:** Two years in and Saber is still feeding this game real content, not just cosmetics. Weekly Clash is a smart move for PvP. Eternal War can get stale running the same loadouts every match, and something as simple as "everybody gets one weapon" or a grenade free-for-all gives people a reason to queue up again. The Vanguard rework is the one I'd jump in to try. A grapnel you can use up close, with Revenge splashing damage onto the crowd, sounds like it fits how chaotic this game's horde fights get. Finishing out the Hive map in Chaos Siege is a nice bonus for the co-op crowd too. If you've had Space Marine 2 sitting in your library, a long holiday weekend is a pretty good time to dust it off.
