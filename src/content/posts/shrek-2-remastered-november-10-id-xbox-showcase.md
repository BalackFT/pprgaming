**The swamp is open for business.** Four days after [the Xbox Store leaked it](/news/shrek-2-remastered-leaks-xbox-store/), Aspyr made it official: **DreamWorks Shrek 2 Remastered** launches **November 10** on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch, Switch 2 and PC (Steam and Epic Games Store) for **$29.99**, [per Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/10/dreamworks-shrek-2-remastered-announced-for-ps5-xbox-series-switch-2-switch-and-pc). The reveal landed during today's **ID@Xbox x IGN Showcase**, and [Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/10/20/id-xbox-ign-showcase-october-2026/) says pre-orders are live on Xbox now, with up to 20% off for Game Pass members and 10% off for everyone else through November 9.

The feature list matches what the leaked store page promised: rebuilt textures and UI at up to 4K, remade cutscenes, a new **Photo Mode**, a **Cheat Mode**, and **up to four players** in couch co-op, online, or a mix of both. You can still swap between heroes at any time to use their abilities, from Shrek's swamp all the way to Far Far Away.

**The rest of the show.** Xbox packed [nearly 20 games](https://www.vgchartz.com/article/469413/idxbox-showcase-featured-nearly-20-games/) into the stream. The highlights:

- **The Wolf Among Us Remastered** got a new story trailer with the first look at Bigby's redesigned werewolf form. It launches digitally **October 29**, and [Telltale](https://telltale.com/news/wolf-remastered-launch-date/) lists PC, PS5, Switch and Xbox One (playable on Series X|S), with a new black-and-white **Noir Mode** and playable deleted scenes from the 2013 original.
- **CRITICAL REFLEX is coming to Xbox.** *Arctic Eggs* was the surprise launch and is out today on Xbox Series X|S. The space-freighter horror hit *Mouthwashing* joins Game Pass on **October 29**, and *No, I'm Not a Human* follows on Game Pass in 2027.
- ***ZERO PARADES: Director's Cut***, ZA/UM's spy RPG, arrives on Xbox Series X|S and PC on **November 3** with about 40,000 words of new story and full English voice-over.
- ***Scramble Knights Online***, an MMO-lite action RPG, launches **October 26** on Xbox and Game Pass.
- ***A Game About Digging a Hole Together*** was revealed as the co-op sequel to the beach-digging hit, out **December 4** on Game Pass.
- New reveals and dates for 2027 included the sci-fi survival adventure *Magenta Down* from the *Unto The End* team, the dark fantasy boss-climber *VANRAN*, the precision platformer *Croak* (March 2027, day one on Game Pass), *Mr. Magpie's Harmless Card Game* with Matt Mercer as its host, the Cold War vampire spy RPG *Red Kiss*, *Drill Keeper*, *Graytail*, *Rustown* (June 30, 2027), *Chronoscript: The Endless End*, *Love is a Roguelite*, *Mr. Records*, *Waveling Island* and *Paper Shredder*.

Xbox says many of the games will support **Xbox Play Anywhere** at launch, so one purchase covers both console and PC.

**PPR take:** Shrek 2 was the obvious headliner, but October 29 is the date we're circling. *The Wolf Among Us Remastered* and *Mouthwashing* on Game Pass land the same day, which makes it a good night for a dark story. We're also keeping an eye on *Magenta Down* and *VANRAN* for 2027.
