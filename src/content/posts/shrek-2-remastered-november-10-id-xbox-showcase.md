**The swamp is open for business.** Four days after [the Xbox Store leaked it](/news/shrek-2-remastered-leaks-xbox-store/), Aspyr made it official: **DreamWorks Shrek 2 Remastered** launches **November 10** on PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Nintendo Switch, Switch 2 and PC (Steam and Epic Games Store) for **$29.99**, [per Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/10/dreamworks-shrek-2-remastered-announced-for-ps5-xbox-series-switch-2-switch-and-pc). The reveal landed during today's **ID@Xbox x IGN Showcase**, and [Xbox Wire](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/10/20/id-xbox-ign-showcase-october-2026/) says pre-orders are live on Xbox now, with up to 20% off for Game Pass members and 10% off for everyone else through November 9.

## What's new in Shrek 2

The feature list matches what the leaked store page promised. Textures and UI were rebuilt at up to 4K, cutscenes were remade, and there's a new **Photo Mode** and a **Cheat Mode**. The big one for a party game is **up to four players** in couch co-op, online, or a mix of both. You can still swap between heroes at any time to use their abilities, from Shrek's swamp all the way to Far Far Away. At $29.99, it's priced as a nostalgia pick-up rather than a full-price release.

## The Wolf Among Us gets a date

**The Wolf Among Us Remastered** got a new story trailer with the first look at Bigby's redesigned werewolf form. It launches digitally on **October 29**, and [Telltale](https://telltale.com/news/wolf-remastered-launch-date/) lists PC, PS5, Switch and Xbox One (playable on Series X|S), with a new black-and-white **Noir Mode** and playable deleted scenes from the 2013 original.

## CRITICAL REFLEX comes to Xbox

Publisher CRITICAL REFLEX had a good show. *Arctic Eggs* was the surprise launch and is out today on Xbox Series X|S, and the space-freighter horror hit *Mouthwashing* joins Game Pass on **October 29**, the same day as The Wolf Among Us. *No, I'm Not a Human* follows on Game Pass in 2027.

## Everything else

Xbox packed [nearly 20 games](https://www.vgchartz.com/article/469413/idxbox-showcase-featured-nearly-20-games/) into the stream. ZA/UM's spy RPG ***ZERO PARADES: Director's Cut*** arrives on Xbox Series X|S and PC on **November 3** with about 40,000 words of new story. ***A Game About Digging a Hole Together***, the co-op sequel to the beach-digging hit, is out **December 4** on Game Pass, and the MMO-lite ***Scramble Knights Online*** launches **October 26**. For 2027, the reveals included *Magenta Down*, a sci-fi survival adventure from the *Unto The End* team, the boss-climbing action RPG *VANRAN*, the precision platformer *Croak* and the Cold War vampire spy RPG *Red Kiss*, among others. Xbox says many of the games will support **Xbox Play Anywhere** at launch, so one purchase covers both console and PC.

**PPR take:** Shrek 2 was the obvious headliner, and a four-player remaster at $29.99 is an easy sell for anyone who grew up with it. But October 29 is the date we're circling. *The Wolf Among Us Remastered* and *Mouthwashing* on Game Pass land the same day, which makes it a good night for a dark story. Indie showcases like this one are also where you find next year's surprise hit, so *Magenta Down* and *VANRAN* are going on our watch list for 2027.
