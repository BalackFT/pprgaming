**The Wasteland has come to the Settled Systems.** To mark Fallout Day on October 23, Bethesda [launched a limited-time bundle](https://x.com/StarfieldGame/status/2113705905069895968) called **Into the Wasteland**, packed with what the Starfield team called "some of our favorite Fallout-inspired Creations." It's in the Creations menu in-game now.

**What's in the bundle**

According to Bethesda's promo image, there are six Creations from three creators:

- **TGs Ranger Outfit/Spacesuit** by Tank_Girl444: a red-eyed, gas-masked armored Ranger look.
- **Vault-Tec Furniture** by Harra_: a curved Vault-style desk, chair and lockers.
- **Commonwealth Furniture Pack** by KaoruSatou: retro wooden dressers, a radio and a little teal TV.
- **Cosmic Diner** by KaoruSatou: a turquoise diner with checkered floors and purple Nuka-Cola Quantum booths.
- **DKS-501 Sniper Rifle** by Harra_.
- **10mm Pistol** by Harra_.

Harra [says the bundle is discounted](https://x.com/Harra__/status/2113627769649185226) and includes "some new workshop items, armor and weaponry." Bethesda hasn't said what it costs or how long it'll be available, so if you want it, don't wait. One fan asked whether the Creations are achievement-friendly, and Bethesda didn't answer.

**PPR take:** Starfield's outpost builders have wanted a proper Vault-Tec look for ages, and a Nuka-Cola diner on a barren moon is exactly the kind of crossover we're here for. Grab the bundle before it disappears.
