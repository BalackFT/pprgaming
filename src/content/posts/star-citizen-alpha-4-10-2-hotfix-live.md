**Good news if 4.10.2 has been crashing on you.** Star Citizen's live Persistent Universe is still on **Alpha 4.10.2**, the patch that went live on **October 9, 2026** and kicked off [RSI Discovery Month](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/transmission/21334-Alpha-4102-RSI-Discovery-Month), with special RSI installations across Stanton and Defense, Transport and Collection missions that feed four reward tracks in your mobiGlas. Since launch, CIG has been working through a couple of nasty crashes, and as of this week the fixes are in for everyone.

## The hotfix is now fully deployed

According to the [Cavalera daily digest for October 14](https://cavalera.news/editions/2026-10-14), hotfix build **12913470** is now live on both the server and client side. The server-side fix, which went out first, addressed a critical "hybrid" crash. The client-side fix targets a high-impact **HDR crash** and had still been in validation on the HOTFIX test channel the night before. With both promoted to Live, the HOTFIX channel was closed, which Cavalera reads as a sign that CIG considers both fixes stable. [SC Advisory](https://www.sc-advisory.space/) tracks the same build.

## Another server fix in canary

Overnight (Oct 14–15), a further server-side build, **DGS 12939222**, began canary testing on several live shards, per [Cavalera's October 15 edition](https://cavalera.news/editions/2026-10-15). Canary means it's running on a handful of servers first before going wider. It reportedly includes multiple server crash fixes and a repair for the **RSI Discovery Month event total score**, which wasn't adding up correctly. That matters because your total is one of the four reward tracks, and the [Discovery Month FAQ](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/transmission/21350-FAQ-RSI-Discovery-Month) says the rewards you unlock there are yours to keep.

> **Unconfirmed:** a CIG developer on the Evocati test thread reportedly said internal testing would pause for a day or two, with hopes of resuming on a new "Genesis" build by Friday. We're relaying this from a community digest and haven't seen it in an official Comm-Link.

## What to watch

Keep an eye on whether the 12939222 server fix rolls out beyond the canary shards, and whether your Discovery Month total starts lining up with what you've actually done. If you're on HDR and were crashing, the client fix should already be on your machine once you've patched.

**PPR take:** Hotfixes landing this fast after a patch is a good sign, and honestly that's the kind of thing CIG doesn't always get enough credit for. Crashes are the worst way to lose a session, especially mid-event, so getting both the hybrid crash and the HDR crash fixed inside a week is a win. The event score bug is the one I'd watch, though. If you've been grinding Discovery Month missions and your total looks off, it may not be you. Hang in there until the server fix reaches every shard, then check your progress in the mobiGlas Journal. o7
