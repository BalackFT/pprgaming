**Time to set sail.** Once Human's biggest free expansion yet, **Isles of Abyss**, went live with update 3.1.1 after maintenance that started at 7 PM ET on October 21, and it's on PC, PS5, Xbox and mobile with cross-play, per the [official Steam announcement](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2139460/allnews/).

**What's in it**, going by [PlayStation.Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/10/21/once-humans-isles-of-abyss-expansion-launches-on-ps5-tomorrow/) and the [3.1.1 patch notes](https://timesaver.gg/blog/once-human-patch-notes):

- **A new ocean scenario:** you wake on a starter island, then explore four more sea regions in any order. It's PvE and runs about 21 days per cycle, with a permanent server expected November 18.
- **Ships you build:** start on a raft and grow it into a mobile sea fortress in Build Mode. It works as both a vehicle and a movable base, and friends can sail together as a fleet.
- **Diving:** diving suits, oxygen tanks and a spear gun for sunken ruins, rare resources and deep-sea hunting.
- **Hazards and bosses:** four Haywire Zones (Mist of Deceit, Black Tide, Time Rain and Skyfire), new sea enemies, and the **Sunken Lord** as the endgame boss.
- **Also new:** an ocean PvP map called Isles of Abyss: RaidZone, a first-person over-the-shoulder aim mode, keyboard-and-mouse keybinds on PS5 and Xbox, and the Halloween Wonder Night event through November 11.

Log in before November 8 at 5 PM ET to grab the free launch rewards from your mailbox, including a Legendary weapon blueprint, 10,000 Starchrom and a 30-day Meta Pass trial. The download is about 26 GB on PC, 20 GB on Xbox and 12 GB on PS5. Day-one fixes are still rolling, and the devs say ship lag and rollback while moving on deck are being fixed urgently.

**PPR take:** A full ocean survival mode with ships you design yourself is a big swing for a free update, and the screenshots players are already posting look great. If you bounced off Once Human, this is a good time to look again.
