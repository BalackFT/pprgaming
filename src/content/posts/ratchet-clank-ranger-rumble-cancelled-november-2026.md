**Another PlayStation spin-off that never made it out of soft launch.** Sony Interactive Entertainment and developer **Oh BiBi** have [ceased development](https://www.rangerrumble.com/) on **Ratchet & Clank: Ranger Rumble**, the free-to-play mobile arena shooter built with Insomniac and PlayStation Studios. The [official notice](https://www.rangerrumble.com/) went up November 6. [IGN](https://www.ign.com/articles/ratchet-clank-ranger-rumble-shutting-down), [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/11/ratchet-clank-ranger-rumble-canceled), and [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/sonys-free-to-play-mobile-ratchet-clank-multiplayer-shooter-has-been-cancelled/) all carried the same timeline.

## The shutdown calendar

Per the official FAQ on [rangerrumble.com](https://www.rangerrumble.com/):

- **November 6, 2026** — final planned update rolled out on the App Store and Google Play
- **November 21, 2026** — delisted; no new downloads
- **May 1, 2027** — servers permanently shut down; the game stops loading and player data is wiped

Existing installs can keep playing until End of Service. Matchmaking and current modes stay up until then. There are **no more planned updates** after November 7. In-app purchases are already gone. The Super Pass is free for the remaining run, and Qwark Pass seasons continue until May 1 so players can burn currency and claim rewards. Refunds, if any, go through Apple or Google — not through a studio portal. Progress does not transfer to any other Ratchet & Clank title, and the notice says this shutdown does **not** affect other games in the series or PlayStation services.

## Soft launch, never global

Ranger Rumble was announced in November 2025 as a fast-paced multiplayer arena shooter with series characters and new Rangers. It soft-launched in the Philippines and Indonesia that month, then expanded through Nordic countries, France, Canada, and the UK through 2026, [VGC notes](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/sonys-free-to-play-mobile-ratchet-clank-multiplayer-shooter-has-been-cancelled/). As recently as July, PlayStation's blog still talked about gearing up for a global launch after a testing phase shaped by player feedback. That full release is off the table. Pre-registration rewards land in the November 7 update inbox for players who log in.

**PPR take:** Killing a licensed mobile spin-off before it ever goes worldwide is a rough look, especially when the studio was still selling a global launch story a few months ago. Soft-launch forever is a pattern we keep seeing: limited regions, live-service hooks, then a quiet delist when the numbers don't work. Credit to Oh BiBi for posting a clear calendar, killing IAPs immediately, and making the Super Pass free so remaining players aren't paying into a dead end. Still, anyone who spent money in soft launch is stuck chasing Apple or Google refunds, and May 1 is a hard wipe. Concord already made Sony look allergic to live-service risk; Ranger Rumble never even got a proper launch day, and that says plenty about how little runway these projects get once the soft-launch metrics disappoint.
