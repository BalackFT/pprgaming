Hey all! IAE week's almost here, but I've still got a few more things from today that didn't get their own write-up.

<h2 id="star-citizen-epoque-geist-monthly-bundle">Star Citizen's November bundle is the stealthy Epoque Geist set</h2>

Today's [Epoque Geist bundle](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/pledge/Gear/Epoque-Geist-Bundle-Warbond) is up in the Pledge Store for $25. It's a deep blue set with ornate gold filigree: the Kilgore and Poole Geist armor, a Gemini C54 SMG, and a matching paint for the RSI Meteor. RSI says the Geist armor is built for stealth, cutting your signature and damping your footsteps, with the compact, high-fire-rate C54 as the backup if you have to fight your way out of a bad spot. The store page is clear that the bundle lands in an upcoming patch, so don't expect it in your inventory the second you buy it. These monthly store bundles have been showing up on the second Tuesday since CIG [set that schedule](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/transmission/20943-Monthly-Store-Bundle-2026) earlier this year, and [This Week in Star Citizen](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/transmission/21445-This-Week-In-Star-Citizen) had today's drop on the calendar.

<h2 id="marathon-symbiosis-december-8">Bungie shows off Marathon's Symbiosis update, coming December 8</h2>

Bungie dropped a [gameplay trailer](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0PXGV5YuTWo) today for **Symbiosis**, and it's dated: the update goes live on **December 8** for everyone who already has Marathon. The [official rundown](https://press.bungie.com/First-look-Marathon-Symbiosis-gameplay-trailer-reveals-whats-coming-De) is a big one. Expedition is phase one of a new PvE-only mode you can run solo or with a crew, built around a three-run series with no PvP hanging over your loot. There's also a Deathmatch beta with short 3v3 Team Deathmatch fights that don't risk your permanent loadout, a new social space called Limbo with firing ranges and a dueling pit, a blade-wielding Runner shell named Bishop, and two new weapons, the V17 Flamethrower and the Maverick revolver. Perimeter is getting a fungal overhaul too, with new spaces like the Burrows and the Reservoir. Open Play Week runs December 8 through 15, so anyone can jump in and try the new stuff. [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/fps/bungie-drops-gameplay-trailer-for-marathons-co-op-pve-roguelite-mode-and-swanky-new-social-space/) has a good look at the trailer.

<h2 id="yugioh-tag-force-gx-gameplay-trailer">Yu-Gi-Oh! TAG FORCE GX gets its first gameplay trailer, and a February 2027 date</h2>

Konami rolled out the [debut gameplay trailer](https://youtu.be/ksdNAc-ahco) today for *Yu-Gi-Oh! TAG FORCE GX*, and the game now has a date: **February 16, 2027** on Steam, Nintendo Switch 2, and Nintendo Switch. The [official post](https://www.konami.com/games/eu/en/topics/19365/) says it's a remake of *Yu-Gi-Oh! GX TAG FORCE 3*, with modern visuals and more than 3,800 cards. You play a Duel Academy student, team up with GX characters for Tag Duels, wander the campus, and work through the school year toward graduation. It also introduces online multiplayer, with Tag Duels supporting up to four players. The Steam version includes a digital book, and the physical Switch editions come with three Yu-Gi-Oh! TCG cards, two of them making their first physical appearance about 20 years after they showed up in the anime: Yubel, Wicked Canon, and Hope for the Future. Pre-orders are already open, with platform-specific bonuses if you jump in early.

**PPR take:** A $25 stealth bundle isn't going to compete with IAE for anyone's attention this week, but the Epoque look is a nice quiet drop, and I'll probably be too busy flying rentals at Tobin to notice it in my hangar until after the Expo anyway. Marathon showing a real PvE mode with a date on it is the more interesting one for me....that game needed a way to play that isn't just getting your loot deleted by another crew. And Tag Force coming back with a trailer and a February date is a fun leftover for the card-game crowd. Three very different "here's a date, go look" stories, and all of them beat staring at another teaser with no timing attached.
