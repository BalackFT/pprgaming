**The free printable stuff Rockstar promised for launch day is finally live.** This morning we said the poster and Leonida map [weren't up yet](/news/gta-6-out-now-us-midnight-unlock-xbox-cloud-gaming-live/). They are now. Rockstar's [Grand Theft Auto VI downloads page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/downloads) lists a printable cover art poster and a printable map of Leonida. And since GTA 6 is [officially out on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S](https://x.com/RockstarGames/status/2123417684985495637), today is also the last day for the Vintage Vice City Pack.

## The poster and the map

Both are in the Artwork & Wallpapers section of the downloads page, each as its own zip. The page lists both printables at **19.25 x 26.33 inches**. The map file is about 45 MB and the cover art poster about 34 MB, and Rockstar's file server shows both were uploaded around 8:47 AM ET Thursday, after our early morning check.

They're free and there's no sign-in, so if you were waiting on a Leonida map for the wall, it's a download and a trip to a print shop now.

The same page has plenty of other official media too: the full wallpaper pack, screenshots, the Ultimate Edition benefits, and 12 screenshots of the Vintage Vice City Pack.

## Last day for the Vintage Vice City Pack

Take-Two's [pre-order announcement](https://taketwointeractivesoftwareinc.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi/) set the cutoff: all pre-orders and purchases **before November 20, 2026** include the pack. That makes Thursday, November 19, the last day. Rockstar's [GTA VI page](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI) still lists it as a "Limited-time bonus."

Per Rockstar, the pack includes:

- The two-tone '55 Vapid Stanier sedan, plus a garage
- Outfits and hairstyles for both Jason and Lucia
- A weapon pattern Rockstar says "echoes the excess of the past"

Take-Two's release says the pack comes with any purchase, not just one edition. The free month of GTA+ was a separate bonus for digital pre-orders only.

## PPR take

Two small launch-day things, and both actually matter to people. The map is the one I'd grab. Rockstar printables have always been a little bonus that people end up framing, and a 19 x 26 map of Leonida beats any blurry fan version going around this week.

The Vice City Pack is a simple heads-up. If you've been on the fence and know you'll get it eventually, buying before tomorrow gets you a car, a garage and some outfits that won't come with it after. That's the official cutoff in Take-Two's release, and we haven't seen anything from Rockstar about extending it. If you're not sure, that's fine too, it's a cosmetic bonus, not part of the game you'd miss.
