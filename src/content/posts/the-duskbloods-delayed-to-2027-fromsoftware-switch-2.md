**Not this year.** FromSoftware has pushed **The Duskbloods**, its Nintendo Switch 2 exclusive, from 2026 to **2027**. The studio [posted the news on X](https://x.com/fromsoftware_pr/status/2115805872190603677) on Thursday at 10 a.m. ET, and a [notice on the official site](https://www.theduskbloods.jp/article/261029_1.html) confirms the change. There's no specific date yet.

Here's FromSoftware's statement in English, as [quoted by Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/10/the-duskbloods-switch-2-release-has-been-delayed-to-2027):

> "While development of 'The Duskbloods' is at the final adjustment stage, we have made the decision to move the launch window to 2027. We apologise to players who have been kindly awaiting the release of this title and thank you for your patience and support. During the network test, we saw active interest in the core concepts of the game, including combat, strategic depth, and player expression. To further enhance these aspects, we have decided to move the launch, allowing time for final adjustments to game rules and balancing."

So the reason given is polish: the studio wants more time on **game rules and balancing** after what it saw in the network test. [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/10/the-duskbloods-delayed-to-2027) describes The Duskbloods as a player-versus-player-versus-environment multiplayer action game.

**PPR take:** With only two months left in 2026 and no date announced, this one wasn't a huge shock. For a multiplayer game that lives or dies on balance, I'd much rather FromSoftware take the extra time than ship it early.
