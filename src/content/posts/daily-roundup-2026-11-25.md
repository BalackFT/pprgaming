Hello all! The day before Thanksgiving still had a couple of things worth passing along, so here they are.

<h2 id="witcher-4-xbox-pc-day-one">CD Projekt says The Witcher 4 hits Xbox and PC the same day as PS5</h2>

Yesterday we covered [CD Projekt's new deals with Sony](/news/witcher-4-sony-marketing-deal-playstation-plus-cd-projekt-2028/), including PlayStation handling part of The Witcher 4's marketing. That had a lot of Xbox players worried, so joint CEO Michał Nowakowski cleared it up on X today. "The Witcher 4 and our other upcoming titles will remain multiplatform," he wrote, and when someone asked about a timed exclusive he answered, per [VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/no-one-gets-left-behind-cdpr-says-the-witcher-4-will-launch-on-xbox-day-one-despite-sony-agreement/): "All the platforms release at the same time – PS5, XB, and PC. No one gets left behind." So this is a marketing partnership, the same kind of setup Rockstar had with Sony for GTA 6. The game still has no release date beyond its 2028 window, and Nowakowski didn't mention Switch 2 either way.

<h2 id="battlefield-6-update-2-1-2-5">Battlefield 6 update 2.1.2.5 lands Thursday</h2>

EA has posted the notes for [Battlefield 6 Game Update 2.1.2.5](https://www.ea.com/games/battlefield/battlefield-6/news/battlefield-6-game-update-2-1-2-5), which rolls out on all platforms Thursday, November 26 at 09:00 UTC (4:00 AM ET). It's a small one. The main gameplay change is a shorter delay before you can fire your weapon after throwing a Supply Pouch, which matters if you're the one keeping your squad stocked mid-fight. EA also lists general crash fixes across Battlefield 6 and REDSEC, a fix for a flag that could stay floating on European Casino after its pole was destroyed, bots that can now climb ladders properly on Vegas Strip, and Supply Pouches showing their proper in-world icon in REDSEC. If you're getting some holiday matches in, expect a quick download first.

**PPR take:** Good on CDPR for answering that straight and fast. A marketing deal is one thing, but after the way Cyberpunk launched, nobody wants to hear their platform is getting the game late. "No one gets left behind" is the right line....now they just have to deliver it in 2028. Battlefield's patch is small, but the Supply Pouch change is the kind of feel fix players actually notice.
