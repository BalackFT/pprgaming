**Another one already.** Pearl Abyss has posted [Crimson Desert patch notes version 2.04.02](https://crimsondesert.pearlabyss.com/en-US/News/Notice/Detail?_boardNo=146), its second hotfix since [Charting the Unknown launched](/news/crimson-desert-charting-the-unknown-out-now-patch-2-04-00/) and a follow-up to the [2.04.00 hotfix](/news/crimson-desert-2-04-00-hotfix-ship-camera-shield-toss-known-issues/) from Thursday night. Most of it targets the new ocean content.

**Where it's out**

Per Pearl Abyss's notice, 2.04.02 is available now on Steam (PC and Mac) and PlayStation. The Xbox, Epic Games Store and Mac App Store versions are still in progress and will arrive later. The same notes are also up on the game's [Steam community hub](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3321460/).

**What's fixed**

- Your spouse or comrade following you into the water
- Your spouse repeatedly behaving abnormally during combat
- Pirates teleporting when they fall into the water
- Leviakan stopping in certain situations
- Sea serpents not attacking you
- NPCs not coming back out after going into the restroom
- Certain ships staying stationary
- Crew members not appearing on certain ships
- Rewards for certain quests and challenges not being granted
- Occasional crashes
- Earnings from management facilities increasing abnormally in certain situations
- Waterfalls displaying incorrectly
- Oongka turning invisible in certain situations
- Some interactions being unavailable while holding up your lantern underwater
- Your character catching fire and being unable to board a ship
- [AMD] Some graphics displaying incorrectly on driver version **26.10.1**

**Improved**

- Pioneering: when you enter an area that's available for pioneering, a notification now appears in the menu on the right so you can start the mission directly.

Pearl Abyss points players to its [known issues notice](https://crimsondesert.pearlabyss.com/en-US/News/Notice/Detail?_boardNo=68) for anything still outstanding.

**PPR take:** Two hotfixes in under a day says the ocean stuff got a real workout from players right away. The AMD 26.10.1 fix is the big one for PC folks who'd been told to roll back their driver, so it's worth grabbing before your next voyage.
