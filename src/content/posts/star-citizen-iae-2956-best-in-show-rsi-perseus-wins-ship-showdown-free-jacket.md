**Ummm, so the big gunship did it.** The **RSI Perseus** is the Ship Showdown 2956 champion. CIG named the winner on the [IAE 2956 Best in Show page](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/comm-link/transmission/21393-IAE-2956-Best-In-Show) over the weekend, and the Final Four that made it to the IAE spotlight were the **Perseus**, the **Drake Ironclad Assault**, the **Origin M80** and the **Drake Corsair**.

## What everyone gets

The winner comes with a freebie for the whole community. CIG says all backers get a **Perseus-themed leather jacket** to wear in the 'verse. Monday's [This Week in Star Citizen](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/comm-link/transmission/21459-This-Week-In-Star-Citizen) calls it a bomber jacket and says every player will receive it.

If one of the Final Four is already in your hangar, there's more. Owners get that ship's **Best in Show paint**, an in-game **challenge coin** and an **IAE 2956 Coin Display Case**, all free. CIG says those land **at the end of IAE**, so don't panic if your hangar looks the same today.

## Old winners come back too

The Best in Show page also brings back every past Best in Show paint for a limited time, from the 2949 Hammerhead and Caterpillar up through last year's Polaris, Asgard, Gladiator and Zeus Mk II. Prices on the page run from $5 for the 2952 C8 Pisces paint to $22 for the 2955 Polaris paint, and CIG says paint bundles save up to 35%. That's optional pledge store stuff, not anything you need to play.

IAE itself is in its [Finale](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/comm-link/transmission/21392-IAE-2956-Finale) stretch now. Free Fly, the rentals and Twitch Drops run until the doors close on **Friday, Nov 27 at 20:00 UTC (3:00 PM ET)**. TWiSC also lists a new **10 From the Community** on Friday, with the Design Team answering questions about the future of professions. Our [IAE 2956 FAQ](/news/star-citizen-iae-2956-faq-stock-limited-waves-prospector-price/) still has the rental and wave details.

**PPR take:** I'll be honest, I didn't have the Perseus winning it all....but I'm not mad about it. That thing is a flying wall of big guns, and the community clearly loves a heavy hitter. The free jacket is a nice touch since it doesn't matter what you own, everybody gets a piece of the win. The paints and coin are the real prize though, and it's a good reminder to check your hangar before Friday. If you've got an Ironclad Assault, M80 or Corsair sitting there, you're getting free stuff for ships you already have. BUT those old Best in Show paints coming back for a limited time is where the wallet gets nervous. Carrack 2952 paint for $19? I see you, CIG..lol. Either way, the clock runs out Friday at 3 PM ET.
