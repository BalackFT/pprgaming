**The critics gave GTA 6 a 99. Metacritic's users are a lot less sure.** When we checked this morning, the PS5 version's [user score on Metacritic](https://www.metacritic.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi/user-reviews/) was **5.6 out of 10**, "Mixed or average," from 84,742 ratings: 41,836 positive, 6,037 mixed and 36,869 negative. The Xbox Series X|S page is at 5.3 from about 6,100 ratings. The [Metascore](https://www.metacritic.com/game/grand-theft-auto-vi/) hasn't moved. It's still 99 from 158 critic reviews, up from the 115 it had when [the reviews dropped on Wednesday](/news/gta-6-reviews-score-metacritic-99-opencritic-98/).

## Why outlets are calling it review bombing

Anyone can rate a game on Metacritic without proving they own it. [Forbes' Paul Tassi](https://www.forbes.com/sites/paultassi/2026/11/21/grand-theft-auto-6-is-being-review-bombed-on-metacritic/) says he sees two main groups. One is Zelda fans trying to keep GTA 6 from beating Ocarina of Time's user score record. He points to lots of accounts with exactly two ratings, a 10 for Ocarina and a 0 for GTA 6, and says GTA fans have since fired back at Ocarina. The other is a loud crowd online calling the game a "life simulator" and complaining about how Jason treats Lucia. Tassi also lists reviews that are gibberish, AI text with the prompt left in, admissions from people who haven't played it, and some that are flat-out misogynist or racist.

[Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/gta-6-ps5-review-bombing-out-of-control-as-zelda-receives-revenge-ratings) says many of the zeros hit the same three notes: 30fps is "unplayable," the series has gone "woke," and the "story is horrible."

Ratings that need a purchase look nothing like that. Forbes counted **4.83 out of 5 on PlayStation and 4.9 on Xbox**, and Push Square had the PS Store at 4.84 from about 170,000 ratings.

## The complaints that hold up

Not every low score is fake. The most common real gripe, per Forbes, is the 30fps cap on console, which Rockstar has said won't change, even on PS5 Pro.

The other one is a bug. [IGN reports](https://in.ign.com/grand-theft-auto-vi/277670/gta-6-players-report-crashes-on-ps5-with-error-ce-108255-1) that some players on PS5 and PS5 Pro keep crashing with error **CE-108255-1**, and for a few of them it blocks story progress. [Sony's support page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/error-codes/ps5/ce-108255-1/) only says "an error occurred with the game/application" and suggests updating the game. IGN says most players aren't hitting it, and Rockstar hasn't announced a fix. One player told [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-crash-ps5-error-code-ce-108255-1/) that Rockstar's support pointed them to PlayStation's hardware team, so it isn't even clear yet whose problem it is.

I don't need an outlet telling me it's the greatest thing ever made, and I don't need a pile of zeros from accounts that never played it telling me it's "woke" either. Giving GTA a zero to protect a Zelda score record? I'm sorry? That's where we are now?🤦‍♂️ The buyer ratings are probably closer to the truth, and the 30fps cap is a fair knock if that matters to you. The crash is the one that actually matters.
