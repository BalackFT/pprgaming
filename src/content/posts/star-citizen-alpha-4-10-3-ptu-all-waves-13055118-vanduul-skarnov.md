**4.10.3 is open to everyone on the PTU now.** Cloud Imperium moved **Alpha 4.10.3** to **All Waves** on Friday. Wakapedia-CIG's [patch notes](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/190048/thread/star-citizen-alpha-4-10-3-ptu-patch-notes-2) went up at about 2:28 PM ET and also show on the [RSI Devtracker](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/community/devtracker). The game should now show **VERSION 4.10.3-PTU.13055118**. It's still the US-only PTU channel with long-term persistence on, and the test focus hasn't changed: stability, bug fixes, LTP between PTU releases, the Vanduul Incursion missions and the LBCO Skarnov rifle. If you're catching up, here's our [first Wave 1 story](/news/star-citizen-alpha-4-10-3-ptu-wave-1-vanduul-incursion-skarnov-rifle/) and the [follow-up on Wednesday's build](/news/star-citizen-alpha-4-10-3-ptu-13032516-skarnov-damage-chain-lightning-dev-notes/).

**Vanduul Incursion changes**

- You should now be able to quantum travel to **Shattered Blade Raid** locations.
- Contracts now correctly award their **blueprint rewards** on completion.
- Vanduul NPCs load their armor properly instead of showing up unclothed.
- Players can no longer stun or tractor beam the Vanduul.
- **Mauler health was reduced** to offset its earlier weapon damage increase.
- An AI pass across the incursion locations adds three distinct attack layers, custom corridor routing to spread defenders out, and reworked loot drops.
- Only one blue Vanduul cryopod now spawns per location.
- The Welcome To Nyx space combat music plays when you're fighting Vanduul.
- Eliminate Specific contract text was revised, and the Neutralize Boss objective is now called **Neutralize Target**.
- Another economy balance pass across contract missions.

**The Skarnov, per the official notes**

- Energy damage per shot: **10 to 30**
- Electron chain jump range: **3.8m to 7.5m**
- The damage falloff floor was set to 30 energy while base damage was only 10, so the minimum was higher than the base. It's now 20 against a base of 30, and the extra distortion and stun floors were removed.
- Rifle mass: **4.9 to 2.9**, and magazine mass: 1.12 to 1.05
- A slight recoil reduction on the rapid fire mode
- All ten of its firing report audio triggers were empty, so the rifle had no firing sound at all. They're all filled in now.
- The barrel attachment port was only taking ballistic barrels instead of energy ones. That's fixed.

Earlier in the week, designer ZacPreece_CIG had described the change as 27 to 30 and said a damage bug was involved. The official notes list it as 10 to 30 energy damage per shot. Either way, the target number is 30, and it's still a PTU build, so things can change before it goes live.

**Bug fixes worth knowing about**

All of these are listed as potential fixes:

- The Collector intro contract not showing in the contract manager, which blocked the Collector mission line
- Autoland not completing the "Land at LEO" objective in Part 3 of the New Player Experience
- A failed shop purchase timing out and then blocking every purchase after it until you changed servers
- mobiGlas landing services getting stuck on "Processing" or "Blocked"
- Lots of duplicate cargo showing up in the freight elevator after a Recover Cargo contract
- Hostile AI ships in bounty and mercenary contracts sometimes not shooting back. They now switch back to SCM mode once an interception finishes.

The build also has new 4.10.3 loading screen art.

**Still unknown:** CIG hasn't given a live date for 4.10.3.

**PPR take:** The shop purchase lockout and that Collector contract bug are both on the fix list, and that is big. And a Skarnov that actually makes noise when you pull the trigger? About time. If you've been waiting for your wave, go jump in and hunt some Vanduul. o7
