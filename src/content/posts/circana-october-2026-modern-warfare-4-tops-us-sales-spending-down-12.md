**Call of Duty still owns its launch month, even in a down October.** Circana's October 2026 US numbers came out Thursday morning, and Mat Piscatella's thread, quoted by [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/call-of-dutys-influence-is-fading-fast-as-modern-warfare-4-fails-to-make-us-top-10), puts **Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4** at No. 1. Piscatella says it's the "19th consecutive year" a Call of Duty release has been the best-selling game of its debut month, a streak that goes back to World at War in November 2008, per [VGChartz](https://www.vgchartz.com/article/469692/us-monthly-october-2026/).

The twist: Modern Warfare 4 isn't in the year-to-date top 10, and [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/call-of-dutys-influence-is-fading-fast-as-modern-warfare-4-fails-to-make-us-top-10) points out the current-gen Black Ops II port still is. Circana's chart covers October 4 to 31 and ranks physical and full-game digital dollar sales, so mobile and add-ons don't count.

<h2 id="the-chart">The rest of the chart</h2>

Microsoft took the top two spots, with **Gears of War: E-Day** at No. 2, and **Star Wars: Galactic Racer** debuted at No. 3. Final Fantasy Resonance, a Kingdom Hearts collection and Nintendo Switch Sports Resort also made the top 10 as new entries, per [Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/best-selling-games-in-the-u-s-for-october-2026-call-of-duty-final-fantasy-resonance-kingdom-hearts-and-nintendo-switch-sports-resort-in-the-top-10/). Remedy's Control Resonant landed at No. 8.

<h2 id="spending-and-hardware">Spending down, hardware dollars up</h2>

Total US spending fell **12%** from a year ago to about **$4.2 billion**, and the year is now 4% behind 2025 at $45.3 billion, according to [VGChartz](https://www.vgchartz.com/article/469692/us-monthly-october-2026/). Content spending dropped 14% to $3.71 billion. Hardware was the bright spot, up 2% to $357 million. PS5 dollar sales rose 20% and Xbox Series jumped 42%, but Piscatella said higher prices were "the primary driver," with Xbox Series units up 10% while its average selling price climbed 29%, as [Pure Xbox](https://www.purexbox.com/news/2026/11/gears-and-gta-6-may-have-provided-a-temporary-reprieve-xbox-hardware-sales-get-a-major-surge-in-the-us) reports. Switch 2 still led hardware in units and dollars for the month and the year, even though its dollar sales fell 11%.

**PPR take:** Number one for the 19th year in a row is still number one, BUT not cracking the year's top 10 is a strange look for Call of Duty. Part of that is just timing. It moved from November to October, and last October was a monster month, so the comparison was always going to be rough. Still, when a Black Ops II port is outselling your brand new Modern Warfare on the year, that tells you something about how crowded 2026 has been. The hardware "growth" deserves the scare quotes too. Dollars went up mostly because consoles cost more, not because a lot more people bought one. And November is going to look completely different with GTA 6 in the mix. That's the chart I really want to see....
