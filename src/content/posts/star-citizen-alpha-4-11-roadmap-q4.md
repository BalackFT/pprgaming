*Updated October 31, 2026: added the full list of Alpha 4.11 cards on the Release View and links to our later coverage.*

**With 4.10.2 live, it's time to look at 4.11.** The official **[RSI Release View](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/roadmap/release-view/1607-RSI-Skylark)** lists Alpha 4.11 as **"Tentative, Q4 2026."** CIG's latest [Roadmap Roundup](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/transmission/21353-Roadmap-Roundup-October-9-2026), published October 9, explains that the Release View shows planned features for the next three months of Persistent Universe updates. Cards can still move, and every 4.11 card is still marked **Tentative**, not Committed.

## The big one: Genesis and Nyx

Most of the 4.11 column is about planets. **Genesis: Planet Tech v5** is described as "a complete rework of how planets are built, populated, and rendered," with biomes driven by physical properties. **Genesis: Starchitect** is a procedural system that places locations across planets from modular tiles, with a **Starchitect Mission Pack** and **Starchitect POIs** to give us things to do down there. There's also a **Population Manager** for NPCs.

Then there's Nyx. **Nyx I** is a coreless planet that Genesis Terraforming has turned into a living ecosphere, **Nyx II** is a hot, high-pressure world where protective gear is recommended, and **Nyx III** is an ice giant. The October 9 Roundup added **DGS Planet Subdivision**, tech that splits planets and moons into smaller territories so one server is never responsible for a whole body. CIG says Nyx I will be the first planet to use it.

## Also on the card list

The column also lists the Heavy Combat Armor "Monolith," the Kastak Arms "Verdict" LMG and "Hellion" pistol, the ASD "Model-K2" DMR, the LBCO "Skarnov" DMR, a Wikelo Tiburon reward, and **Item Recovery: Ship Loadout Improvements**, which covers timed claims, optional expedite fees and freight elevator item claims. Since this story went up, CIG has said that recovery card needs more time and that 4.11 will refresh the freight elevator claim UI instead ([our story](/news/star-citizen-alpha-4-11-freight-elevator-claims-nvidia-621-vulkan-fix/)), and the Skarnov has already turned up for testing in the [4.10.3 PTU](/news/star-citizen-alpha-4-10-3-ptu-all-waves-13055118-vanduul-skarnov/).

> **Speculative:** community trackers ([Star Citizen Public Advisory](https://www.sc-advisory.space/)) expect 4.11 around **November, alongside IAE**. That's community expectation, not an official CIG date.

**Tip:** when a patch drop gets announced, the usual advice applies. Store your ships properly and hold off on in-game purchases until the patch lands.

**PPR take:** On paper, this is the patch I'm most excited about all year. Planet Tech v5 and Starchitect change what being on a planet even means in this game, and three new Nyx planets would make that system feel a whole lot bigger. But look.... it's ALL Tentative, and we've been around long enough to know what that word means on this roadmap. If even the Genesis tech and Nyx I make it in Q4, I'll be a happy backer. I'm watching each Roadmap Roundup for cards flipping to Committed, because that's the real tell.
