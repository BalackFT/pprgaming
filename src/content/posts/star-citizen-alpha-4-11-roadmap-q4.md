With Alpha 4.10.2 live, attention turns to **Alpha 4.11**. The official **RSI Release View** lists 4.11 as **"Tentative, Q4 2026."** CIG's latest Roadmap Roundup was published on October 9.

As CIG notes on the roadmap itself, the Release View shows planned features for the next three months of Persistent Universe updates; the cards represent high confidence in timing, but features can still move.

## What might be in it

> **Speculative:** community trackers (Star Citizen Public Advisory) say 4.11 may arrive around **November alongside IAE**, and expect **Genesis v5, Nyx I and new missions**. This is community expectation, not an official CIG date.

## Tip

When a patch drop is announced, the usual community advice applies: store your ships properly and hold off on in-game purchases until the patch lands.
