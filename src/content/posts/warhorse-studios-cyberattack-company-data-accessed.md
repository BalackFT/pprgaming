**Warhorse has been breached.** Warhorse Studios, the Czech team behind **Kingdom Come: Deliverance**, confirmed on October 27 that it was hit by a cyberattack. "The attack involved unauthorized access to company systems as well as access to company data," the studio said in a statement on its website, [GamesRadar+ reports](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/action-rpg/kingdom-come-deliverance-2-studio-hit-by-cyberattack-ceo-confirms-this-is-a-serious-incident-for-warhorse-studios-and-everyone-affected/). It's working with cybersecurity experts and the relevant authorities.

**What the CEO said.** "This is a serious incident for Warhorse Studios and everyone affected," CEO **Martin Frývaldský** said. He added that the first priority is to secure the studio's systems, find out how far the breach went and protect employees, partners and players.

**What we don't know yet.** Warhorse hasn't said what data was accessed, who was behind the attack or how they got in, and it won't do interviews until it has verified details, [GamesHub notes](https://www.gameshub.com/news/article/warhorse-studios-confirms-cyberattack-is-investigating-breach-2918336/). No leaks tied to the studio have shown up so far.

**Why it matters.** Warhorse is working on an open-world **Lord of the Rings** RPG and a new Kingdom Come game. [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/kingdom-come-lord-of-the-rings-studio-warhorse-hit-by-a-cyberattack/) points out that unreleased project files would be the obvious target, the kind of leak that hit Rockstar in the run-up to GTA 6, though player data or something else could also be involved.

**PPR take:** Rough news for one of the best RPG studios around. If you have a Warhorse account, keep an eye out for an official update, and be wary of anything claiming to be "leaked" footage until the studio says more.
