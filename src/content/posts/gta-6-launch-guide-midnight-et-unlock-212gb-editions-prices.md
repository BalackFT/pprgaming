**Grand Theft Auto VI unlocks at 12:00 AM Eastern on Thursday, November 19, and everybody in the US gets it at the same moment.** That's 9 PM Pacific on Wednesday. Rockstar is releasing it at midnight local time country by country, so a lot of the world is already playing. New Zealand got it at 6:00 AM ET this morning.

## When it unlocks

Rockstar's schedule is midnight in each country, on both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Converted to Eastern, via [IGN's rundown of Rockstar's list](https://in.ign.com/grand-theft-auto-vi/276047/gta-6-preload-times-and-install-size-confirmed-rockstar-announces-physical-cover-art-poster-and-map):

- New Zealand: 6:00 AM Wednesday
- Australia (Melbourne): 8:00 AM Wednesday
- Japan and South Korea: 10:00 AM Wednesday
- India: 1:30 PM Wednesday
- Central Europe: 6:00 PM Wednesday
- UK and Ireland: 7:00 PM Wednesday
- Brazil: 10:00 PM Wednesday
- US and Canada: 12:00 AM Thursday (9:00 PM PT Wednesday)
- Mexico: 1:00 AM Thursday

Xbox Cloud Gaming runs on its own clock. [Xbox Wire says](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/11/10/grand-theft-auto-6-comes-to-xbox-cloud-gaming-november-19/) streaming starts at 1:00 AM ET on November 19, on Xbox One and Series X|S, at Xbox Series S graphics settings. You still have to buy the game.

## Pre-load and size

Pre-loading has been open since November 10. Rockstar tells you to leave room for [up to 212GB, depending on your device](https://www.theverge.com/games/1025271/gta-vi-storage-space-rockstar-games). Both consoles have also had pre-launch updates, most recently [Version 01.002.001 on November 16](https://in.ign.com/grand-theft-auto-vi/277131/gta-6-gets-another-day-one-patch-and-its-1gb-bigger-on-xbox), so check that yours is current. If your pre-load is sitting at 100%, you're done.

## Editions and prices

- **Standard Edition:** $79.99. Digital, or boxed as a code-in-box copy with no disc, per [PlayStation Support](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/support/games/gta-vi-support/).
- **Ultimate Edition:** $99.99, digital only. It adds an exclusive collection of vehicles, weapons, apparel and more, per [Take-Two](https://taketwointeractivesoftwareinc.gcs-web.com/news-releases/news-release-details/rockstar-games-announces-pre-orders-grand-theft-auto-vi/).
- **Vintage Vice City Pack:** included with anything bought before November 20. Digital pre-orders also come with a free month of GTA+.
- On launch day Rockstar is putting a printable cover poster and a map of Leonida on its site as free downloads, per [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-preload-when-is-ps5-xbox-times-file-size-gb-poster-map-rockstar/).

## What it isn't

There's no PC version announced. Rockstar still describes GTA VI as a single-player experience, and it hasn't announced an online mode. GTA Online in GTA V keeps running as its own game. Reviews are out now, and they're huge: [99 on Metacritic and 98 on OpenCritic](/news/gta-6-reviews-score-metacritic-99-opencritic-98/).

## Before midnight

Check your free space, make sure the console is up to date, and sign in a little early. Sony and Microsoft both told people to pre-load early because of demand. If you care about spoilers, mute the game's name on your socials today, because New Zealand and Australia are already well into it.

**PPR take:** The part that stings is waiting while the rest of the world plays for the better part of a day, but a single midnight ET unlock for the whole US is at least clean. Pre-loading was the smart call from Rockstar, and I wouldn't wait until 11:55 PM to find out whether your install is actually finished. Free space is the one thing you can't fix at midnight.
