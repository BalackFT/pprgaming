**Eight days to go.** Pearl Abyss has posted a **[Features Overview](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/3321460/view/687519694976977072)** for **Crimson Desert Enhanced: Charting the Unknown**, the game's first paid expansion, with a [new video](https://youtu.be/k9WnZqkAFis) and, more usefully, a rundown of what you need to have done before each new feature opens up. The DLC arrives **October 29 at 6 p.m. ET** (3 p.m. PT, 22:00 UTC), or 7 a.m. October 30 in Korea and Japan. Pearl Abyss says more details will come in patch notes on release day.

**What's in it, per Pearl Abyss:**

- **Three new stories**, one each for Kliff, Oongka and Damiane, which start from the Journal menu **after you finish the main story**. A separate ocean-focused storyline unlocks after the main-story quest "Howling Hill."
- **Diving and the ocean:** all three characters can dive and explore underwater ruins, treasure chests, and new creatures and mounts. Diving has no requirements, but staying under uses breath, so gear like a diving suit helps.
- **Your own ship:** once your Greymane Camp is set up at Howling Hill, a questline lets you build a ship (by sending comrades on a ship-building dispatch at a port) or buy one from a Fleet Office manager, then sail the Great Ocean of Pywel.
- **Romance and festivals:** you can deepen bonds with some of Pywel's inhabitants, which "may eventually blossom into romance and even marriage." Friends may visit an upgraded Greymane Camp.
- **Upgraded housing:** build a house from the ground up, unlocked after the "Luke's Request" quest in the Grounds of the Sunrise faction line.
- **Greymane Castles:** three castles you can liberate and claim after the main story, then decorate inside and out.
- **Management:** rent and run businesses across Pywel, from a restaurant in Vellua to a smithy at Zargan Tankworks.
- **Garden of Trials:** a combat mode where you fight bosses from your adventure one after another, unlocked after the main questline, with special rewards for finishing.

**The fine print.** A lot of this is gated behind **finishing the main story**, so if you've been putting off the ending, now's the time. As we covered when [Pearl Abyss's Q3 outlook](/news/pearl-abyss-q3-profit-crimson-desert-dlc-dokev-2028/) came out, the PlayStation Store lists the DLC at $24.99.

**PPR take:** Sailing and diving were the headline, but the castles, businesses and a boss rush make Charting the Unknown look much more like a life-in-Pywel expansion than a quick add-on. The big question is whether the post-game gating keeps newer players out of the fun bits on day one. If you're still mid-campaign, you've got just over a week to see the credits.
