**Robert Robertson's first year on the job went pretty well.** AdHoc Studio says more than **five million players** have now clocked in to **Dispatch**, its episodic superhero comedy, and it marked the game's first anniversary on October 22 with a [big list of extras](https://adhocstudio.com/news/dispatch-one-year-anniversary).

**What's confirmed:**

- **Cast & Crew Commentary:** over **16 hours** of commentary across every episode, with separate Cast and Crew tracks. Aaron Paul, Laura Bailey, Travis Willingham and more join the writers, directors and designers. Different choices unlock different commentary, so a new playthrough can turn up new stories.
- **Storyboard Mode:** play every episode start to finish as the original storyboard animatics. You can switch it on mid-scene and run it with commentary at the same time.
- **Price:** both modes are **free for Digital Deluxe Edition owners**. Just update and pick them in Settings. Everyone else can upgrade to Deluxe for **$10.99**, which also adds the digital art book and four digital comics, and the upgrade is 20% off on Steam and Xbox right now. Switch players need to download a new DLC pack from the eShop first.
- **Mobile:** Dispatch launches on **iOS, Android and Google Play Games on PC on December 9, 2026**, rebuilt for touch and vertical play. The first four episodes are free and the full game is $19.99. It won't launch in China or Japan.
- **Live show:** "Dispatch: Night Shift" is a one-night, 21+ concert at AVALON Hollywood in Los Angeles on **December 3**. Composer Andrew Arcadi and the SDN Company Band will play the score, with THOT SQUAD, Bershy, Primer, Caroline Kingsbury and Fartbarf. Email subscribers get presale codes on October 28, and [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/gaming/news/dispatch-adhoc-5-million-players-live-concert-1236908475/) says presale is limited to two tickets per person.

There's more for collectors: a first look inside the physical Deluxe Edition for PS5, Xbox Series X and Switch 2, a "Design Works" making-of book planned for 2027, an anniversary merch line, and community giveaways running through November 22.

**PPR take:** Commentary tracks that change with your choices are a smart way to get people replaying a story game they've already finished. Five million players for AdHoc's first original IP is a huge result, and the $19.99 mobile version should push that number higher.
