**The biggest news out of the Worlds final wasn't only the trophy.** Bilibili Gaming beat Gen.G 3-1 at Barclays Center on Saturday, but in between games Riot dropped a two-minute teaser for **League of Legends: New Dawn**, an update it calls "all-encompassing" and "large-scale." It's coming in 2027, and Riot says the full reveal lands in January.

## What Riot actually confirmed

Not a lot of detail yet, but the outline is big. Per [GamesBeat](https://gamesbeat.com/at-worlds-esports-event-riot-games-unveils-league-of-legends-new-dawn/) and [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/league-of-legends-with-new-dawn-reveal-worlds-2026/), New Dawn covers:

- A brand-new game client. Polygon notes the last time Riot rebuilt that architecture was 2016.
- A refreshed Summoner's Rift, with updated map visuals.
- New looks for champions. The trailer showed Blitzcrank, Lux, Leona, Sett, Poppy, Volibear, Riven, Jhin and Ahri, with gameplay snippets of Master Yi and Morgana.
- "Evolved gameplay" and "new ways to experience" champions, plus changes to runes and pre-game systems.

At a press event before the final, executive producer Paul Bellezza told Polygon that Riot sees League as "multi-generational" and would love for it to run "100 years."

## What people spotted

[Dot Esports](https://dotesports.com/league-of-legends/news/lol-new-dawn-2027) went frame by frame through the reveal images. The Dragon Pit looks like it has two entrances instead of one, the Red Brambleback looks meaner, minions change expressions when they fight, and there's a rune-style option called Volt that doesn't exist in the current game. Riot hasn't explained any of that yet. There are also leaks going around that runes get swapped for champion-specific loadouts. That's not confirmed, so treat it as a rumor until January.

In the meantime, nothing changes. [Esports Now](https://esportnow.com/news/league-of-legends/league-of-legends-new-dawn-reveal/) notes patch 26.23 is still due November 18 with a normal round of balance changes, and next year's First Stand in China is set to be bigger than ever.

**PPR take:** This is Riot doing what a lot of long-running games are doing right now: rebuild the old house instead of moving out. World of Warcraft has Forever, and now League gets New Dawn. That's the right call for a 17-year-old game with a client people have joked about for years. A new client alone would be welcome. A refreshed Rift and rune changes are a much bigger swing, though, and that's where Riot has to be careful. League players know that map down to the bush, and they don't love surprises in muscle memory. The good news is we get a proper look in January, well before anything ships. I'd hold the hot takes until then. A two-minute trailer is mood, not patch notes.
