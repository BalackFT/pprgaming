**GTA 6 doesn't have an online mode, but it does have a monthly membership now.** Rockstar's [GTA+ page](https://www.rockstargames.com/gta-plus/benefits) has added a Grand Theft Auto VI section: "GTA+ Members can unlock a bonus sports car and garage, discounts, and other bonuses to use across Leonida in-game." The [PlayStation Store listing](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/product/UP1004-PPSA16755_00-GTAPLUS00001T01M) says the same thing. [Zack Shutt flagged it on X](https://x.com/ZackShutt/status/2123773425848062096) Friday morning, and the point he made is the one that matters: with no online mode yet, every one of these perks lands in story mode.

GTA+ costs **$7.99 a month** in the US (£6.99 or €7.99), per [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/grand-theft-auto/gtaplus-subscription-gta-6-exclusive-unlocks-benefits/). It used to be mostly about GTA Online, where members still get GTA$500,000 a month.

## What members get in GTA 6

[Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/gta-6-plus-membership-benefits-exclusive-car-garage/) and GamesRadar+ list the same set of perks:

- A **'90 Annis Venturas BLVD Elegy** with an exclusive widebody design, plus a **single-car garage in Crosstown** with a weapon locker and a spot to fence stolen goods. Both unlock after you reach Chapter 3.
- Free bike, Seashark and scooter rentals, free public transit, and premium tiers of the in-game RydeMe and Scooter Bros services.
- A **10% bonus** when you sell eligible owned vehicles.
- **10% off** at select weapon, clothing, hair, tattoo and vehicle mod shops around Leonida.

The catch is in the fine print. Polygon says the benefits only last while your membership is active, so if you cancel, the car, the garage and the rest go with it. GamesRadar+ adds that you need an internet connection to claim them.

## You can turn them off

If you'd rather play without them, Polygon says you can open the pause menu, go to the **Account** tab, find **GTA Plus Boosts** and switch them off. Pre-orders came with a free month of GTA+, and GamesRadar+ notes that it **auto-renews** until you cancel, so check your subscriptions if you claimed it.

Players aren't thrilled. Polygon points to a Reddit thread with thousands of upvotes, where people argue the car and garage belong in the base game and note that even players who bought the pricier editions still need GTA+ for these perks. Rockstar hasn't commented on the reaction.

All of this comes days after Take-Two said GTA 6 did [$3.79 billion in day-one retail sales](/news/gta-6-3-79-billion-day-one-sales-32-5-million-pre-release-take-two/).
