**So yeah, this one's about jobs.** CIG posted a new episode of [10 From the Community](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/comm-link/transmission/21466-10-From-The-Community) called Professional Pursuits, and it covers mining, medicine, bounty hunting, salvage, crafting and commerce. In the [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tho2qES1ZJ0), a system designer and an assistant design director work through ten (or eleven, as CIG puts it) backer questions. No dates anywhere, but plenty of direction.

## Medics get their own missions

The team admits medicine is different from most careers right now. Miners and haulers can go find work on their own, but medics mostly wait for someone else to get hurt. The plan is **NPC-driven medical missions** built around rescue and investigation, with some jobs needing specific tiers of medical bed. That gives dedicated medical ships something only they can do. Medical armor is also planned so you actually look the part.

## Bounty Hunting 1.5

**Bounty Hunting 1.5** is in active work as a step between today's missions and the bigger V2 concept, which is still planned. Contracts get phases: a setup where you dig up info on your target (talking to a bartender or another NPC, for example), the hunt itself, and then the kill. Bringing back proof of the job is being discussed, but the team says that isn't locked in yet.

## Trading, salvage and mining

- **Player auction houses** are in active development and treated as a priority. Trading starts in existing commerce areas, with some goods only sold at certain hubs, and player shops at your own base come later with base building.
- **Bricked ships** will be fixable through legal and illegal routes tied to reputation, including towing them to insurance locations.
- **Mining** gets biome-specific resources and better visual cues for good rocks with Planet Tech V5, and refining is set to let you upgrade lower-quality material.

The episode also covered a **player-to-player contract system** using beacons with a rating system, and more ground professions. With V5 planet tech, some resources will only be reachable on foot or by ground vehicle, with road and planetary defense improvements to match.

**PPR take:** Honestly, the medic answer is the one I've been waiting on. Medical ships have been glorified respawn points for way too long, and missions that need a certain bed tier finally give them a real job. Bounty Hunting 1.5 is a smart move too. Rather than waiting years on V2, they're making the hunt an actual hunt now....talk to the bartender, track the guy down, finish it. And player auction houses? The community's been trading on outside sites forever, so getting that in game is overdue. Just remember, zero dates on any of it. Treat this as a roadmap of intent, not a patch list..lol. 🚀
