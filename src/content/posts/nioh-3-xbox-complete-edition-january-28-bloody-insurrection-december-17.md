**Ten years in, Nioh is finally showing up on an Xbox.** KOEI TECMO and Team NINJA opened the series' 10th anniversary with [a press release](https://koei-tecmo-europe-pressroom.prezly.com/koei-tecmo-europe-kicks-off-the-10th-anniversary-celebrations-of-the-nioh-franchise) announcing **Nioh 3: Complete Edition** for **January 28, 2027** on PS5, Steam and, "as a first for the series," **Xbox Series X|S**. The standard digital version of the base game lands on Xbox the same day.

## What's in the Complete Edition

Per [Team NINJA's Complete Edition page](https://www.teamninja-studio.com/nioh3/us/ce/), it bundles the base game, DLC 1 **Hell Rising**, DLC 2 **Bloody Insurrection**, the 14-piece Infernal Weapons set and a handful of armor sets and netsuke charms that used to be pre-order or Deluxe bonuses. You have to play up to a certain point in the base game before the DLC opens up.

Digital pre-orders are open only on Xbox right now. The [Xbox Store listing](https://www.xbox.com/en-US/games/store/nioh-3-complete-edition/9p97zmgbtpds) shows **$59.99**, Xbox Play Anywhere, online co-op for two to three players and cross-platform play. KOEI TECMO says PS5 and Steam won't have digital pre-orders.

## Bloody Insurrection arrives first, December 17

Before all that, the second DLC drops **December 17** on PS5 and Steam. It sends Takechiyo to **Shimabara in 1638**, caught between rebel forces, alchemists, yokai and the shogunate army, and KOEI TECMO says it wraps up the story running through both DLCs. Tachibana Muneshige is back, and rebel leader Amakusa Shiro is one of the new faces.

The parts endgame players will care about:

- **New Martial Arts for every weapon type**, plus new Transformed Arts, Ninjutsu, Onmyo Magic and Summoning Seals.
- **Nioh's Journey**, a new top difficulty. You need both DLCs and a clear of Enlightened One's Journey (the third playthrough) to unlock it, and it drops **Ethereal** gear, the highest rarity.
- **Battlefield of Carnage**, an endgame mode of enemy-packed floors where beating each battleground's boss grants effects you can stack with your build, plus special Yokai Realm gear you can only upgrade there.

If you're on PS5 or PC and want in now, the standard edition is 50% off on the PlayStation Store through November 30 and on Steam through December 1, with the Season Pass 25% off.

**PPR take:** This is a big one for Xbox players who've watched the Souls-like crowd rave about Nioh for a decade. Getting the third game as a Complete Edition, with both DLCs and the bonus gear, for $59.99 and Play Anywhere on top, is about the best way a series can show up late to a party. Nioh is brutal though....if you're new, expect to get humbled early and often. For PS5 and PC players, the real news is Bloody Insurrection. Nioh's Journey and Ethereal drops are the kind of endgame chase that keeps a loot game alive for months, and Battlefield of Carnage sounds built for people who live in their builds. Watch that half-price sale too. If you were on the fence, buying the base game now and the Season Pass on sale could beat waiting for January.
