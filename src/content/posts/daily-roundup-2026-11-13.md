Happy Friday, all! IAE kept us busy today, but a few other things popped up that are worth a look before the weekend.

<h2 id="god-of-war-sons-of-sparta-ps4-november-16">God of War: Sons of Sparta comes to PS4 on November 16 with two new modes</h2>

The 2D God of War spin-off that launched on PS5 back in February is heading to PS4 this Monday, November 16, [per Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/11/god-of-war-sons-of-sparta-coming-to-ps4-on-november-16-alongside-free-content-update). Made by Mega Cat Studios with Santa Monica Studio, *Sons of Sparta* follows a young Kratos and his brother Deimos during their Spartan training as they search for a missing cadet. The PS4 release arrives alongside a free update with two new modes. **Hermes Mode** is built for speedrunners, with a split timer and a persistent scoreboard, and it unlocks after your first clear or with a very familiar-looking button code in the start menu. **Erebus Mode** is the brutal one: you and every enemy go down in a single hit. You pick a mode when you start a new save, and it can't be changed afterwards.

<h2 id="gta-vi-macca-and-friends-teaser">Rockstar teases Macca & Friends, a cartoon inside GTA VI</h2>

With six days to go before launch, Rockstar released a teaser for *Macca & Friends*, an animated series that exists inside the world of *Grand Theft Auto VI*, [Gematsu reports](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/11/grand-theft-auto-vi-in-game-animated-series-macca-friends-teaser-trailer). The show follows Macca the Gator and his wetland friends on their adventures across Leonida, the game's take on Florida. It's a small thing, but Rockstar's in-game TV has always been part of the fun, and a gator mascot is about as Florida as it gets. *GTA VI* launches on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

<h2 id="vindictus-defying-fate-let-the-righteous-judge-trailer">Vindictus: Defying Fate shows off its "Let the Righteous Judge" cinematic trailer</h2>

Yesterday we mentioned NEXON's promised cinematic trailer for *Vindictus: Defying Fate*, and it's out now. "Let the Righteous Judge" offers a deeper look at the story and the dark fantasy world players will explore, [per Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/11/vindictus-defying-fate-let-the-righteous-judge-cinematic-trailer). It's a medieval action RPG rooted in Celtic mythology, with a roster that includes series favorites Lann, Fiona, Karok and Delia, and huge Fomorian monsters to take down. The [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2814860/Vindictus_Defying_Fate/) lists single-player and online co-op. It's due on PS5 and PC in 2027, with no exact date yet.

**PPR take:** Erebus Mode is the kind of challenge that'll have people posting clears all weekend, and putting *Sons of Sparta* on PS4 gets it in front of a lot more players. The Macca teaser is pure Rockstar: a throwaway joke that tells you how much stuff is packed into that world. And Vindictus keeps looking good, though I'd like to see more gameplay than cinematics before 2027.
