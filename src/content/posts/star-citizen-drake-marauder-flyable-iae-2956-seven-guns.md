**Drake day is here, and the Marauder is in the air.** The Drake Interplanetary hall opened at IAE 2956 today (November 17–18), and the Marauder is now flight-ready in Alpha 4.10.3. RSI put up the [Marauder ship page](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/comm-link/transmission/21345-Drake-Marauder) ("Seven guns, one pilot, zero restraint."), plus a [new ships Q&A](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/comm-link/engineering/21448-Q-A-IAE-2956-New-Ships) with the Vehicle and Gameplay teams. Shark-CIG also opened the official **[4.10.3 LIVE Feedback] Drake Marauder** thread on the [Dev Tracker](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/community/devtracker).

## What the Marauder actually is

Back on November 11 all we had was "one of the most armed medium fighters on the market." Now we have numbers. Per the ship page, the Marauder is 20.5m long, 18.5m wide and 6.1m tall, and it carries **seven swappable Size 3 guns**, two Size 2 missile racks holding two Size 1 missiles each, and two Size 1 shields. The [Drake IAE page](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/comm-link/transmission/21407-IAE-2956-Drake-Interplanetary) lists it at a $150 ship value with a special upgrade offer, and like everything at the show you can fly it free through November 27.

## The trade-offs, straight from CIG

The Q&A is pretty honest about where the bill comes due. Drake says the Marauder keeps the brand's firepower-over-defense attitude, but it handles like a traditional "fighter" instead of Drake's usual "interceptor." It gives up some straight-line speed and forward thrust for agility, though boost still gets it to a fight ahead of many medium fighters. CIG says that gives it a different job than the Buccaneer.

Against the Sabre and Hornet Mk II crowd, the answer is blunt: lots of pilot-facing guns "at the cost of durability and average maneuverability." And if you load all seven slots with energy weapons, "more than 2x the Gladius's firepower" comes with a lower total max ammo count than ships with lighter loadouts. CIG adds its usual disclaimer that these answers reflect intentions at the time of writing and can change with feedback and balancing.

## A Liberator note in the same Q&A

The same Q&A has news for Liberator owners. Repair, rearm and refuel is still limited to the internal garages "to mitigate current exploits," but CIG says it's "fast-tracking a potential solution" to enable RRR on all of the Liberator's landing pads and garages. That's a plan, not a patch date. It also says it knows ships are sliding off the pads when they shouldn't and is looking into it.
