**Wuhu Island is back.** **Nintendo Switch Sports Resort** is out today, October 22, exclusively on **Switch 2**, at **$49.99** (£41.99), per [Nintendo Life](https://www.nintendolife.com/news/2026/10/round-up-the-reviews-are-in-for-nintendo-switch-sports-resort). It has 12 motion-controlled sports, with classics like tennis, bowling and boxing next to new ones like skateboarding and thumb wrestling, plus local play for up to four and online play.

**The reviews are good, not great.** It's sitting around **79 on Metacritic** and **79 on [OpenCritic](https://opencritic.com/game/20827/nintendo-switch-sports-resort)**, with 79% of critics recommending it. A few scores:

- **GameSpot:** 9/10
- **Nintendo Life:** 9/10, calling it "the Nintendo Sports series at its best" in [its review](https://www.nintendolife.com/reviews/nintendo-switch-2/nintendo-switch-sports-resort)
- **Shacknews** and **TheSixthAxis:** 8/10
- **Eurogamer** and **VGC:** 4/5
- **IGN:** 7/10, saying the quality of the sports "varies drastically"

**What critics liked:** As [Polygon's roundup](https://www.polygon.com/nintendo-switch-sports-resort-review-roundup/) notes, the 79 is a big jump over 2022's Nintendo Switch Sports (72) and right in line with Wii Sports Resort (80). Skateboarding is the standout. You lay a Joy-Con 2 flat in mouse mode, pull back to build speed, flick to jump and tilt to steer, and Eurogamer's Matt Wales said he loved it. Reviewers also liked the single-player **Island Crown** mode, where you climb a ladder of opponents, and the better character customization. The main complaints are that some sports are shallow once you get good, and online play has a few small annoyances.

**PPR take:** This is the party game Switch 2 owners were waiting for over the holidays. If you have people over, it's an easy pick. If you mostly play alone, Island Crown helps, but this is still a game you'll enjoy most with a couch full of friends.
