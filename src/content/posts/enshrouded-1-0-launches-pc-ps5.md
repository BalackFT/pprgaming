The fog lifts. **Keen Games** has taken **[Enshrouded](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1203620/Enshrouded/)** out of Steam Early Access: version **1.0 is live today, October 15**, on **PC and PlayStation 5**. The studio's own news page puts it simply — "[Enshrouded 1.0 is LIVE](https://enshrouded.com/en-US/news/enshrouded-10-is-live-check-out-our-highlights-video)" — and Steam now lists the full release date as Oct 15, 2026.

Enshrouded entered Early Access on **January 24, 2024**. If you already own the PC version, Keen Games says you **do not need to buy it again**. Early Access players also get three exclusive decorative base props via Redeem Add-Ons.

**What's in 1.0, per Keen Games:**

- The **full Embervale map**, including the new northern biome **Cinder's Cradle** — lava ruins below, floating Ancient islands above, linked by the Shroud
- **Two new Hollow Halls** (Albaneve Summits and Veilwater Basin), plus expanded edges of Springlands, Blackmire and Kindlewastes
- Player level cap raised to **50**, gear upgrades to **55**, and a new Flame level before you enter Cinder's Cradle
- A **conclusion to the current Embervale story** — Keen Games is clear the Shroud and the Fell aren't gone for good
- **Full voice acting** for lore notes and major survivors in English, German and Mandarin Chinese
- **Sparkworks**, a new logic system for player-built switches, traps, puzzles and shared adventures
- A new "brick renderer," HDR support, mage-focused combat additions, and a long list of QoL and performance fixes

**Price and platforms:** Steam and PS5 at **$39.99 / €39.99**, with a **10% launch discount for the first 14 days** (per Keen Games' press release). Xbox Series X|S is planned for **Spring 2027**.

One practical note from the [changelog](https://enshrouded.com/en-US/news/release-trailer-changelog-everything-you-need-to-wait-for-tomorrow): the **first time you load an existing save in 1.0, your skill tree resets** — spend the points again before you head out.

**PPR take:** Two-plus years of Early Access and the map finally opens all the way. Survival ARPGs that actually finish their story are rare; Keen Games shipping that on PC and a console debut the same day is a real milestone for Embervale.
