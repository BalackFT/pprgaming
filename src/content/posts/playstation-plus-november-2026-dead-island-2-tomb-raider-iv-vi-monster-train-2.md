**November's PS Plus lineup is set.** Sony has [announced](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/10/28/playstation-plus-monthly-games-for-november-dead-island-2-tomb-raider-iv-vi-remastered-monster-train-2/) the PlayStation Plus Monthly Games for November: **Dead Island 2**, **Tomb Raider IV-VI Remastered** and **Monster Train 2**. All three can be claimed from **Tuesday, November 3 through Monday, November 30**.

**The games:**
- **Dead Island 2 (PS5, PS4):** a zombie action RPG set in a ruined Los Angeles ("HELL-A"), with melee combat and online three-player co-op.
- **Tomb Raider IV-VI Remastered (PS5, PS4):** the remastered "Darkness Trilogy" of The Last Revelation, Chronicles and The Angel of Darkness. You can switch between the original polygon models and the remastered graphics at any time.
- **Monster Train 2 (PS5):** a single-player deckbuilding roguelite with five new clans and three vertical levels to defend.

**Don't forget October's games.** You have until **Monday, November 2** to add October's lineup, **F1 25**, **Hunt: Showdown 1896** and **Earth Defense Force: World Brothers 2**, to your library.

**PPR take:** Two big names and a roguelite to round it out. Dead Island 2 in co-op is perfect for a night with friends, and three classic Lara Croft adventures for the price of a sub you already pay for is a good deal.
