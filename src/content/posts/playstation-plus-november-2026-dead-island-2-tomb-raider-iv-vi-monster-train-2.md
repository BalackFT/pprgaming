**November's PS Plus lineup is set.** Sony has [announced](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/10/28/playstation-plus-monthly-games-for-november-dead-island-2-tomb-raider-iv-vi-remastered-monster-train-2/) the PlayStation Plus Monthly Games for November: **Dead Island 2**, **Tomb Raider IV-VI Remastered** and **Monster Train 2**. All three can be claimed from **Tuesday, November 3 through Monday, November 30**, and the lineup was also reported by [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/10/playstation-plus-monthly-games-lineup-for-november-2026-announced).

## Dead Island 2 (PS5, PS4)

The headliner is Dambuster Studios' zombie action RPG, set in a quarantined, zombie-filled Los Angeles that the game calls "HELL-A." It first came out in April 2023, [per publisher Deep Silver's launch announcement](https://press.plaion.com/DEAD-ISLAND-2-IS-OUT-NOW), and it's built around brutal melee combat, with a big pile of weapons and mods to craft. You can play it solo or in **online three-player co-op**, which makes it the pick of the month if you have friends who are also subscribers.

## Tomb Raider IV-VI Remastered (PS5, PS4)

This is Aspyr's remaster of the "Darkness Trilogy": *The Last Revelation*, *Chronicles* and *The Angel of Darkness*. It launched in February 2025, [according to PlayStation's store page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/tomb-raider-iv-vi-remastered/). The standout feature is that you can switch between the original polygon models and the remastered graphics at any time, which is a fun way to see how far the series has come.

## Monster Train 2 (PS5)

The third game is Shiny Shoe's single-player deckbuilding roguelite, which launched in May 2025 [under Devolver Digital's Big Fan label](https://www.gamespress.com/All-Aboard-Monster-Train-2-Departs-for-May-Release-Date-on-PC-Consoles). This time, angels and demons team up against the Titans, who have taken over Heaven. Sony's post highlights **five new clans** and the series' three vertical levels to defend, and the game also has an Endless Mode and daily challenges, so it can stay installed for a long time.

## Grab October's games first

You have until **Monday, November 2** to add October's lineup, **F1 25**, **Hunt: Showdown 1896** and **Earth Defense Force: World Brothers 2**, to your library.

**PPR take:** Two big names and a roguelite to round it out is a solid month. Dead Island 2 in co-op is perfect for a night with friends, and three classic Lara Croft adventures for the price of a sub you're already paying for is a good deal, especially with the old-graphics toggle for the nostalgia. Monster Train 2 is the sleeper here. If you've never tried a deckbuilder, this is a low-risk way to find out whether the genre clicks for you. Just don't forget to claim October's games before Monday.
