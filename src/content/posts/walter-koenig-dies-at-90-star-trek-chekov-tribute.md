**Walter Koenig has died.** The actor who gave us Ensign Pavel Chekov, the eager young Russian navigator on the bridge of the USS Enterprise, died Sunday night, October 25, of old age at his home in North Hollywood, with his daughter and niece beside him. He was 90 ([Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/news/walter-koenig-dead-chekov-star-trek-1236931312/), [AP](https://apnews.com/article/walter-koenig-dies-ad6aed51fc41417a8812bffccec1393c), [BBC](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/cv0ldjzg9xppo)).

"Walter helped inspire audiences with his optimism, humor, and adventurous spirit," his representatives said in a statement. "He understood what Star Trek meant to people and embraced the community that grew around it... Thank you, Walter, for the memories, the inspiration, and for boldly going where few have gone before" ([Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/news/walter-koenig-dead-chekov-star-trek-1236931312/), [LA Times](https://www.latimes.com/entertainment-arts/story/2026-10-26/walter-koenig-star-trek-original-chekov-dies-at-90)).

**"Keep navigating a steady course."** George Takei, who sat beside him at the helm as Sulu, said goodbye on Instagram. The two met on the Enterprise set in the 1960s and stayed friends for nearly six decades, and Koenig was Takei's best man in 2008. "Walter was more than Chekov," Takei wrote. "He was a writer, a teacher, a thoughtful man, and a loyal friend. To the fans who loved him for so many years, thank you. He loved you too. Rest well, my dear friend. Keep navigating a steady course" ([Variety](https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/walter-koenig-tributes-george-takei-1236931414/)).

**A life on the bridge, and well beyond it.**

- **Chekov.** Koenig joined *Star Trek* in its second season in 1967, a Russian officer on the bridge at the height of the Cold War ([Gold Derby](https://www.goldderby.com/film/2026/walter-koenig-star-trek-chekov-obit-george-takei-tribute/)). He carried the role through seven feature films, with a big part in *Star Trek II: The Wrath of Khan* ([Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/news/walter-koenig-dead-chekov-star-trek-1236931312/)). He even came back for a voice cameo in *Star Trek: Picard* in 2023 ([Gold Derby](https://www.goldderby.com/film/2026/walter-koenig-star-trek-chekov-obit-george-takei-tribute/)).
- **Bester.** A generation of sci-fi fans also knows him as Alfred Bester, *Babylon 5*'s chilling Psi Cop, a role he played in 12 episodes from 1994 to 1998 ([Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/news/walter-koenig-dead-chekov-star-trek-1236931312/)).
- **The writer.** When Chekov was left out of *The Animated Series*, Koenig wrote one of its episodes, "The Infinite Vulcan." He also wrote the memoirs *Chekov's Enterprise* and *Warped Factors*, the novel *Buck Alice and the Actor-Robot*, and the comic *Raver*. On top of that, he taught acting and directed for the stage ([Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/news/walter-koenig-dead-chekov-star-trek-1236931312/)).
- **The gamer connection.** Koenig voiced Chekov in several *Star Trek* video games, including 1998's *Starfleet Academy: Chekov's Lost Missions* ([Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/news/walter-koenig-dead-chekov-star-trek-1236931312/)). Plenty of us first heard that accent through a PC speaker.

He never stopped working. This year his performance in the short film *Mr. Fleischmann* won him the Platinum Award for Best Actor at the 2026 Independent Shorts Awards. He is survived by his daughter, Danielle. His son Andrew died in 2010 ([Variety](https://variety.com/2026/film/news/walter-koenig-dead-chekov-star-trek-1236931312/)).

**PPR take:** Star Trek promised that the future would have room for everybody, and Walter Koenig's Chekov was part of that promise: a young Russian kid at the navigation console, sure of himself and endlessly proud, at a time when that meant something. He spent the rest of his life at conventions shaking hands with the fans that promise helped raise. Our thoughts are with his family, his friends and the whole Trek community tonight. Rest easy, Walter. He'll always be our Chekov.
