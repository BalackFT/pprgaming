**December's PlayStation Plus lineup is here, and it's a skater, a brawler and a scare.** Sony's Adam Michel announced on the [PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/2026/11/25/playstation-plus-monthly-games-for-december-tony-hawks-pro-skater-34-dragon-ball-xenoverse-2-amnesia-the-bunker/) Wednesday that **Tony Hawk's Pro Skater 3 + 4**, **Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2** and **Amnesia: The Bunker** will be available to all PlayStation Plus members from **Tuesday, December 1 through Monday, January 4**.

## The three games

**Tony Hawk's Pro Skater 3 + 4 (PS5, PS4)** is the Cross-Gen Edition of Activision's remake of the two classics, with parks from both games plus new ones, the two-minute run format, and expanded Create-A-Skater and Create-A-Park modes. The blog says you can make custom goals to share with friends for the first time, and the PS5 version runs at native 4K and 60fps in Fidelity Mode. It's listed at $49.99 on the [PlayStation Store](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/product/UP0002-PPSA18010_00-TH34RSTNDBUNDL01/), with cross-platform online play for up to eight skaters.

**Dragon Ball Xenoverse 2 (PS5, PS4)** has you create your own fighter, explore Conton City and team up with characters from the series, with online battles and Raid Battles against giant bosses.

**Amnesia: The Bunker (PS4)** is first-person horror from the makers of Soma. You play French soldier Henri Clément, stuck in a World War I bunker with a revolver, one bullet and a noisy dynamo flashlight, while something hunts you by sound. Sony notes that randomization means no two runs play the same.

## Grab November's games first

If you haven't added November's games yet, you have until **Monday, November 30**. That's Dead Island 2, Tomb Raider IV-VI Remastered and Monster Train 2, which we [covered last month](/news/playstation-plus-november-2026-dead-island-2-tomb-raider-iv-vi-monster-train-2/). Sony also says lineups can differ by region.

**PPR take:** Tony Hawk is the headliner here, no question. THPS 3 and 4 were peak couch-co-op days for a lot of us, and getting the remake as part of the sub is a nice holiday bonus, especially when the store still has it at fifty bucks. Xenoverse 2 is an older game at this point, but it's the kind of thing you can sink a ton of hours into if you missed it. And Amnesia: The Bunker is a great pick for anyone who likes being scared out of their chair....even if it's only the PS4 version. Not a bad month at all. Just don't forget to add the November games before Monday, because once they're gone, they're gone.
