Nintendo is not done showing off **[The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time](https://www.nintendo.com/us/store/products/the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time-switch-2/)** for Switch 2. Two new trailers landed today, and [as Polygon spotted](https://www.polygon.com/legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time-new-trailers-dark-link/), one of them gives a quick look at **Dark Link** around the 15-second mark, flipping onto Link's sword the way fans of the Water Temple will remember.

That trailer also shows Ganon at the piano, Link aboard a pirate ship, and a run of dungeon fights. The second trailer, posted to Nintendo's Japanese YouTube channel, focuses on the ocarina, the Master Sword and exploring Hyrule.

**What we know from Nintendo's [five-minute overview trailer](https://www.nintendo.com/us/whatsnew/you-have-been-summoned-to-watch-the-new-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time-overview-trailer/) on October 14:**

- The game is "completely reborn" for Switch 2, with **overhauled visuals**, **fully voiced cutscenes with expanded dialogue**, and **full symphonic orchestral music**
- You can **hum or play a song for real**, and the Switch 2 microphone will make Link's ocarina respond in game
- A new hall of statues lets you **read up on monsters you've beaten and rematch them**; Nintendo's page calls it the Hall of Fallen Heroes, while [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/10/the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time-remake-overview-trailer) and other coverage of the trailer call it the Hall of Fallen Foes
- [GameObserver reports](https://gameobserver.com/the-legend-of-zelda-ocarina-of-time-remake-shows-new-features-in-its-overview-trailer/) two new **young Link and young Zelda amiibo** arrive in 2027 and unlock special in-game masks, and existing Zelda amiibo give rewards too

Hands-on previews went up this week as well, and Polygon's writeup called the remake "snappy, streamlined, and polished in ways that make it feel totally fresh."

**Release:** November 5, 2026, exclusively on **Nintendo Switch 2**.

**PPR take:** Ocarina is the game a lot of us measure every other 3D adventure against, so Nintendo has almost no room for error here. Teasing Dark Link this late suggests they're holding a few surprises back for launch day, and with GTA VI landing two weeks later, November is shaping up to be brutal for anyone's free time.
