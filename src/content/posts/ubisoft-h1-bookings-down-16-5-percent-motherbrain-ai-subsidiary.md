**A day after confirming 166 layoffs in Canada, Ubisoft's half-year numbers are out, and they're rough.** In its [first-half 2026-27 earnings release](http://staticctf.ubisoft.com/8aefmxkxpxwl/4rjH83tyAju38erlDGV0sm/5f6d973ac0db88ac2bef4d21fe1a4835/Ubisoft_FY27_H1_Earnings_PR_EN_vF.pdf), the publisher reported net bookings of **€644.7 million** for April through September, down 16.5% from €772.4 million a year ago. The second quarter alone came in at €388.9 million, down about 21%, though that was €19 million above Ubisoft's own guidance.

## The money

The adjusted operating result swung from a €27.1 million profit to a **€155.9 million loss**, [Game Developer reports](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/ubisoft-misses-forecast-and-bets-big-on-ai-development), and operating cash flow was negative by €323.9 million for the half. Looking ahead, Ubisoft now expects around €250 million in third-quarter net bookings, blaming a "particularly crowded holiday season." Game Developer says the shares dropped about 11% on Friday, leaving the stock down roughly 42% for 2026.

There were bright spots. Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced beat expectations, helped by a lot of players buying premium editions, and Rainbow Six Siege has "renewed momentum," CEO Yves Guillemot said on the [earnings call](https://stockanalysis.com/quote/epa/UBI/transcripts/714657-q2-26-27/). Ubisoft also said that excluding partnership deals, its bookings were up double digits for the half.

## Meet Motherbrain

The bigger long-term headline is AI. Ubisoft has turned the team behind Teammates, its first playable generative AI experiment, into a dedicated subsidiary called **Motherbrain**. Per the release, it's building in-house tech for "smart NPCs with memory" and "AI game masters that manage large-scale systemic worlds," and it wants to serve creators "both within Ubisoft's Creative House ecosystem and beyond," including partnerships with outside tech companies, [Gaming.net notes](https://www.gaming.net/ubisoft-establishes-motherbrain-as-dedicated-gameplay-ai-subsidiary/).

The reorganization around Creative Houses is now fully in place too. Creative House 2, home to The Division, Ghost Recon and Splinter Cell, is now named Massive Entertainment, Houses 3 and 5 were merged under Julien Barès, and a separate unit will try to get value out of brands that don't belong to any house. Ubisoft says it's down close to 1,100 employees over the past six months, and Game Developer points out that the announced slate leans heavily on remakes from the Rayman and Heroes of Might & Magic series.

## PPR take

Ubisoft just had its best launch in a while with Black Flag Resynced, and the stock still dropped 11%. That tells you how much trust is gone. Then on the same day it cuts 166 jobs in Canada, it gives the AI team its own company. And named it "Motherbrain." The Metroid final boss. You can't make this up..lol. To be fair, NPCs that actually remember you could be cool in the right game. BUT "AI game masters" running whole worlds sounds a lot like something you'd use instead of designers, not to help them. Ubisoft says it's about empowering creators. We'll see what actually ships. Right now the plan seems to be fewer people, more AI, and a lot of remakes....and I'm not sure that's the comeback fans were hoping for.
