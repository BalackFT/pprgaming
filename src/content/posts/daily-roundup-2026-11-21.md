<h2 id="deltarune-chapter-6-structurally-complete">Deltarune Chapter 6 is playable start to finish, and Chapter 7 work has begun</h2>

Toby Fox's [Fall 2026 newsletter](https://toby.fangamer.com/newsletters/fall26/) says Deltarune Chapter 6 is now "a structurally complete draft that can be played from the beginning to the end." Every map, event and battle is in, with one optional event still dragging, per [PC Gamer's write-up](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/rpg/deltarune-chapter-6-is-structurally-complete-while-development-has-begun-on-chapter-7-according-to-toby-fox/). Localization and early testing are starting, but Fox says some scenes are "still undercooked" and "I won't be able to rush this." Chapter 7, the last one, has "only just started." Fox read its story aloud to the team and stopped right before a critical moment. In his [summer newsletter](https://toby.fangamer.com/newsletters/summer26/), Fox said he believes Chapter 6 can release in 2027, on its own. The fall letter doesn't add a date, and the next update comes when localization is done.

<h2 id="darkest-dungeon-ii-butchers-asylum">Darkest Dungeon II's Butcher's Asylum DLC brings an endless arena and the Ringmaster in 2027</h2>

Red Hook Studios [announced The Butcher's Asylum on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1940340/view/1849827110434935), a paid DLC for Darkest Dungeon II coming in 2027. It's a standalone mode where your party fights endless waves of enemies from the Confessions and Kingdoms modes in new combinations. A Crowd Meter tracks the audience. Fill their requests and you get buffs, ignore them and they start cheering for the enemy. Your score turns into tickets for the Ringmaster's Prize Wheel, which can hand out trinkets, quirks or nasty battle modifiers. The Ringmaster herself, the villain of the first game's Butcher's Circus, becomes the 16th playable hero with bleed attacks, and she works in all three modes. Red Hook also explained that, after original narrator Wayne June's death in 2025, her new narration plays only inside the Asylum, as [Nintendo Everything](https://nintendoeverything.com/darkest-dungeon-2-unveils-the-butchers-asylum-dlc/) reports.

<h2 id="crimson-desert-mac-game-of-the-year-nomination">Crimson Desert is up for Mac Game of the Year at the App Store Awards</h2>

Pearl Abyss [posted today](https://crimsondesert.pearlabyss.com/en-US/News/Notice/Detail?_boardNo=158) that Crimson Desert is nominated for Mac Game of the Year at Apple's App Store Awards 2026. The studio thanked the "Greymanes" for joining it on Mac and said winners will be announced "in the coming weeks." That fits a busy few weeks for Mac players. Patch 2.05.00 brought the Charting the Unknown expansion to Mac, and this week's 2.05.01 hotfix let you examine treasure maps and wanted posters straight from your inventory. It's also another award run for a game that just took the grand prize at the Korea Game Awards and PC Game of the Year at the Golden Joysticks. We'll update when Apple names the winners.

**PPR take:** Toby Fox saying "I won't be able to rush this" is exactly what you want to hear from a dev. Take the time. As for the Ringmaster, giving the narration a different voice and keeping it inside the Asylum is a classy way to handle Wayne June's legacy....that's respect. And Crimson Desert just keeps stacking nominations. A big open-world action game running well on a Mac? A few years ago nobody would've bet on that.
