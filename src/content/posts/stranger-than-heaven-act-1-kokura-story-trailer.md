**RGG Studio**, the team behind *Like a Dragon*, has released the first story trailer for **[Stranger Than Heaven](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4260840/)**, and it starts the game's 50-year saga at the very beginning.

The [Act 1: Kokura trailer](https://youtu.be/mo3QZshU5HQ) is the first in a series of videos covering **five eras and five cities between 1915 and 1965**. According to [SEGA's announcement](https://ryu-ga-gotoku.com/asia_en/info/detail/004716.html), it follows **Makoto Daito** as he flees persecution in the United States, stows away on the ship of a merchant named **Orpheus**, and lands in the booming steel town of **Kokura, Fukuoka**. There he meets fellow stowaway **Yu Shinjo**, runs into the same discrimination he faced in America, and takes his first steps toward becoming a showman.

**Who you meet in Kokura:**

- **Makoto Daito** (Yu Shirota), who repays the kindness of a local boss by working as one of his underlings
- **Yu Shinjo** (Dean Fujioka), a fellow stowaway who survives on his wits
- **Orpheus** (Snoop Dogg), the merchant whose ship they sneak aboard, who tracks them down with "other motives in mind"
- **Heigo Yashima** (Akio Otsuka), boss of the Kuroganecho district, who takes Daito and Shinjo in
- **Tae Matsumoto** (Moeka Hoshi), a café waitress who welcomes Daito
- **Tadashi Hataoka** (Takashi Ukaji), a militant underling of Yashima's rival, who keeps a close eye on Daito's rise

SEGA says more trailers covering Daito's life across Japan are on the way. [Gematsu has the trailer embedded](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/10/stranger-than-heaven-act-1-kokura-story-trailer) if YouTube isn't your thing.

**Release:** Stranger Than Heaven launches **January 15, 2027** on **PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X|S, Xbox Game Pass and PC**. Pre-orders are open and include the exclusive **Dosu: Kagekiri** DLC weapon.

**PPR take:** A period crime drama spread across five decades is a big step out of Kamurocho for RGG. If each era gets a trailer this focused, January can't come soon enough.
