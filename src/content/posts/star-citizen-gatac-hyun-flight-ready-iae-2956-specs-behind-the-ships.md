**The last new ship of IAE 2956 is out, and it's a one-seat mining and salvage hybrid.** RSI [launched the Gatac Hyūn on X](https://x.com/RobertsSpaceInd/status/2124201339198619882) this afternoon: "Industry Refined. Alien Enhanced." It lands on [Day 9 of IAE](https://x.com/RobertsSpaceInd/status/2124173262154235925), the alien manufacturers day with Aopoa, Banu, Esperia and Gatac on the New Babbage show floor. When we [first wrote about the Hyūn on November 12](/news/star-citizen-gatac-hyun-mining-salvage-iae-2956-november-21/), CIG had shown art and named its role, nothing more. Now there are real numbers.

## What the Ship Matrix says

CIG's official [Ship Matrix entry](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/pledge/ships/hyun/Hy-n) lists the Hyūn as **flight-ready**, so this isn't a concept sale. The key specs:

- **Crew:** 1 (minimum and maximum), size Large, type Industrial, focus "Generalist"
- **Size:** 36.8 m long, 22.6 m wide, 31.9 m tall, about 744,000 kg
- **Industrial gear:** two Size 2 Baler salvage heads and one Size 1 mining mount
- **Defense:** four Size 3 weapon mounts, a Size 5 missile rack, and two Size 2 shield generators
- **Cargo:** 32 SCU, with an SCM speed of 160 m/s

The ship's description says it's Gatac's "first mining/salvage hybrid, with capabilities comparable to dedicated trade ships," built so "you'll always be in the right ship to recover any resources you encounter." CIG didn't put the price in its posts, so check the [pledge page](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/pledge/ships/hyun/Hy-n) for the current IAE offer.

## Two new videos

The ship team's [Behind the Ships: Gatac Hyūn](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SPXJNcLABHE) went up today, billed as an answer to "What is an Industrial Generalist and how does this first one work?" That makes the Hyūn the first ship in a new category, not just a Prospector with salvage arms bolted on. Jax also took it out in [Galactic Gear](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5r8M8FvUNLE), which RSI called the show's season finale.

One thing to watch: the 4.10.3 PTU notes in November raised the Hyūn's internal salvage buffer from 32 to 40 SCU. The Ship Matrix 32 SCU figure is listed as cargo, and CIG hasn't said how the two numbers relate, so we're not going to guess.

**PPR take:** This is the ship a lot of solo players have been asking for....one hull that scrapes a wreck and cracks a rock on the way home. I like that CIG gave it a new label, "Industrial Generalist," because it tells you what it's for. It isn't trying to out-mine a Prospector or out-salvage a Vulture. It's the jack of all trades you take out when you don't know what you'll find. BUT a single Size 1 mining mount is small, so I'd expect it to be a "grab what's on the way" miner, not a full-time rock cracker. Four Size 3s and a big missile rack means it can actually take care of itself, too. With 5.0 putting more stuff on planets and in the black, a ship that can do a bit of everything makes a lot of sense. Gatac has been quietly building one of the coolest lineups in the game, and this might be the most useful one yet. I'm digging it!
