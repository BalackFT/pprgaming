**Tau finally has a date.** Digital Extremes used its November Devstream today to confirm that [Warframe: Tau](https://www.warframe.com/en/tau) arrives on **December 9, 2026**, free on PC, console and mobile. Back in October the studio only promised [December](https://www.digitalextremes.com/news/october-warframe-devstream-highlights), with today's stream billed as the second deep dive. Per the [official announcement](https://www.gamespress.com/Warframe-Tau-Arrives-Free-for-All-Platforms-Dec-9), it came with a new gameplay trailer.

Tau is a whole new star system, the next Star Chart beyond the Origin System that players have been running around for 13 years. Digital Extremes says the system will keep expanding over time, and that Origin isn't being left behind.

## Fornax and the new hubs

The centerpiece is **Fornax**, a ringed city that Digital Extremes calls Warframe's first interconnected social hub system. There are three hubs full of characters and vendors, and you can move between them seamlessly. Walk it on a scenic Night Walk and gather resources along the way, or take the Highway Network, which the studio describes as a car mini-game.

## New modes and remixed missions

Fornax is a crime-riddled city, and the new modes lean into that:

- **Racket** has squads recovering stolen funds and delivering them back to extorted neighborhoods.
- **Mayhem** is about wrecking the mob's operations, quite literally, by causing destruction.
- **Four remixed mission types**, including a vault heist take on Spy missions, plus new Tau damage types that can be combined.
- **Tau's Steel Path** adds the Hokra Brothers as hulking mini-bosses.

Syndicates also change shape in Tau. A new side story feature called **The Fornax Informer** has you collecting clues and interrogating three sub-factions: Larynde's Dazzlers, The Haulers and a third group that hasn't been revealed yet. Ranking up unlocks exclusive rewards.

## Kullervo Prime and what comes after

**Kullervo Prime Access** launches the same day, with more armor and energy than the base frame plus two Prime weapons, the Rauta Prime shotgun and the Vitrica Prime heavy blade. As always, Kullervo Prime can be earned free through play or unlocked instantly. A new TennoGen Tau collection lands alongside it, and Heirloom skins for Nezha and Loki follow in early 2027 with a round of Loki ability balancing.

Further out, a new Warframe trailer is set for The Game Awards on December 10, and watching for 30 minutes with a linked Twitch account earns a free Caliban. [TennoCon 2027](https://www.warframe.com/news) returns to London, Ontario, on July 16-17, and tickets go on sale February 10 at 2 PM ET.

**PPR take:** Thirteen years of Tau teases, and now we're just under two weeks out. What jumps out at me is Fornax. Warframe has always been a mission-select game with hubs bolted on, and three linked social hubs you can drive between is a real change in how the game feels between missions. Racket and Mayhem sound like Digital Extremes having some fun with the crime-city setting too, and a detective-style syndicate is a smart way to make reputation grinding feel like it's going somewhere. December 9 is also a tight squeeze, right before The Game Awards and in the middle of a packed holiday window. But it's free, it's on every platform, and Kullervo Prime is farmable like always. If you've been away a while, this looks like the moment to jump back in.
