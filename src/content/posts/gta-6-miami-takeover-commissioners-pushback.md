With just over a month until launch, **Rockstar** has turned real-life Miami into a billboard for **[Grand Theft Auto VI](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI)**. Not everyone in county government is happy about it.

According to [GameSpot](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/gta-6-is-taking-over-miami-amid-pushback-from-officials-were-depicting-miami-like-a-murder-capital/), the campaign that started with the GTA VI logo being airlifted onto Miami's **Kaseya Center** has grown into a citywide push. People have spotted **GTA-style cars** on the streets, a **Vice City blimp** over the city and **branded beach chairs and umbrellas** on South Florida beaches. Big ads have also turned up in **Chicago, New York's Times Square** and on **Hollywood Boulevard**.

**The pushback:** Several Miami-Dade County commissioners questioned taking Rockstar's money to promote a game built around crime. "We're depicting Miami like a murder capital," Commissioner **René Garcia** said, as quoted by the *Miami Herald*. Commissioner **Micky Steinberg** added that she felt the county "should not be moving forward."

**The other side:** Mayor **Daniella Levine Cava's** office expects the partnership to bring in millions, and her chief of staff called the activations a "love letter" to the city. Commission chair **Anthony Rodriguez**, who sponsored the legislation authorizing the talks, told colleagues: "This is a fictional game. We are all adults in this community."

The mayor can approve deals under $1 million without a commission vote, which is how the **$975,000** Kaseya Center project went through. Further deals for airports and seaports are still up for debate.

**Release:** GTA VI launches **November 19** on **PS5 and Xbox Series X|S**. If you missed it, the [official launch trailer](https://www.rockstargames.com/VI/launch-trailer) is up on Rockstar's site.

**PPR take:** Rockstar has always built Vice City out of Miami's flash and its excess, so a little local friction is almost on brand. A blimp over Biscayne Bay is about as GTA as marketing gets.
