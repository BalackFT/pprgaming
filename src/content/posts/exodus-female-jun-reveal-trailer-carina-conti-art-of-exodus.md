**The other Jun is here.** Archetype Entertainment put out the [FemJun Reveal Trailer](https://x.com/EXODUSGame/status/2122709452612849679) for **EXODUS** on November 17 ("The wait is finally over."), along with a behind-the-scenes featurette and a Dark Horse art book announcement. It's our first real look at female Jun, one of the two playable versions of the game's lead, and Archetype also named the actors behind both.

## Who's playing Jun

Archetype's announcement, [carried in full by RPG Site](https://www.rpgsite.net/news/21786-exodus-artbook-release-dark-horse-female-jun-trailer), says the trailer follows Jun "from a scrappy salvager dreaming of adventure to a Traveler" heading out into a new galaxy. You choose male or female Jun at the start, and your decisions shape the story either way.

- **Carina Conti is female Jun.** Her TV work includes Good Trouble and HBO's Winning Time, and gamers know her as Isabella Torrisi, the female lead in Mafia: The Old Country.
- **Stephen Oyoung is male Jun.** He played Mister Negative in Insomniac's Marvel's Spider-Man games and Red Hood in Gotham Knights, and has film credits including Mission: Impossible – The Final Reckoning and Twisters.

The featurette has the dev team talking about what makes Jun tick. The official account spent this morning answering fans and pointing people at a November fan art contest on Discord, so they know this one landed.

## The Art of EXODUS

Dark Horse is publishing **The Art of EXODUS**, a 208-page hardcover of concept art and designs: hyper-evolved beings, Awakened animals and old tech artifacts from across the Centauri Cluster. It arrives April 13, 2027, six days after the game, and you can pre-order it now through Dark Horse Direct.

## The game, in short

EXODUS is a choice-driven sci-fi action RPG from Archetype, published by Wizards of the Coast. You're a salvager trying to save your homeworld, digging through ancient ruins, fighting Celestials and rival human factions, and recovering Remnants to reshape your civilization with a crew along for the ride. It launches **April 7, 2027** on PS5, Xbox Series X|S and PC (Steam).

**PPR take:** Back in October, Archetype [reworked male Jun's face](/news/exodus-updated-jun-redesign-player-feedback/) after player pushback and said female Jun's updated look was coming. This is them following through, and the fan reaction under the trailer is about as warm as these things get. Casting Carina Conti is a smart pick, too. Mafia: The Old Country players already know she can carry a story-heavy game. With BioWare gone and Mass Effect on ice, EXODUS is the space RPG a lot of those players are watching, and a studio that keeps showing its work five months out is a good sign. The real test is the 40-minute gameplay looks holding up at launch, but so far so good.
