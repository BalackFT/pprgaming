**New Atlantis is supposed to be the capital of the United Colonies, but it's always felt more like a handful of nice plazas you tram between.** **[New Atlantis: A Living Capital](https://www.nexusmods.com/starfield/mods/18748)** by modder **checker90** wants to fix that. It went up on Nexus Mods on **November 6**, hit Bethesda's Creations store for every platform on **November 9**, and it's already on its third beta build.

**Who made it.** If the name sounds familiar, checker90 is the modder behind [New Atlantis Awaits](https://www.nexusmods.com/starfield/mods/13614), one of the better-known city overhauls on Nexus with nearly 700 endorsements. According to the author, A Living Capital started as a planned New Atlantis Awaits 2.0 and grew into its own separate project, built from the ground up. Awaits isn't abandoned, though. checker90 says they still plan to support it and fix its existing issues.

**What it does.** This goes beyond a reskin. According to the [mod page](https://www.nexusmods.com/starfield/mods/18748), the first beta adds:

- **New roads** built for the REV-8 and other vehicles, so you can actually drive around the city.
- **Pedestrian pathways** that make getting around on foot feel more natural.
- **Clean water.** A water purification system clears the algae out of the lake.
- **Three new districts**, Arcadia, Haven and Vista, each with its own identity.
- A pile of smaller details around the city to make it feel lived in.

The beta v1.5 update added more detail to Arcadia, a new fountain in front of the university, new lighting, better navmesh and collision, and some optimization work. The current **beta v1.6** adds more fixes and minor additions. A unique player home is planned for a later update.

**It's a beta, and checker90 says so plainly.** No major optimization has been done yet, so if you run into performance problems, the author asks for patience and says big optimization passes are coming. checker90 is especially asking console players how the city runs for them. The plan is to reach full release after Beta 5.

**Platforms, price and requirements:**

- **Platforms:** PC on [Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/starfield/mods/18748), plus PC, Xbox and PS5 through [Bethesda's Creations store](https://creations.bethesda.net/en/starfield/details/538f4827-05e2-47ce-80f4-58bd42ed07b0/New_Atlantis__A_Living_Capital).
- **Price:** free during the beta. checker90 says that once the full version is out, it'll be submitted as a **paid, achievement-friendly Creation**.
- **Requirements:** no other mods needed. Install it with your mod manager or drop the files in your Starfield Data folder.
- **Compatibility:** it's **not** compatible with New Atlantis Awaits or any other mod that makes major changes to New Atlantis. If you're switching over, the author recommends traveling somewhere far away like Venus, saving, removing the old overhaul, waiting at least 48 in-game hours, saving again, and only then installing A Living Capital.

**PPR take:** Being able to actually drive the REV-8 through the capital is the part that sold me. New Atlantis always looked great from the spaceport and then felt small once you walked it, so new roads and three whole districts go right at the problem. It's early and checker90 is upfront that performance isn't there yet, so console folks especially should expect some rough edges. But this is a modder with a real track record on this exact city, and with three builds out in four days, I'm gonna keep an eye on this one all the way to Beta 5.
