**Walk into the Tobin Expo Center and look up.** Right past the welcome screens there's a Banu Merchantman model sitting on a display platform, and the ticker running around its base says it plainly: **"MERCHANTMAN... COMING IAE 2957..."** [Beyond the Verse grabbed the shot](https://x.com/BTV_Cast/status/2121275870270652556) on opening day, and it's been making the rounds ever since.

## What's actually on the floor

This didn't come out of nowhere. Back when the expo halls were still in testing, players found an empty hologram spot next to the elevators with a big Banu logo and blank signs around it, as [Neckarus' Hangar](https://neckarushangar.de/en/event/iae-2026/) and [StarZen](https://www.starzen.space/t/banu-merchantman-hologram-reportedly-found-in-star-citizen-iae-test-build/40325) both noted. Neckarus points out CIG did something similar with a Drake Kraken hologram before that ship was revealed at DefenseCon. Now the spot is filled in, and the sign has a year on it.

IAE 2956 is this year's show in the in-game calendar, so IAE 2957 is the next one. If CIG keeps its usual November slot, that points to next fall.

## Why it lines up

In the [October Monthly Report](/news/star-citizen-october-2026-monthly-report-300-contracts-nyx-i-merchantman/), CIG said two artists had been assigned to restart the Merchantman after "many months of discussion." The ship was paused with a whitebox interior and greybox exterior, and the team is reworking it around the new Player Trading design. On the [RSI ship matrix](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/pledge/ships/merchantman/Merchantman) she's still listed as in concept, a 160-meter heavy freighter with 2,880 SCU of cargo, and owners are still flying a Hull C, Defender and Hercules C2 as [loaners](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/360003093114-Loaner-Ship-Matrix).

## Implied, not promised

To be clear, this is in-game set dressing, not a comm-link or a dev post. CIG hasn't given the Merchantman a release window, and a sign at a trade show isn't a launch date. Banu also shares a manufacturer day with Aopoa, Esperia and Gatac on November 21, the same day the Hyūn launches, so that's the day to watch for anything official.

**PPR take:** Ummm, so this is a fun one. The Merchantman is one of the oldest promises in this game, and seeing her model on the show floor with "Coming IAE 2957" running around the base is CIG having a little fun with us....and I'm okay with that. It also tracks with what they told us in October: real people are back on the ship, and she's being rebuilt around Player Trading instead of the version that got pitched years ago. That's the right order to do it in. A Merchantman without a trading loop to live in would just be a really big Hull C with a cool shape. So I'm treating this as a "we haven't forgotten" note, not a date. If you're at Tobin this week, go stand under her and grab your own screenshot. And keep an eye on Banu day on the 21st. If CIG wants to say anything official, that's the stage for it.
