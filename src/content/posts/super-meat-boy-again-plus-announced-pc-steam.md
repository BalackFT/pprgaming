**Sixteen years later, Meat Boy is running back through the saws.** Team Meat has announced **Super Meat Boy Again+**, a rebuilt version of the 2010 platformer, and put up a [Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4269060/Super_Meat_Boy_Again/) for it. It's PC only for now, and there's no release date yet, as [Gematsu](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/11/super-meat-boy-again-announced-for-pc) noted when the page went live late Friday.

## Same game, new everything

The studio's pitch is "everything you remember from Super Meat Boy presented through the wonderful lens of nostalgia with stuff added to it." In practice, per the Steam page, that means:

- **Over 300 levels**, the same tough-but-fair run-and-jump challenges the original was known for.
- **A new soundtrack from Danny B**, the original composer.
- **New art, new animated cutscenes and new secrets**, alongside the old ones.

The "+" is the part aimed at veterans. Team Meat says everyone already knows every secret and has every character unlocked, so it's adding new material for longtime fans "to pull their hair out over." It won't say what that is. The idea is you go in blind, like you did the first time. For newcomers, the studio jokes that you may have missed the original because it "came out like four years after YouTube was invented."

[PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/super-meat-boy/again-plus-remaster) notes Team Meat is now led by programmer Tommy Refenes, after co-creator Edmund McMillen's departure, and that Danny B's return may be the biggest draw for fans of the original.

**PPR take:** This is a smart move. Super Meat Boy aged well because the controls were tight and a restart took half a second, and none of that needs fixing. A new coat of paint, a new Danny B score and a pile of brand-new secret stuff for people who already 100%'d it is a good reason to go back. The art change will split people, since McMillen's look was a big part of the original's charm, and fair enough. Wishlist it and judge it on gameplay once there's a trailer and a date. For now, at least we know who it's for: the people who never stopped dying on those saws, plus a whole new crowd who never got the chance.
