**This is the last week to change your mind.** Since October 1, CIG's new **Game Library** system has marked one pledge on every account as the protected **Game Access pledge** for Star Citizen, plus one for Squadron 42 if you own it. A protected pledge can't be melted for store credit or gifted away. You can still move that protection to a different pledge, but only until **November 1, 2026**. After that, [RSI's support article](https://support.robertsspaceindustries.com/hc/en-us/articles/43887330494487-Setting-Your-Game-Access-Pledge) says the selection is locked in.

**Why it matters.** If you've never touched the setting, the system picked for you. According to [CIG's FAQ](https://lemmy.mrm.one/posts/lemmy.mrm.one/c/starcitizen@lemmy.world/comments/1510730), a pledge that already can't be melted (like a High Admiral VIP reward) gets protected first. Otherwise it's your **oldest** pledge that grants the game. That may be the very package you planned to melt or CCU later. As [SCRC points out](https://starcitizenreferralcodes.com/star-citizen-protected-game-access-pledge/), a pledge you restored through Buy Back counts as new from the day you restored it, so the auto-pick can surprise people.

**How to check it (takes a minute)**

1. Log in on the RSI website and open **My Hangar** from your account menu.
2. Pick **Game Access** in the left-hand list. You'll only see pledges that include a game. Ship-only packages don't show up, no matter how pricey they are.
3. The protected pledges carry an orange lock labelled **SC GAME ACCESS** or **SQ42 GAME ACCESS**.
4. To move it, hit **SELECT SC** or **SELECT SQ42** on another eligible pledge. The switch happens immediately, and the old pledge goes back to being meltable and giftable.

**The fine print**

- You can switch as many times as you like before the deadline. Protection is per game, so you can split Star Citizen and Squadron 42 across two pledges.
- A pledge you buy or buy back during the window counts too.
- Ships inside a protected pledge can still be upgraded with CCUs.
- **CIG won't send a reminder.** The FAQ is explicit about that, so this is your reminder.

**One more deadline: Twitch Drops end tomorrow.** RSI Discovery Month's [Twitch Drops](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/comm-link/transmission/21341-Twitch-Drops-RSI-Discovery-Month) wrap up on **October 27**, according to the [Open Hangar calendar](https://openhangar.space/news). Watch four hours total of any Drops-enabled Star Citizen stream and you get the free **Phoenix Insignia Display**, the glowing hangar hologram pictured above. Also on today's calendar is This Week in Star Citizen, and Alpha 4.10.3 is still on the [Wave 1 PTU](/news/star-citizen-alpha-4-10-3-ptu-wave-1-vanduul-incursion-skarnov-rifle/).

**PPR take:** It's not glamorous, but it's the one Star Citizen task this week that can't be undone. Spend sixty seconds in your hangar before Sunday, then go farm that Phoenix hologram.
