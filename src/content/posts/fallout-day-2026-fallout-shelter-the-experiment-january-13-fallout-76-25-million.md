**Happy Fallout Day.** October 23 is the day the bombs fell in Fallout's timeline, and Bethesda closed out its week of celebrations with two pieces of news.

**Fallout Shelter: The Experiment has a date.** Prime Video's unscripted competition series premieres **January 13, 2027**, with the first four episodes at once and the remaining three weekly through January 27, per [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/entertainment/sci-fi-shows/fallout-shelter-the-experiment/) and the [official Fallout on Prime account](https://x.com/falloutonprime/status/2113646641571848591). Contestants face drills, psychological dilemmas and "morally challenging behavior trials" in Vault-Tec shelters, and the last dweller standing wins **$1,000,000**. It comes from Studio Lambert, the producers of The Traitors and Squid Game: The Challenge, with Jonathan Nolan, Lisa Joy and Todd Howard as executive producers.

**Fallout 76 tops 25 million players.** [Bethesda Game Studios](https://x.com/BethesdaStudios/status/2113675002885071253) thanked players for the milestone, which it revealed in its World Pets update video. As [VGChartz](https://www.vgchartz.com/article/469456/fallout-76-tops-25-million-players/) notes, that's up from 23 million in June 2025. [KitGuru](https://www.kitguru.net/tech-news/mustafa-mahmoud/fallout-76-hits-new-milestone-with-25-million-players/) points out that players aren't the same as sales, since the game has been on Game Pass and PlayStation Plus. **World Pets** arrives **December 1** and lets you take cats, dogs, radhogs and even deathclaws out of your C.A.M.P. as companions you can name, customize and level up.

Bethesda also [confirmed](https://x.com/BethesdaStudios/status/2113662136161366256) that next year's Fallout Day, for the series' 30th anniversary, will be a live, in-person celebration in Washington, D.C.

**PPR take:** No Fallout 3 or New Vegas remaster dates this year, but 25 million players for a game that launched as rough as 76 did is a real comeback story.
