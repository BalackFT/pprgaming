**Starfield's silent protagonist has been one of the game's oldest complaints: everybody around you is fully voiced, and you just stand there picking lines off a menu.** **[Player Voice](https://www.nexusmods.com/starfield/mods/18804)** by modder **EagleFour** fixes that. It went up on Nexus Mods on **November 13**, it's already past 100 endorsements, and it got its latest update (version 1.3.3) this morning.

**What it does.** When you pick a dialogue response, your character actually says it, with matching lip animations. According to the [mod page](https://www.nexusmods.com/starfield/mods/18804):

- Five voices to choose from: English male, English female, a lower, "slightly smoky" English Female B, German male and German female.
- The base packs cover Starfield, Free Lanes and the base Trackers Alliance content. Separate add-on packs cover Shattered Space, Terran Armada, and Bethesda's Escape, The Perfect Recipe and Trackers Alliance: The Complete Bounty Series Creations.
- A helmet voice. With a visible helmet on, your lines get the same helmet effect NPCs get.
- Smart little fixes: skipping a line lets the NPC answer right away, lines that belong to a companion are left to the companion, and dialogue that used to include your character's name was reworded so it works for any name.

**How it's made.** The author says the voices were designed and generated locally with the [Qwen3-TTS](https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-TTS) speech model, and the mod is tagged AI-generated on Nexus. EagleFour is upfront about it, with a long statement on the page explaining why. The author also says there are more than 28,000 dialogue lines in total, so only a sample was spot-checked a sample rather than every line. EagleFour also won't make voices based on real actors, citing licensing and permission issues. One known quirk: hailing ships from the pilot seat still makes you sound like you're on the radio.

**Platforms, price and requirements:**

- **Platform:** PC only. It's on Nexus Mods, not Bethesda's Creations store, so there's no Xbox or PS5 version.
- **Price:** free on [Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/starfield/mods/18804).
- **Required:** the Starfield Script Extender (SFSE). Grab the PlayerVoice plugin from Main Files, then pick a voice pack from Optional Files. The add-on packs need the matching DLC or Creation (Escape and the Bounty Series are paid, The Perfect Recipe is free).
- **Compatibility:** mods that add or rewrite dialogue can leave lines silent. A Royal Rewrites voice pack is available for players running that mod.

**PPR take:** Honestly, this is the one I didn't think anybody could pull off. A silent lead in a game this size always felt off to me....everyone's chatting away and I'm just standing there nodding. AI voices aren't gonna be everybody's thing, and I get that. BUT if it holds up across a full playthrough, this goes in my load order for sure!
