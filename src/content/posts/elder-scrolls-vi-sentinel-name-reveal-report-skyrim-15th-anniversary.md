**Eight years after that teaser, a name.** According to [The Verge's Tom Warren](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1022023/microsoft-exit-offer-layoffs-notepad), Bethesda is about to announce that the next Elder Scrolls game will officially be called **The Elder Scrolls VI: Sentinel**. Warren says the announcement "could come as soon as next month," during the stretch that marks **15 years since The Elder Scrolls V: Skyrim** came out.

This is a report, not an announcement. Bethesda and Xbox haven't confirmed the name, and Warren cites "sources familiar with Bethesda's plans."

**What to expect from the reveal:** Not much beyond the title. Warren writes that he's "only expecting a name reveal next month and nothing more." No release date, window or platforms have been announced.

**It's being played, at least internally.** Warren reports that Bethesda has been showing off Sentinel gameplay in recent months, and that Xbox CEO Asha Sharma got a live playthrough in August, praising the scale of the game and saying "the story is even greater." As [Gematsu points out](https://www.gematsu.com/2026/10/report-the-elder-scrolls-vi-subtitled-sentinel), Sharma teased the subtitle back then with eight asterisks, the same number of letters as "Sentinel," and fans had already landed on the name before this report, [GameSpot notes](https://www.gamespot.com/articles/you-all-guessed-elder-scrolls-6s-name-and-now-heres-the-games-setup-report/).

**Where it's reportedly set:** Warren also says Sentinel takes place in **Hammerfell**, a province that's shown up before in The Elder Scrolls Adventures: Redguard and The Elder Scrolls Online. [VGC notes](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/xbox-is-reportedly-set-to-officially-name-the-elder-scrolls-6-sentinel-as-soon-as-next-month/) that the setting isn't expected to be revealed alongside the title. The Verge's piece includes some reported story details too; we've left those out here so nothing gets spoiled.

**PPR take:** If the timing holds, Skyrim's birthday on November 11 lands just eight days before GTA 6. A title card won't compete with that, but after this long, even just a name would be a nice reminder that this game is real.
