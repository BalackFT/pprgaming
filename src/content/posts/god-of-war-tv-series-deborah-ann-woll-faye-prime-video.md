**The God of War show just made the easiest casting call in the building.** Deborah Ann Woll, who voices and performs **Faye** in the games, will play Faye in Prime Video's live-action God of War series too. [Amazon MGM Studios announced it](https://press.amazonmgmstudios.com/us/en/press-release/deborah-ann-woll-to-reprise-her-role-as-faye-in-pr), and [Deadline](https://deadline.com/2026/11/god-of-war-deborah-ann-woll-faye-amazon-series-1237230639/) broke it Monday.

## Who Faye is, and why this matters

Faye is Kratos' late wife and Atreus' mother. Her death and her wish to have her ashes scattered from the highest peak kick off the 2018 game, which is the story the show is adapting. Woll played her in **God of War Ragnarök**, and she's the lead in **God of War Laufey**, which Santa Monica Studio [recently pushed to May 20, 2027](/news/god-of-war-laufey-delayed-may-20-2027/). So the same actor will be the face of Faye on PS5 and on TV next year-ish.

Deadline also points out this is not a recast. Production stopped earlier this year after original Kratos actor Ryan Hurst was seriously injured, and the show restarted from scratch with **Dave Bautista** as Kratos and none of the earlier footage. According to Deadline, Faye wasn't in the episodes shot before the shutdown, and Woll was always the producers' choice.

## Where the show stands

- **Filming is underway in Vancouver** on a **two-season order**, after restarting earlier this fall, per Deadline.
- **Ronald D. Moore** (Battlestar Galactica, For All Mankind) is showrunner, with Cory Barlog among the executive producers.
- The cast now includes Callum Vinson as Atreus, Mandy Patinkin as Odin, Ed Skrein as Baldur, Ólafur Darri Ólafsson as Thor, Alastair Duncan as Mimir and Sonya Walger as Freya, among others.
- **No release date** has been announced.

**PPR take:** This is the right call, plain and simple. Game adaptations get in trouble when they treat the source like a suggestion, and bringing over the actor who already *is* Faye to a lot of players tells you the people making this respect the games. It also matters more than it might look. Faye is barely on screen in the 2018 story, but everything Kratos and Atreus do is about her, and Laufey is putting her front and center. Having one actor tie the show and the next game together is smart for PlayStation and good for fans. The bigger question is still time. A full restart with a new Kratos means we're probably waiting a while, so keep the hype in check until Amazon gives a date.
