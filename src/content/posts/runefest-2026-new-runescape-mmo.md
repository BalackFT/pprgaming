RuneScape turns 25 this year, and Jagex marked it with its biggest [RuneFest](https://www.jagex.com/news/runefest-2026-jagex-unveils-fourth-mmo-in-the-runescape-franchise-plus-runescape-reignited-old-school-menaphos-and-more-at-biggest-runefest-ever) yet, with more than 6,000 fans at the NEC in Birmingham. The headline: a **fourth MMO in the RuneScape franchise**.

## A new RuneScape MMO in Unreal Engine

The new game doesn't have a name yet. Jagex says it's being built in **Unreal Engine** "for a new generation of players," with the goal of combining RuneScape's depth, freedom and community-led spirit with modern technology and design. It's also **still early in development**, so there's no footage to judge, no platforms and no release window. It joins the original RuneScape, Old School RuneScape and the survival spin-off RuneScape: Dragonwilds, which Jagex launched in 2026 as part of a push to grow RuneScape into a wider family of games.

## RuneScape Reignited on December 2

The nearer-term news is for the original game. **RuneScape Reignited launches December 2** as a fresh start, with a remastered early journey and Adventure Guide, a fresh economy and brand-new hiscores. Jagex says players get full Reignited access **without membership from December 2 to 12**, which makes it an easy time for lapsed players to look back in. RuneScape's 2027 content is split into three story arcs, starting in January with Hoard of Dragons, a sequel to the Dragon Slayer quest.

## Old School and Dragonwilds

**Old School RuneScape** is finally opening the gates to **Menaphos in 2027**, after 20 years, with a new Master quest and Old School's first skilling dungeon. Before that, the Fractured Archive raid lands October 20 and Hardcore Worlds arrives November 4. **Dragonwilds** gets its Luminance update in December, then the Blood Crystal Saga in 2027, plus official modding through CurseForge on every platform, consoles included.

## An animated series

Jagex is also working on **an animated RuneScape adaptation** with Charlie White (MoistCr1TiKaL) and LA-based Lyrical Media. No release details were given.

**PPR take:** "Built for a new generation" can mean a lot of things, and until there's footage, I'd treat the fourth MMO as a statement of intent rather than a game you can judge. Jagex has said it's early, and that honesty is welcome. The more interesting near-term move is Reignited. A fresh economy and new hiscores, with ten days of free access, is a smart way to bring back people who drifted away. Menaphos finally opening after 20 years will make a lot of Old School players happy too. And between the new MMO and Dragonwilds console mods, Jagex clearly wants RuneScape to be more than one game.
