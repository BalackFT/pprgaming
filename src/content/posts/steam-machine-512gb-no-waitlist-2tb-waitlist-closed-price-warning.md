**Five months after launch, the Steam Machine is finally something you can just buy.** Well, one of them. In a Steam post on Thursday, Valve said it has filled every reservation for the **512GB model**, and that version is now in a plain "add-to-cart" status while supplies last, [The Verge reports](https://www.theverge.com/games/1038010/valve-steam-machine-waitlist-supply).

## 512GB: in stock, for now

When The Verge checked Thursday, the 512GB versions with and without a bundled controller showed delivery estimates of three to five business days. Valve says that when the current stock runs out, it will mark the model out of stock instead of starting another waitlist, per [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/valve-says-memory-and-storage-costs-are-so-bad-it-wont-do-steam-machine-waitlists-anymore-as-it-doesnt-know-how-many-it-can-make-or-what-theyll-cost/), which quotes the post directly.

## 2TB: waitlist closed

The bigger model is a different story. Valve has sent purchase invites to all remaining 2TB reservation holders, but it says there are more people on the waitlist than units to sell, so it is closing the waitlist to new sign-ups and showing the 2TB model as out of stock. People already in line keep their place, but nobody is guaranteed a unit. You can switch to a 512GB machine instead, but [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/hardware/steam-machines/valve-may-raise-steam-machine-prices-yet-again-memory-and-storage-costs-continue-to-increase-at-unprecedented-levels/) notes you give up your 2TB spot if you do.

## The price warning

Here's the part that matters for anyone still on the fence. Valve wrote that "memory and storage costs continue to increase at unprecedented levels," and that it doesn't yet know how many more it can make "and at what price we'll be able to offer them." It also says future 2TB batches will come in smaller quantities than planned. Valve expects to share more on supply in January, per [Ars Technica](https://arstechnica.com/gaming/2026/11/valve-says-it-might-have-to-raise-the-price-of-supply-limited-steam-machines/).

For reference, the Steam Machine launched in June starting at **$1,049** for the 512GB model without a controller, and **$1,349** for the 2TB model without one, according to The Verge. Valve hasn't announced a price change. It just won't rule one out.

## PPR take

Good news, bad news, and the bad news wins. Being able to click "buy" on a 512GB box with no lottery is how this should have worked from day one. BUT $1,049 was already a tough sell, and now Valve is basically saying the next batch might cost more and there might not be many of them. That's the RAM crunch landing right on PC gamers again. And 512GB isn't much these days, PC Gamer points out games over 100GB are pretty common now....so if you go that route, plan on extra storage. If you've been waiting for a "deal," this isn't it. If you were going to buy one anyway, now is probably the window.
