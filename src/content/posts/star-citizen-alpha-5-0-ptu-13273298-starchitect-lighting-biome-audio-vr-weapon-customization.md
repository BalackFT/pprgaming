**Another day, another 5.0 build!** CIG pushed **Alpha 5.0 PTU build 13273298** at about 12:48 PM ET today, and Wakapedia-CIG has the [patch notes up on Spectrum](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/190048/thread/star-citizen-alpha-5-0-ptu-patch-notes-6). It's still **Wave 3** testers only on the **US-only** PTU channel, with **long-term persistence** on and the **25x reputation multiplier** still running. It lands one day after [build 13260598](/news/star-citizen-alpha-5-0-ptu-13260598-ship-armor-mfd-wet-terrain-bounty-contracts/). Live is still Alpha 4.10.3 per the [RSI status page](https://status.robertsspaceindustries.com/).

The test focus list is the same as before: Genesis, Nyx I, Starchitect locations, mission boards, intraplanetary server meshing, vehicle TTK, the missile lock interface and VOIP.

## Starchitect outposts look more lived in

Most of this build is polish, and the biggest chunk is on the procedurally built Starchitect locations. Maintenance, generic, command and habitation buildings all got a **lighting and fog pass**, and CIG says compound lighting was reworked so light **stops leaking through walls**. Safes moved out of kitchens and into storage rooms (about time..lol), exposed ladders got backs, and a pile of floating props and decal problems are listed as potential fixes.

## Nyx sounds and looks better

The audio fix is the one I'd notice first. CIG says biome sound emitters were **moving around while you stood still**, so the ambience stacked on itself and jittered. That's now a potential fix, and **Levski** gets a mix fix too, since its ambient audio was carrying across the whole station. On the visual side, Nyx I plants got a wind pass, and ocean reflections now fade out over long distances to stop mirrored artifacts you could see from orbit.

## VR, contracts and crashes

VR players get a **rebuilt weapon customization screen** as a 3D object, with attachment ports drawn right on the gun. CIG calls these VR changes "very experimental." Contract fixes include the Covalex locker breaking after another player used it (STARC-218728) and Recover Cargo containers not counting as mission cargo. There's also a potential fix for GPU crashes from bad math in the wind and particle shaders (STARC-223665). The [full notes](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/190048/thread/star-citizen-alpha-5-0-ptu-patch-notes-6) have the rest.

**PPR take:** No headline feature this time, and that's fine. This is what a patch looks like when it's getting close to the finish line....lighting, sound and crash fixes instead of new systems. The biome audio bug is a big deal even if it sounds small. If the ambience stacks and jitters every time you stop moving, the whole planet feels broken, and Nyx is supposed to be the showcase for 5.0. Same with light bleeding through walls at outposts. That kind of thing kills immersion fast when you're clearing a compound. The Recover Cargo fix matters for anyone grinding rep at 25x, too. Four Wave 3 builds since last Thursday is a good sign, BUT CIG still hasn't given a Live date, so don't plan your weekend around it yet.
