**The factory must grow, one last time.** Wube Software has marked **Factorio 2.1** as the stable release after a summer in experimental. The announcement came in [Friday Facts #449](https://www.factorio.com/blog/post/fff-449), exactly two years after Space Age launched. The studio says 2.1 "isn't about new planets, enemies or research trees" but is "a big collection of quality of life, small features, polish, and the things we wanted to do for 2.0 but didn't have time for."

**The highlights:**

- **Space logistics:** rockets can now carry multiple different items in one launch, platforms can trade items with other platforms in orbit, an "Import from Any" option pulls from every location, and a Radar "Universe mode" shares circuit signals between planets and platforms.
- **Platform balance:** asteroid damage now depends on the asteroid's remaining health rather than speed, and drag is based on mass instead of width. That means "the 'pencil ship' is no longer the only way to go fast."
- **Landing pad unloading bay:** a cargo bay that inserters can unload straight into trains, belts or chests.
- **Circuit network:** reads and writes can go to different wire colors, and labs, boilers, heat exchangers, pipes and even land mines (as tripwires) can now connect.
- **Quality is optional:** Space Age can be played without Quality, and the "space casino" is closed because Quality modules no longer work in asteroid reprocessing.
- **Fixes and new graphics:** fluid mixing prevention is back, pumpjacks can be flipped, biter expansions now scale with evolution, and there are new assemblers, a new oil refinery, a new rocket silo and more. There's also a native ARM64 Linux build, which [GamingOnLinux](https://www.gamingonlinux.com/2026/10/factorio-2-1-is-out-now-with-the-native-arm64-linux-build-to-run-on-steam-frame-raspberry-pi-and-more/) notes runs on devices like the Steam Frame and Raspberry Pi.

**Before you update:** 2.0 saves will load, but Wube warns that changes like the circuit input/output split may break existing designs, and saves and blueprints can't be downgraded. Back them up first, and check that your mods have been updated.

As [GamesRadar+](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/simulation/factorio-2-1-the-last-big-update-to-the-sim-icon-that-got-234-000-glowing-steam-reviews-without-ever-going-on-sale-is-officially-out/) points out, Wube has called this its "last major update" for the game. Factorio has more than 234,000 positive Steam reviews and still has never gone on sale.

**PPR take:** Plenty of games end with a whimper. Factorio is ending its big updates with mixed rockets and the end of pencil ships. If you've been sitting on a Space Age save, back it up and dive back in this weekend.
