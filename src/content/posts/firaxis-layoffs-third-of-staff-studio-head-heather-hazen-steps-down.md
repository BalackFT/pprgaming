**Rough day in Maryland.** Firaxis Games, the Take-Two studio behind Civilization and XCOM, laid off around 60 people on Tuesday, roughly a third of the studio, according to [Bloomberg's Jason Schreier](https://news.bloomberglaw.com/us-law-week/take-twos-firaxis-video-game-studio-cuts-a-third-of-its-staff), citing people familiar with the cuts. Studio head Heather Hazen is leaving too.

## What 2K confirmed

2K Games, the Take-Two label that runs Firaxis, didn't comment on the headcount, but it confirmed the layoffs and Hazen's exit in a statement to [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/gaming-industry/civilization-dev-firaxis-reportedly-lays-off-a-third-of-its-staff-studio-head-departs/). "Firaxis is restructuring and recentering around the studio's core strength — designing world class strategy games," the spokesperson said, adding that the studio is being reduced "to focus on design, technology and the player experience, which means saying goodbye to talented colleagues who helped build Firaxis."

On Hazen, 2K said that with the studio "entering this reset, she feels this is the right, deliberate moment to hand over the reins." She took over as studio head in 2023 after more than 25 years at Firaxis. No replacement has been named.

## How Firaxis got here

This isn't the first round. PC Gamer notes about 30 people were let go in 2023 and an unknown number in 2025. Marvel's Midnight Suns reviewed well but sold modestly, and Civilization 7 still sits at "mixed" on Steam with player counts behind Civilization 6, even after a long run of updates since its 2025 launch.

Bloomberg also reports that a new XCOM game in development at Firaxis has struggled and missed a major milestone earlier this year. Work on it will reportedly continue, but it may change because of the layoffs. That part is reported, not confirmed by 2K.

**PPR take:** This one stings. Firaxis has been the gold standard for "one more turn" for 30 years, and a third of a studio is not a trim, it's a gut punch to real people right before the holidays. The timing is hard to ignore too. Take-Two is two days out from launching GTA 6, the biggest release the company has ever had, and on the same Tuesday one of its most respected studios is cutting deep. I get that Civ 7 didn't land the way Civ 6 did, BUT "recentering around core strength" is the kind of corporate phrase that usually means fewer swings and safer bets. The XCOM news worries me most. That series has a loyal crowd, and a troubled project after a third of the team walks out the door is never a great sign. To everyone affected, hope you land somewhere that deserves you.
