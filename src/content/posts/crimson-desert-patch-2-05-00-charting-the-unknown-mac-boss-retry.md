**Mac players finally get to set sail.** Pearl Abyss posted the [patch notes for Crimson Desert version 2.05.00](https://crimsondesert.pearlabyss.com/en-US/News/Notice/Detail?_boardNo=157) on November 13 (05:50 UTC), and this one is a bigger drop than the run of small hotfixes we've been tracking since [2.04.08](/news/crimson-desert-hotfix-2-04-08-framerate-ship-wheel-leviakan/). The headline: the **Charting the Unknown** DLC is now on **Apple Mac**, and you can buy it from the "Buy DLC" menu on the title screen. The patch is listed as available now on Steam (PC and Mac), PlayStation, Xbox, the Epic Games Store and the Mac App Store.

## The changes worth knowing about

The full list is long, so here are the ones that stand out:

- **Boss retries got kinder.** Picking "Retry" on certain boss fights now starts you from the last combat phase you reached. Pearl Abyss tucks the names behind a spoiler tag, so I'll leave them there too.
- **The Greymane gear duplication bug is fixed.** Items equipped on your Greymane comrades were getting duplicated, and that loophole is closed.
- **Calmer seas.** There are fewer hostile creatures in the Central Waters, the Great Coral Reef and the Great Rocky Reef.
- **Housing fixes.** A big batch of Free Construction fixes, including furniture that could vanish when you moved the Greymane camp to Pailune and a screen freeze when you walked up to a house you built.

There's also a fix for AMD RDNA 4 crashes with the cinematic option on (driver 26.10.1 or newer), the Harpoon Gun now actually hits the Sea Serpent, and the Assault Ship of the East can be boarded from either side.

**PPR take:** The phase-retry change is the one I'd tell a friend about. Nobody wants to run a long boss fight from the top again because they got clipped on the last phase. That's not difficulty, that's just your time getting wasted, and Pearl Abyss clearly heard that. Getting Charting the Unknown onto Mac is a big deal for the people who've been sitting on the base game while everyone else was out on the water. Fewer hostiles on the reefs reads like a straight response to players getting swarmed while trying to explore, and that's the whole point of this expansion. The pile of housing fixes tells you a lot of people are building, and building a lot. After a string of small hotfixes, this feels like a real cleanup pass. Not finished, but moving the right way.
