**Game over in March.** The Pokémon Company says service for **Pokémon UNITE** on both Nintendo Switch and mobile will end on **March 23, 2027, at 00:00 UTC**, which is 8 PM ET on Sunday, March 22, according to its [end-of-service notice](https://support.pokemon.com/hc/en-us/articles/55009302346132-Pok%C3%A9mon-UNITE-End-of-Service-Notice). After that, nobody will be able to log in or play a match. The free-to-play MOBA launched on Switch in July 2021, so it will have run for a little under six years.

**The money side shut off today.** Aeos Gem sales and new **UNITE Club** memberships ended on October 27, and auto-renewal for existing members was switched off too, the [official Japanese notice](https://www.pokemonunite.jp/ja/topics/notice/20261027/) says. Gems you already have still work until the end, current memberships run out on their normal date, and players with **unused paid gems** will be able to ask for a refund through an in-game form after service ends. That notice asks players with leftover paid gems not to delete the game until their refund goes through.

**A send-off update on Nov. 12.** An update on November 12 at 04:00 UTC (11 PM ET on Nov. 11) adds a **Unite Coin** exchange, Unite Coin missions and a log-in bonus, plus a farewell event that hands out licenses and Holowear from the game's history, [Pokemon.com details](https://www.pokemon.com/us/news/special-event-and-library-feature-added-to-pokemon-unite). It also adds a **library** that keeps your battle records, licenses and Holowear viewable after shutdown, but only if you finish the library data download before March 23.

**Esports carries on for now.** The **Aeos Crown 2027 Tokyo** tournament is still happening, with qualifiers starting December 5, per the [shutdown schedule](https://support.pokemon.com/hc/en-us/articles/55009302696084-Schedule-Until-Pok%C3%A9mon-UNITE-End-of-Service).

**PPR take:** UNITE was the easiest way to get a non-MOBA crowd into a MOBA, and it had a real competitive scene. The library feature and gem refunds are a more graceful exit than most live-service games get, but it's another reminder that a game you can't run offline is only around as long as the servers are.
