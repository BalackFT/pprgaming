Happy Monday, folks! A couple more things worth your time before the day wraps up.

<h2 id="iae-2956-day-4-tumbril-crusader">IAE 2956 Day 4 belongs to Tumbril and Crusader</h2>

The expo floor in New Babbage changed hands again this morning. RSI [kicked off Day 4](https://x.com/RobertsSpaceInd/status/2122360320270221514) around 11 AM ET with a land-or-sky pitch: Tumbril Land Systems "brings the ground game," while Crusader Industries "takes innovation to new heights." Both manufacturers got their own IAE pages on the Comm-Link, one for [Tumbril](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/comm-link/transmission/21408-IAE-2956-Tumbril-Land-Systems) (billed as "the biggest name in the deadliest ground vehicles") and one for [Crusader](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/comm-link/transmission/21409-IAE-2956-Crusader-Industries), each with its lineup available to pledge. Per the official schedule, this block runs through Tuesday, so if you've been meaning to check out Tumbril's ground vehicles or Crusader's ships at the expo, there's still time.

<h2 id="the-game-awards-2026-nominees-november-17">The Game Awards 2026 nominees drop tomorrow at noon ET</h2>

Geoff Keighley will [reveal this year's nominees](https://thegameawards.com/news/nominees-announced-tuesday-november-17) in a livestream on Tuesday, November 17 at **noon ET** (9 AM PT) on YouTube and Twitch. Per [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/tv/news/game-awards-nominees-special-ps5-livestream-winners-1236946910/), the special covers the games, studios and talent up for this year's awards, Game of the Year included, and fan voting opens on TheGameAwards.com right after. The nominees are picked by a global jury of media and creator outlets, and the show itself is December 10 at the Peacock Theater in Los Angeles. That's two days before GTA VI comes out, which makes for some awkward timing.

**PPR take:** Crusader day is always a fun one, and Tumbril gets to remind everybody that the 'verse isn't all ships....some of the best moments in this game happen on the ground. As for The Game Awards, I'm curious how the jury handles a GOTY list that's locked in before GTA VI even launches. Should be an interesting ten minutes tomorrow!
