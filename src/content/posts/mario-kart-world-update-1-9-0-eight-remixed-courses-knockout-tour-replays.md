**Mario Kart World just got its biggest content drop in a while.** Nintendo released the **Ver. 1.9.0** update for the Switch 2 kart racer on November 24, and it adds **eight new courses**, **two new Knockout Tour routes** and a proper **Replay** option on the main menu. Nintendo laid it out in its [announcement post](https://www.nintendo.com/us/whatsnew/mario-kart-world-update-adds-eight-courses-and-more/), and the full change list is on the [Nintendo Support page](https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/68580/~/how-to-update-mario-kart-world).

## Eight "2" courses

The new tracks are remixes of courses that are already in Mario Kart World. Nintendo says they take you down routes you haven't driven before, and some run the familiar course in the opposite direction. Here's the lineup:

- Mario Bros. Circuit 2 and Wario Stadium 2
- DK Pass 2 and Shy Guy Bazaar 2
- Desert Hills 2 and Koopa Troopa Beach 2
- Airship Fortress 2 and Salty Salty Speedway 2

They show up in VS Race, Time Trials and online and wireless races. You pick each one from the menu of the course it remixes.

## Two new Knockout Tour rallies

Knockout Tour is the 24-driver elimination mode that runs across the open world, with checkpoints cutting the field as you go. Version 1.9.0 adds the **Feather Rally** (Mario Bros. Circuit to Boo Cinema) and the **Lucky Cat Rally** (Great ? Block Ruins to Sky-High Sundae). You unlock both by playing any existing rally. Nintendo also says **more Knockout Tour routes are planned** for future updates.

## The other changes worth knowing

- **Replays save automatically.** Recent races and battles are stored for later viewing, and the oldest get deleted when you hit the limit. Replays from 1.8.0 and earlier don't carry over.
- **P Switch missions now work in online Free Roam**, plus wireless and LAN play (not in 2-player or events).
- **Spectating got better.** You now see the item slot, you can switch who you're watching after they finish, and battles show HIT and CRASH.
- **Item box odds were adjusted** in races and Knockout Tour. Nintendo doesn't list the exact numbers.

There's a long list of course tweaks and bug fixes beyond that, so check the [full notes](https://en-americas-support.nintendo.com/app/answers/detail/a_id/68580/~/how-to-update-mario-kart-world). One heads-up from Nintendo: everyone needs the same version to play together online, so update before game night.

**PPR take:** This is the kind of free update that keeps a launch game alive. Eight remixed tracks is basically a new cup, and the remix approach is smart. It gets more use out of courses people already know without asking anyone to pay for a booster pack. The Replay mode is the sleeper pick for me, though. It's honestly wild it took this long, and anyone who makes clips or just wants to relive a last-lap blue shell disaster gets a real tool now. I'd keep an eye on the quiet item odds change. Nintendo didn't share numbers, and small item tweaks can change how Knockout Tour plays more than any new track does. Nintendo saying more rallies are coming is good news too. Knockout Tour is the mode that makes World feel different from Mario Kart 8, and it's the one that deserves the most new content.
