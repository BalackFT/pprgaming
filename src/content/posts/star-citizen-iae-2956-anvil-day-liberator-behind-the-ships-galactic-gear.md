**Day 3 at Tobin, and it's Anvil's turn.** [@RobertsSpaceInd opened Anvil Day](https://x.com/RobertsSpaceInd/status/2121999176468767089) a little after 11 a.m. ET, pointing straight at the headliner: the **Anvil Liberator**. CIG's [IAE 2956 press release](https://www.gamespress.com/PLAY-STAR-CITIZEN-FOR-FREE-DURING-INTERGALACTIC-AEROSPACE-EXPO-2956-FE) had the Liberator down for its debut on November 15, and she's been flyable since [Alpha 4.10.3 went live](/news/star-citizen-alpha-4-10-3-live-vanduul-incursion-galaxy-liberator-iae/), so today is really her coming-out party. RSI backed it up with two videos, a [Behind the Ships breakdown](https://x.com/RobertsSpaceInd/status/2122006859473096861) and [Episode 2 of Galactic Gear](https://x.com/RobertsSpaceInd/status/2121999311890194891).

## What the Liberator actually is

In [Behind the Ships](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A5G9beD9Lko), the devs call her Anvil's "logistical powerhouse" and describe her as a car park in space. The idea is simple. A small group loads its fighters and ground vehicles onto one ship, burns one ship's fuel to get deep into the black, and uses her as a mobile base out there. The pads on top are sized for small fighters like the Hornet, and the team says straight up they expect players to min-max what fits up there.

She's not just a bus, though. Per the video, there's a manned top turret with two size 5 guns, two size 3 remote turrets the pilot controls when no gunner is on them, four size 4 missile racks and two point defense turrets for incoming missiles. The bridge is a tight pilot-and-gunner setup the devs compare to sitting in a tank.

## The changes from the concept

A couple of things are new since the 2951 concept. The Liberator picked up **rearm, repair and refuel**, and she has **400 SCU of cargo** in a dedicated side room, so the cargo doesn't eat into the garage. The front ramp also got windows, so you can see (and shoot) out through the armor before your crew rolls out.

## Galactic Gear, round two

Jax is back too, now banished to "the basement" after last episode. [Galactic Gear Episode 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=nQiSzl9YMWg) has three Jimmys loading up the Liberator's three pads with their own ship picks. Jax's summary of the internal hangar is "officially two ground vehicles".... "but as every good citizen knows, that's merely an opening bid." 

**PPR take:** This is the ship a lot of small orgs have been waiting on since 2951. Not everybody has the crew for an Idris or wants to babysit a Kraken. The Liberator sits right in that gap, and the devs even said their own weekly playtests have been using an Idris or a C2 to group up, and this is going to be their new go-to. That tells you something. Rearm, repair and refuel is the big one for me....your fighters land, top off, and go right back out without a station run. Free Fly is still on through November 27, so if you've got two or three buddies, grab some Anvil rentals today, park them on her and go somewhere you probably shouldn't. Then decide if she's worth it before you spend a dime. And honestly....Galactic Gear is quickly becoming the best thing CIG puts out during IAE.
