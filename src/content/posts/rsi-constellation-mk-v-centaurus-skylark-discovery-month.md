**The next Connie is here, and you can fly it for free.** CIG has finished revealing the **RSI Constellation Mk V Centaurus**, the next generation of one of Star Citizen's most iconic ships, along with its companion snub fighter, the **[Skylark](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/roadmap/release-view/1607-RSI-Skylark)**. The reveal on October 14 came with a [ship page](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/transmission/21335-RSI-Constellation-Mk-V-Centaurus), a [Q&A with the Vehicle and Gameplay teams](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/engineering/21377-Q-A-RSI-Constellation-Mk-V-Centaurus) and a [Behind the Ships video](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/transmission/21381-Star-Citizen-Behind-The-Ships-RSI-Constellation-Mk-V-Centaurus-Video).

## What's different from the Mk IV

According to the Q&A, the Mk V has grown in every dimension so it can carry more cargo, including "some of our largest current ground vehicles," loaded up a descending ramp. It's tuned to a heavier class than the Mk IV, so it turns and accelerates more slowly, but CIG says it's far more durable, carries a second S3 shield generator and reaches a higher NAV speed.

The pilot still controls four S5 guns like on the Mk IV. The turrets are now quad S3 mounts, remote controlled from the bridge by separate crew members. The missiles were consolidated into two matching racks for a total of **32 S2 missiles**, with storage right behind them so the crew can reload in flight. The bridge is all new, with the crew still in one room, and the old window struts are gone for a much better view.

## Meet the Skylark

The Skylark docks under the Mk V's hull. Per the Q&A, it has a prone pilot position, three S2 guns, two 5xS1 missile racks and, unusually for a snub, **two shield generators**. The trade-off is that it sits at the heavier, less agile end of the snub class. It's the only ship that can dock with the Mk V (no Merlin or Archimedes), the Mk V can't refuel or rearm it, and CIG plans to sell the Skylark on its own at this year's IAE.

## How to try it for free

The [RSI Discovery Month FAQ](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/transmission/21350-FAQ-RSI-Discovery-Month) says completing the **Constellation Rental Offer** mission, a special reward on the event's Transport track, gets you a **5-day rental** of the Centaurus and its Skylark. CIG is also running [Twitch Drops](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/transmission/21341-Twitch-Drops-RSI-Discovery-Month) from **October 14 to 27**: link your Twitch and RSI accounts, watch any Drops-enabled Star Citizen stream for a total of **four hours**, and you unlock the **Phoenix Insignia Display**, a hangar hologram inspired by the classic Constellation Phoenix. The event also brings this year's Digital Goodies Pack, with 15 new in-game items.

**PPR take:** The Connie has been part of this game for as long as most of us have been playing, so a new generation is a big deal. I like that CIG is upfront about the trade-off. It's slower and heavier than the Mk IV, but tougher, with crew-run turrets and in-flight missile reloads, and that sounds like a real crew ship rather than a solo brick. The Skylark with two shields is an interesting twist for a snub too. Best part? You don't have to spend a dime to find out. Run the rental mission, take her out for five days and decide for yourself before you think about a CCU. 😎
