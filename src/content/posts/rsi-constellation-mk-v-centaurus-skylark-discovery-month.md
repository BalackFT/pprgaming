Cloud Imperium Games has completed the reveal of the **RSI Constellation Mk V Centaurus**, the next generation of one of Star Citizen's most iconic ships, along with its companion fighter, the **[Skylark](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/roadmap/release-view/1607-RSI-Skylark)**.

In its official [Comm-Link](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/Patch-Notes/) posts, CIG pitches the ship simply: "The Mk IV's better and the Mk V's bigger," with *versatile* as the watchword. The reveal included a ship page, a **Q&A with the Vehicle and Gameplay teams**, and a **Behind the Ships** video.

## How to try it for free

- **Constellation Rental Offer:** an RSI Discovery Month mission grants free access to the Centaurus and Skylark *(details per community coverage — check in-game for exact requirements)*.
- **Twitch Drops:** watch any Drops-enabled Star Citizen stream for a total of **four hours** to unlock the **Phoenix Insignia Display**, a hangar hologram inspired by the classic Constellation Phoenix. Community trackers list **October 27** as the deadline.
- **Digital Goodies Pack:** RSI Discovery Month also brings in-game wearables, weapons, paints and décor.

## Also this week

CIG launched the October monthly store bundle (the "Runic" pack), which drew pushback on Spectrum over its warbond-only status, according to community coverage.
