Hey all, happy Saturday! Origin day at IAE was a quiet one for news, so here's a couple of things worth knowing before the weekend's out.

<h2 id="the-game-awards-2026-nominees-november-17">The Game Awards 2026 nominees get revealed Tuesday, November 17</h2>

Geoff Keighley will reveal this year's nominees during a livestream on **Tuesday, November 17 at 12 PM ET** on YouTube and Twitch, [Polygon reports](https://www.polygon.com/the-game-awards-2026-nominees-announcement-date/). The special usually runs about ten minutes, with Game of the Year saved for last, and fan voting opens as soon as it wraps. The timing is interesting: that's two days before *Grand Theft Auto VI* comes out. It's still eligible, because main-category games only need to be out by November 20 at 9 PM PT, and the Players' Voice cutoff is November 30. The show itself airs December 10 at 7:30 PM ET (pre-show included), and this year it's also on Disney+, Live TV on PS5 and Roblox.

<h2 id="skyrim-15th-anniversary-creations-bundle">Skyrim turns 15 with an anniversary Creations bundle and a big sale</h2>

Bethesda marked Skyrim's 15th birthday with a curated [Anniversary Bundle](https://elderscrolls.bethesda.net/en-US/news/skyrim-15-anniversary-creation-bundle) of five Creations from its Verified Creators. You get the voiced faction questline *Legacy of Orsinium*, the romanceable, custom-voiced follower *Kaitryn Fair-Shadows*, two dungeons ported over from ESO in *Legendary Dungeons: Dwarven Delves*, the Dwemer hand cannon *Arquebus*, and a player home outside Riften called *Shadetree Lodge*. It's on Xbox, PC and PlayStation until December 12. [Push Square](https://www.pushsquare.com/news/2026/11/skyrim-celebrates-15th-anniversary-with-creation-club-dlc-bundle-big-sales-and-more) puts it at 1,500 Creation Credits, half the 3,000 the five would cost on their own. Skyrim Special Edition is also 80% off, down to $7.99 on PS5 and PS4.

**PPR take:** Nominations two days before GTA VI drops is either perfect timing or a little awkward, depending on how you look at it. It's eligible, but almost nobody will have played it when the list goes up, so don't be surprised if the Players' Voice vote turns into a GTA landslide later on. As for Skyrim....fifteen years and people are still finding reasons to go back to it. A hand cannon in Skyrim? Ok, I'm listening!
