**Raise your hand if you've ever tried to stuff a full armor set in a backpack and watched it bounce.** Yeah....me too. Wakapedia-CIG posted the [patch notes for Alpha 5.0 PTU build 13201184](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/190048/thread/star-citizen-alpha-5-0-ptu-patch-notes-1/9237938) on Spectrum around 12:17 PM ET today, just days after [build 13189690](/news/star-citizen-alpha-5-0-ptu-13189690-ttk-balance-missile-lock-squadron-42/) and its TTK test. It's still Wave 1 only, on a US-only PTU channel, with long term persistence on. The focus list hasn't changed much: Genesis, Nyx I, Starchitect locations, mission boards, Intraplanetary Server Meshing and the new missile lock interface, with the TTK pass still in the build for feedback.

## Inventory gets sane

This is the big one. Armor, clothing and magazines used to take up their measured physical volume, which is why a full kit wouldn't fit in a backpack and a few mags filled your chest. Now space is **assigned by category and usefulness**. Full armor sets fit in backpacks (the BUL-H4 super heavy fits a Combat Heavy pack), shirts, pants, jackets and gloves squeeze into any armor or undersuit pocket, flight suits take up the same room as undersuits, and magazines are tiered by weapon class from pistol up to heavy machine gun. Light combat and utility backpacks now take rifles, shotguns, SMGs, the GP-33 and the crossbow too.

## VR theater mode, rebuilt

For the VR crowd, theater mode is now a **virtual cinema** instead of a flat panel floating in your face. You get a curved screen in a room it actually lights up, size presets from Monitor up to Giant Screen, a "Screen Follows Me" option, environments like Stars, Aurora, Underwater and Orbit, and an option that shows the game in stereoscopic 3D using scene depth.

## Nyx I and the rest

Reputation and rewards are now set up across the remaining guilds on Nyx I, career board contracts unlock in order, and rep is running at **25x** for testing. Genesis also gets a fix for those straight-line cracks and mismatched textures along terrain patch borders, plus new reeds and wind-bending tree imposters. The ARGO MPUV modules are listed as purchasable now too. It's PTU....anything can change before LIVE.

**PPR take:** Honestly, the inventory pass is the kind of change nobody makes a trailer for, but everybody feels it. I've lost count of how many times I've played Tetris with a backpack at a med bay, so tiering by "usefulness" instead of real-world volume just makes sense for a game. Physics-based space was a cool idea on paper....in practice it was a chore. The VR cinema stuff is a nice surprise. It's a niche feature, but CIG clearly put real time into it and that tells me they're not ignoring the headset folks. And seeing reputation wired up across all of Nyx I's guilds is another sign 5.0 is getting closer to feature complete. If you're in Wave 1, go break it and leave feedback!
