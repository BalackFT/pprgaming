**CIG's latest Roadmap Roundup landed Wednesday afternoon, and the big news is on the Alpha 5.0 side.** The [November 18 Roadmap Roundup](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/comm-link/transmission/21455-Roadmap-Roundup-November-18-2026) moves 12 new cards into Committed for 5.0, and the [official Star Citizen account](https://x.com/RobertsSpaceInd/status/2123174173438558226) called out three of them by name: the Vehicle Combat Balance Pass, the Loot Refresh and ten faction outfit updates.

## What got committed for Alpha 5.0

The **Vehicle Combat Balance Pass** is the one most pilots will care about. CIG describes it as a balance pass on ship weapons with the focus on fighter combat, adjusting weapon performance, time to kill, and the balance between energy and ballistic weapons. There are no numbers in the card yet, so we don't know which direction any of that moves.

The **Loot Refresh** rebalances loot across sandbox locations and, the interesting part, puts rewards that used to be Wikelo exclusives into the general loot pool. CIG didn't list which items.

The other ten cards are **faction outfit updates for Nyx**. Eight are named factions (The Moraine, The First Legion, The Claw Salamanders, The Shattered Blade, The People's Alliance, Covalex, Rayari Inc. and Shubin Interstellar), each getting outfit variants and matching backpacks. The last two cover generic security and generic outlaws in Nyx.

On the [Release View](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/roadmap/release-view), "Committed" means a card has passed CIG's final review and has "a very low chance" of being pushed back. That's CIG's own wording, and it is still a roadmap, not a patch note. Alpha 5.0 is on the [PTU right now](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/spectrum/community/SC/forum/190048/thread/star-citizen-alpha-5-0-ptu-patch-notes-2) (build 13228337, Wave 3) with no Live date yet.

## IAE ships marked Released for 4.10

The Alpha 4.10 column caught up with IAE. The RSI Galaxy, the Anvil Liberator and the Drake Marauder are all marked **Released**, along with the Lightning Bolt Co. "Skarnov" DMR and the InterSec Defense Solutions Mission Pack, both of which shipped in 4.10.3. The **Gatac Hyūn**, Gatac's first combined mining and salvage ship, moved to **Committed** and will flip to Released once it's in the PU.

## PPR take

The combat pass is the card I'm watching. I fly the Marauder now, seven Size 3 guns and one pilot, so any change to time to kill or the energy vs ballistic split hits that ship right away. CIG says fighter combat is the focus, which is where the Marauder lives....so yeah, I'm curious and a little nervous. No numbers yet, and a Committed card isn't a patch note, so I'm not going to guess what moves until it shows up on the PTU.

The Loot Refresh is the sleeper. Pulling Wikelo-only rewards into the general pool could change how people chase gear in Nyx, and it depends a lot on which items CIG actually means. We'll see when the notes drop.

And it's nice seeing the Galaxy, the Lib and the Marauder all sitting in the Released column together. The Galaxy is a fine ship that still feels a little unfinished inside, BUT it's out, and the Hyūn is next in line.
