Fellow space nerds, take note: **No Man's Sky** is heading to **iPhone and iPad on October 28**, with pre-orders open now on the App Store as part of Apple's Indie Games Week.

According to Hello Games, this isn't a cut-down spin-off. The iOS version includes the game's many years of major updates, Voyagers and the recent Cosmos update among them. It runs on hardware as old as the **iPhone 11 and 3rd-gen iPad Air**, and the download is about 5GB.

**What to expect at launch:**

- **Free to try**, with a one-time **$19.99** in-app purchase to unlock the full game (per MacStories)
- New touch controls, plus controller support, and mouse and keyboard on iPad
- **No multiplayer** at launch, and planetary settlements need an iPhone 15 Pro or newer
- MacStories reports no cross-save or cross-play at launch either

Sean Murray compares it to the game's original Switch launch, where support grew over time based on player feedback. Hello Games also says it put a lot of work into battery life and heat, which is the real test for a game this big on a phone.

**PPR take:** a ten-year-old game that started rough and just kept getting better, now in your pocket. It's hard not to respect that.
