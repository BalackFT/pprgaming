**Capcom won the most trophies. Pinhead won the night.** The fifth Horror Game Awards went down Friday, November 20 at 26 Leake Street in London, the show's second year in person, and the [official winners list](https://www.thehorrorgameawards.com/news/your-winners-for-the-horror-game-awards-2026) ends with a bit of a surprise: **Clive Barker's Hellraiser: Revival** is Game of the Year, ahead of **Resident Evil Requiem**.

## Hellraiser's big night

Saber Interactive's first-person action horror game [launched on Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/1551980/) on October 8 and went into the show with eight nominations, [per our nominations story](https://pprgaming.com/news/horror-game-awards-2026-november-20-reanimal-resident-evil-requiem-nominations/). It left with Game of the Year and **Best Performance for Doug Bradley**, back as Pinhead, [according to Bloody Disgusting](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3976498/hellraiser-revival-wins-game-of-the-year-at-the-horror-game-awards-2026/).

## Requiem still cleaned up

Resident Evil Requiem came in with nine nominations and won four:

- **Players' Choice** (the fan vote)
- **Best Narrative**
- **Scariest Horror Game**
- **Most Accessible Horror**

**Best Adaptation** went to Zach Cregger's Resident Evil film. The upset is on the other end. Tarsier's **REANIMAL** led everyone with 10 nominations and won one, **Best New Multiplayer Horror**.

## Silent Hill, indies and the rest

Silent Hill had a good night. **Silent Hill: Townfall** won the new Critics' Choice award, Bloober Team's upcoming **Silent Hill** remake won Most Anticipated, Bloober won Best Developer, and composer **Akira Yamaoka** got the Lifetime Achievement award. On the indie side, **Tenebris Somnia** won Best Indie Horror and Best Art Direction, and its developer Saibot Studios won Best Indie Studio. **ROUTINE** won Technical Achievement, **Dead by Daylight** took Best Ongoing Horror, and **Creature Kitchen** won the new Best Cozy Horror award.

There were trailers too. Serafini Productions [showed six](https://bloody-disgusting.com/video-games/3976543/serafini-productions-drops-six-trailers-during-the-horror-game-awards-watch/), including the world premiere of **Ugly Within**, a third-person survival horror game from Japanese studio Yamaitachi.

## PPR take

I like this result. Requiem is the bigger game, and the fans clearly loved it, since Players' Choice is the one award that's purely their vote. But the juries went with Hellraiser for the top prize, and the show didn't just hand the crown to the biggest publisher in the room. That's the whole point of a show like this.

Doug Bradley winning for Pinhead is the cool part for me. The man played that role in the films back in the late '80s, and he's still the voice people hear when they think of the Cenobites. Getting recognized for it in a game, decades later, is a great moment.

Mrs. PPRGaming played both Requiem and Hellraiser on the PS5, and her vote goes to Resident Evil. She still thought Hellraiser was good, just with a lot of S&M. That's Hellraiser for you. She also played Silent Hill: Townfall and calls it the best of the recent Silent Hill games, though not on Requiem's level.

REANIMAL going 1 for 10 stings a little, though. Leading the nominations and leaving with one trophy happens, but Tarsier deserved better. And with The Game Awards still not giving horror its own category, the Horror Game Awards keep earning their spot on the calendar.
