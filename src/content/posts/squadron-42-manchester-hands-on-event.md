*Updated October 31, 2026: replaced tracker summaries with CIG's own words from This Week in Star Citizen and the Letter from the Chairman, and corrected the release window note. Squadron 42's official Steam page lists Q2 2027.*

**Around 140 backers got their hands on Squadron 42 this weekend, and the rest of us are still waiting on a date.** There was no CitizenCon in 2026. CIG said earlier this year there would be no event, in person or digital, while the studio focused on Star Citizen updates and getting Squadron 42 out ([Massively Overpowered](https://massivelyop.com/2026/05/07/star-citizen-hypes-its-may-14-defensecon-event-alpha-4-8s-group-activity-and-april-dev-progress/)). Instead, CIG held an **invite-only Squadron 42 hands-on event at its Manchester studio, October 9–11**.

## What CIG says happened

In its October 12 [This Week in Star Citizen](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/transmission/21374-This-Week-In-Star-Citizen), CIG said it "welcomed around 140 community members to Manchester for the first hands-on experience." Attendees spent **five hours playing Squadron 42**, followed by an extra hour in **a unique chapter of the story aboard an adrift Xi'an ship**. CIG added that sharp-eyed viewers may have caught a glimpse of that moment during CitizenCon Manchester 2954. The weekend also lined up with Star Citizen's birthday on October 10.

CIG said photos from the weekend are coming soon and pointed everyone to the Squadron 42 trailer and the Steam wishlist. The [Squadron 42 Steam page](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4045720/Squadron_42/) went live for wishlists on October 10.

## So where's the release date?

**No specific date was given at the event.** In the August 27 [Letter from the Chairman](https://robertsspaceindustries.com/en/comm-link/transmission/21301-Letter-From-The-Chairman), Chris Roberts said CIG had planned to release in Q4 2026 around IAE, but didn't want to launch "into the attention buzz saw of GTA6," and that "Q2 provides a better time for Squadron 42 to release." He also wrote that CIG would make "further announcements about our release plans during this time and a specific release date shortly afterwards."

Right now, the official Steam page lists the release as **Q2 2027**. That's a window, not a date, so the "shortly afterwards" part is still the thing to watch. We'll update the [Star Citizen Hub](/category/star-citizen/) the moment CIG names a day.

**PPR take:** I'll be honest, I'm a bit jealous of the folks who were in that room. Five hours of hands-on plus a Xi'an chapter is a LOT more than a vertical slice, and it tells me CIG has a lot of game ready to put in front of people. The part I keep coming back to is Chris Roberts saying a specific date would come "shortly afterwards." CIG put that in writing, so now it's on them to deliver it. Until then, Q2 2027 on Steam is the best we've got. If you haven't wishlisted it yet, go do it, and keep an eye out for those weekend photos. 🚀
