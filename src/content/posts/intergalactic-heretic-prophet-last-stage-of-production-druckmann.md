**Naughty Dog's sci-fi game is further along than a lot of people assumed.** Speaking with Patton Oswalt at Comic Creators Block Party 2026, studio head and director Neil Druckmann gave the first real status check on *Intergalactic: The Heretic Prophet* in months.

"We're kind of at the last stage of production, where we can play it, and we're refining and polishing it, and I can't wait to show it to the world," Druckmann said, [per VGC](https://www.videogameschronicle.com/news/intergalactic-is-in-the-last-stage-of-production-where-its-playable-and-being-refined-neil-druckmann-says/). He kept the details locked down, but he did describe what the studio is going for. Naughty Dog has been making "relatively grounded" games with Uncharted and The Last of Us since 2005, he said, and wanted to go back to sci-fi for the first time since Jak and Daxter, combining the pulp action of Uncharted, the serious drama of The Last of Us and the fantasy side of Jak. The goal, in his words, was to keep the studio's cinematic storytelling "and go even deeper with gameplay systems."

## When we'll see it

Not this year. Druckmann [told Oswalt](https://kotaku.com/neil-druckman-intergalactic-the-heretic-prophet-2027-playable-polish-2000756229) that "we're going to show a lot next year. We're going to show gameplay, more of the story," adding that he thinks it will "really surprise people with what they think the game is and what it actually is." He also said the game has a really big cast, and that Naughty Dog wants players to discover who's in it by playing rather than through early reveals.

That lines up with what he said in September, when he promised the studio would stay quiet for the rest of 2026 and then "fully reveal" the game in 2027. There's still no release date or platform list beyond Sony's own announcement history. [Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/intergalactic-heretic-prophet-naughty-dog-neil-druckmann-update-playable/) notes the game, first announced in 2024, is Naughty Dog's first new IP since The Last of Us in 2013, and that development reportedly involved overtime to hit deadlines. [Kotaku](https://kotaku.com/neil-druckman-intergalactic-the-heretic-prophet-2027-playable-polish-2000756229) also points to rumors of a 2027 target, but Druckmann didn't confirm a release year here, only that the big showing is next year.

## PPR take

"Playable and being polished" is a meaningful milestone for a studio that has said almost nothing since the 2024 reveal. It means the systems are in and the team is tuning rather than building, which is usually when a release window starts to firm up. I'm careful not to read a 2027 launch into this, though. Druckmann promised a 2027 reveal, not a 2027 release, and Naughty Dog has a history of long polish phases. The part that interests me most is that line about deeper gameplay systems. Naughty Dog's games have always been gorgeous corridors with great writing, and a sci-fi bounty hunter that plays with more freedom would be a real change for them. The crunch reports are the one sour note, and I hope the studio gets through this last stretch without burning people out. Either way, if they're going to "surprise people" next year, I'm in for it.
