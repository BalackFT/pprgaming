# Realm dispatch, October 2026

Hey all,

Welcome to the very first PPRGaming newsletter! October gave me a LOT to cover on pprgaming.com. Here's what I think was worth your time, Star Citizen first (obviously).

## Around 140 backers played five hours of Squadron 42

CIG held an invite-only hands-on in Manchester on October 9–11, and attendees got five hours of Squadron 42 plus an extra hour in a chapter aboard an adrift Xi'an ship. Still no release date though.....the Steam page says Q2 2027, and Chris Roberts put in writing that a specific date would come "shortly afterwards." Now it's on CIG to deliver it.

[Read the full story](https://pprgaming.com/news/squadron-42-manchester-hands-on-event/)

## Nyx I tech preview: Starchitect, Guild Halls and one planet on three servers

The third Genesis tech preview went to Evocati with the Starchitect location tool, Guild Hall contract boards and Nyx I split across three servers. This is the first time Nyx I sounds like a place you could actually base out of. If the "hub and spoke" loop works, I can see myself staying planetside for hours.

[Read the full story](https://pprgaming.com/news/star-citizen-nyx-starchitect-guild-halls-tech-preview/)

## The Connie Mk IV becomes a "modern classic"

The Connie everybody already owns got one of its biggest refits in years: a clearer cockpit, a habitat with suit lockers and weapon racks, physical components, and reworked lifts, turrets and snub bay. Owners get a free commemorative paint too. It's nice to see the old workhorse get real love instead of just new ships getting all the attention.

[Read the full story](https://pprgaming.com/news/star-citizen-constellation-mk-iv-gold-standard-modern-classic/)

## 4.10.3 is open to everyone on the PTU

All Waves got build 13055118 on October 30. You can now quantum to the Shattered Blade Raid locations, contracts actually pay out their blueprint rewards, and the Skarnov got a real rework (it even makes a firing sound now....lol). No live date yet, but this is the build where 4.10.3 starts to look like a patch I actually want to play.

[Read the full story](https://pprgaming.com/news/star-citizen-alpha-4-10-3-ptu-all-waves-13055118-vanduul-skarnov/)

## EA is closing BioWare

After more than 30 years, the studio behind Baldur's Gate, KOTOR, Mass Effect and Dragon Age is closing, and early work on the next Mass Effect is over. A week later, Bloomberg reported EA chose to fund Star Wars Battlefront III instead, a game that hasn't even started development yet. That one hurt.

[Read the full story](https://pprgaming.com/news/bioware-closing-ea-mass-effect-cancelled/) and [the Bloomberg follow-up](https://pprgaming.com/news/bioware-closure-bloomberg-star-wars-battlefront-iii-over-mass-effect/)

## GTA 6's Leonida is nearly three Rockstar maps combined

Rockstar told Edge that Leonida is nearly as big as the GTA IV, GTA V and Red Dead Redemption 2 maps combined, with around 600,000 NPC animations, way more interiors and weather that actually moves. No spoilers in this one, just the world and the systems.

[Read the full story](https://pprgaming.com/news/gta-6-map-size-600000-npc-animations-edge-rockstar/)

## Starfield mod spotlights

Two good ones this month. Seamless Planet Takeoffs by 0xBobby gets rid of the takeoff loading screen, so you keep climbing right up into space. Holsters SFSE by ScarecroOw finally puts your guns on your back and hip instead of making them vanish.

[Seamless Planet Takeoffs](https://pprgaming.com/news/starfield-seamless-planet-takeoffs-mod-spotlight/) and [Holsters SFSE](https://pprgaming.com/news/starfield-holsters-sfse-visible-holstered-weapons-mod-spotlight/)
